About Baywatch

Your Waterfront
Sanctuary

Located at 1150 Fort Pickens Road, Baywatch Condominiums has been a cherished Pensacola Beach community for over two decades. Our 87 two-bedroom, two-bathroom units overlook the serene Intracoastal Waterway, offering residents a peaceful retreat just a short drive from the island's best restaurants, shopping, and nightlife.

Amenities

Everything You Need

Baywatch offers a range of amenities designed for the coastal lifestyle — relax by the pool, fish off the pier, or stroll the boardwalk.

Community Pool

Heated pool with sundeck overlooking the bay. Summer hours 7 AM to 10 PM daily.

Bayside Beach

Private beach access on the Intracoastal. The Gulf of Mexico is just across the street.

Community Pier & Dock

Private pier for fishing and relaxation. Watch dolphins, pelicans, and stunning sunsets.

Pond Boardwalk

Scenic boardwalk with fountains and wildlife — turtles, ducks, herons, and pelicans.

Trolley Access

The Pensacola Beach trolley stops nearby — ride to restaurants, bars, and attractions for free.

Community Events

Regular social gatherings, holiday celebrations, and board meetings keep our community connected.
