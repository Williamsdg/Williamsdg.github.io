Office Toss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

The short version

Office Toss does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Period.

Data collection

The app collects no personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, location data, contacts, or any other data about you or your device. It contains no third-party analytics SDKs and no advertising SDKs, and it makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, best scores, and unlocked locations) is saved in a small file on your device only. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example, adding online leaderboards), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
