More Than a Museum. A Memorial to Courage, Service, and Sacrifice.

A Legacy of Service

90 Years of American History

Commissioned into Service

The USCGC Ingham (WPG-35) is commissioned at the Philadelphia Navy Yard as a Treasury-class cutter, one of the largest and most powerful in the Coast Guard fleet.

1936
Treasury-class cutter, Philadelphia Navy Yard • USCG Photo

World War II — Battle of the Atlantic

Earns her first Presidential Unit Citation for convoy escort duty in the North Atlantic, protecting Allied supply lines from German U-boats in some of the war's fiercest battles.

1942
Ingham in heavy seas, convoy escort duty • NARA / U.S. Navy

U-626 Engagement

Credited with sinking German submarine U-626 during convoy operations, demonstrating the crew's extraordinary skill and bravery under fire.

1943
Ingham escorting convoy, circa 1943 • NARA / U.S. Navy

Vietnam War Service

Receives her second Presidential Unit Citation for operations in the Western Pacific, making the Ingham the only Coast Guard cutter to receive this honor twice.

1967
Ingham off Vietnam, 1968–69 • U.S. Navy

Decommissioned After 52 Years

After more than half a century of continuous service spanning every major conflict, the Ingham is decommissioned with full military honors.

1988
Ingham at Patriots Point, 1990 • NPS / James P. Delgado

National Historic Landmark

Permanently berthed in Key West, Florida, the Ingham serves as a floating museum and memorial, welcoming thousands of visitors annually.

TODAY
Ingham at Key West, 2023 • U.S. Navy Photo
