The Origin

Built for the Conversations
That Change Everything

“The best deals in history weren't closed in boardrooms. They were closed on the back nine.”
CartPath ATL was founded on one belief: that the most meaningful relationships in business are built between the tees. We curate private golf experiences that pair Atlanta's top executives with the athletes and coaches who defined SEC football, NFL greatness, and Southern sports legacy. This isn't a tee-time app. It's a private community for those who understand that four hours on the course are worth more than four years of networking events.
