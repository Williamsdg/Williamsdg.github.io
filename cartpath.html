The CartPath Concept

Where Business Meets the Fairway

CartPath is a private equity-style membership platform that curates exclusive golf experiences between C-suite executives and retired professional athletes. Every round is an opportunity — for networking, deal-making, and unforgettable moments on the world's finest courses.

Curated Pairings

Our concierge team matches executives with athletes based on interests, industry, and playing level for the perfect round.

Elite Access

Play alongside Hall of Famers, Super Bowl champions, and All-Stars at courses you've only seen on television.

Private Network

Join a vetted community of founders, investors, and executives who understand that the best deals happen between the tees.

The Experience

How CartPath Works

1

Apply for Membership

Submit your application with professional credentials. Our membership committee reviews each applicant to maintain the exclusivity and caliber of our network. Acceptance rate: 12%.

2

Browse the Athlete Roster

Explore our curated roster of retired professional athletes rated 1 to 5 stars. Filter by sport, personality, handicap, and availability. Each athlete profile includes their story, playing style, and what makes a round with them unforgettable.

3

Book Your Round

Select your athlete, choose from our portfolio of premier courses, and let our concierge handle every detail — from tee times and caddies to post-round dining at the clubhouse.

4

Play, Connect, Close

Enjoy 18 holes with a legend. The relationships forged on the fairway become partnerships in the boardroom. Every round is photographed and documented for your personal archive.

The Inner Circle

More Than a Round

CartPath is not just a booking platform — it is a private community built around the game. Members gain access to exclusive updates, meaningful connections, and gatherings that extend far beyond the 18th hole.

Member Updates

Receive exclusive dispatches from the inner circle — tournament recaps, new athlete additions, member milestones, and insider perspectives you won't find anywhere else.

The Clubhouse

A private digital space where members connect, share experiences, exchange course recommendations, and build relationships that transcend the fairway.

Annual Gatherings

From the Founders' Dinner to charity scrambles, CartPath hosts in-person events that bring the community together for unforgettable experiences beyond the screen.
