01 — The Approach

02 — Phase 1: Launch Site

Where We Are Right Now

The site is live, applications are flowing, and the backend is built. This phase is about collecting founding members and building anticipation for the app.

What's Already Built

Pitch Site (williamsdigital.io/cartpath) — Full marketing site with clubhouse loading animation, ATL athlete roster with pricing, coaches section, community feed preview, merch showcase, membership tiers, and application flow.

Live Scores (williamsdigital.io/cartpath-scores) — Masters-style leaderboard with real SportsData.io integration, upcoming tournament schedule, and major championship countdown.

Application System — Multi-step form → AWS Lambda → branded Resend emails (thank you + notification). Applications stored in PostgreSQL on AWS.

Admin Dashboard (williamsdigital.io/cartpath-admin) — Full application management with approve/decline/waive fee, branded emails, search, filters, CSV export, internal notes, and timeline tracking.

AWS Infrastructure — Cognito auth, RDS Postgres, S3 storage, DynamoDB for chat/feed, API Gateway, CloudFront CDN. Scalable to 50,000+ members.

Mobile App (code complete) — 18+ screens in Expo/React Native. Auth, roster, coaches, booking, live PGA scores, Discord-style chat, community feed, member profiles. Ready for beta.

Week 1 — Now

  • Share williamsdigital.io/cartpath directly

Expand outreach. Get the founding members talking about CartPath. Target: 15-25 approved members.
  • Athlete referrals — have Ronnie Brown, Jason Campbell share with their network
  • UGA/Auburn alumni events — Mayo attends with the pitch site on his phone

Weeks 4-6
First Rounds & Content

Key Insight: The site doesn't need the app to generate revenue. Members can pay via Stripe links in the approval email and book rounds through Mayo's concierge service. The app enhances the experience — it doesn't create it. Don't wait for the app to start making money.
03 — Phase 2: Beta App

Private Beta with Founding Members

Before the app goes public, test it with the people who are already invested. Their feedback will make the public launch bulletproof.

Beta Criteria

Launch the beta when you have at least 10-15 approved, paying members. This is the minimum needed for the chat to feel active and the feed to have content. An empty app kills momentum.

Why 10-15? With 10 members and 3 chat channels active, you'll see 5-10 messages per day. That's enough to feel alive. With 5 members, the chat will be dead and members will stop opening the app.

Beta Timeline

June 2026 — Week 1
TestFlight / Internal Distribution
Deploy to Apple TestFlight and Android internal testing. Invite founding members personally.
  • Build production app with EAS Build
  • Upload to TestFlight (iOS) and Google Play internal track (Android)
  • Send personal invite emails to each beta member
  • Include "Beta Tester" badge in their profile — makes them feel special
June — July 2026
4-6 Week Beta Period
Let members use the app for real. Collect feedback aggressively.
  • Dedicated #beta-feedback channel in the chat
  • Weekly check-in with 2-3 members for detailed feedback
  • Track: what do they use most? What's confusing? What's missing?
  • Fix bugs in real-time — fast iteration builds trust
  • Mayo books at least 5 rounds through the app during beta
Late July 2026
Beta Graduation
Polish based on feedback. Prepare for public App Store launch.
  • Final round of bug fixes
  • App Store screenshots from real member data (not mocks)
  • Testimonials from beta members for the App Store listing

06 — Risks & Mitigations

What Could Go Wrong

Risk: Not enough members sign up

Mitigation: Waive fees for 3-5 high-profile founding members who bring credibility and their own networks. One CEO posting about CartPath on LinkedIn is worth 100 cold emails. Also, start with personal outreach, not ads — this is a relationship business.

Risk: App launches with empty community

Mitigation: That's exactly why we're doing site-first. Don't launch the app until you have 10-15 paying members. Use the beta period to seed the chat with real conversations. Mayo should post in the feed daily during beta to set the tone.

Risk: Athletes don't commit

Mitigation: Start with the most accessible athletes first — Jason Campbell, Ronnie Brown, David Pollack are all "Available" and connected in Atlanta. One great round with photos and a testimonial sells the next athlete. Don't need all 17 on day one.

Risk: Tech issues on launch day

Mitigation: AWS infrastructure is enterprise-grade and auto-scales. The beta period stress-tests everything before public launch. Williams Digital is on retainer for immediate bug fixes.
