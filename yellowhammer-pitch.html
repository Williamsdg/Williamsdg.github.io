Common Questions

The Honest Answers

Why is it so much cheaper than agencies?

Two reasons. First, the site is already built — there's no design discovery phase, no wireframing weeks, no team of 4 people billing hours. Second, my overhead is intentionally low — no office, no middle managers, no upsell motion. The savings get passed through to you instead of paying for a downtown agency lease.

What happens if I want changes later?

Two options. Either send me an email and I'll quote one-off edits at $75/hr (most small changes are 15–30 minutes). Or pick up the optional $150/mo retainer which covers unlimited small edits, new menu pages, seasonal banners, and quarterly content refreshes — no contract, cancel any month.

Can my team edit the site ourselves?

Yes — if someone on the team is comfortable with basic HTML. If not, that's exactly what the monthly retainer is for. Many coffee shops prefer that route because their staff is focused on coffee, not code.

What about online ordering and gift cards?

Both are optional add-ons. Online ordering integrates with services like Toast, Square, or a custom checkout — $750 setup. Gift cards integrate with whatever POS you're using — $850 setup. We can scope these in or hold off until you're ready.

What if I want to switch developers later?

You get the full source code on day one. Any developer in the world can pick it up because it's standard HTML/CSS/JS — not a proprietary builder. You're never locked in.

How do you handle hosting, email, and the domain?

Hosting is free via static CDN (your only cost is the ~$15/yr domain renewal). Email is typically Google Workspace at $6/user/mo if you want @yellowhammercoffee.com addresses — I'll set it up for you at no extra charge. The domain stays in your name, on your account.

Do you do social media or photography?

Not directly — I focus on the website. But I have trusted referrals for both in the Mobile / Gulf Coast area and can introduce you if needed. The website itself is built to make great photography shine, so it's worth pairing.
