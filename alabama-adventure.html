What We Found
Six gaps costing you real money
We analyzed your current site, guest reviews, and competitive landscape. Here's where the biggest opportunities are hiding.
🐌

Slow Mobile Experience

Heavy JavaScript frameworks (Revolution Slider, Elementor, Jetpack, jQuery) stacked on each other. Families Googling "Alabama Adventure tickets" on their phone hit a slow page — every second of load time costs ~7% in conversions.

Revenue Impact
🚪

Ticket Handoff Breaks Trust

Clicking "Buy Tickets" sends visitors to centeredgeonline.com — a different domain with different branding. This is a known conversion killer. Guests feel unsure, and some abandon the purchase entirely.

Conversion Loss
👥

Group Revenue Under-Marketed

Birthday parties, church groups, corporate events, and school field trips each need targeted landing pages. Right now they share one generic "Groups" link — leaving significant revenue on the table.

Revenue Impact
🎢

Attractions Feel Like a List

Rides and slides are listed, not sold. No "must-do" energy, no categories, no punchy descriptions. Guests should browse attractions like Netflix — not a spreadsheet.

Experience Gap
🎫

Season Pass Isn't Pushed

Season passes are where parks make recurring revenue. There's no comparison table, no "visit twice and it pays for itself" messaging. This is money walking out the door.

Revenue Impact
⭐

No Social Proof On-Site

No testimonials, no review ratings, no guest photos. With mixed online reviews, controlling the narrative on your own site is critical for building trust with first-time visitors.

Conversion Loss
