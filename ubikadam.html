// QUESTIONS

Frequently transmitted questions

Is this a real AI or is it just Adam? +

It's both, and that's the magic. Adam does the planning with AI tools — heatmap modeling, channel optimization, anomaly detection — then Adam, an actual human with an actual ladder, does the install. The AI never falls off a roof. Adam tries not to.

Do I have to buy Ubiquiti gear from you? +

Nope. We quote hardware at cost and you own all of it. If you already have UniFi gear, even better — we'll fold it into the design. No vendor lock-in, no monthly cloud ransom.

Will the install look ugly? +

No. Clean mounts, hidden cable runs, in-wall drops where possible, and color-matched APs that disappear into the ceiling. Your spouse will not file a complaint.

What if the AI is wrong about where to put an access point? +

Then we move it. Coverage is verified on-site with real measurements before we call a job done — the model proposes, the meter disposes. If a spot underperforms, we relocate at no charge.

Can you really cover the barn? +

The barn is our love language. Outdoor-rated access points, directional antennas, and point-to-point wireless bridges mean detached buildings get the same speeds as the living room.
