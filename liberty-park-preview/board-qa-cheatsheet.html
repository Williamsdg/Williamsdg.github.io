Board Q&A Cheat Sheet

12 questions you'll get + your one-paragraph answers · Confidential, internal reference
Liberty Park Board Meeting May 11, 2026
Opening Stance
Lead with the savings number ($18K–$84K/yr vs AppFolio). Frame the decision as "rent vs. own" — they're not picking between vendors, they're picking between continuing to rent a platform or starting to own one. Most board questions are really about risk; have a confident, specific answer for each one.
Q1PRICING
"What if AppFolio drops their price after we sign?"
It doesn't change the math. AppFolio's per-door model still scales against Liberty Park as you add homes — even at half their published rate, you'd pay $13K–$47K/yr. Our flat $500/mo doesn't move. Plus, you own the platform; AppFolio is rented forever.
Q2CONTINUITY
"What if you get hit by a bus?"
Two safeguards: (1) Source code escrow — your codebase is held in a third-party Git escrow that releases to Liberty Park automatically if WD ceases operations. (2) Standard infrastructure — runs on AWS / Vercel / Supabase, which any developer can pick up. You'd have 30–60 days to find a replacement with zero service disruption.
Q3SECURITY
"How safe is homeowners' financial data?"
Card data never touches our servers — Stripe handles 100% of card processing as a PCI-DSS Level 1 provider. Database encryption at rest (AES-256), TLS 1.2+ in transit, daily encrypted backups across multiple AWS regions. Hosting partners hold current SOC 2 Type II certifications; their attestation reports are available on request.
Q4DATA
"What happens to our data if we leave?"
It's yours, in writing. Within 5 business days of a termination notice: (1) full database export in CSV, JSON, and SQL, (2) source code repository handed over, (3) domain transferred. Zero offboarding fees. The agreement says so explicitly — no auto-renewal traps, no extraction charges.
Q5FAKE NOTICES
"Could a homeowner screenshot a fake compliance notice?"
Every notice has three layers of authenticity: (1) signed PDF receipt with a tamper-evident hash, (2) full audit log entry visible to staff and the homeowner, (3) every notice is queryable by ID in the portal — if the homeowner can't pull it up at /notices/CN-2026-148, it's not real. The audit log answers "who saw what when" in a compliance dispute.
Q6PERMISSIONS
"Can the board see who's in arrears without exposing it to staff?"
Yes — role-based permissions. Five tiers: board, director, compliance officer, vendor, homeowner. The board gets read-only views of financials and compliance with full audit log access; staff see only the residents in their assigned neighborhoods; vendors see only their assigned work orders. Configured during discovery week.
Q7MIGRATION
"How do we move 1,547 records from the current system?"
Standard playbook: direct API import if the current vendor offers one, or CSV/spreadsheet export as fallback. We migrate residents, 5 years of payment history, ARC requests with photos, violations, work orders, governing docs. Spot-check 50 records by hand. Two-week parallel run before cutover. Migration cost is included — no per-record fees.
Q8UPCHARGES
"What costs extra that we should know about?"
Three things: (1) Stripe fees (2.9% + $0.30 card / 0.8% capped at $5 ACH) — typically passed to homeowners or absorbed via a small convenience fee, your call; (2) SMS overage beyond 2,000/mo at $0.012 each — typically not hit; (3) major add-ons like a native app ($8K–$15K) or QuickBooks sync ($2K setup). Everything else is included.
Q9SUPPORT
"What if there's a problem at 9pm on Saturday?"
Critical issues — payment outage, mass login failure — answered in <1 hour, 24/7. Standard tickets: <24 business hours. Direct line to me — email, text, or call. Your director gets my mobile number day one. No help desk, no ticket queue. Status page at status.libertypark.com shows uptime in real time.
Q10REFERENCES
"Who else have you done this for?"
Honest answer: Liberty Park would be our flagship HOA client. That's why the rate is structured the way it is — we're trading a multi-year reference relationship for the Flagship rate. We have other small-business and pitch-stage clients (Buy Auburn, Plainsman Art) — happy to share. You'd be the first HOA, and we're investing in making this one a case study.
Q11SCALE
"What if we add a 16th neighborhood next year?"
Cost stays flat. That's the whole point of a non-per-door pricing model. Per-door SaaS would charge for every new home; we don't. The Professional plan handles up to ~3,000 homes comfortably. If you ever cross that, we'd talk about the Premium plan ($750/mo) — still a fraction of per-door alternatives.
Q12TIMELINE
"Why 8 weeks? AppFolio claims 3."
AppFolio is configuration of an existing template — quick because you're squeezing your workflow into their system. We're building around your workflow, with two rounds of design revisions and a parallel run before cutover. Going live July 6 means your fall financials and 2027 budget cycle run on the new system from day one — instead of mid-cycle, which is its own headache.
If you don't know an answer: Don't fake it. Say "Great question — let me get back to you tomorrow with a precise answer rather than guess." Boards trust honest "I don't know" more than smooth bullshit. Then send the answer within 24 hours via email + cc Jennifer.

If they table the decision: Don't push. Ask "What additional information would be useful before the next vote?" Get specific — that's your follow-up roadmap.

The real close: "I'd rather you say no today than yes for the wrong reason. But if you're ready to move forward, I can have the service agreement in your inbox by tomorrow morning."
