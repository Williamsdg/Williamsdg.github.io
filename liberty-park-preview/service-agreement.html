1. Scope of Services

Williams Digital ("WD") will design, build, host, and maintain custom web software for Liberty Park Community Association ("Client") consisting of: (a) a public-facing community website, (b) a homeowner portal, and (c) a staff/board admin portal. Detailed deliverables and acceptance criteria are described in the attached SOW.

2. Term & Renewal

Initial term is twelve (12) months beginning at production launch. After the initial term, the Agreement renews month-to-month and may be terminated by either party with sixty (60) days' written notice. There is no auto-renewal beyond month-to-month and no multi-year price lock-in for the Client.

3. Fees & Payment Schedule

One-time setup fee: $3,000, billed 50% at kickoff and 50% at production launch. Recurring fee: $500/month (Professional plan) for the first 24 months ("Flagship Period"). After the Flagship Period, fees may adjust no more than the U.S. Consumer Price Index (All Urban Consumers) annual change. Invoices are Net-30 via ACH or check. Stripe processing fees on Client transactions are passed through to homeowners or Client at Client's election.

4. Service-Level Commitments

  • Uptime: 99.9% monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance announced ≥48 hours in advance.
  • Critical issue response: < 1 hour, 24/7 (payment outage, mass login failure, data loss).
  • Standard ticket response: < 24 business hours, M–F 8am–6pm Central.
  • Service credits: Monthly fee is prorated for downtime exceeding the 99.9% commitment.

5. Data Ownership & Portability

Client owns all Client Data — resident records, payments, ARC requests, work orders, documents — at all times. WD provides full data exports in CSV, JSON, and SQL formats on request, at no charge, within five (5) business days. Source code for the Client's instance is delivered to a Client-controlled Git repository on request and may be retained by Client perpetually.

6. Termination & Offboarding

If either party terminates, WD will: (a) deliver a complete data export within 5 business days, (b) hand over source code repository access, (c) initiate domain transfer within 5 business days, and (d) charge no offboarding, exit, or data extraction fees. Client may continue to operate the platform on its own infrastructure post-termination.

7. Security, Privacy & Compliance

WD will host the platform on infrastructure providers (AWS, Vercel, Supabase) holding current SOC 2 Type II certifications. Card data is processed exclusively by Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified); WD does not store payment card numbers. Data at rest is encrypted with AES-256; data in transit uses TLS 1.2+. WD will notify Client of any confirmed data breach within 24 hours of discovery.

8. Confidentiality

Each party will treat the other's non-public information as confidential and will not disclose it except to employees and agents who need it to perform under this Agreement. This obligation survives termination by 3 years.

9. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement exceeds the total fees paid by Client to WD in the 12 months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, or special damages.

10. Insurance

WD will maintain Errors & Omissions / Professional Liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and General Liability insurance throughout the term. Certificate of insurance available on request.

11. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Alabama. Any dispute will be resolved in the state or federal courts of Jefferson County, Alabama.

12. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the attached SOW, constitutes the entire agreement and supersedes all prior discussions. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
