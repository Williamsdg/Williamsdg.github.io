QUESTIONS WE GET ASKED

Frequently Asked Questions

Don't see your question? Email Dylan directly — you'll get an answer the same day.

How is this different from AppFolio, Buildium, or PayHOA? +
Those tools charge per door — at 1,547 homes, that's $700–$7,700/month forever. Williams Digital is a flat-rate custom platform built specifically for Liberty Park's workflow. You also own the codebase outright; with the SaaS tools, you're renting and locked in.
Can homeowners pay by ACH/bank draft, not just credit card? +
Yes. The platform integrates with Stripe, which supports both ACH bank draft (lower fees, ~$0.80 per transaction) and credit/debit cards (~2.9% + $0.30). Most associations route assessments through ACH and use cards as a fallback. Auto-pay works for both.
What about board members — do they get separate access? +
Yes. Role-based permissions support board, director, compliance officer, vendor, and homeowner views. Board members typically get read-only access to financials, ARC, and compliance dashboards plus a private board document repository. We configure roles during the discovery phase.
What happens to our data if we ever decide to leave? +
It's yours. You get a full database export (CSV, JSON, SQL), the source code repo, and your domain — transferred within 5 business days. Zero offboarding fees. The full offboarding terms are written into your service agreement, not buried in a TOS.
Who handles the day-to-day — us or your team? +
Your team handles operations (residents, payments, ARC, work orders) just like today — the platform makes it faster. Williams Digital handles the technical side: hosting, security patches, feature improvements, bug fixes, and direct support via Slack. Monthly check-ins keep things aligned.
What if we need a custom feature down the road? +
Two paths: small adjustments (text changes, new fields, dashboard tweaks) are included in your monthly plan. Larger features get a written quote with a fixed price — no hourly billing surprises. Most asks fall in the “included” bucket.
Is there a contract minimum? +
12-month minimum to recoup the build investment. After that, month-to-month with 60 days' notice. No auto-renewal traps, no “3-year” pricing locks. The flagship-customer rate holds for the first 24 months.
What happens if Williams Digital goes out of business? +
A fair question. Two safeguards: (1) Source code is held in a third-party Git escrow that releases to you if Williams Digital ceases operations. (2) The platform runs on standard infrastructure (AWS / Vercel / Supabase) that any developer can pick up — nothing proprietary. You'd have a 30–60 day window to find a new partner with no service disruption.
