How push works

When a resident first opens the portal on their phone, they're prompted (one time) to add it to their home screen. From there, the portal behaves like a native app:

  • ✔ App icon on home screen, no “Liberty Park.app” in browser bar
  • ✔ Native push notifications, including Apple Push Service on iOS 16.4+
  • ✔ Offline access to recently viewed documents
  • ✔ Full functionality without an app store download
  • ✔ $0 cost — no Apple/Google developer fees

If you want a true native app down the road, that's a $8K–$15K add-on. Most associations find PWA push covers 95% of what they need.

Quiet Hours

Time windows when only emergency alerts get through.

Daily quiet hours 9 PM – 7 AM
Weekend quiet hours All Saturday
Vacation mode Off

Emergency alerts (severe weather, lockdowns, gas leaks) bypass quiet hours by default. Everything else is queued and delivered at the next allowed window.

Multiple Recipients per Home

Each household can list multiple emails/phones — spouses, property managers for rentals, family caretakers. Each recipient sets their own preferences independently.
