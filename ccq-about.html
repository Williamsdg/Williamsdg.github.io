OUR STORY

She's Strong, Yet Sweet

The Cuban Coffee Queen is more than a name — she's the head of the family, the heart of the culture, and the reason everyone gathers around the table.

Born on the Waterfront

It started with a window on Margaret Street and a simple idea: bring real Cuban coffee to Key West. Not the watered-down, mass-produced kind — the strong, sweet, soul-warming cafecito that families across Cuba have been making for generations.

From day one, the line wrapped around the block. Word spread fast — locals, fishermen, cruise visitors, chefs, bartenders — everyone needed their fix. What began as a single coffee window became five locations, a full roasting operation, and a brand that represents Key West as much as the sunset itself.

Coffee is more than just a beverage — it's a cultural experience that connects people and communities around the world.

Roasted Right Here in Key West

Every bag of Cuban Coffee Queen coffee is roasted at our Key Lime Square roasting facility, just steps from our Downtown location. We source the highest quality beans from South America, Central America, and Africa, and roast them in small batches to bring out the perfect flavor profile.

Our Queen's Roast has won the Bubba Award for Best Coffee in Key West four consecutive years — and we're not slowing down. Whether you like it dark and bold or light and fruity, we roast it fresh and ship it nationwide.

OUR JOURNEY

From a Window to a Key West Icon

The Beginning

Margaret Street Waterfront

Cuban Coffee Queen opens its first window on Margaret Street at the Key West waterfront. One espresso machine, one mission: real Cuban coffee.

Downtown Expansion

Key Lime Square

The demand grows. We open our Downtown location at Key Lime Square, right at the corner of Southard and Duval — the heart of Old Town.

Clinton Square

Cruise Port Location

Recognizing the cruise port opportunity, we open at Clinton Square to serve the thousands of visitors arriving by ship every week.

Upper Duval

1130 Duval Street

Our Duval Street location opens at 1130 Duval, bringing Cuban Coffee Queen to the quieter, residential end of Key West's most famous street.

The Roasters

In-House Roasting Facility

We build our own roasting facility at Key Lime Square. Every bean is now roasted in-house, giving us full control over freshness and quality.

Today

Five Locations & Growing

Five locations, an award-winning roast, nationwide shipping, and a subscription service. The Queen isn't done yet.
