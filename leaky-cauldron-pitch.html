About The Leaky Cauldron

⚔

The Leaky Cauldron has been the wizarding world's most trusted independent fan news source since the earliest days of the internet. Like its namesake — the famous pub on Charing Cross Road that serves as the gateway between the Muggle and magical worlds — we bridge the gap between fans and the franchise they love.

We are not associated with J.K. Rowling, Warner Bros., or any of the official producers of the Harry Potter franchise. We are fans, writing for fans, powered by a love of magic.
