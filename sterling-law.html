What We Do

Areas of Practice

Our attorneys bring decades of specialized experience across a range of legal disciplines to protect your interests and secure the best possible outcomes.

Corporate Law

Strategic counsel for mergers, acquisitions, joint ventures, and corporate governance. We guide businesses through their most critical transactions.

Litigation

Aggressive courtroom representation in high-stakes disputes. From trial preparation to appellate advocacy, we fight tenaciously for every client.

Personal Injury

Dedicated advocacy for injury victims. We pursue maximum compensation for medical malpractice, auto accidents, workplace injuries, and wrongful death.

Real Estate

Comprehensive legal support for commercial and residential property matters, including transactions, zoning disputes, title issues, and landlord-tenant law.

Intellectual Property

Protecting your innovations, brands, and creative works. Expert counsel in patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, and IP litigation.

Criminal Defense

Vigorous defense of your rights and freedom. We handle federal and state charges including white-collar crimes, DUI, drug offenses, and violent crimes.
