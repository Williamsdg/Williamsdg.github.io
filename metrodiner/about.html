Welcome to Metro Diner

Metro Diner started as a small neighborhood diner in Jacksonville, Florida, with a simple mission: serve honest, homestyle comfort food in a place where everyone feels welcome. What began as a local favorite quickly grew into something much bigger.

After being featured on Guy Fieri's hit show Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives, word spread fast. People from all over came to try the Fried Chicken & Waffles and the Iron City Meatloaf — and they kept coming back.

"Whatever you want, we say go for it. Want breakfast for dinner? You got it. Craving a burger at 8 AM? We won't judge."

Today, Metro Diner has grown to locations across the Southeast and beyond, but the heart of what we do hasn't changed. Every dish is made from scratch with the same care and passion as day one.
