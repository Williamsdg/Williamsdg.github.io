The Artist

Where Color Finds
Its Balance

Shannon Harris is an original artist whose work lives at the intersection of bold color and quiet emotion. Working from her studio in Vestavia, Alabama, she creates paintings that transform spaces with warmth, personality, and meaning.

"My best gift as an artist is color balance and color placement." Using scrapers, hard edges, and traditional brushwork, each piece is an improvisation — a conversation between intuition and medium.

Giving Back

Chord & Canvas

10% of every purchase supports Chord & Canvas — Shannon's outreach program bringing art and music therapy to individuals facing substance abuse, grief, mental illness, and other life challenges. Sessions are held at the studio and on-site at rehabilitation centers, hospitals, and community spaces.
