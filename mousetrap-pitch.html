What's Holding the Current Site Back

The content is incredible. The platform isn't keeping up.

🚫

Generic WordPress Theme

The Apex News theme is used by thousands of sites. Nothing about it says "Mouse Trap News" or reflects the satire brand's personality.

💤

Every Article Looks the Same

No visual variety between posts. Reader fatigue sets in fast. Visitors scan headlines and leave instead of exploring deeper.

🔌

No Social Media Integration

500K+ TikTok followers and no social feed on the site. That's traffic and engagement left on the table.

🐢

Slow Page Loads

WordPress + heavy plugins + scattered ad scripts = slow experience. Every second of load time costs pageviews and ad revenue.

📈

Weak SEO Control

Limited control over meta tags, structured data, and sitemaps. For a traffic-driven site, this is leaving money on the table.

💰

Under-Monetized Articles

Ad placements are auto-scattered with no strategy. No mid-article newsletter capture. No sticky "read next" to boost pages per session.

What We've Built

A custom platform designed specifically for Mouse Trap News.

✨

Custom Enchanted Design System

A unique visual identity that matches the brand's personality — magical, whimsical, and distinctly Mouse Trap News. Not a template anyone else can buy.

🎨

5 Rotating Article Themes

Every article can have a different visual theme — from enchanted gazette to neon dark mode. Readers stay engaged because nothing feels stale.

📱

Social Media Hub

TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook content pulled directly into the site. Your 500K+ social audience now drives on-site engagement too.

⚡

Lightning-Fast Performance

Static-generated pages served from a global CDN. Pages load in under 1 second — better for SEO, better for ads, better for readers.

📝

Easy Content Management

Sanity CMS gives you a modern editing experience — real-time preview, drag-and-drop media, custom fields. Same ease as WordPress, more power.

🚀

SEO-First Architecture

Proper meta tags, Open Graph images, structured data (schema.org), and auto-generated sitemaps. Built for Google from day one.
