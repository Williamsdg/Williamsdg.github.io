1. Scope of Work

Williams Digital agrees to design, develop, and deploy a complete brand and e-commerce ecosystem for Shannon Harris Art, including a public storefront, an Auburn-only wholesale portal, and an internal fulfillment dashboard. The build is delivered as three coordinated products that share a unified brand identity and connect into Shannon's existing Shopify store.

Custom Brand & Identity
Wordmark logo, color palette (cream, gold, sage, blush), typography
Public Storefront Site
Hero, collections, about, products, Chord & Canvas, newsletter
Six Curated Collections
Nature, Skylines, Coastal, Abstracts, Faces, Spiritual
Collegiate Section
Auburn, Alabama, Georgia, Ole Miss, Samford, Troy callout
Auburn Wholesale Portal
Password-gated catalog with SKUs, wholesale pricing, order history
Wholesale Cart & Checkout
Bulk ordering with PO numbers, ready for Shopify B2B integration
Three-Role Admin Dashboard
Owner, Printer, and Shipper views with role-based access
Fulfillment Pipeline
Kanban-style order flow: Pending → Printing → Shipping → Shipped
SLA Timer System
2-week fulfillment tracker with green/yellow/red status
Print & Ship Queues
Print queue with specs/SKUs, ship queue with addresses & labels
Customer & Reports Views
Customer LTV, retail vs Auburn revenue, top-selling products
Shopify Storefront API Hooks
Cart UI ready for Shopify checkout integration
Mobile-First Responsive
All three sites polished for phone, tablet, and landscape
SEO & Open Graph Setup
Meta tags, social previews for iMessage/Slack link previews

2. Timeline

  • Phase 1 (Week 1): Brand assets, photography, and product catalog gathered. Storefront content populated with real imagery and copy from Shannon's Shopify store.
  • Phase 2 (Week 2): Shopify Storefront API integration, live cart and checkout flow on the public site and Auburn wholesale portal.
  • Phase 3 (Week 3): Admin dashboard wired to live order data, role-based authentication for Shannon, printer, and shipper, team training.
  • Phase 4 (Week 4): Domain cutover to shannonharrisart.com, final QA, launch.

Estimated completion: 4 weeks from contract signing and deposit receipt.
