Home/About

A developer who cares how your business actually runs.

Williams Digital is run by Dylan Williams, a Birmingham-based developer and Auburn University Computer Science graduate.

The studio started with a simple observation: most small businesses do not just need a prettier website. They need the website to do something — take the booking, capture the lead, sell the product — and they need somewhere to manage what comes in. So we build both, as one system.

That approach has produced client work like Vardar Transportation’s booking site and dashboard, Buy Auburn’s national business directory, WeAddALogo’s online store, and the Steve R. Skipper Studio gallery — and our own products: Milestone Journey+ on the App Store, Ridgepole for builders, and Canvas Sports for local leagues.

How we work

  • You work directly with the person building your project.
  • You see a real preview early, and progress every few days.
  • Everything is hand-coded — no page builders, no bloated themes.
  • Nothing invented: we never put fake reviews, stats, or awards on your site.
  • You own your domain, your content, and your data.
