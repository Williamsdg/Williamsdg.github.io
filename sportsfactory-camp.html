Parent Peace of Mind

SAFETY & SUPERVISION

Your child's safety is our top priority. Here's what we do to keep every camper secure and cared for.

Secure Facility

Single entrance/exit for drop-off and pick-up. Lead camp counselor manages daily check-in and check-out.

10:1 Staff Ratio

Approximately 10-to-1 to 12-to-1 staff-to-camper ratio ensuring focused supervision at all times.

Trained Staff

All staff trained in First Aid and CPR, plus AED and EpiPen support on-site at all times.

Behavior Policy

Clear expectations that prioritize safety and respect. Verbal warnings, written notices, and parent communication.

Nut-Free Camp

Peanut and tree nut-free facility. Emergency medications and EpiPens stored securely and clearly labeled.

Personal Lockers

Each camper gets a locker. Full-season campers receive a dedicated locker for the entire summer.

Food, Snacks & Lunch

Bring your own lunch or purchase on-site. Snack time built into every day. Refrigeration available.

Common Questions

CAMP FAQ

Think "gym class all day." Each day includes organized games, sports, creative and enrichment time, snack and lunch breaks, downtime, and special theme activities. Campers rotate activities and rarely stay in one activity longer than 45 minutes. The split is roughly 70% sports and games, 30% creative and enrichment.
Standard camp day runs 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Early drop-off is available starting at 7:00 AM, and late pick-up extends until 5:00 PM for $10 per use.
Campers are divided into three age-based groups. Children may flex up or down depending on the activity. Siblings and friends are accommodated whenever possible. Counselors focus on inclusion and encouragement.
We offer full-season passes, weekly passes, day passes, a 5-week credit pass, a 10-day flex pass, and a new Monday/Wednesday/Friday all-season pass. Sibling discounts are also available.
Campers may bring lunch (refrigeration available) or purchase lunch for $7/day or $25/week. Snacks are parent-provided with daily snack time built in. Camp is peanut and tree nut free. Emergency medications and EpiPens are stored securely.
Registrations are non-refundable. However, camp weeks may be changed with 24 hours' notice due to illness or scheduling changes.
Our facility has a single entrance/exit for secure drop-off and pick-up. Staff-to-camper ratio is approximately 10:1 to 12:1. All staff are trained in First Aid/CPR with AED and EpiPen support on site. We have clear behavior expectations that prioritize safety and respect.
Parents receive a weekly camp newsletter plus access to a dedicated camp Instagram account with photos and updates. Minor concerns are discussed at pick-up; serious issues are communicated during the camp day. For questions, contact Melissa at melissa@sportsfactorylv.com.
