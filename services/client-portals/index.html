// Client portals

Client portals your customers actually want to use

Email threads and lost attachments make your business look disorganized. A client portal gives every customer a secure login where they can see progress, upload files, message your team, and pay invoices — all under your brand.

Everything a client needs, in one place

Secure accounts

Every customer gets their own login and sees only their own projects, files, and invoices.

  • Individual customer logins
  • Project & matter switching
  • Privacy between clients

Files, progress & messaging

  • File uploads & downloads
  • Progress & status tracking

Payments & approvals

// Who portals are for

Built for businesses that manage ongoing client work

Client portals fit any business where the work spans days or months and the customer keeps asking "where are we on this?" — law firms, contractors and builders, agencies, member clubs, breeders, property managers, and professional services.

We've built member portals for private clubs, client portals for professional services firms, and customer-facing progress tracking for service businesses. Each one is connected to an internal dashboard, so what your client sees is always in sync with what your team is doing — nobody updates two systems.

Williams Digital is a Birmingham, Alabama studio. We design, build, host, and maintain the portal end to end.

// Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What does a client portal cost?

Portal projects typically start at $1,500 as part of a website + dashboard build, and grow with the features you need. You'll get a written quote up front.

Is a client portal secure?

Yes. Portals use individual authenticated accounts backed by a real database with row-level security, so each client can only see their own data.

Can clients pay invoices in the portal?

Yes. We integrate Stripe so clients can pay invoices, subscriptions, or deposits directly inside the portal, with payment history visible to both sides.

Can the portal match my brand?

Completely. The portal is custom-built under your domain with your logo, colors, and voice — it looks like your product, not third-party software.
