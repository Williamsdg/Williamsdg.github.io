FAQ

Custom dashboards: common questions

What is a custom dashboard?

It is a private, logged-in part of your website where you and your team manage the business — requests, jobs, bookings, customers, invoices — built specifically for how you operate instead of adapting to off-the-shelf software.

How much does a custom dashboard cost?

Website-plus-dashboard projects start at $5,000, depending on how many workflows are involved. We scope it with you and give a fixed quote.

Can it replace the software I pay for monthly?

Often, yes — especially if you only use a fraction of an expensive platform. We will tell you honestly when an existing tool is the better choice.

Who owns the data?

You do. Your data lives in a database in your name or ours on your behalf, and you can export it at any time.
