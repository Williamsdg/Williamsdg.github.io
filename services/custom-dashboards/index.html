// Why it works

Connected to your website, not bolted on

Off-the-shelf software makes your business fit its workflow. A custom dashboard is built around yours — the trades you run, the way you quote, the statuses your team actually uses.

Because we build the public website and the dashboard as one system, a booking or estimate request on your site becomes a job in your dashboard instantly — no copy-pasting, no missed leads. We've built operations consoles for contractors and roofers, booking systems for tourism operators, admin tools for sports media publications, and member management for clubs.

Every dashboard runs on a real database (Supabase), supports secure logins for your team, and is hosted and maintained by us — a Birmingham, Alabama studio, one point of contact for everything.

// Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom dashboard cost?

Website + dashboard projects typically start at $1,500, and larger custom platforms are quoted individually. Every project gets a written quote before any work starts.

Can a dashboard connect to my existing website?

Yes. Dashboards work best when we build the site and the dashboard as one system, but we can also connect a dashboard to an existing site using forms, webhooks, or a shared database.

Do my staff need training to use it?

No. Because the dashboard is designed around the way your team already works, most teams are comfortable the first day. We also record a walkthrough for onboarding new staff.

What happens if something breaks?

We host, monitor, and maintain everything we build. You have one point of contact — no vendor finger-pointing.
