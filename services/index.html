// Who we work with

One studio for your website and your operations

Most businesses need two things: a public-facing website that wins customers, and an internal dashboard that runs the day-to-day. Hiring separate vendors for each means handoffs, mismatched data, and finger-pointing when something breaks.

Williams Digital builds both as one connected system. The bookings, orders, and members your customers create on the website flow straight into the tools your team uses. That means less manual work, fewer mistakes, and one point of contact for everything.

We're based in Birmingham, Alabama and work with local businesses across the metro — as well as clients nationwide — including transportation companies, retailers, artists, hospitality groups, sports media, and community associations. Whether you need a brand-new website, a custom dashboard, or both, we design it, build it, host it, and support it.

// Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with small businesses in Birmingham, AL?

Yes. We're based in Birmingham, Alabama and work with local small businesses as well as clients across the US. We handle design, development, hosting, and support from one studio.

What does a custom dashboard do?

It's an internal tool your team uses to run the business — managing bookings, members, orders, or operations — connected to your public website so customer data flows straight in.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

Yes. We set up Vercel hosting, configure custom domains and DNS, connect email, and maintain the site after launch.

Can you add online payments?

Yes. We integrate Stripe and Stripe Connect for payments, subscriptions, and marketplace payouts, backed by a Supabase database when needed.
