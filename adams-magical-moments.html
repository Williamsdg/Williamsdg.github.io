Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it cost extra to use your planning service?+
Nope — our standard planning service is 100% free to you. We're compensated directly by Disney as an authorized travel planner. You pay the same price (or less) as booking directly with Disney, but get personalized expert planning on top of it.
Can you help with dining reservations?+
Absolutely! Dining reservations are one of the most stressful parts of Disney planning — popular spots book up in minutes. We handle the entire process, from recommending the best restaurants for your family to actually making the reservations.
Do you help with Genie+ and Lightning Lane?+
Yes! We create a personalized Genie+ plan for your trip that tells you exactly which Lightning Lane selections to make, when to make them, and in what order to maximize your ride time.
What if I've already booked my trip?+
No problem at all! If you've already booked directly with Disney, we can transfer your reservation to us in most cases — at no extra cost. Even if a transfer isn't possible, we can still help with dining, itineraries, and park strategies.
When should I start planning my Disney trip?+
The earlier the better! We recommend 6–12 months in advance for the best resort availability. Key milestones: book resort 6–8 months out, dining reservations open at 60 days, final payment at 30 days. That said, we've planned amazing last-minute trips too!
What makes you different from booking directly with Disney?+
When you book directly, you get a reservation. When you work with us, you get a complete vacation experience — personalized resort picks, optimized park strategies, dining plans, daily itineraries, packing lists, and ongoing support. Think of us as your personal Disney concierge.
