The Vision

One family. Sent everywhere.

HC graduates are leading worship in Cambodia, planting churches in Atlanta, pastoring students in Seattle. The alumni network is where they stay tethered — to each other, to Birmingham, and to the calling that formed them.

What's Inside

Built for the HC family.

Every feature shaped around how Highlands College people actually live, lead, and serve.

Chat Channels

Discord-style channels by graduating class, school, region, and ministry track. DMs, threads, reactions, presence.

Staff Announcements

Pinned updates from HC leadership — chapel, reunions, conferences, prayer calls. Emailed digest for those who want it.

Student Shouts

A dedicated wall for prayer requests, wins, testimonies, and asks. Tag, react, and encourage.

Ministry Job Board

Verified church openings across the country. Filter by role, region, denomination. Apply in-platform or externally.

Alumni Map

Opt-in map of where HC grads are serving worldwide — find alumni in your city, see the global reach of HC.

Staff-Verified Access

Every applicant is reviewed by HC staff before gaining access. This is a private family, not a public forum.

How It Works

Gated by design. Open by heart.

Four simple steps from application to active member of the network.

Apply

Name, email, grad year, school, current ministry, and your HC story.

Staff Reviews

An HC staff member verifies your connection to the school — usually within 48 hours.

Welcome In

You get a welcome email, access to channels, and a personalized feed by school and class year.

Build Together

Post, chat, share openings, mentor students, find mentors. Iron sharpening iron, for life.

The Feed

A home feed that feels like home.

Staff pin the updates that matter. Alumni post wins, launches, and prayer needs. Students see what the calling actually looks like once you're in it.

  • Pinned announcements from HC leadership
  • Ministry launches, ordinations, baby announcements
  • Inline reactions, comments, and prayer-hand button
  • Filter by school, class year, or region
