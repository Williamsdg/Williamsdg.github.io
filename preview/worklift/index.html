From profile to interview in four steps.

Most job seekers finish the first three in an afternoon. Employers see the result the moment it's done.

  1. Create your profile

    Two minutes. Tell WorkLift the roles you want, where you can work and when you can start.

  2. Lift your résumé

    Pick a template for your role and build a clean résumé and cover letter with lines written for that job.

  3. Take a short assessment

    Five questions, about four minutes, specific to the role. Employers see your result next to your profile.

  4. Get matched

    My WorkMatch lines you up with employers searching for your role. They can request an interview, and your contact details stay private until you accept.

Private until you say yes.

Employers can find your profile, but your phone number and email stay hidden until you accept an interview request.

You approve every contact

An employer sends a request. You accept, decline or propose another time. Only an accepted request shares your details.

Results on your terms

Assessment results help employers see what you can do. You choose whether they are shown, and they never decide a hire on their own.

Your data stays yours

Download everything you have entered, or delete your account, from your profile at any time.
