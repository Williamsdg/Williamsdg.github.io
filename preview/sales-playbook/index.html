Universal Sales Process

Five steps, in order, for every vertical. The demo is step 3 — never step 1.

  1. 1

    Research

    Review their website, Google reviews, Facebook, and mobile experience. Document 3 things done well and 3 opportunities before any contact.

  2. 2

    Discovery

    Ask questions. Don't pitch. Understand:

    • Revenue goals
    • Operational problems
    • Growth goals
  3. 3

    Show the demo

    Only show the relevant demo:

    • Home Services → Summit
    • Healthcare → Oak Ridge
    • Legal → Harrison & Cole
  4. 4

    Paint the future

    BadWe can redesign your website.
    GoodImagine every lead, appointment, message, and customer interaction happening in one modern platform.
  5. 5

    Close

    Ask"If we could build something similar for your business, what would that mean for your team over the next 12 months?"
    Then"Is there anything preventing us from moving forward?"
