Industry Playbooks

Pain points to listen for, questions to ask, the pitch to deliver, and where the deal grows.

Home Services

HVAC · Plumbing · Roofing · Landscaping · Pressure Washing · Electricians · Builders
Pain points

Not enough leads. No online booking. Poor Google ranking. Website looks outdated. Competitors look better.

Discovery questions
  1. How are most customers finding you today?
  2. Do you know how many leads your website generates each month?
  3. How often do you update your website?
  4. Can customers request estimates online?
  5. Who manages your website now?
The pitch
Say it like this"Most home service companies lose customers because homeowners judge credibility in about 5 seconds. If a competitor has a cleaner website and easier contact process, they usually win the lead."
Upsells
Online estimates Service area maps Review integration Project galleries Customer dashboard

Healthcare

Dentists · Chiropractors · Physical Therapy · Pediatrics · Urgent Care · Private Practices
Pain points

Appointment scheduling. Patient experience. New patient acquisition. Mobile usability.

Discovery questions

  2. Are patients filling out forms online?

The pitch

Appointment requests Intake forms Patient portals FAQ center Resource libraries

Prospect Qualification

Spend your time on green lights. A red light isn't a challenge — it's a pass.

Green light Pursue now
  • Website looks old
  • Not mobile friendly
  • No clear call-to-action
  • No online forms
  • No CRM
  • Owner answers the phone directly
  • Recent Google reviews
  • Active social media
Yellow light Probe on the call
  • Newer website but poor functionality
  • Limited SEO
  • No lead tracking
Red light Move on
  • Brand-new custom website
  • Large internal development team
  • Long-term agency contract
  • National corporation

Sales Process

Five steps, in order. The free mockup at step 3 is what separates us from every other agency calling them.

  1. 1

    Website Review

    Before any contact, record 3–5 quick, specific observations about their current site. Specifics earn the call.

  2. 2

    Discovery Call

    Understand three things before pitching anything:

    • Current lead flow — where customers come from today
    • Pain points — what's costing them time or deals
    • Growth goals — what "better" looks like to them
  3. 3

    Free Homepage Mockup

    We build a visual preview of their new homepage before they pay a dollar.

    Williams Digital advantage: most agencies show nothing until after payment. We show the result first.
  4. 4

    Proposal

    Present side by side: their current site, the mockup, the concrete benefits, the price, and the timeline. Let the contrast do the selling.

  5. 5

    Close

    The closing question"If we can build this for $X and have it live in Y weeks, is there anything preventing us from moving forward?"

Objection Quick Answers

Every objection is answered by returning to the outcome, never by defending the website.

They sayYou say
"It's too expensive." "What's one new customer worth to you? If the site brings in even two or three extra jobs a month, it pays for itself in the first quarter."
"We get enough business already." "That's great — this isn't about surviving, it's about which jobs you get. A stronger web presence lets you win the bigger, better-fit customers instead of whoever calls first."
"My nephew / a friend handles our site." "That's a common setup, and it usually means updates wait weeks. You'd have direct access to the developer — changes happen in days, and there's someone accountable for results."
"I need to think about it." "Totally fair. What's the one thing you'd need to see or know to feel confident? Let me answer that directly." (Isolate the real objection — then handle it.)
"We tried a website before and it didn't do anything." "Most websites don't, because they're built as brochures. This one is built to generate leads — and we track them, so you'll see exactly what it produces."
