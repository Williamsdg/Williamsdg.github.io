Hi Brent,

My name is Dylan Williams. I run Williams Digital, a web design and development studio here in Birmingham. I've been building concept work around Alabama high school athletics, and I wanted to put the newest piece in front of you.

It's called AHSAA Pressbox. It gives every member school one login to submit rosters, scores and player reports. Those reach AHSAA as soon as they're entered, and when a score is late, Pressbox sends the reminders automatically so your staff doesn't have to chase it.

To be clear up front: it's designed to sit on top of DragonFly, not replace it. A school enters a score or roster once in Pressbox, and it syncs to DragonFly, the region standings, AHSAA NOW and the media.

The overview: every school, every score, one pressbox.
WHAT'S IN THE WORKING DEMO
▸ Score entry in under 30 seconds on a phone. The home team enters it, the visitor confirms it, and any disputes come to your staff with both versions side by side.
▸ A reminder ladder for late scores. The coach gets a link at the final whistle, the AD is added the next morning, and the principal and AHSAA are notified at 24 hours.
▸ Rosters checked against AHSAA eligibility rules before the principal certifies them: age, academics, physicals, concussion and cardiac-arrest forms, and the new transfer asterisk.
▸ Live region standings, plus pitch counts, ejection fines and a compliance scorecard for every school.
▸ A statewide staff console showing who's reported, who hasn't, and what the missing scores are holding up.
