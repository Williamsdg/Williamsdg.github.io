A statewide school data platform · concept

Every school.
Every score.
One pressbox.

One login for every AHSAA member school. Coaches and ADs submit rosters, scores and player reports once, and they reach AHSAA, the region standings, DragonFly and the media the same second. If a score is late, Pressbox sends the reminders so AHSAA staff don't have to.

The problem

The rules are clear. The follow-up is manual.

AHSAA's handbook already says what schools owe: schedules by set dates, rosters before the first contest, and scores posted immediately. What's missing is anything that makes those deadlines easy to meet, and anything that chases a school when it slips.

"Immediately"
When scores are due

Regular-season scores are due right after the contest, tournaments within 24 hours, or the school may be fined. Playoff hosts have 45 minutes.

5+
Systems per school

DragonFly, GameChanger, MaxPreps, GoFan, NFHS Network, plus email to every local paper. Each has its own login and they don't share data.

$2,000
Fine per school, Georgia 2026

Unreported basketball scores forced GHSA to reseed Class 3A brackets less than a day before round one. 12 teams got new opponents. AJC ↗

0
Automated late-score reminders

We didn't find a single state association that automatically reminds a school when a score is late. It's still done with phone calls and fines.

Busy athletic directors miss scores because they run out of hours, not because they're trying to get away with something. More fines won't fix that. Reminders at the right moment and entry that takes under 30 seconds will.How Georgia's executive director framed their missed-score fines, paraphrased

What's inside

Built for Friday night and the day after

🏟

Score Center

Quarter-by-quarter or set-by-set entry in under 30 seconds on a phone. The home team enters, the visitor confirms, and disputes go to AHSAA with both versions side by side.

📋

Rosters & eligibility

Returning players roll over from last year with grades advanced. Every athlete is checked against age, academic, physical, concussion and cardiac-arrest requirements. The principal e-certifies, and the roster goes to AHSAA and DragonFly together.

📊

Player reports

Postgame stat lines feed statewide leaders and the media. Player of the Week nominations and all-region ballots happen in the app. Concussion reports stay private.

🏆

Live region standings

Built from verified scores, with win-loss percentage in region, the top-four playoff line and tiebreakers applied automatically. This replaces the standings PDF.

⚾

Pitch counts

AHSAA's grade-based limits and rest days are built in, and both coaches sign off. Pressbox warns before a pitcher reaches the limit instead of fining after. It can import from GameChanger.

🛡

Compliance scorecard

A single score per school covering on-time scores, certified rosters, complete athlete files and coach CPR/AED certification. It shows fine exposure before anything becomes a fine.

🟥

Sportsmanship & fines

Ejections come in from officials' reports. Fines are calculated by offense number, the deadline for the course discount tracks itself, and the Sportsmanship Award list keeps itself current.

🌡

Heat & safety logs

WBGT readings every 30 minutes, with AHSAA's practice limits applied automatically. A concussion return-to-play lock keeps an athlete out of the lineup until an MD clears them.

📣

Broadcast & audit

Staff can message schools by role, sport, district or class and see who opened it. Every change is logged with who made it, when and from where.

Built for Alabama

AHSAA's own rules, built in

Pressbox enforces the rules AHSAA already has. It doesn't add new ones. Each rule becomes a check or a deadline that schools can see coming.

Classes
6A–1A public, AA and A private (2026–28)

Built on the new alignment after 7A was eliminated, including separate private-school brackets.

Eligibility
Principal certifies 48 hours before a student plays

A certified birth certificate must be on file. A student who turns 19 before Aug 1 is ineligible. Academic minimums are checked against the transcript sync.

Transfers
Asterisk on public rosters

Per the 2026 Legislative Council rule, students who transfer after the 3rd day of 7th grade are marked on public rosters.

Health
Physical, concussion, cardiac arrest

An annual Form 5 physical, valid through the end of the month it was signed. Alabama's concussion law and the 2023 Sudden Cardiac Arrest Prevention Act, including coach CPR/AED training every 2 years.

Scores
Immediately · 24 hrs · 45 min

Separate deadlines for the regular season, tournaments and playoff hosts, each with its own reminder schedule.

Pitch counts
120 / 100 / 85 by grade, with rest days

76+ pitches means 3 days of rest, 51–75 means 2, 26–50 means 1. Pressbox warns at the limit instead of fining after it.

Ejections
$300 → $100 with the NFHS course

Escalating penalties for 2nd and 3rd offenses are applied automatically, and each school's Sportsmanship Award eligibility is tracked.

Heat
WBGT limits

Above 87.1°: 2-hour practice cap. 90.1–92°: 1 hour. Above 92°: practice is cancelled.

Rules are drawn from public AHSAA handbook material and news coverage as of Sept 2026. AHSAA would confirm every rule and threshold before launch.

Privacy & roles

These are kids. The access model reflects that.

Medical data stays inside the school. A student's public profile is off by default and respects each district's FERPA directory-information opt-outs. Athlete data is never used for marketing. Families can ask for a profile to be removed with one click.

Works with what you have

On top of DragonFly, not instead of it

AHSAA already made the big move from C2C to DragonFly in 2020–21. Pressbox doesn't redo that work. It sits in front of DragonFly and the other tools as the one place a coach or AD opens at 10 PM on a Friday, then syncs to all of them.

🐉 DragonFly

Eligibility and roster data stays in DragonFly. Pressbox reads from it and writes back to it, so nothing gets entered twice.

⚾ GameChanger

Pitch counts import automatically and both coaches confirm them. Violations are caught before first pitch instead of in the next day's report.

🎟 GoFan · 📺 NFHS · 📱 AHSAA NOW

Schedules flow to ticketing and streaming listings. Verified scores go to the public scoreboard within seconds.

Rollout

Pilot small, then go statewide

PHASE 1 · SPRING 2027

One-district pilot

About 55 schools, starting with baseball and softball, when pitch counts make the value obvious right away.

  • Score Center + reminder ladder
  • Pitch-count logging
  • DragonFly read sync
  • On-time rate measured against last season
PHASE 2 · FALL 2027

Statewide scores & rosters

Every member school, launching for the 2027 football season.

  • Rosters, eligibility and principal certification
  • Live region standings
  • AHSAA NOW, MaxPreps and media feeds
  • Staff operations console
PHASE 3 · 2028

Full compliance layer

Timed to the next reclassification cycle.

  • Sportsmanship, fines and payments
  • Heat and safety logs
  • All-region and all-state balloting
  • Open data API for partners
