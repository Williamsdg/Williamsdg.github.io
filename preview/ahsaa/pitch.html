Why Now

A New Communications Era Deserves a New Front Door

This summer has been a big one for the Association's public profile — and it's exactly the right moment to modernize the platform all of that news lives on.

June 22: Executive Director Heath Harmon named Birmingham TV veteran Scott Griffin as the AHSAA's new Director of Communications — a clear investment in how the Association tells its story.

June 28–29: Steve Savarese was enshrined in the NFHS National High School Hall of Fame, and Mark Jones received the NFHS National Citation Award — national moments that deserved a national-caliber web presence.

Great stories are being made. The current site makes them hard to see. A modern platform turns every announcement, championship, and student-athlete feature into the front-page moment it should be.

The Opportunity

Today's Site vs. The Concept

ahsaa.com Today

  • Homepage reads like a directory — dense link lists, minimal imagery
  • Key info lives inside PDFs (minutes, alignments, forms, fee schedules)
  • Deeply nested menus make simple questions take five clicks
  • Championship results scattered across pages and external platforms
  • Parents and fans — the biggest audience — have no clear path
  • Design language hasn't kept pace with the caliber of the events

The Redesign Concept

  • ESPN-style live scores banner — real-time games with quarter and clock, right at the top
  • Championship Central dashboard — brackets, tickets, streams, venue maps & parking for every sport
  • Searchable school directory — 438 schools by name, city, or class, plus an interactive Alabama map
  • Classification hub & record book — alignments and a century of history as live pages, not PDFs
  • Dedicated officials portal — registration, clinics, recordings, and fees out of the nested menus
  • One data dashboard — GoFan, NFHS, DragonFly, Scorebook Live, MaxPreps & Hudl integrated, plus a mobile bottom nav for parents in the bleachers

Scope

What a Full Engagement Includes

01

Full Site Architecture & Redesign

Every section of today's site — School/Parent Resources, Calendar, Sports, Schools, AHSADCA, Officials, Media, Sponsors — reorganized around how people actually search.

02

Live Scores & Championship Platform

ESPN-style live scoreboard, brackets, and event pages for every championship — integrated with GoFan, NFHS Network, Scorebook Live, MaxPreps, and Hudl.

03

News & Media Center

A modern publishing workflow for the communications team — stories, photos, press resources, and social-ready pages instead of posts buried in a feed.

04

PDF-to-Web Migration

Classification alignments, board minutes, fee schedules, and forms converted to searchable, mobile-friendly pages (with originals still downloadable).

05

Easy Content Management

The AHSAA staff updates scores, news, and documents themselves — no developer required for day-to-day changes.

06

Accessibility, Speed & Mobile

Fast, accessible, and built mobile-first — because most of this site's traffic happens from the stands on a Friday night.
