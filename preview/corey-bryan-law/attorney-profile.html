Corey D. Bryan, Attorney at Law

Serving Andalusia & surrounding areas

Corey D. Bryan was born and raised in Covington County and graduated from Andalusia High School. He attended the University of Alabama, where he played football for four years for the Crimson Tide, earning all SEC academic honors and playing on the 1999 SEC Championship team. Playing football strengthened Corey’s resolve and team-player mentality, qualities that continue to influence his legal practice today.

Corey has practiced criminal defense and personal injury law since 2004. He began his practice as an associate at the Powell Law Firm in Andalusia, Alabama. He left the Powell Law Firm in 2007 to partner in the Law Firm of Merrell & Bryan, LLC. In February of 2011, his legal partner left the private practice realm due to his election into public office, and Corey continued practicing in his own firm, Corey D. Bryan Attorney at Law, LLC.

Corey’s goal as an attorney is quite simple: to obtain the best results that he can for his clients, ethically and honorably.
