Before you call

What to do right now.

The steps you take at this point in time can dramatically influence your life. Pick the situation you are in.

After a car accident

Some simple guidelines to follow at the scene.

  1. Check on others involved in the accident.
  2. Report the accident to police immediately.
  3. Exchange information with other parties: name, address, contact information, driver’s license number, license plates, and auto insurance information.
  4. Take pictures of the accident – the cars involved, street signs, etc.
  5. Never leave the scene.
  6. Do NOT admit fault.

After an arrest

Do not wait to involve an attorney. Never forget:

  1. You have the right to an attorney.
  2. You have the right to remain silent.
  3. You cannot be deprived of your life, liberty, or property without due process of law.

More in Criminal Defense.

After a DUI stop

Arrested for DUI? The fight isn’t over yet. These are the questions Corey asks.

  1. Did the arresting officer have probable cause to pull you over?
  2. Were field sobriety tests administered and graded properly?
  3. Were you read your Miranda rights?
  4. Were you lawfully arrested?
  5. Was the breath/blood test properly administered?
  6. Was evidence mishandled in any way?
