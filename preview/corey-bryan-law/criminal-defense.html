Protecting Your Constitutional Rights & Freedom

When it comes to criminal charges, the full protection of your constitutional rights is absolutely crucial. Never forget:

  • You have the right to an attorney.
  • You have the right to remain silent.
  • You cannot be deprived of your life, liberty, or property without due process of law.

As an Andalusia criminal defense lawyer, Corey D. Bryan is passionate about advocating for his clients’ rights inside and outside of the criminal courtroom. This includes investigating evidence gathering procedures, potential civil rights violations, and every other aspect of the charges they face. When you come to our firm, you will experience firsthand how the criminal justice system can work in your favor. We pursue the best possible result – ethically and honorably.
