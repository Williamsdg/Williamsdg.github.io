The consequences of a theft charge

The term theft can be simply defined as unlawfully taking another’s property. Under Alabama law, there are many different types of theft-related offenses that may range from misdemeanors to felonies punishable by community service alone or decades in prison. The circumstances surrounding the offense and the value of property involved will influence the severity of charges and penalties, as may a defendant’s criminal record, if any.

Criminal Defense for Theft in Atmore, Andalusia & Enterprise

Depending on the value of property involved and whether violence and/or weapons were used in the commission of the crime, varied penalties may be imposed for theft in Alabama. Robbery and burglary in the first degree, for example, are Class A felonies punishable by 10-20 years to life in prison. Theft of property valued at over $2,500 is a Class B felony, punishable by 2-20 years in prison.
