Causes of Car Accidents & Your Right to Compensation

There are many different potential causes of car accidents, and some collisions have more than one cause. It is important to identify cause in order to establish liability, or legal responsibility, for your injuries. By carefully investigating physical evidence and witness testimony, including working with accident reconstruction specialists, Andalusia car accident attorney Corey Bryan will work to determine cause to support your claim for compensation.

Auto accidents may be caused by:

  • Texting while driving
  • Driving while distracted
  • Speeding
  • Drunk driving
  • Unsafe lane changes
  • Failure to yield the right of way
  • Severe weather
  • Dangerous roads
  • Defective auto parts

What to Do After a Car Accident

Here are some simple guidelines to follow after an auto accident:

  1. Check on others involved in the accident.
  2. Report accident to police immediately.
  3. Exchange information with other parties. Key information: name, address, contact information, driver’s license number, license plates, and auto insurance information.
  4. Take pictures of the accident – the cars involved, street signs, etc.
  5. Never leave the scene.
  6. Do NOT admit fault.
