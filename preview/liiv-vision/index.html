Chapter 01

The vision on one wall.

Three cards. That's the whole idea — everything else on this board is just the plan to pay for it.

1.

A church from Atlanta, for every city

What God started in Atlanta — three locations, one church family — doesn't have to stay in Atlanta. The next LIIV church is a city that doesn't have it yet.

2.

Every three years, on purpose

Not a growth counter — a covenant. Three years is long enough to raise the team, fund the room, and launch healthy. Then the clock starts again.

3.

Donors fund a thing, not a fund

Every dollar on this board maps to a line you can point at — a lease, a kids wing, a sound system. When it launches, you'll know exactly what you built.

"To see all people flourish — by knowing God, finding freedom, discovering purpose, and making a difference."The LIIV mission · liivatl.com
