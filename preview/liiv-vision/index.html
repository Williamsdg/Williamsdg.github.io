01 The vision

A church planted
every three years.

Three years is not slow. Three years is what it takes to raise a campus pastor, build a dream team, fund a room, and hand a city a church that can carry itself from day one. LIIV would rather plant five churches that last than fifteen that don't.

01 / KNOWN

Atlanta proved the model

Three locations, one church, one team. Every system a new city needs already runs every Sunday across Atlanta — the run sheets, the kids check-in, the group structure, the volunteer pipeline.

02 / NEXT

Charlotte is already the story

A launch team forms three years out, not three months out. People move, groups start, prayer starts, giving starts — long before there is a room with a sign on it.

03 / WHY IT MATTERS

See all people flourish

Knowing God. Finding Freedom. Discovering Purpose. Making a Difference. The mission does not change from city to city. Only the zip code does.
