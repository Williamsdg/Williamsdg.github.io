Why Active Wealth

Have you ever been asked to just hang in there when the market loses value?

We approach investing differently than a lot of advisors and brokers. You worked hard to provide for your family. We will work hard to protect and grow your money.

  • i.

    Actively managed, not parked

    We employ a tactical asset allocation strategy and rebalance your portfolio monthly without charging additional fees.

  • ii.

    Fiduciaries who put you first

    We place the needs of our clients first, and we provide third-party analysis so you can make an informed decision before investing.

  • iii.

    Fee-efficient by design

    We invest primarily in Exchange Traded Funds and do our best to shield you from mutual fund fees that can drag on a portfolio.

  • iv.

    Your money stays in your name

    We will never take possession of your funds. Custodians hold your assets, and we have only limited trading authority to implement your plan.

Services

Financial Planning

A comprehensive evaluation of your current and future financial state, using known variables to project future income, asset values and withdrawal plans.

02

Portfolio Analysis

Third-party analysis of your portfolio detailing risk as measured by standard deviation, average annual rate of return over the past 10 years, and correlation.

03

Portfolio Management

Matching investments to objectives and balancing risk against performance. We actively manage our portfolios to better adjust to market conditions.

04

Life Insurance

We help you assess your life insurance and legacy planning needs, and we are extremely selective about the A rated carriers we work with.

05

College Planning

We take great pride in helping clients plan financially for their children’s college education: tuition, room, board and the rest.

06

Health Care Coverage

Access to independent health insurance and health care coverage experts who are just a phone call away.

01

Buy-Sell Insurance

02

Key Person Insurance

Financial Planning

Portfolio Analysis

Business owner portfolios are unique. We analyze the financial performance of every asset, including non-traditional and illiquid ones.

SEP IRAs

06

Roth Ladder Conversions

A conversion plan developed and implemented with your long-term tax picture in mind.
