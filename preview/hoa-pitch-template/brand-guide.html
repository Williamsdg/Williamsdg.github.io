01 — Brand Story

Who We Are

The foundation of the Briarwood Community Association brand starts with our purpose, our vision for the future, and the values we carry into every interaction.

Our Mission

To elevate the standard of HOA management through transparent communication, expert financial stewardship, and genuine partnership with every community we serve.

Our Vision

To be Alabama's most trusted name in community management — where every homeowner feels heard, every board feels supported, and every neighborhood thrives.

Our Core Values

Transparency
Open books, open doors, open communication. We believe trust is built through visibility and honesty in every decision.
Partnership
We work alongside your board, not above it. Every community relationship is a collaboration built on mutual respect.
Excellence
From financial reporting to vendor management, we pursue the highest standard in every facet of community operations.
Community
Neighborhoods are more than addresses. We champion the connections and shared spaces that make a community truly feel like home.
Integrity
We do what we say and say what we mean. Ethical stewardship of community funds and trust is non-negotiable.

Typography Do's & Don'ts

Do
Clean Heading Hierarchy
Body text with generous line height (1.6-1.8) creates a comfortable reading experience. Pair headings in Jakarta Sans with body in Inter for clear visual separation.
Don't
ALL CAPS BODY TEXT IS DIFFICULT TO READ AND FEELS AGGRESSIVE. AVOID USING UPPERCASE FOR PARAGRAPHS OR LONG PASSAGES OF CONTENT IN ANY CONTEXT.
Tight letter spacing on body text makes content hard to read and feels cramped.

Photography & Imagery Style

Photography should reflect the warmth and community spirit of Alabama neighborhoods. Imagery should feel authentic, optimistic, and grounded — never staged or corporate.

Neighborhood Scenes
Tree-lined streets, well-kept homes, community entrances
People & Community
Families, neighbors gathering, board meetings, events
Alabama Landscapes
Southern landscapes, rolling hills, warm golden-hour light
Amenities
Pools, clubhouses, parks, playgrounds, walking trails
Professional
Clean offices, organized workspaces, team collaboration
Trust Signals
Handshakes, signed agreements, award ceremonies

Photography Treatment

Warm color grading preferred. Slightly desaturated with lifted shadows. Avoid cool, clinical tones. Natural lighting over studio flash. If overlaying text, use a navy gradient overlay at 60-80% opacity.
