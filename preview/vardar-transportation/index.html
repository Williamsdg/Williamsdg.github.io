07 / Questions

Things people ask before booking.

Still need a hand? Call or text 205-566-9401 — Vardar's dispatch line is staffed 7 days a week, 7 AM to 11 PM.

What does the chauffeured rate include?

Chauffeured service is $125/hour and includes the vehicle and a licensed driver. A 20% gratuity and a 10% fuel surcharge are added at checkout. Wait time between stops is built into your booked hours. Maximum 7 passengers (the driver takes one seat in the 8-passenger van; 9 passengers in the Black Van).

How does self-drive work?

Self-drive is $400/day with a 3-day minimum, plus a one-time $150 prep fee. You get 100 miles free per day and any extra miles bill at $0.35/mile. Renters must be 25+ with a valid license and a clean driving record. We run a quick verification, take a refundable security deposit, and walk you through the vehicle at pickup.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and SEC home-game weekends, we recommend reserving 4–8 weeks out. For airport runs and corporate trips, 48–72 hours is usually fine. We try to accommodate same-day requests — just call.

Where do you operate?

Vardar is based in Birmingham. We routinely serve all of central Alabama, plus Tuscaloosa, Auburn, Huntsville, Atlanta, Nashville, Destin, and New Orleans. Longer trips are by quote.

Can I bring drinks on board?

On chauffeured trips, yes — passengers may bring beverages (including alcohol for parties of legal age). We provide ice, cups, and a cooler at no extra charge. No alcohol or smoking in self-drive rentals.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel 72+ hours before pickup for a full refund. Within 72 hours, the deposit is retained. Within 24 hours, the full booking is charged. Weather and force majeure are handled case-by-case.
