  1. Scope of service. Vardar Transportation (the “Owner”) will provide the service described above on the date and time shown. Routing, stops, and wait time reflect what was discussed during quoting; material changes may adjust the price.
  2. Fees & deposit. A deposit reserves your date and is applied toward your total. The remaining balance is due before service begins. Deposits are non-refundable once the date is held, except as noted in Cancellation below.
  3. Cancellation & changes. Cancellations made more than 7 days before the service date receive a full refund of any balance paid beyond the deposit. Inside 7 days, the deposit is forfeited; inside 48 hours, the full quoted amount may apply. Reschedules are accommodated subject to availability.
  4. Passenger conduct & safety. For everyone's safety, occupancy may not exceed the vehicle's rated capacity, all passengers must wear seat belts, and the driver may decline or end service in cases of unsafe or unlawful behavior. Vardar carries commercial auto coverage on all chauffeured trips.
  5. Indemnity. Regardless of insurance coverage, the Renter shall fully indemnify the Owner for any loss, damage, and legal actions, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that the Owner suffers due to the Renter's use of the Vehicle during the term of this Agreement — including but not limited to damage to the Vehicle, damage to the property of others, injury to the Renter, and injury to others. This provision survives the termination of this Agreement.
  6. Insurance. The Renter warrants to the Owner that the Renter possesses car insurance that covers personal injury to the Renter and others, as well as the Vehicle and the property of others.
  7. Owner warranty. The Owner represents that, to the best of its knowledge and belief, the Vehicle is in sound and safe condition and free of any known faults or defects that would affect its safe operation under normal use.
  8. Renter warranties. The Renter agrees that the Renter will not (a) use the Vehicle to carry passengers other than the Renter and the Renter's permitted guests; (b) allow any unauthorized person to operate the Vehicle; (c) operate the Vehicle in violation of any laws or for an illegal purpose — and if the Renter does, the Renter is responsible for all associated tickets, fines, and fees; (d) use the Vehicle to push or tow another vehicle; (e) use the Vehicle for any race or competition; or (f) operate the Vehicle in a negligent manner.
  9. Arbitration. If the parties cannot amicably resolve a dispute or damage claim, they agree to resolve it by arbitration conducted in Birmingham, Alabama, under the rules of the American Arbitration Association then in effect, with one (1) arbitrator selected by mutual agreement (or, failing agreement, by the AAA from its National Panel of Arbitrators). Alabama law applies. The arbitrator may not award punitive damages, and the parties agree to be bound by the arbitrator's findings; judgment may be entered in any court having jurisdiction.
  10. Quote validity. This quote and agreement are valid for 72 hours from the issue date shown above. After that, pricing may be requoted based on changes in scope, fuel, or scheduling.
  11. Governing law & entire agreement. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Alabama, without regard to conflict-of-law principles, and constitutes the entire agreement between the parties on this subject. No modification is valid unless in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect. No action arising out of this Agreement may be brought more than one (1) year after the cause of action accrues.
