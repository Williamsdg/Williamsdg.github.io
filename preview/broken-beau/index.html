03 — Why It Holds Up

The polo, disassembled.

Four-way stretch. Pima-cotton blend. A collar that doesn't curl after one wash. The details a man notices the second time he wears it.

01

Pima Stretch

92% Peruvian Pima, 8% elastane. Breathable through 95° afternoons; structured enough for a 65° clubhouse.

02

Self-Fabric Collar

Cut from the same cloth as the body — no stiff knit-collar tell. Holds shape through hangers, hampers, and humidity.

03

Reinforced Placket

Mother-of-pearl buttons, double-stitched placket, internal facing. The first thing people see — built to outlast the polo.

04

Tailored Hem

Mid-rise hem, side-vented. Wear it tucked or out. We tested the silhouette on a dozen body types before we cut a single inch.

A Note From The Founder

Broken Beau started in a truck bed in Coleman, Texas — with one good polo and one bad one.

The good one came apart in a week. The bad one is still in the closet, somehow. We started Broken Beau because every "performance polo" was either a tech tee with a collar or a country-club holdover that didn't move. We wanted something a man could wear to a meeting, to a wedding, and to the back nine without changing his thinking — or his shirt.

We make them slowly. We name them carefully. We back every stitch.
