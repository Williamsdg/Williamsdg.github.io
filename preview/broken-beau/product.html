Materials & Construction +
92% Peruvian Pima cotton, 8% elastane. Self-fabric collar, double-needle placket, mother-of-pearl buttons, side-vented hem. Pre-shrunk and color-locked. Stitched and finished in the United States.
Fit & Care +
Tailored fit — true to size for most. If between sizes, size up for layering or down for a leaner silhouette. Machine wash cold, hang or lay flat to dry. Iron low. Do not bleach. The collar will hold its shape — that's the whole point.
