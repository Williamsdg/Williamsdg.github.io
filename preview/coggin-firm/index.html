Our Story

Fifty years of Northeast Alabama, in one family.

"I love helping people."John D. Coggin, Senior Partner

John Coggin began practicing law in Centre in the 1970s, serving as municipal judge for Centre and Cedar Bluff before spending fifteen years as a District Court Judge — years spent presiding over thousands of family, criminal and juvenile matters.

Gina Coggin came to the practice by a different road: Cumberland School of Law, the National Trial Team, and years as an Etowah County prosecutor before turning to the other side of the courtroom to represent injured workers and employees.

Today they practice together, out of two offices, in the counties where both of them grew up.
