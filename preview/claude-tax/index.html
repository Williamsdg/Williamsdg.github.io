Part IIIGood to know

The house rules, in plain English.

a

Keep a list of every deduction

You must have a list of all your personal and business deductions on file with Claude and in your prepared taxes. That leaves no doubt about what was claimed.

b

Mileage logs are mandatory for business

If you claim business mileage, send the log with your organizer.

c

Bring a government-issued ID

A driver’s license, state ID or other government-issued ID is needed to file. Why it’s required

d

Fees are due on completion

All preparation fees are due when the return is completed. Ways to pay

e

Need more time? Ask for an extension

An extension gives you more time to file, not more time to pay any tax owed. How extensions work

No chargeStudent returns

As in the past, clients’ children who are still attending school full-time are not charged for a tax return, as long as they are dependents and earn under $20,000 (not including tips).
