What we found
01

The strongest line is used as a tagline

“Own nothing. Control everything.” is a positioning statement most firms in this category would pay to own. It explains the entire discipline in four words. On the current site it sits as a header line rather than the idea the whole page is built to prove.

02

The record is one click away

UCLA School of Law. A California State Assembly Fellowship. The first African American attorney to establish a law practice in Century City, in 1983. Six companies founded since. That is the reason to trust the firm, and it lives on a separate page a visitor has to go looking for.

03

Wealth ENsurance™ is given a sentence

A trademarked idea that distinguishes JE from every insurance-led competitor — protection built before the event rather than a payout after it — currently reads as one line among many. It is strong enough to carry a section of its own.

04

Seven audiences at identical weight

Entrepreneurs, real estate investors, physicians, entertainers, athletes and high-net-worth families are six client types. “Anticipating divorce, paternity actions or litigation” is not a client type — it is a deadline, and it is the most urgent thing on the page. Listed seventh, in the same size as the rest, it reads as an afterthought.
The direction

Make the visitor
open the vault.

Private banking, not crypto. Near-black, ivory editorial type, bronze used only where JE wants your attention. The centrepiece is The Safe — a vault door built specifically for this site, controlled by the visitor’s scroll. The combination turns, the bolts retract, light escapes around the seam before the door has moved, and the door swings and carries you through into the vault, where the five services sit as compartments to pull open.

The whole page is arranged so that sequence lands: quiet, then exposure, then the vault, then calm again. Nothing else on the page competes with it.

01 — Identify
Who is exposed?
Six client profiles, then the one that isn’t a profile at all: when exposure has a deadline. Divorce, paternity, litigation.
02 — Protect
The Safe
A real mechanism, about six seconds end to end. It is the thing people will send to someone else.
03 — Structure
Inside the vault
Five brushed-metal compartments in a dark room. Pull one open and the service explains itself.
