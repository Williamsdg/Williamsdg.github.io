How it comes together

Considered, not instant.

A private session is arranged personally with the studio — nothing is booked until the details are agreed.

  1. iShare the occasion

    Tell the studio what you’re celebrating, who’s joining, and when you’ll be on the island.

  2. iiDiscuss concept & setting

    Talk through designs, the location and what the space needs for a proper setup.

  3. iiiThe studio confirms

    Arrangements are confirmed directly with you — including weather, for sessions on the water.
