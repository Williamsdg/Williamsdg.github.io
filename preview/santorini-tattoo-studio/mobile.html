What changes on a phone

  1. i
    The offer before the photoHeadline, a one-line promise and Request an Appointment sit above the hero image, so nobody scrolls past a full-screen picture to learn what the studio does.
  2. ii
    Request · Call · Map, always in reachAfter the hero, a bar docks at the bottom with the three things a visitor in Fira actually needs. It hides while the menu or a photo is open and while you type.
  3. iii
    One tap to call or find the studioCall opens the dialler with the studio’s number; Map opens directions to Dekigala 137 — no copying addresses on a hot street in Fira.
  4. iv
    A portfolio made for thumbsTwo columns, subject filters that scroll sideways, and a full-screen viewer with swipe, visible arrows, an obvious close button — and “Request something in this style”.
  5. v
    A request in four short stepsLarge fields, the right keyboards for email and phone, questions that change for tattoo, piercing, tooth gem or a private session, and an editable summary before sending.
