Our Heart

No One Should
Serve Alone

Faithful missionaries and pastors stand on the front lines of the Great Commission, and many of them are weary. Like Aaron and Hur, we believe no one should carry that calling alone.

Among the Nations exists to stand beside them, and to invite local churches to stand beside them too.

"When Moses' hands grew tired, they took a stone and put it under him and he sat on it. Aaron and Hur held his hands up—one on one side, one on the other—so that his hands remained steady till sunset."— Exodus 17:12

What We Do

i.

Strengthen

We encourage, support, and care for missionaries and pastors around the world so they can finish their race well.

ii.

Mobilize

We help local churches move from interest to involvement, sending their people to serve, pray, and give.

iii.

Connect

We host and facilitate relationships between local churches across the world, building partnerships that last for years, not just a trip.

Church Partnership

Discover. Connect. Go. Partner.

A pathway for your church into long-term global partnership, and we walk every step with you.

i.

Discover

We get to know your church: your heart for the nations, your people, and your capacity to engage.

"Commit to the Lord whatever you do." — Proverbs 16:3
ii.

Connect

We introduce you to a missionary or pastor who fits your church, and we host the relationship from the very first conversation.

"Your partnership in the gospel from the first day until now." — Philippians 1:5
iii.

Go

We help your people visit, serve, and see the ministry firsthand, with trip planning and logistics handled alongside you.

"Here am I. Send me!" — Isaiah 6:8
iv.

Partner

Your church commits to lasting prayer, encouragement, and support, holding up the arms of those on the front lines.

"Aaron and Hur held his hands up." — Exodus 17:12

Sending the Next Generation
