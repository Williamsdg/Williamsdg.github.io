Our Mission

A Bridge
Between
Churches

Among the Nations exists to serve as a bridge between the local church in the United States and the local church worldwide. We come alongside churches as an extended Missions Pastor, helping them build a healthy, sustainable global missions strategy that leads to mobilization and long-term support of global leaders.

"How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news." — Romans 10:15

What We Do

Equip. Strengthen. Mobilize.

Three movements, one commission — carried out through the local church in every nation.

i.

Equip

We equip churches, leaders, and missions teams with the tools and vision to lead their church into global engagement.

"Equip the saints for the work." — Eph 4:12
ii.

Strengthen

We strengthen leaders and create sustainable structures so missions become part of the DNA of the church.

"Strengthen what remains." — Rev 3:2
iii.

Mobilize

We mobilize the church into action by creating real pathways for engagement in the mission of God.

"Whom shall I send?" — Isaiah 6:8
