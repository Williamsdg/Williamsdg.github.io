The neighborhood

Hidden in plain sight, in the heart of Seagrove.

The Grove is a quiet pocket of cottage-style homes with deep porches, shaded by old-growth pines, palms and native Florida plants. Walking and biking are the norm, and the Gulf is a short walk away.

  • WhereSeagrove Beach, Santa Rosa Beach, FL 32459
  • CountyWalton County
  • StreetsN Andalusia Avenue, Cullman Avenue
  • HomesCottage-style, two to five bedrooms
  • NearbySeaside, about half a mile west on 30A

Getting to the beach

The Gulf is a short walk south across 30A. In Walton County, personal items left on the beach overnight are removed, and sea turtle nesting season runs May 1 through October 31, so fill in holes and keep lights off the sand at night.
