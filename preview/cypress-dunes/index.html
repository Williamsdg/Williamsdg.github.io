The community

One of the few places on 30A that will never be overbuilt.

Cypress Dunes sits against the protected lands of Topsail Hill Preserve State Park. Majestic cypress trees, quiet walking paths and sunset views set the tone, with the beaches, boutiques and dining of 30A a few minutes away.

Every home here is custom and no two are alike. Approved builders let you choose your own interior selections, and architectural guidelines keep the neighborhood’s refined coastal character intact.

  • Gated, low-density neighborhood
  • Bordering Topsail Hill Preserve
  • Views of a coastal dune lake and the Gulf
  • Boardwalks over a preserved cypress pond

Building here

From a homesite to your own front porch.

Custom building in Cypress Dunes is guided, not open-ended. The builders give you a budget and a specification sheet up front, so you know what you are choosing from.

  1. Choose your homesiteHomesites are available in the community now, several bordering the preserve or the cypress pond.
  2. Pick a plan, or draw your ownSelect one of the builders’ floor plans or work with an approved architect on a custom design.
  3. Review the budget and spec sheetWindow colors, paint, lighting, cabinets, tile, plumbing fixtures, flooring, countertops, appliances and hardware.
  4. Make it yoursThe builder provides four hours of complimentary professional design help to get you started.

Life on 30A

More a way of life than a two-lane highway.

Scenic Highway 30A runs 24 miles from west Santa Rosa Beach to Inlet Beach, past Seaside, WaterColor, Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach. Cypress Dunes is on the west end, five minutes from Sandestin and Grand Boulevard.
