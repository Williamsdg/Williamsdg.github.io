1.The Project

Williams Digital will design, build, and launch a custom website and online store for Embroidery by Me. The goal: a professional storefront where customers can buy and personalize your retail products online, and where businesses, teams, and organizations can request quotes for custom embroidery and DTF work.

2.What's Included

  • Custom-designed website — Home, Shop, Custom Orders, Services (Embroidery / DTF / Alterations), About, and Contact. Designed for Embroidery by Me around your logo and photography — not a template.
  • Online store — your full retail lineup (no product limit) with sizes, colors, placements, and personalization options, with secure online checkout.
  • Custom-order system — a quote request form with artwork/logo upload that delivers each request to your email, so commercial and bulk jobs never get stuck in a shopping cart.
  • Order numbering — every retail order carries an Embroidery by Me order number (EBM-####) in your payment records.
  • Mobile-first build — the full experience is designed and tested for phones as well as desktop.
  • Launch on your domain — connected to your existing domain, with basic search-engine setup for "custom embroidery Leeds, Alabama" searches.

3.Payments & Your Stripe Account

Customer payments are processed through Stripe, a payment platform used by millions of businesses. The most important principle of this agreement:

Your store. Your money. The Stripe account belongs to you, and every customer payment deposits directly from Stripe into your bank account. Williams Digital never collects, holds, or has withdrawal access to your revenue at any point.

Setup happens in this order:

  1. You create your Stripe account at stripe.com in your name (or your business's name). Williams Digital will walk you through it — it takes about 15 minutes.
  2. You link your bank account and complete Stripe's identity verification, so payouts deposit directly to you.
  3. Once your account is verified and connected to your bank, you add Williams Digital (Dylan@williamsdigital.io) as a team member on your Stripe account so we can set up your products, connect the website's checkout, and configure order notifications.
  4. We build and test checkout. Williams Digital's access is used for setup and support only. You remain the account owner, you can see everything we do, and you may remove or reduce our access at any time after launch.

Stripe's processing fee (currently about 2.9% + $0.30 per online sale) is deducted by Stripe automatically from each transaction. There is no monthly Stripe fee — you only pay when you make a sale. Williams Digital receives no portion of your sales.

4.What We Need From You

  • Your logo, product photos, and examples of finished embroidery/DTF work.
  • Your product lineup — as many products as you want to launch with — with prices, sizes, colors, and personalization options.
  • Login access to your current Squarespace account so we can connect your domain to the new site.
  • Your Stripe account created, verified, and connected to your bank, with Williams Digital added as a team member (Section 3).
  • Timely feedback during the build — a reply within 2 business days keeps the schedule on track.
