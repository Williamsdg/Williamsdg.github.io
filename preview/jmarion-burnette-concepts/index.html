The point isn't the template. It's which story we're telling.

All three directions carry the same verified record — the yards, the state title, the recruitment, the transfer, the season that starts September 5. What changes is the argument each one makes about him.

The Program says he's already proven. Kinetic says the best is ahead. Friday Night says he never left Andalusia behind. A brand deciding whether to sign him is reacting to that framing as much as the numbers.

Rather than hand over one comp and hope it lands, I'd rather pick the direction together — then build it out completely. Every concept here is production-ready, not a mood board.
