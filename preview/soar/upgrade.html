Quick questions

Things you're probably wondering.

What happens to my current Mindbody / Acuity / Mailchimp accounts?

You'd cancel them after we launch. I'll help you export everything cleanly — client list, class schedule, member balances, email subscribers — so nothing is lost. Most studios save $200–280/mo by consolidating.

What if I send way more than 2,000 texts in a month?

Texts above 2,000 are billed at cost — about 1¢ per message. So 3,000 texts in a busy month would add ~$10. I'll alert you before it ever gets out of hand.

Can I edit class schedules, prices, and members myself?

Yes. The dashboard is built for you to run day-to-day operations without needing a developer. Add classes, change prices, message clients, manage parties — all from one place.

What about my website (soar-bungee-fitness.com)?

Your website stays exactly as it is and is included in the $185/mo. The dashboard is a separate URL (admin.soar.fit) that only you and your team can access.

What if I want to add a new feature later?

Small changes (new automations, template tweaks, schedule updates) are included. Larger features (e.g., a member-facing mobile app) we'd quote separately. You'll always know the cost up front.

Can I cancel?

Anytime with 30 days' notice. I'll help you export your data to wherever you go next. No long-term contract, no early termination fee.

Why is this cheaper than what I'm paying now?

Because I built it custom instead of paying a SaaS company markup on every feature. The SaaS tools (Mindbody, etc.) charge you for software they sell to thousands of studios with sales teams, ads, and shareholders. We just pay raw hosting + messaging costs and add a fair fee for my time.
