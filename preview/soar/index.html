Why Bungee?

Cardio, strength, and
core — all suspended
in mid-air.

Each class harnesses you into a heavy-duty bungee cord that offsets up to 80% of your body weight. The result is a workout that's easier on your joints but harder on your muscles — think dance, HIIT, jumps, and resistance moves you couldn't do on the ground.

FLY HIGH ✦
✦

Low impact, high intensity

Bungee assistance protects your knees, ankles, and back while you push hard. Hop, jump, and dive without the impact.

✦

Built for beginners

Every class starts with a harness check and basics. Your first flight is supervised step-by-step with a certified coach.

✦

Community first

Small class sizes, certified trainers, and a studio that feels more like a flight crew than a gym.
