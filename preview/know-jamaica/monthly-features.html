Core Services

The five things required to run the platform.

These are non-negotiable for a working booking site and operator console. Each runs on a per-month subscription paid directly to the vendor — Williams Digital configures it on your behalf at launch.

Required

Hosting & application platform

Powered by Vercel Pro
Runs the public booking site and the operator console. Includes global CDN, automatic SSL, preview deploys for every change, and the serverless functions that handle bookings, webhooks, and approval flows.
To turn on
  • A Vercel account created under Know Jamaica's email — owner stays the account holder
  • DNS access for knowjamaica.com (we'll point it at Vercel)
  • A payment method on file (Vercel bills monthly)
Monthly
$20/mo
Vercel Pro — required for commercial use, faster cold starts, included analytics.
→ Vercel
Required

Booking database, auth & staff chat

Powered by Supabase Pro
The brain of the operation. Stores every booking, every customer, every approve/deny decision, every signed waiver pointer, and every staff chat message. Built-in authentication for staff logins and real-time updates for the dashboard's live timeline and excursion staff chat room.
To turn on
  • A Supabase organization created under Know Jamaica's email
  • Daily backups enabled (included in Pro)
  • Williams Digital provisions the schema, RLS policies, and seed data
Monthly
$25/mo
Postgres DB + auth + storage + realtime chat — all in one bill.
→ Supabase
Required

Transactional email

Powered by SendGrid Essentials
Sends booking confirmations, receipts, cruise-day reminders, waiver links, approval/denial notices, and review requests. Templated, branded, and tracked — you'll see open rates and bounces in the operator console.
To turn on
  • A SendGrid account with your business address
  • DNS records added to verify knowjamaica.com as the sender domain (we'll send the exact records)
  • An approved sender email like bookings@knowjamaica.com
Monthly
$19.95/mo
50,000 emails/mo. Free tier (100/day) is too low for cruise-day volume.
→ SendGrid
Recommended Dual

Payments — Stripe + WiPay

Card payments via Stripe · Jamaica-native via WiPay
Stripe handles the majority of bookings (US/Canadian cruisers paying with cards). WiPay covers Jamaican locals who want to pay in JMD via bank transfer, NCB, Scotiabank, JN, or Lynk. Both have no monthly fee — only per-transaction.
To turn on
  • Stripe: Jamaican Ltd. company registration, TRN, business bank account — same-day approval
  • WiPay: TRN, business registration, bank letter — 2 weeks underwriting (start day 1)
  • Williams Digital wires up webhooks and the dual-checkout UI
Monthly
$0+ per-tx fees
Stripe · 2.9% + 30¢ per charge
WiPay · 3.5% + JMD$25 per charge
→ Stripe · WiPay
Required

E-signature waivers

Powered by Dropbox Sign (formerly HelloSign)
Sends liability waivers, accessibility-care forms, and minor-consent docs via email or WhatsApp link. Guests sign on their phone. Signed PDFs are auto-archived to the booking record. Recommended over DocuSign — same legal weight, cleaner API, $25/mo cheaper.
To turn on
  • A Dropbox Sign Standard subscription (unlimited templates + API access)
  • Upload your existing waivers (or we draft new ones with your attorney's review)
  • Williams Digital configures templates and webhook callbacks
Monthly
$20/mo
Unlimited templates, API access, audit trails included.
→ Dropbox Sign
Recommended Add-ons

Optional — but they pay for themselves fast.

These aren't required for the platform to function, but they directly reduce no-shows, drive direct bookings, and protect your business. Pick what you want and skip what you don't.

Strongly Recommended

WhatsApp + SMS reminders

Powered by Twilio (WhatsApp Business + SMS)
Cruise-day "your guide will meet you at Gate B at 7:30 AM" reminders the night before — measurably reduces no-shows. WhatsApp first (most tourists already use it), SMS as fallback for guests without WhatsApp. Same Twilio account powers both.
To turn on
  • A Twilio account + a Jamaican phone number ($1.15/mo)
  • Facebook Business verification for WhatsApp (free, takes 1–2 weeks — start day 1)
  • Williams Digital wires up automated reminder flows in Supabase
Monthly (est.)
$30–60/mo at current volume
WhatsApp ~$0.005–0.04/msg · SMS ~$0.045/msg · Jamaica number $1.15/mo. Scales with bookings.
→ Twilio
Recommended

Branded email inboxes

Powered by Google Workspace
Real bookings@knowjamaica.com and jay@knowjamaica.com inboxes that you can read and reply from like normal Gmail. Required if you want guests replying to confirmations to actually land in your inbox (instead of into the void).
To turn on
  • A Google Workspace subscription
  • DNS access to verify knowjamaica.com (we'll send the records)
  • Pick how many inboxes you need — most start with 2
Monthly
$6/user/mo

Optional

Error monitoring & uptime alerts

Powered by Sentry + UptimeRobot
If a guest gets an error during checkout, you'll know about it before they email you. UptimeRobot pings the site every 5 minutes and alerts you if it's ever down. Both have free tiers that cover Know Jamaica's current scale.
To turn on
  • Sentry account (free tier OK to start)
  • UptimeRobot account (free tier covers 50 monitors)
  • Williams Digital wires alerts into your email or WhatsApp
Monthly
$0–26/mo
