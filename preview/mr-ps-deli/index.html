        The family

        Four generations of Pilleteris

        The Pilleteri family has been serving Birmingham fresh-cut meat since 1934, and the tradition carries on at Mr. P's in Bluff Park. The shop is owned and run by the third and fourth generation. Steaks and pork chops are cut to order. The Italian sausage is made in-house every other day.
