Featured event

2026 Montevallo ArtWalk

24Saturday, October 24
10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

An eclectic and affordable art walk on Main Street. Local and regional artists sell their original work from booths downtown. Free to attend. A collaboration of the City of Montevallo, the Montevallo Arts Collaborative and the University of Montevallo.

  • WhereMain Street, downtown Montevallo. Orr Park and Owl's Cove Park are a short walk.
  • WhenSaturday, October 24, 2026, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • VendorsOriginal creations only. No resellers of pre-made or second-hand goods, no independent consultants, no recruiters. Informational booths for community non-profits are welcome. The City has waived the business license requirement for the event.

Concert series

Friday Nights at the Cove

Free live music at Owl's Cove Park, 737 Main Street, hosted by Montevallo Main Street. Bring a chair or a blanket, grab dinner to go from a downtown restaurant, and settle in. Music starts at 6:30 p.m. The 2026 season:

Reminders

Hear about it before it happens

The City already sends text alerts. Text "Montevallo" to 91896 and reply YES. Street closures, the storm shelter opening, and event-day changes come straight to your phone.

Text "Montevallo" to 91896

Organizing an event downtown?

Street closures go through City Hall, and the Council approves them at a regular meeting. Start with the City Clerk at (205) 665-2555 x105. Montevallo Main Street can help with the downtown district.
