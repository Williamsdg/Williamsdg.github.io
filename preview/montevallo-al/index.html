Business & growth

A college town with a Main Street that works

The University of Montevallo, the American Village and the Alabama National Cemetery bring students and visitors through town all year. A nationally accredited Main Street program, an active Industrial Development Board, and quick access to I-65 and rail make it a place to open, not just to visit.
