05 Guides, gear and the weather

Safety first,
always

Our guides are highly experienced, with extensive paddling in the Greek seas and abroad. They have all completed training in England and hold BCU and ACA certifications; our guides are certified by ASI and the British Canoe Union. Practice continues year-round — we run staff expeditions annually to check and improve our skills.

The Aegean is blessed with the north winds of the meltemia. Our paddling area is on the south coast, where the cliffs provide shelter — which makes it an ideal spot for paddling, even for beginners. On some days paddling is safe yet still exciting; when that happens we always tell our guests about the conditions and leave the choice to join to them. Tours are typically only cancelled in cases of strong winds, heavy rain or potential thunderstorms — and if we cancel for weather, you are refunded in full or rescheduled.

Getting to the base

  1. Driving

    We strongly suggest renting a car — it lets you explore more of the island around your paddle. Parking is at Akrotiri Beach.

  2. Local bus

    Buses run to Akrotiri every 35 minutes during the summer months. The base is a short walk along the beach.

  3. Pick-up & drop-off

    Our 9-seater minivan, on request: €30 per person return or €20 one way. Different rates may apply to or from Santorini Airport and Athinios Port. We get as close to your accommodation as we can.

  4. Arriving by cruise ship

    Tender to the old port of Fira, then take the cable car up to Fira town. Our van waits at the closest pick-up point to the cable car, Caldera Yachting – Santorini. Check your first and last tender times before you book. Map of the cruise pick-up point →

  5. The day before

    We call or email around 19:00 the evening before your activity to finalise the meeting point or the transfer details.

07 How booking works

A €30 deposit
holds your seat

No booking is confirmed until the deposit payment is completed. Everything after that is settled at the base.

Step 01

Pay the deposit

€30 per person, online by PayPal, debit or credit card. €30 for one guest, €60 for two, €90 for three, €120 for four.

Step 02

Fill in the booking form

You are redirected to the booking form straight after payment. We confirm your program and date by email.

Step 03

Pay the balance at the base

The remaining balance is paid in cash (euros) when you arrive at Akrotiri Beach. Paying by card on arrival carries a 4% surcharge.

Special offer: choose to complete your payment in cash on arrival and you get a 10% discount. Day-tour balances are cash (euros) only at the base.

Cancellation — day tours

  • More than 5 days before€10 per person cancellation fee
  • 2 – 4 days before50% of the total amount per person
  • Less than 48 hours / no-show100% charge
  • Cancelled for weatherFull refund — or reschedule to another available date

Multiday expeditions run on a separate schedule: a 50% deposit secures a place, with 100% refunded 90–31 days before the trip, 50% refunded 30–8 days before, and no refund inside 7 days. A €8.80 bank fee is deducted from any refund. Full terms are published on the Santorini Sea Kayak Policies & Standards page.

10 Before you ask

Frequently asked

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. A €30 deposit per person confirms your booking, paid in advance by PayPal or debit/credit card. No booking is confirmed unless the deposit payment is completed. The balance is paid at the base.

How can I pay?

Our online booking system accepts PayPal, debit cards and credit cards for the deposit. On arrival we accept cash in euros. If you choose to pay by card on arrival a 4% surcharge applies — and if you complete your payment in cash on arrival, you get a 10% discount.

When are the activities available?

Every day from April to October, weather permitting. In certain years we may extend the operational season beyond these months. Note that the Caldera Crossing runs only in March, April, October and November.

Do I need kayaking experience?

It depends on the trip:

  • South Discovery, Sunset South Discovery and the 1-Hour Red Beach paddle are for all — swimming knowledge is mandatory, and tandem kayaks are used for all guests.
  • Family Sea Kayak Tour and Stand Up Paddling are beginner level.
  • Round the Lighthouse is moderate to difficult and needs good paddling experience.
  • Caldera Crossing is recommended for experienced kayakers only.
Can I bring children?

The Family Sea Kayak Tour is built for children aged 6 to 14 and runs with a minimum of 6 participants — if fewer are booked we reserve the right to cancel the activity or offer an alternative private tour option. The South Discovery, Sunset and 1-hour tours and SUP are 14 and above; Round the Lighthouse is 16 and above; the Caldera Crossing is 21 and above.

What happens if the weather turns?

Your safety comes first. Our guides monitor the weather and sea conditions and reserve the right to modify or cancel an activity when conditions are not suitable. Tours are typically only cancelled in cases of strong winds, heavy rain or potential thunderstorms. If we cancel, you will be notified in advance and offered a reschedule to another available date — or a full refund of the deposit if you cannot reschedule or there is no availability. On days where paddling is safe yet still exciting, we tell you the conditions and leave the choice to join to you.

Where do we meet, and when will I get the details?

Our base is on Akrotiri Beach, 300 m below the Prehistoric Excavations of Akrotiri and right before the Red Beach, next to the restaurant “The Cave of Nikolas.” We get in touch the day before your paddling activity — around 19:00 — to finalise the meeting point or your transfer details.

Is there a pick-up and drop-off service?

Yes, on request, in our 9-seater minivan: €30 per person for a round trip or €20 per person for a one-way transfer. These rates do not apply to pick-ups or drop-offs from or to Santorini Airport or Athinios Port, where a different rate may apply. We will do our best to get as close to your accommodation as possible.

I'm arriving from a cruise ship — how does that work?

Tender to the old port of Fira, then take the cable car up to Fira town. Our van waits at the closest pick-up location to the cable car: Caldera Yachting – Santorini. Before finalising your booking, check the times of the first and last tender back to your ship so you have time to arrive comfortably and return without any rush.

What about photos from the tour?

Photos are optional, at €3 to download. The charge supports our guides, who capture these on their own mobile devices, and covers the cloud service we use to share the files. Our guides are dedicated to delivering an exceptional experience, but the safety of our guests remains their top priority. Files are retained for three months and then permanently deleted.

What kayaks and paddles do you use?

Double sea kayaks with rudders from Prijon, Riot, Necky and Wilderness Systems. For more confident kayakers, single kayaks with rudders or skegs are available on request and subject to availability — Zegul, Tahe Marine, Prijon, Riot, Necky, Wilderness Systems, Northshore and Valley. Our paddles are carbon, made by Werner, Zegul and SCK.

What are your office hours?

09:00 to 18:00. Call +30 6951 801051 or +30 22860 85237, or email santoriniseakayak@gmail.com. For emergencies during an activity, +30 6951 800162.
