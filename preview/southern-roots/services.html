01  —  Service

NIL Representation

Expert representation for professional and college athletes, maximizing NIL value through strategic partnerships.

We handle inbound and outbound deal flow, vet every partner, and make sure the athlete understands what they are signing before they sign it. You keep the relationship with your fans and your school; we handle the paperwork, the negotiation and the follow-through.

02  —  Service

Contract Negotiation

Line-by-line review and negotiation of every agreement, led by a licensed attorney and former Army judge advocate.

Every deal gets read in full and explained in plain language before anyone signs. We negotiate term, exclusivity, usage rights, renewal and termination — and we tell you when a deal is not worth taking.

03  —  Service

Brand Strategy & Endorsements

Securing high-impact brand partnerships and endorsements that align with your athlete-first career values.

We build the story before we sell it: positioning, content direction, social growth and the kind of partner that still makes sense for you in five years.

04  —  Service

Post-Career Advising

End-to-end strategy for a successful transition from college NIL deals to long-term professional success.

Careers in sport are short. We plan for what comes after — education, network, credentials and the financial habits that make the NIL years matter long after the last season.

05  —  Service

NIL Strategy

Custom NIL roadmaps tailored to monetize your influence during and after your collegiate sports career.

A written plan for the season and the offseason: which categories fit, what your audience is actually worth, and where to invest your limited time for the biggest return.

06  —  Service

Athlete Management

Day-to-day representation and coordination so athletes and families can focus on competing.

Scheduling, appearances, brand communication, compliance paperwork and the constant small decisions that come with being a represented athlete — handled, tracked and reported back to you.
