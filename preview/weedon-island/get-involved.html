Get involved

The Center depends on the commitment and dedication of its volunteers. If you like meeting people, learning about the environment or spending time outdoors, there's a place for you.

Volunteer roles

Apply through GivePulse, or contact Center staff to ask about a role.

Apply to volunteer on GivePulse

Center docent

Greet visitors, answer questions about the preserve, its exhibits and programs, and run the Center's welcome desk.

Tour guide

Lead visitors on tours that share the plants, animals and history of Weedon Island. Trail tours usually run October through May.

Like hands-on science? Watch the events calendar for the Volunteer Archaeology Lab, where you help archaeologists clean, sort and identify artifacts. More: Pinellas County Volunteer Services and its volunteer FAQs.

Friends of Weedon Island

A nonprofit founded in 1992 to promote public understanding of the preserve as an ecological and cultural resource.

The Friends sponsor key programs and events in close partnership with Pinellas County Parks and Conservation Resources. They funded the tank that conserved the prehistoric canoe, supported the film Gulf Islanders, and host the Gopher Weedon 8K Trail Run. Membership is open to anyone, and to corporate sponsors, who wants to help preserve the history, culture and environment of Weedon Island.
