01 — The marks

Eight directions.

Four palettes, all built on red.

05 — Cost

$300, once.

What you'd get

  • Your primary logo, finished and drawn properly
  • A stacked version and a compact version for tight spaces
  • A one-color version for shirts, signs and truck lettering
  • A square icon sized for Facebook and Instagram profile pictures
  • Your colors with exact codes, so every sign shop matches
  • Your fonts, and a short plain-English guide on using it all
  • Files any printer, sign shop or embroiderer can use (SVG, PDF, PNG, JPG)
  • Two rounds of revisions on the direction you pick

What it isn't: not a monthly fee, and not required for the website. The logo files are yours to keep and use anywhere, forever, even if we never work together again.
