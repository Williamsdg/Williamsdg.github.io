La storia

Named for a hilltop town of palaces, vineyards and poets.

Ariccia Cucina Italiana opened in 2004 in partnership with Auburn University and its sister campus in Ariccia, Italy, home to the Joseph S. Bruno Auburn Abroad in Italy program.

We honor classic central Italian cuisine: the freshest seasonal products, Italian meats and cheeses, signature porchetta, house-made focaccia and Auburn's best wood-fired pizzas. Dine by candlelight inside, or al fresco in a “secret garden” of hedges, a large fountain and a pergola strung with twinkle lights.

The signature

Porchetta di Ariccia

This Italian staple, originating from 15th century Ariccia, Italy, is celebrated annually at the festival “Sagra della Porchetta di Ariccia.”

The classic dish is seasoned with salt, pepper, rosemary, garlic and wild fennel, then rolled, slow roasted for eight hours and sliced to order. It was included in the “100 Dishes to Eat in Alabama” list.

  1. SeasonSalt, pepper, rosemary, garlic and wild fennel
  2. RollRolled, then slow roasted for eight hours
  3. SliceSliced to order, every time

The cellar

1,500 bottles, and a reason behind them.

Ariccia's wine program holds Wine Spectator's “Best of Award of Excellence” for 2020 and 2021, given to restaurants with extensive selections, significant vintage depth and excellent breadth across multiple regions.

The program is overseen by Adam Keeshan. Many of the wineries on the list host Auburn's Hospitality Management students each year.

“Winemakers across Northern California, Italy, Oregon, Argentina and Chile dedicate their time, knowledge and open their homes annually to our students. It's one small way we can show our appreciation … by featuring their wines on our list.”

Hans van der Reijden, The Hotel at Auburn University and Dixon Conference Center

    Cocktails and jazz

    Auburn & Opelika's only true jazz lounge: a tucked-away retreat for specialty cocktails, live jazz, blues and soul music, small bites and an extensive wine list.

    House-made

    The cellos

    Our “cellos” are made in-house using 190 grain alcohol, infused for 30 days at 50 degrees and blended with sugar and water for another 15 days before being bottled and served ice cold.
