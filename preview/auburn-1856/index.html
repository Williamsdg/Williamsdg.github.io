Roof to table

The garden is upstairs.

1856 – Culinary Residence’s menu is shaped by the produce grown on the rooftop garden of the Tony & Libba Rane Culinary Science Center.

Our vegetables, produce and herbs come from an operational rooftop garden, created and managed by Auburn University’s Horticulture Program, and looking out over our iconic Samford Hall. When in season, herbs, flowers and seeds from the garden find their way into the food and the cocktails.

Questions

Before you visit.

Still have a question? 1856@auburn1856.com

Where do I park?

Guests can use the complimentary valet parking at The Laurel Hotel & Spa, 130 E Thach Ave, Auburn, AL 36830. Let the valet staff know you are dining with us.

What is the dress code?

We do not have a dress code, but many of our guests wear “elevated casual.” Jackets are not required, though many guests do opt to wear them.

Do you allow children?

1856 – Culinary Residence is a small, intimate teaching restaurant. We do not admit children under the age of 12 to dine in the restaurant, and children’s menus cannot be accommodated.

Is there a deposit for dinner?

We no longer require a deposit for reservations. Your card information will be on file in order to hold your reservation. Cancellations made within 24 hours of a reservation will not receive a refund.

Is a service charge or gratuity included?

All dinner reservations include a 20% service charge and 9% tax. The service charge ensures that all of our employees are paid a fair wage and receive benefits all year long. Additional gratuity is at the discretion of each guest, and 100% of discretionary tips are paid to non-salaried front of house employees.

Can you accommodate allergies and dietary preferences?

The tasting menu can be customized for most allergies and dietary restrictions. Please list them on your reservation. If you have severe allergies or are booking at short notice, contact us before booking at 1856@auburn1856.com. Common allergens are used throughout our kitchen in close proximity to other ingredients.

Can separate bookings be seated together?

The majority of our tables are four-tops and cannot be combined. For a large party, ask about a buyout or the private dining room.

Do you allow corkage?

We do not permit outside alcohol inside 1856 – Culinary Residence.

Do you accept cash?

1856 – Culinary Residence does not accept cash for payment.

How do gift cards work?

Gift cards may be purchased inside the restaurant Tuesday – Saturday from 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and can only be used for charges incurred at the restaurant. Any remaining balance can be used for future visits.

When do graduation reservations open?

Reservations open a month before each graduation date: Spring (April 5th), Summer (July 5th), Fall/Winter (November 5th).

Are you open on holidays?

For 2026, we will be open on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and New Year’s Eve. We will be closed on Christmas Day.

How often do the menus change?

Our lunch changes seasonally and our dinner tasting menus change every 4 weeks.
