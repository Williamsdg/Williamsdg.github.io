The community

A quiet corner of 30A, kept that way on purpose.

The Preserve sits just west of Grayton Beach and WaterColor, between Grayton Beach and Blue Mountain Beach. State forest borders the community, walking trails run through its own preserved coastal landscape, and a private boardwalk carries owners and their guests to the sand.

  • LocationWest end of Scenic Highway 30A, Santa Rosa Beach
  • CountyWalton County, Florida
  • Next doorPoint Washington State Forest
  • HomesCustom single-family residences
  • Managed byCMA, Community Management Associates

Owners, guests & renters

One link to send before every stay.

Many homes here welcome family, friends and rental guests through the year. A single guest page means owners and rental managers stop retyping the same instructions, and the board stops fielding the same questions.

Arriving at the gate

Gate access is arranged by the owner or rental manager before arrival. This section carries the association's own entry instructions, so every guest gets the same ones.

Pools, courts and fitness center

Hours and house rules for the two pools, the tennis and pickleball courts and the fitness center, taken directly from the association's rules and kept current by the board.

The boardwalk and the beach

The private boardwalk leads to the community's deeded beach access. In Walton County, personal items left on the beach overnight are removed, and sea turtle nesting season runs May 1 through October 31, so fill in holes and keep lights off the sand at night.

Parking, trash and quiet hours

Where guests may park, which days collection runs and when quiet hours begin. The details come from the association's rules, in plain language.

Buying & selling

Closing on a home in The Preserve.

Title companies, agents and buyers all need the same things from the association. Putting the process on the site saves a round of phone calls on every sale.

Request the estoppel

The seller or closing agent requests an estoppel certificate from the management company. Florida law generally requires it within 10 business days of a written request.

Get the documents

The declaration, bylaws, rules, current budget and assessment information, available to download in one place.

Welcome the new owner

New owners receive a portal login, the gate and amenity guide and the board's welcome note on day one.
