Step Three

What I need from you to start the AI build

To get the AI site off the ground, here's everything you can prep on your side. Most of it you already have from the main shop — we're just pointing it at a new use case.

  1. Confirm the use case in your own words
    Send me a short voice memo or text describing what you want the AI to do for visitors. The clearer this is, the less back-and-forth we have. I'll write back with how I'd build it before we start coding.
  2. Pick 15–25 of your strongest pieces to "train" on
    Doesn't have to be perfect — just paintings you'd be proud to have the AI surface or recommend first. Photos from Shopify are fine. Send the list or just tag them in Shopify with ai-feature and I'll pull them.
  3. Write 2–3 short sentences in your voice
    When the AI talks to visitors, it should sound like you — not like a generic chatbot. Send me a few lines you'd say to a collector who walked into your studio. Tone, words you use, what you'd ask first. I'll model the AI's voice on that.
  4. Decide who can use it (and when)
    Public on the site? Private link you share with designers? Behind an email-gate so you grow your list? Tell me your preference — I'll explain the trade-offs and we'll pick together.
  5. Send 3 examples of AI tools you've liked (or hated)
    Doesn't have to be art-related. Could be anything — a shopping assistant, a recipe site, a customer-service chat. Helps me understand the vibe you want and the vibe to avoid.
  6. Block 30 minutes for a kickoff call
    Once the deposit lands and I have the above, we'll do a quick call to lock the scope, timeline, and what the first version actually does. Everything else flows from that conversation.
