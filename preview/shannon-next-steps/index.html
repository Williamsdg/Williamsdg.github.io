The AI room design tool runs on four services. All four are owned by you and billed to your card — I never have standalone control. Easiest way to knock all of this out: book a 30-minute screen-share call and we walk through them together.

1

Google Cloud + Gemini AI Studio

Powers the AI room rendering.
Cost
~$0.04 per render (we cap at $50/mo to start)
Time
~10 minutes
  1. Go to aistudio.google.com → sign in with your Google account
  2. Click "Get API key" → "Create API key" → "Create API key in new project"
  3. Copy the API key and send it to me (treat it like a password)
  4. Go to console.cloud.google.com/billing → link a billing account with your card
  5. Set up a $50/month budget cap with email alerts at 50%, 90%, and 100% under "Budgets & alerts"
  6. Turn on 2-factor authentication at myaccount.google.com/security

Step Four · Content

What I need from you to build it

To get the AI room design tool off the ground, here's what to prep on your side. Most of it you already have from the main shop — we're pointing it at a new use case.

  1. Confirm the use case in your own words
    Send me a short voice memo or text — what should the AI do for someone landing on the page? "Help me picture what painting works in my dining room" is the working idea, but I want to hear how YOU'D describe it.
  2. Pick 15–25 of your strongest pieces to "train" on
    Doesn't have to be perfect — paintings you'd want the AI to recommend first to a new collector. Either send me the list, or tag them in Shopify with ai-feature and I'll pull them automatically.
  3. Write 2–3 short sentences in your voice
    When the AI talks to visitors, it should sound like you — not a generic chatbot. Send me a few lines you'd say to a collector walking into your studio. The words you actually use, the questions you'd ask first. I'll model the AI's voice on that.
  4. Decide who can use it (and when)
    Public on the site? Private link you share with designers? Behind an email-gate so you grow your list? Tell me your preference — I'll explain the trade-offs and we'll pick together on the kickoff call.
  5. Send 3 examples of AI tools you've liked (or hated)
    Doesn't have to be art-related — could be a shopping assistant, a recipe site, anything. Helps me understand the vibe you want and the one to avoid.
  6. Block 30 minutes for the kickoff call
    Once the kickoff payment lands and you've sent the above, we'll do a quick call to lock scope, timeline, and what the first version actually does. Everything else flows from that conversation.
