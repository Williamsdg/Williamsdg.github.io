Quick answers

What you're probably asking right now.

What if my building doesn't have Wi-Fi I trust?

That's actually Anchor's specialty. Players sync over your wired or wireless LAN, not the public internet. Once content is cached on each player (within a few minutes of pairing), the network only matters for updates. Real-time playback is local.

What if we already have screens and hardware?

Better. Anchor pairs with any Apple TV, Pi, mini PC, or laptop you already have. If your screens have HDMI and an internet-capable player, we're good.

What about updates to content from outside the building?

The Hub can publish a secure, optional cloud relay so you (or staff) can update playlists from anywhere — phone, laptop, home. Your media still lives locally; only the *control plane* uses the relay.

What if we want to cancel?

Cancel any time, no fee. Your media is on your machine — it doesn't go anywhere. The Hub even keeps running in standalone mode until you replace it. Our churn-by-cancellation rate is the only churn rate that should worry a vendor.

Who actually supports this if something breaks?

Me. Direct phone, direct email. Williams Digital is small and Anchor isn't a side project — it's a real product I build, support, and stand behind. You're not getting routed to tier-1 outsourced support.
