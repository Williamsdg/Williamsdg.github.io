How every project runs.

Missed deadlines and poor communication turn an exciting project into a frustrating one. Our process is built so that doesn't happen.

  1. Free estimate

    We come to you, measure your space and give you straightforward pricing — no hidden costs or vague numbers.

  2. Plan & timeline

    Your project manager lays out every stage. If we find a structural concern, we explain it plainly and give you the best plan forward.

  3. Build

    Flooring and remodeling specialists work as one crew, with furniture moving, prep and clean-up handled for you.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We walk the finished space with you. Every project is backed by our one-year workmanship warranty.
