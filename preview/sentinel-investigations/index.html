Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Is everything I share kept confidential?+

Absolutely. Confidentiality is the foundation of our work. Your inquiry, your identity, and every detail of your case are protected from your first call through case closure.

Are your investigators licensed?+

Yes. Sentinel Investigations is fully licensed and insured to operate throughout the region, and our work is conducted in full compliance with state law so evidence holds up if your case goes to court.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve all of the Tri-County region — including the metro area, suburbs, and surrounding counties — with statewide reach for surveillance, process service, and investigations.

How quickly can you start?+

In most cases, immediately. Time is critical in investigations, so we offer fast response and 24/7 availability. After your consultation we'll build a strategy and begin field work as soon as you approve it.

Will I receive a report I can use in court?+

Yes. Every case concludes with clear, detailed documentation — including court-ready reports, video, and evidence logs — prepared to support attorneys and stand up before a judge.

Do you work with attorneys and insurers?+

Frequently. We partner with attorneys, corporate clients, and insurance providers, and offer a dedicated attorney portal for requesting investigations and accessing case materials securely.
