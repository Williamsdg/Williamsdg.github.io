// What makes this version better

Eight specific changes.

Each one is something I found on the current site, and what the concept does about it.

Area
Current site
Concept
First impression
Current siteA stock photo of a gavel, the firm name, and a welcome paragraph. The same template many small firms use.
ConceptA headline in the firm's own words, who the firm represents, and the AV rating, all on the first screen.
Who you serve
Current siteCarriers, employers, individuals and other law firms all read the same page.
ConceptA four-way starting point on the homepage that shows each visitor the practice areas that apply to them.
Practice areas
Current siteNot in the top menu. They sit behind a small side link, as one long bulleted list.
ConceptA numbered list on the homepage, one click from the menu.
Local counsel
Current siteContract services and local counsel work is the last paragraph of the Practice Areas page.
ConceptIts own section, with a short form for requesting a case or project budget.
Calling
Current siteThe phone number is plain text, and the site tells phones not to turn it into a link.
ConceptA call button in the header on every screen, plus David's direct dial as a button.
Office address
Current siteThe sidebar says 4268 Cahaba Heights Court. The Contact page says 4328.
ConceptOne address, shown once, with a map and a directions button. I used 4268 and will correct it if that is the wrong one.
Spelling
Current siteTypos in visible places: "Effeciency" in a page headline, "greather" in the State Bar disclaimer, and "compensaiton" in David's bio.
ConceptProofread. The disclaimer is kept, with the one misspelling corrected.
Google
Current siteA generic description and no law-firm data. The footer still shows the site builder's Login link.
ConceptA written title and description naming the practice and the city, plus the name, address and phone data search engines read.

Observations taken from the firm's public website on October 7, 2026. Nothing about the firm on the concept is invented. Which practice areas appear under each type of visitor is my first draft and is yours to correct.
