Attorney

David L. Dean

David Dean is a partner of the firm, where he holds a diverse practice primarily focused on workers' compensation law, general civil liability defense, products liability and mass torts.

David has taught continuing education courses in workers' compensation law, where he has extensive experience. He has earned an AV* Preeminent Peer Review Rating from Martindale-Hubbell, which is its highest rating for ethical standards and legal ability.

Born in Portsmouth, Virginia, David attended Livingston University, where he also played football. He lives in Birmingham.
