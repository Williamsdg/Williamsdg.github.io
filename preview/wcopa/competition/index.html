Three Rounds

The Road to the
Grand Final

Round01

Preliminaries

Thousands compete within their age brackets. A panel of judges scores every performance on the 100-point system — this round determines the gold, silver and bronze medalists.

Medals Decided Here
↓
Round02

Semifinals

Medalists advance to compete within their age divisions for the title of Overall World Champion in each category. The field narrows — only the top scores move on.
