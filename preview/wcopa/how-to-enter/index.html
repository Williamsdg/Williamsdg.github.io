How to Enter

Your Journey
Starts Here.

Five steps separate you from representing your country on the world stage.

Discipline: ✓
01

Find Your Country

Every journey begins with your National Director — WCOPA's official representative at home.

02

Submit Your Audition

Complete the application with your legal name and a link to your talent video.

03

Get Selected

The selection committee reviews your audition — or invites you to audition live in your country.

04

Register

Accepted performers register with their director's guidance, using the Official Handbook.

05

Represent Your Country

Join your national delegation and take your talent to the World Championships.

Singers, dancers, actors, models, instrumentalists and variety artists are all welcome.

The Waiting Game, Solved

What Happens
After I Audition?

1–2

Your Audition Is Reviewed

Your National Director or the selection committee watches your video — or schedules your live audition.

✓

You Receive Your Decision

Accepted applicants receive an official acceptance email with next steps for their country's delegation.

→

You Register

Your director walks you through registration using the Official Handbook — events, entries and paperwork.

★

You Join Your Team

Uniform, credentials, schedule — you're officially part of your country's team headed to the Championships.

Plan for the Full Picture

Participation involves a WCOPA entry fee, optional additional competition events, companion badges for family, your country's team uniform, and travel & lodging. Your National Director explains every cost up front through the Official Handbook — no surprises.
