The Journey

Your Road to the
World Stage

01

Submit Your Talent

Fill out the application with your legal name and a link to your talent video. Your country's National Director takes it from there.

≈ 5 minutes
02

Audition

The selection committee reviews your video — or your National Director invites you to a live audition in your country.

Online or In-Person
03

Represent Your Country

Accepted performers receive their official acceptance and join their national team — one flag, one delegation.

Official Acceptance
04

Prepare & Train

Register with your National Director's guidance, then sharpen your act with year-round training from industry professionals.

Official Handbook

Join thousands of performers from 70+ countries for a week of competition, showcases, industry seminars and the parade of nations.

Championship Week
