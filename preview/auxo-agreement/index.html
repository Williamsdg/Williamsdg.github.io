1.What this is

AUXO is built as a sports publication that happens to publish episodes — not a podcast landing page. Episodes arrive automatically from the YouTube channel; written reporting, quick takes and crew pages are published by AUXO from an admin area built for people who have never used a CMS.

The platform described below is already built and running. This agreement covers its release to AUXO, the brand work around it, and launch on AUXO's own domain.

2.What gets delivered

Automatic episode sync
Every public upload on the channel appears on the site within the hour — title, thumbnail, runtime and date. Nobody uploads an episode to the website, ever.
The newsroom
Full article publishing with a plain-English editor, seven sections, bylines, hero images, tags, drafts and scheduling.
Quick takes
Short posts any of the crew can fire off from a phone in under a minute, with their own byline.
Crew pages
A page per person, with their photo, bio, socials, and everything they have written or appeared on.
Admin area
One sign-in, three permission levels. Contributors write, editors publish. No technical knowledge required.
Mailbag and newsletter
Question submissions and email capture, landing in an inbox inside the admin — audience AUXO owns, off YouTube.
Sponsor management
Partner logos on the homepage and a partners page, with an enquiry form behind it.
Built for search
Structured data on every article, an automatic sitemap, and social share cards — so the writing reaches people who have never found the channel.

4.Brand identity

Ten logo directions have been presented as concept work. One direction — or a combination of two, if that is the preference — is chosen by AUXO and developed into a finished mark.

The finished mark is delivered as SVG, PNG at three sizes, and PDF; in full colour, all-white, all-black and a single-colour version suitable for embroidery and one-colour print; with horizontal, stacked and avatar-cropped lockups, and a written minimum-size and clear-space rule.

Until final payment is received, the concept directions remain the property of Williams Digital, are licensed to no one, and are shown watermarked. On final payment, full ownership of the chosen mark transfers to AUXO.

6.Hosting and ongoing service

Twelve months of hosting, database and maintenance are included in the price above, running from the launch date. That covers site hosting, the database and file storage, the automated episode sync, SSL, backups, security and platform updates, and email delivery for form notifications.

Before the twelve months are up, Williams Digital will quote a continuing service plan. AUXO is under no obligation to take it — but the site depends on a hosted database, so it cannot run unhosted. If AUXO chooses to move the site elsewhere at that point, an export of all content and a copy of the source code are provided at no charge.
