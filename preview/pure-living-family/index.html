Why we exist

We built the community we couldn't find.

When our son was diagnosed, we were surrounded by people who loved us — and still felt completely alone. The appointments, the advocacy, the worry that never fully switches off. Somewhere in it, we stopped taking care of ourselves.

Pure Living Family started because we needed it too. Today we create the experiences every special-needs caregiver deserves: space to breathe, people who truly get it, and permission to rest without guilt.

S&A
Shawn & AngelaFounders, Pure Living Family
