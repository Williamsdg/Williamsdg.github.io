FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the process work?

We handle everything, from design and installation to maintenance and takedown. You choose your style, and we make it shine.

How much does Christmas light installation cost in Birmingham?

Most Birmingham-area homes land between $600 and $2,500, depending on roofline size, tree count, and the design you choose. Every quote is free and built around your specific property.

Do I need to buy my own lights?

Nope! We provide all commercial-grade lighting, extension cords, and timers. Everything is included and custom-fitted to your home or business.

What happens if a bulb goes out?

You're covered by our Stay-Lit Guarantee. We'll replace any bulbs or fix issues fast at no additional cost. Send a service request.

When should I book my installation?

The sooner, the better! Most clients secure their spots between August and October to guarantee scheduling before the season rush.

What areas of Birmingham do you serve?

We install throughout the Birmingham metro, including Homewood, Mountain Brook, Vestavia Hills, Hoover, and Greystone. If you're just outside those areas, give us a call. We often can still help.

Do you offer year-round lighting?

Absolutely. We offer permanent lighting, café lighting, and event lighting for a beautiful glow every season.
