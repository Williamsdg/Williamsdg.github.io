The DNS change, at iPage

You were right and my first set of instructions was wrong — my apologies for sending you round to Wix. The domain is registered at iPage, so iPage is where this is done, exactly as you said. Below is what to set, and the order that keeps their email safe throughout.

THE ONE THING THAT MATTERS

Their email lives on a single DNS row.

The only mail record this domain has is one MX row pointing at mx.accountsupport.com. Right now it is held in the Wix zone. The moment the nameservers move away from Wix, that row stops being used — so it has to exist at iPage before you switch, or their email stops arriving.

That is the whole reason for the order below: records first, nameservers last.

What you are replacing the nameservers with

Not another company's nameservers — iPage's own. Keep the domain's DNS at iPage, where you already manage it and where their mail provider sits, and simply point the website records at our server. Your reseller panel will have the option worded something like use our nameservers or default nameservers; whichever hostnames it fills in are the right ones, so there is nothing for you to type.

I would rather you kept control of the DNS in the account you know than hand it to a nameserver of mine. If you would prefer the opposite, say so and I will set up a zone on our side and send you two hostnames instead.

Step 1 — set these three records at iPage

In the iPage DNS editor for cornerstoneadj.com. If the editor is read-only while the nameservers still point at Wix, do Step 2 first and then come straight back here — in that case please set the MX row before the other two.

RECORD 1 OF 3 — TYPE A

Host @ (your panel may show this as blank, or as the domain itself). One value only:

75.2.60.5

If a value is already there, replace it. There should be exactly one.

RECORD 2 OF 3 — TYPE CNAME

Host www, pointing at:

cornerstone-claims.netlify.app

If there is already an A record on www, remove it — a host cannot hold both.

RECORD 3 OF 3 — TYPE MX · THIS IS THEIR EMAIL

Host @, priority 10, pointing at:

mx.accountsupport.com

iPage may well have this row already, since it is their own mail service — if it is there and reads exactly as above, leave it alone. Do not switch the nameservers until this row exists. It is the one record that carries their email, and the domain has no others — no SPF, no DKIM, nothing else to carry across.

Step 2 — then move the nameservers

Once those records are saved, change the domain's nameservers off the two Wix ones (ns14.wixdns.net and ns15.wixdns.net) to iPage's own, using whatever the panel offers. That switch is what makes it all take effect.

What happens next

It usually takes effect within an hour, occasionally up to 48. Nothing further for you to do. I will watch for it, check their email is still delivering, and issue the security certificate — that part is automatic once the domain resolves. I will confirm to you and Kerissa when the new site is live.

If the panel doesn't offer one of these, or warns you, or anything simply doesn't match what is written here — please stop and send me a screenshot rather than guessing. You know these panels far better than I know iPage's, and I would rather look than have you working blind.
