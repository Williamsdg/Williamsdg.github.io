The two settings to change

This points the Cornerstone web address at the new website. It is two settings, and it should take about five minutes. Nothing here affects their email.

FIRST, THE IMPORTANT BIT

There is one row you must not touch.

On the same screen you will see a row of type MX, pointing at mx.accountsupport.com. That row is their email. Leave it exactly as it is. Nothing we are doing today goes near it, and as long as that row is untouched, their email cannot be affected.

Before you start

Take a screenshot of the screen once you reach it, so you have your own before-and-after record. The original values are also written down at the bottom of this page, so if anything ever needs putting back, it can be put back exactly.

Where to go

The domain was bought from iPage, but the settings we need are held at Wix, because that is where the current website is hosted. So it is the Wix login you will need.

  1. Sign in at Wix with the account that holds the Cornerstone domain.
  2. Click your account icon, top right, then Domains.
  3. Find the Cornerstone domain in the list and click Manage next to it.
  4. Open Manage DNS Records. Depending on the screen, it may sit under an Advanced heading.

Change 1 — the A record

SECTION HEADED “A RECORDS”

There will be one row with the host shown as @ — it may appear as a blank, or as the domain name itself. It currently holds three values, all starting 185.230.63.

Replace all three with this single value:

75.2.60.5

When you are done that row has one value, not three — delete the other two.

Change 2 — the CNAME record for www

SECTION HEADED “CNAME RECORDS”

Find the row where the host is www. It currently points to cdn3.wixdns.net.

Change that value to:

cornerstone-claims.netlify.app

Then save. That is everything — please don’t change anything else on the page.

What happens next

The change usually takes effect within an hour, though it can take up to 48. There is nothing else for you to do. Dylan will be watching for it and will confirm to you and Kerissa as soon as the new site is live and the security certificate has issued — that part is automatic.

If anything looks different

If the screen doesn’t match what is described here, or Wix shows a warning, or you simply can’t find one of these — please stop and send a screenshot rather than guessing. Far better to take a look than to have you working blind.
