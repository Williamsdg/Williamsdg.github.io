Hey friend!

Here’s everything you might need to know about ordering from us, and all the ways to get in touch.

Shipping

All U.S. orders ship via UPS (unless unavailable for your address) at a flat rate of $8, with a 1 to 5 business day transit time. Orders over $125 ship free. We do our best to get orders out within 24 to 48 hours of placement, Monday through Friday, so you can expect your package quickly.

Local pickup

Local to Birmingham? Skip the shipping altogether. Select local pickup at checkout, it’s free, and your order will be ready within about two hours. Choose either of our two store locations at checkout.

Returns

We know things don’t always go as planned, so we make returns as easy as we can. We offer store credit only, within 14 days of the date of delivery. You can find that date with your tracking number. Please include a return form with your order.

If you live local to Birmingham, you can drop your return off at either of our store locations, or in our 24/7 return bin at our warehouse in Hoover.

Cancellations

Once an order has been placed, we can’t cancel it and refund back to your credit card or PayPal. The only option is store credit, and that includes orders placed more than once by accident. Please check your order before you hit submit.

Gift cards

Gift cards are available online and in either store, and they never go out of style.

Contact us

For online orders, text us at (205) 540-8088 or email online@thepinktulipclothing.com. You can also message us on Facebook or Instagram, but a text or an email is your best bet.

Homewood store: (205) 637-0710. Hoover store: (205) 637-0031.
