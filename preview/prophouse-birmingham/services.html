What is a prop?

Prop (n), derived from the word “property”, is something used to create or enhance a desired effect.

Props of all kinds are commonly used in photo shoots, movie sets, special events, corporate gatherings, weddings, and fundraising galas.

Make it happen!

Hothouse: overall design. PropHouse: butterfly, banquette and stanchions. InkHouse: floor wrap, custom signage and custom mirrored acrylic for the butterfly.

FAQs

Still wondering? We’d love to help.

Ask us anything
What deposit will I need to reserve?

A 50% non-refundable deposit will be required to reserve your props.

What happens if I need to cancel my prop pull?

If you cancel 31 days or more prior to the event, you will receive funds paid minus your initial 50% non-refundable deposit/retainer. If you cancel within 30 days of your event, you forfeit your deposit/retainer and will be charged an additional 25% re-processing fee. We understand that things happen—plans change! Therefore, we are here to help you in any way we can!

Can I rent props hourly?

Unfortunately, we do not offer hourly rentals.

I can't find what I'm looking for…

We are always looking for new and fun props. Can’t find it here? Let us know! We may consider adding the item to our inventory!

How far will you deliver?

If our trucks can drive to your destination, then we will likely be able to deliver! However, we do have a hard goods order minimum for delivery services depending on the proximity of your venue to our showroom. For additional information on delivery minimums, please submit a website inquiry.

Well… I broke it… now what?

Accidents do happen. Items returned broken or damaged will be assessed and billed to the agreed renter for the disclosed replacement/repair cost.

Do props have to be delivered, or can I just pick them up?

Depending on your rental order and mode of transportation, you can pick up your order at our warehouse by selecting “Will Call” rather than “Delivery” at checkout. We would love to see your smiling face!

What if I need props for a full week or two?

No worries! We offer a great long-term rental program for extended stays.

I have no clue how to style these props together, can you help me?

Of course! PropHouse offers event, wedding, and party styling services as well! Just make sure you communicate your styling needs with us in advance. Fees may apply.
