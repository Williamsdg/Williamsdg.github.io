A Foundation Built on Experience

Eleven seasons in the league. Every lesson at your service.

Most agents pitch a track record. C.J. Mosley lived it. Five Pro Bowls. A first-round selection. Career-defining contracts negotiated, endorsements signed, charities founded, retirements planned. The wins, the holdouts, the surgeries, the trades — he's been on the player's side of every conversation our clients will ever have.

Alongside co-founder Brandon Wassel, that perspective shapes everything Legacy Trust does. We don't sell representation as a transaction. We build long careers on top of trust earned in locker rooms.

We started Legacy Trust because we believe athletes deserve agents who've actually been in the arena — not just around it. — C.J. Mosley & Brandon Wassel, Co-Founders
