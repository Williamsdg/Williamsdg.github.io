Why Independent

The seat belongs to you, not to a party.

"Alabama's motto is Audemus jura nostra defendere: we dare defend our rights. Nothing in there about waiting for a caucus to tell you how."

Most votes in Montgomery are decided before anyone walks onto the floor. An independent doesn't owe a leadership team, a national committee, or a donor list. Every vote starts with one question: is this good for the people back home?

  • No PAC or lobbyist moneyThis campaign is funded by individual people. That's the whole list.
  • A town hall every monthIn person, open mic, no screened questions. In session and out.
  • Every vote explained in writingPosted within 48 hours, in plain English, where anyone can read it.

Priorities

Kitchen-table work, not cable-news fights.

Six things I'll work on from day one. None of them need a party's permission.

01

Cost of living

Finish the job on the grocery tax and stop treating working families as the state's piggy bank.

02

Rural health care

Keep hospitals and ERs open. Nobody should drive an hour to deliver a baby or survive a stroke.

03

Schools & skills

Pay teachers like we mean it, and build a straight road from high school to a good trade or degree.

04

Roads & broadband

Fix the county roads people actually drive, and get real internet to the last mile, not just the map.

05

Clean government

Stronger ethics laws, open records that actually open, and no more deals written behind a closed door.

06

Farms & veterans

Back the family farms that feed this state and the veterans who served it. Both have waited long enough.
