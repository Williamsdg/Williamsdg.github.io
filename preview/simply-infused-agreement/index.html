1.How the Storefront Works

This project is a headless storefront. It is not a Shopify theme. Williams Digital will build a new, custom website for customers to browse and shop, and connect it to Simply Infused's existing Shopify store.

Shopify remains the back office. Products, prices, sizes, photos, descriptions, inventory, orders, customers and checkout all stay in Simply Infused's Shopify account, managed the same way they are today. When a customer checks out, the cart is handed to Simply Infused's own Shopify checkout.

The storefront is organized through Shopify collections and tags. The shelf a bottle sits on, the flavor filters it appears under, its Best Seller badge and the bottles it pairs with are all read from the collections and tags assigned to that product in Shopify. Adding a product, changing a price or moving a bottle to a different shelf is done in Shopify, and the storefront updates to match.

Williams Digital will set up the collections and tags for every current product, provide a short written guide, and walk Simply Infused through how they work. No experience with collections or tags is needed beforehand.

Simply Infused's current Shopify theme is not edited or removed by this project.

4.Making Changes After Launch

Product changes are made by Simply Infused in Shopify: adding or removing products, prices, sizes, photos, descriptions, inventory, collections and tags. These appear on the storefront without involving Williams Digital.

Changes to fixed content are made by Williams Digital on request: tasting room hours, the delivery schedule, the list of shops, and homepage photos and wording. Reasonable updates of this kind are included in the Monthly Website Service, and at no charge during the 30 days after launch before that service begins.

5.Not Included

  • A Shopify theme. The storefront is a separate website connected to Shopify, as described in How the Storefront Works.
  • Changes to the Shopify checkout, shipping rates, delivery settings, taxes or payment processing. These remain as Simply Infused has them set today.
  • Shopify plan fees, Shopify apps and payment processing fees. Simply Infused continues to pay Shopify directly.
  • Photography, copywriting or logo design. The storefront uses Simply Infused's existing logo and wording, and the photos Simply Infused supplies.
  • Writing or rewriting product descriptions. Product information is shown as it is entered in Shopify.
  • Customer accounts, subscriptions, loyalty programs or product reviews.
  • Email newsletters, social media management, paid advertising or an ongoing SEO retainer.
  • Domain registration or business email.

6.Hosting & Third-Party Services

Simply Infused keeps its domain name and its Shopify account. Simply Infused remains responsible for its Shopify plan, which the storefront depends on for products and checkout.

The storefront is hosted separately from Shopify. Williams Digital hosts it, and hosting is covered by the Monthly Website Service with no separate hosting charge. The Shopify plan is a separate cost paid by Simply Infused to Shopify and is not part of the monthly fee.

At launch the domain simply-infused.com points to the storefront, and Shopify checkout continues on a Shopify address such as shop.simply-infused.com. Providers, including Shopify, may change their pricing or features. That is outside Williams Digital's control.

10.Customer Data

Orders, payments and customer records are collected and held by Shopify in Simply Infused's account. The storefront does not store payment details.

The Private Tasting Social form and the newsletter signup collect names and contact details and deliver them to Simply Infused. Simply Infused owns and controls all data collected through the storefront. Williams Digital provides and maintains the mechanism only, and will not use, sell, share, or train any system on that data.

Williams Digital will delete any stored form records within ten (10) business days of a written request.

11.Ownership & Rights

On receipt of final payment, Simply Infused owns the finished storefront: all page files, styling, the features listed in the Scope of Work and any custom graphics produced for this project.

Simply Infused owns and retains its domain name, its Shopify account, its logo, its photos, its product information and all content it publishes. Williams Digital claims no interest in any of them.

Williams Digital retains ownership of any underlying reusable code, libraries or components not created specifically for Simply Infused, and retains the right to display the finished work in its portfolio and marketing. Customer data is never shown.

12.Revisions & Support

The fee includes two rounds of revisions during the build phase, including photo and color changes. A round is a consolidated set of feedback delivered at one time.

Structural changes requested after approval (additional pages, new functionality, or reworking an approved section's structure) are out of scope and billed under the Fee & Payment Terms section.

Williams Digital will correct any defect in delivered work at no charge for ninety (90) days following launch, whether or not the Monthly Website Service is active.

If Williams Digital cancels, Simply Infused receives all work completed to date and a pro-rata refund of any amount paid for work not performed.
