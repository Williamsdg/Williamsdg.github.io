Camp & cabins
Can I buy tickets at the gate?

No. Tickets are sold online only, for a specific night and a one-hour arrival window.

How long can I stay?

Your ticket sets the hour you arrive. Arrival windows start every hour from 4 to 8 PM, and the park closes at 10 PM.

Can I get a refund or change my night?

Tickets are non-refundable. You can change your date or time if space allows.

Do kids need tickets?

Children 3 and under are free and don't need a ticket. Everyone 4 and up needs general admission.

What's included?

Park admission and one standard train ride. An Express Train Pass is $15 and covers up to 2 guests.

Which nights are closed?

Nov 16–19, plus Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. Every other night from Nov 13 to Jan 2 is open.

When is Santa there?

Santa and the Mean One appear on select dates. The schedule is posted on the ticket page.

Where do I park?

Enter at 1500 Noccalula Road, Gadsden, AL 35904. That's the park side of Black Creek, not the campground address.
