Our story

Started in the trunk
of a car.

The original Toodle Boys
  1. Three boys who love the game

    ToodleBoys Sports was inspired by our 3 sons: Shadrick Jr., Shadarius and Shamar Toodle. They've played basketball, baseball, football or track since they were small kids.

  2. Mom and dad in the stands

    We decided to represent our sons with custom shirts and gear at every game. That sparked the idea: make custom game day gear for other parents, too.

  3. 2015: park to park

    We started selling team gear out of the trunk of my car. I went from park to park making sure parents had the gear to represent their kids.

  4. Now: just about every school

    Several years later we're in just about every school in Mobile County, Baldwin County and beyond. Come see us in person or online. You won't be disappointed.

Shadrick Toodle, Sr.Owner & CEO

The fine print

Payments

We accept major credit cards including Mastercard, Discover and Visa. You may also pay in cash at our store at the Mobile Flea Market.

Shipping

USPS is our designated carrier for shipments under 100 lbs. Shipping is calculated by product weight and method. A street address is required; we can't ship to a P.O. Box.

Returns & exchanges

A 20% restocking fee applies on returns due to customer error. Defective merchandise will be exchanged or refunded if returned within 30 days of shipping. Report shortages or defects within 72 hours of receipt.
