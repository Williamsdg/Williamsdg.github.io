Rep for your kids — the idea that started it all

Our story

It started with
three boys.

ToodleBoys Sports was inspired by our 3 sons: Shadrick Jr., Shadarius, and Shamar Toodle. They fell in love with sports at young ages — basketball, baseball, football and track since they were small kids. We decided to represent our sons with custom shirts and gear at every game. That's where the idea was sparked: make custom game day gear available for other parents so they can represent their kids in quality custom gear.

In 2015, we started selling team gear out of the trunk of my car. I went from park to park making sure parents had the necessary gear to represent their kids. Several years later, we're in just about every school in Mobile County, Baldwin County and beyond.

— Shadrick Toodle, Sr. · Owner & CEO
