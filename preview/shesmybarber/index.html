About the owner

She's my barber
is Erica Jackson.

Erica started young, styling her dolls and then her family and friends. She went to cosmetology school in high school and barbering school as a young adult, and by 28 she had built a clientele that keeps growing.

Today SHESMYBARBER provides exceptional haircuts, straight razor shaves, loc maintenance, and beard and mustache trims for a wide range of clients.

Before you book

Things to know.

A few house rules so your appointment goes the way it should.

01

Come alone

That includes children, unless they are 8 and under or you have made a special request.

02

Keep your day clear

Appointment times are approximate and you may experience a wait.

03

Arrive product-free

Come with your hair free of any oils or leave-in conditioner.

04

Running late? Call.

Call if you are more than 15 minutes late. There is a $20 late fee, and the appointment may be cancelled or rescheduled if it conflicts with another.

05

Booking fee

Your booking fee is non-refundable, even if you cancel. If Erica has to cancel, it is refunded in full.

06

Sundays & holidays

All Sunday and holiday appointments are an additional $20.

Questions before you book? Call (833) 274-2209.
