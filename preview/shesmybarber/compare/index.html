// SHESMYBARBER · current site vs. concept

Same brand. Easier to book.

A side-by-side look at shesmybarber.com as it is today and the concept we built from it. Everything in the concept, from the photos to the prices and policies, comes from the current site. Nothing was invented.

Open the concept site Open the current site

// 01 · first screen on a phone

What a new client sees first

Today the first screen is the logo and a tagline. In the concept it is who Erica is, what she does, and a Book button.

// 02 · home page on a computer

The top of the home page

On a wide screen the current logo is stretched until it is cropped and soft. The concept leads with a photo of the actual work.

// 03 · services and prices

Finding the price

The current menu is one list of 18 services in no particular order, on a separate page. The concept sorts them into four groups on the home page.

// 04 · point by point

What changes, and why it matters

Each line on the left was checked on the live site on September 30, 2026.

Area
Current site
Concept
Booking
Current siteOn a phone, the only Book button on the home page is about five screens down. The other Book link is inside the menu.
ConceptA Book button on the first screen, and a Book bar that stays at the bottom of the phone while you scroll.
Prices
Current siteNo services or prices on the home page. On the booking page, prices are written three different ways: “$75.00”, “50.00”, and “30.”
ConceptAll 18 services on the home page in four groups, each with its time, its price in one format, and its own Book button.
The work
Current siteThe haircut and loc photos are on a separate Gallery page with no captions. The home page shows apparel photos.
ConceptCuts and locs on the home page with captions. Tap to see any photo full size, swipe through them on a phone.
Policies
Current siteOne block of seven bullet points, below a photo that is cropped to a thin strip.
ConceptSix short cards, in Erica's own wording, with the Sunday and holiday fee also shown right under the prices.
Hours & location
Current siteA table of hours at the bottom of the page.
Concept“Open now” or “Opens Thursday at 6:00 am” at the very top, today highlighted in the hours, one tap for directions or a call.
Getting found on Google
Current siteThe page title is “Home | Shesmybarber” and the description is “Professional licensed barber.” Neither mentions Hoover, locs, or haircuts.
ConceptTitle and description name the services and Hoover, AL, and the page carries the address and hours in the format Google reads for local businesses.
Speed
Current siteThe home page loads about 200 separate files before it is finished.
ConceptFewer than 65 files, most of them the Google map, with photos sized for the screen they are shown on.
Accessibility
Current sitePinch-to-zoom is turned off, and 10 of the 28 images have no description for screen readers.
ConceptZoom works, every photo is described, the whole page works from a keyboard, and all text passes the standard contrast check.
Details
Current siteThe header logo is dark on a black background and hard to see. The About section has a few typos.
ConceptThe gold SMB logo in the header and footer, and the About section proofread.

How the numbers were taken: the phone figures come from an iPhone 13 sized screen. “Files” counts every request the home page makes while loading and scrolling to the bottom.

// 05 · what stays exactly the same

Nothing about how Erica runs the shop changes

Square bookingEvery Book button opens the same Square appointments page clients use today. Calendar, deposits, and reminders stay in Square.
Gift cards and Crown VaultThe Square eGift card link and the Amazon Storefront link are the same ones on the current site.
The brandBlack and gold, the SMB logo, Erica's photos, and her own words. Reorganized, not replaced.
