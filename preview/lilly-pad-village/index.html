Real stones, real finds

Every bucket holds something worth keeping.

Our rough is sourced from working mines across the Southeast and South America — no plastic, no painted rocks. We salt every bucket with at least a half-dozen identifiable stones, then add fossils, arrowheads, and the occasional sapphire just to keep things interesting.

Bring your finds to the sorting table and we'll help identify, polish, and (if you'd like) wire-wrap them in the gift shop. Identification is always free.

Quartz & Citrine

Crystal-clear and golden. The most common find — and often the most beautiful once polished.

Amethyst

Deep purple Brazilian rough. The kids' favorite — and what most parents end up taking home.

Ruby & Garnet

Red gemstones from North Carolina mines. Smaller but stunning — great for jewelry settings.

Emerald

The pride of our deluxe bucket. Rough green crystals straight from the Hiddenite belt.
