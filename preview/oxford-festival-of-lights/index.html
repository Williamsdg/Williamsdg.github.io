FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Email festivaloflights@oxfordal.gov

Where can I buy tickets?

Online through our ticket store, or at the gate.

Are tickets refundable?

No. Once purchased, tickets are non-refundable. Festival of Lights runs rain or shine, and refunds aren't issued for weather while the event is open.

Do I need to use my ticket on a specific day?

Yes. We sell weekday (Monday–Thursday) and weekend (Friday–Sunday) tickets. Check the ticket details before you buy so the category matches the night you plan to visit.

What's included in my admission?

Admission covers the drive-through light show for everyone in your vehicle: $20 per car Mon–Thu and $25 Fri–Sun; vans $30 / $35; buses and limos $50 / $55.

Santa's Village is free. Wristbands ($20 first, $10 each additional) are only needed for the major attractions. Food and merchandise are extra.

How many people can come in one car?

A car entry covers up to 8 people, and every passenger needs their own seat. No sitting on laps or riding in floorboards, hatchbacks or other cargo spaces. Vans are 9–15 passengers; buses and limos are more than 15.

What payments are accepted?

At the gate, cash and card. Inside Santa's Village it's card only: any major credit or debit card, Apple Pay or tap to pay. You can swap cash for a Santa Bucks card at the front tent.

How can I find discounts and special offers?

Theme nights are $15 per car. See them on the calendar, and follow us on social media for announcements.

Where is Festival of Lights?

Choccolocco Park, 954 Leon Smith Parkway, Oxford, AL 36203. Take I-20 exit 188.

What are the days and hours?

November 20 – December 31, 2026. Gates open at 5 PM and close at 9 PM Monday–Thursday and 10 PM Friday–Sunday. From December 18 on, gates stay open until 10 PM every night. Closed Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Tap a date on the calendar for that night's hours.

Do I need to turn my headlights off?

Yes, please. Once you pass the ticket booth, turn your headlights off so everyone gets the full show. Staff at the booth can help if you're unsure how.

What should we wear?

It's mostly outdoors, and Alabama weather is unpredictable. Bring layers, comfortable shoes and something for rain. Don't forget a toboggan, gloves and especially your jacket.

Are there age or height limits on attractions?
  • Snow tubers must be 48 inches or taller.
  • Skaters under 6 need a parent or guardian with them, and the guardian has to wear skates too.
  • Inflatables are kids only. Sorry, adults. We're disappointed too.
What skate sizes do you have?

Children's size 6 through adult size 13.

Can I take pictures?

Of course! Tag us on Facebook and Instagram so we can see and share your memories.

Can I bring my pet?

Pets are welcome in vehicles during the drive-through. Only service animals are allowed in Santa's Village. Please don't leave pets unattended in your vehicle.

Can passengers ride in a truck bed or an open trunk?

No. For safety, everyone stays seated inside the vehicle. No truck beds, open trunks, exterior parts of the vehicle or trailers.

Can I get out of my car during the light show?

No. Stay in your vehicle unless you're entering Santa's Village.

Can I bring food or drinks?

Outside food and drinks aren't allowed in Santa's Village. There's plenty to eat and drink inside.

Is smoking allowed?

No smoking or vaping inside the village.

What isn't allowed at the venue?
  • Alcohol
  • Drones
  • Outside food & drinks
  • Laser pointers
  • Weapons or knives (without a permit)
  • Throwing items

This list may change without notice at the venue operators' discretion.

How can I work at Festival of Lights?

We'd love to have you on the team. Fill out the seasonal help application.

Can my business sponsor or be a vendor?

Absolutely. Email festivaloflights@oxfordal.gov and we'll get back to you with the details.
