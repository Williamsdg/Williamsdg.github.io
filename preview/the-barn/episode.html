About This Episode

Auburn is two weeks into fall camp, and the picture is finally starting to clear up. On this episode, the guys break down the biggest position battles — starting with the quarterback room — and hand out their early "who's separating" verdicts.

We also get into the freshmen who've flashed enough to force the issue, why the offensive line might quietly be the most important story of the fall, and where things stand with a four-star defensive back Auburn is pushing hard for. Plus: a look ahead to what the approved Jordan-Hare North project means for the program's future.
