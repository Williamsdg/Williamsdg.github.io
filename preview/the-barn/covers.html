Barn Covers

Feature stories rendered as mini sports-magazine covers — each sport gets its own visual identity, so you can recognize what kind of Auburn content you're looking at before you even read the label. Used sparingly as signature moments, mixed into the cleaner everyday card system.

Every cover keeps the same DNA — THE BARN wordmark, condensed headline, navy/orange/cream, author & category treatment. Only the sport's signature element changes.
The Covers

Football = playbook & yard lines. Basketball = courtside geometry. Baseball = vintage scorecard. Recruiting = scouting dossier.
