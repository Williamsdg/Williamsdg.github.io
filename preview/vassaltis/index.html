Vassaltis means basalt

Tradition, evolved.

In 2010, Yannis Valambous left a career in finance in London to revive the vineyards his father left him. In 2014, Vassaltis was born: a thoroughly modern boutique winery on a plot among those four hectares.

“We do not seek to return to our roots. We seek to grow on them.”
