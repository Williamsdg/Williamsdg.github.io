The collection

Ten wines,
one island.

Vassaltis is dedicated to Santorini’s native grapes: Assyrtiko above all, but also Aidani, Athiri, Mandilaria and Mavrotragano. Each wine is held in the cellar until it’s ready to drink on release.

Current vintages and rare vinifications are poured in the tasting room. Some, like the Aidani, are only offered there. The club site handles member releases; its shop is currently closed.
