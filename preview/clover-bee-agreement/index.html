1. Scope of Work

Williams Digital will design, build and launch a custom storefront website for Clover + Bee that connects to and displays the Client's existing Shopify store. Shopify remains the Client's system of record for products, inventory, orders, customers, discounts and checkout. The work comprises:

  • Complete Shopify catalogue connection

    The new site connects directly to the Client's Shopify store and displays all published products — titles, descriptions, variants, sizes, photography, prices and live inventory — with no re-entry of product data by the Client. Products the Client adds, edits, sells out or removes in Shopify appear on the new site automatically. This is a live connection, not a one-time import, so the catalogue cannot fall out of date.

  • Custom desktop and mobile storefront

    Homepage, collection pages, product pages, quick-shop, shopping bag and site-wide search, designed specifically for Clover + Bee. The mobile experience is designed independently rather than adapted down from desktop.

  • Merchandising features

    Shop by Occasion, Shop My Season (driven by the Client's existing colour-season product tags), Complete the Look outfit building, editorial collection layouts and curated homepage sections, as demonstrated in the concept referenced above.

  • Six seasonal and holiday themes

    Six coordinated seasonal themes built at launch, each consisting of campaign imagery placement, an accent colour treatment and campaign headline text, switchable from the admin page described below. The six themes will be agreed in writing during the build; the anticipated set is Fall, Holiday, Winter, Spring, Summer and one flexible slot for a dated occasion such as Mother's Day or Graduation. Additional or replacement themes beyond these six are quoted separately.

  • Two game day themes

    Auburn and Alabama campaign treatments, each with its own colour treatment and product filtering, driven by the Client's existing product tags and switchable from the admin page. Additional schools are quoted separately.

  • Admin page

    A password-protected admin page, separate from Shopify, allowing approved Clover + Bee staff to make the following changes without contacting Williams Digital:

    • Replace the featured image in each defined image slot across the homepage and campaign pages — eighteen slots at launch, including the homepage hero, occasion tiles, season cards, game day tiles and editorial features
    • Edit the headline, eyebrow and button text for each of those sections
    • Edit the announcement bar text and promotional code
    • Switch the active seasonal theme and turn game day treatments on or off
    • Update store hours, address and phone number

    Changes made in the admin page appear on the live site without a developer. Product data continues to be managed in Shopify as it is today.

  • Launch, redirects and search foundations

    Permanent 301 redirects from existing product and collection URLs to their matching new pages so existing search rankings carry over, plus per-page titles and descriptions, structured data, XML sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs and analytics configuration.

  • Performance and accessibility

    Built to WCAG 2.2 Level AA and targeting Core Web Vitals in the "good" range on mobile, with photography optimised and sized per device.

2. Content, Photography & Client Approval

The concept and the finished site are built on the Client's own product photography and catalogue content, drawn from the Client's Shopify store. The Client confirms that it holds the rights to all product photography, campaign photography, logos and copy supplied or published, and that these may be used on the new site.

Williams Digital will not originate factual claims about the business, its history, its staff or its products. Any campaign or lifestyle photography beyond the Client's existing product photography is supplied by the Client. All launch content is subject to the Client's written approval before the site goes live.

Client materials will not be used to train artificial-intelligence models, and no AI-generated text will be published on the site without the Client's review and approval.

3. Not Included

Important — please read before signing

Because the new storefront is custom-built rather than a Shopify theme, Shopify apps that install themselves into a theme will not appear on the new site. This commonly affects product review apps, loyalty and rewards apps, pop-up and email-capture apps, upsell widgets and some subscription apps. Before work begins, the Client will provide a list of installed Shopify apps and the parties will agree in writing which must be carried across. Each app that must be replaced or reconnected is quoted separately and is not included in the fee below.

  • Photography and content production. Campaign, lifestyle and storefront photography, styling, models and copywriting beyond the launch content described in Section 1.
  • Shopify's own costs. The Client's Shopify subscription, transaction and payment-processing fees remain the Client's and are unchanged by this agreement.
  • Checkout customisation. Checkout continues to run on Shopify and keeps its current appearance and behaviour. Shopify restricts checkout customisation to its Plus plan.
  • Third-party app subscriptions and any app replacement work, as described above.
  • Email and SMS marketing, paid advertising and review solicitation.
  • Domain registration. The Client owns and retains shopcloverandbee.com. Williams Digital will configure DNS at launch; registration and renewal remain the Client's.
  • New page types, layout changes or design changes requested after launch, beyond the edits available in the admin page described in Section 1.
  • Third-party costs — premium fonts, licensed stock imagery or paid services — billed at cost and only with prior written approval.
