A small newsroom, made for one city.

Hoover Sun is independent, locally focused, and built around the idea that a neighborhood deserves the kind of journalism it can’t get from anyone else.

We started in 2010 with one reporter, a borrowed laptop, and a list of every PTA email address in the district. Fifteen years later, we’re still here. Still local. Still independent.

Hoover Sun covers what a city actually lives through — the city council vote, the kindergarten graduation, the Friday-night lights, the new restaurant opening on a corner that used to be a tax office. We’re not a wire service. We don’t aggregate. Every story you read here was written by a person who lives in this zip code or one next to it.

That mission is supported entirely by local advertising, sponsored content, and the readers who choose to subscribe to our free email newsletter. There’s no paywall. There won’t be.
