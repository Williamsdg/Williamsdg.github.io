Spain Park clinches area title with walk-off blast

Junior catcher Cole Yarbrough sent the first pitch he saw in the seventh over the right-center wall, sealing the Jaguars’ first area crown since 2019 and a top-eight playoff seed.

The Spain Park Jaguars trailed for six innings Tuesday night. They led for fewer than four minutes. It was enough.

Junior catcher Cole Yarbrough ambushed a 1-0 fastball in the bottom of the seventh, sending a no-doubt drive over the wall in right-center to give Spain Park a 6-5 walk-off win over Thompson and the program’s first Area 5 title since 2019. The Jaguars finish the regular season 24-6 and secure a top-eight seed for the Class 7A state playoffs.

How it happened

Thompson struck first, scoring twice in the top of the first off Spain Park starter Bo Whitlock on a pair of doubles and a sacrifice fly. The Jaguars answered with two in the bottom of the second on RBI singles from Whitlock himself and shortstop Tate Maddox.

The score traded blows through the middle innings. Thompson manufactured runs in the third and fifth on small ball — bunts, hit-and-runs, productive outs — while Spain Park leaned on extra-base hits and a tight defense that turned two double plays.

The seventh inning

Spain Park entered the bottom of the seventh down 5-4. Leadoff hitter Sam Pruitt drew a five-pitch walk against Thompson closer Drew Latham. Pruitt advanced to second on a fielder’s choice, putting the tying run in scoring position with one out.

Latham induced a fly out for the second out, bringing up Yarbrough. The first pitch was a fastball away that Yarbrough took for ball one. The second was the same pitch — this time a hair more inside — and Yarbrough didn’t miss it.

What’s next

The Jaguars open the Class 7A playoffs Friday at Cooper Stadium against the seventh seed from Area 7, with the matchup to be determined Wednesday. Head coach Will Smith said the program’s focus this week will be on rest and short bullpen work.

“We’ve been the team that runs out of innings late in playoff series the last two years,” Smith said. “That changes now.”
