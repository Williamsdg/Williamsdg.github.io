Know Before You Go

The essentials

🎉

Where to enter

Main gates are at 100 Ben Chapman Drive. Reserved-seat ticket holders may use the south VIP gate. Free overflow lawn entry on family nights at the east lot.

🌮

Weather policy

Shows go on rain or shine. The series is canceled only in the case of severe weather warnings. No refunds for partial shows, but the city offers free transfers to the next concert if a show is canceled mid-run.

🍲

Food & drink

Six rotating Hoover food trucks on-site each Friday. Outside food permitted on the lawn; no glass containers. Beer & wine sold inside the gates for 21+ with ID.
