The Mayor’s Minute

A summer of progress — and a few good neighbors along the way

This is the season Hoover comes alive. The pools open, the Met fills up on weekends, and our parks are full of kids on bikes. It’s also when our city does some of its most important work — from paving projects on the south end to the start of the Patton Chapel safety corridor we’ve been planning for two years.

None of it happens without you. The volunteers, the small business owners, the teachers wrapping up another remarkable school year. Thank you for making Hoover the kind of place people want to come home to.

— Mayor Brocato
