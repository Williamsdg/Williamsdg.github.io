Our Story

Founded in 2010.

PlumLine has hung and finished drywall for general contractors and homeowners across Jefferson and Shelby County since 2010. We work on new construction, additions, basement finishes, and the painful punch-list repairs after a flood or a fire.

Our crews hang square-off-the-truck sheets — square edges in, factory edges to the corners — and our finishers run level-three on most jobs and level-five where it'll show. We don't cut corners on the corner bead.

Texture matching, popcorn removal, tape-and-bed repairs after plumbing or electrical, full-room re-skims when previous work just won't sand out. If the wall is bad enough that the painter is complaining, we're the call.
