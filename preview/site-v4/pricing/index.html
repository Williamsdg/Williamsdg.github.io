Pricing FAQ

The fine print, in plain English.

Why do you show prices?

Because you should know roughly what something costs before you spend time on a call. Your quote will be fixed and in writing.

Is a care plan required?

No. Care plans are optional. You own the site, and you can host and maintain it yourself or with someone else.

What is included in a care plan?

Hosting, SSL, backups, uptime monitoring, security updates, and a set amount of changes each month: up to 1 hour on Site care ($50), 2 hours on Site + dashboard care ($100), and 5 hours on Platform care ($250). Anything beyond that is quoted before the work starts.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes — typically half to start and half at launch. Larger projects can be split into milestones.

Are there hidden fees?

No. Third-party costs like your domain name or Stripe processing fees are paid directly to those providers, and we tell you about them up front.

Can I start small and grow?

Yes, and we recommend it. Many clients start with a website and add a dashboard, portal, or store later — it is built to connect.

How is marketing billed?

Marketing materials are billed once, per piece. Monthly plans are billed at the start of each month, with no long contract, and can be cancelled with 30 days notice. Ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta.

Do I need to buy a website to get marketing?

No. Marketing plans and materials are available on their own.
