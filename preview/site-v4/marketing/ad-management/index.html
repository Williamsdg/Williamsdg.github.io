FAQ

Ads & ad creative: common questions

How much does ad management cost?

Ad management is a flat $450 a month for one platform. Your ad budget is separate and is paid directly to Google or Meta.

How much should I spend on ads?

We suggest at least $500 a month in ad spend so a campaign gets enough clicks to judge. Below that, local SEO is usually a better use of the money.

Do you take a percentage of my ad spend?

No. The fee is flat, whatever you spend.

Can you guarantee leads?

No. We can guarantee that tracking is set up correctly and that you see exactly what your budget produced.
