Marketing FAQ

Common questions

What marketing services do you offer?

Marketing materials priced by the piece (logos, brand kits, print, social kits, and ad creative) and monthly plans for local SEO, social media and email, and ad management.

How much do your marketing plans cost?

Local visibility is $350 a month, Social + email is $650 a month, and Full marketing is $1,200 a month. Ad management on its own is $450 a month. Ad spend is separate and paid directly to Google or Meta.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Plans are month to month and you can cancel with 30 days notice.

Do I need a Williams Digital website first?

No, but it helps. Marketing sends people to your website, so if the site is slow or confusing we will say so before you spend money on traffic.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We do not guarantee rankings, followers, or leads, and we do not buy fake ones. You get the work, on schedule, and an honest report every month.
