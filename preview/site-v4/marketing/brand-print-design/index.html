FAQ

Logo, brand & print: common questions

How much does a logo cost?

A logo and brand kit starts at $750. It includes the logo in several layouts, a color palette, fonts, and a one-page guide.

Is printing included?

No. Prices cover design and print-ready files. You can use any printer, and we are glad to send the files to them directly.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds of revisions are included on every piece. Most projects are settled in one.

Do I own the logo?

Yes. Once the invoice is paid, the logo and its source files are yours.
