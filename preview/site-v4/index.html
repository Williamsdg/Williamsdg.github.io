Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else? Call (205) 202-9193 or email Dylan@williamsdigital.io.

Where is Williams Digital located?

We are based in Birmingham, Alabama, and work with businesses across Alabama and beyond. Most projects run remotely, and we are glad to meet in person around Birmingham and Auburn.

How much does a website cost?

Business websites start at $2,500, website-plus-dashboard projects at $5,000, and custom platforms at $10,000. Every project gets a fixed, written quote. Optional care plans with hosting, monitoring, and monthly updates run $50–$250 a month.

Do you offer marketing?

Yes. Alongside web development we design marketing materials (logos, print pieces, social kits, and ad creative, with design pieces from $150) and run monthly marketing plans for local SEO, social media, email, and ads, starting at $350 a month.

How long does a project take?

Most websites are live in two to six weeks. Dashboards and platforms usually take four to ten weeks, released in stages so you are using it early.

Do I own my website?

Yes. You own your domain, your content, and your data. On custom builds, you own the code.

Do you help with SEO?

Every site includes technical SEO — titles, descriptions, structured data, sitemaps, fast load times, and mobile readiness — plus help setting up your Google Business Profile. Ongoing local SEO is available as a monthly plan from $350.

What happens after launch?

Care plans cover hosting, security, backups, monitoring, and a set amount of monthly changes, so your site keeps improving instead of going stale.
