The Opportunity

Four things holding cityofopp.com back

01

The homepage is cluttered

Dozens of links, menus, calendars, and notices compete for attention with no hierarchy. Residents can't find the one thing they came for — usually paying a bill or reporting an issue.

02

The brand is invisible

The Rattlesnake Rodeo, Frank Jackson State Park, the Farmers Market, historic downtown — genuinely marketable assets that never appear in a compelling way. Visitors don't get excited about Opp.

03

Economic development is buried

A business owner considering Opp should see workforce data, available properties, incentives, and a contact within seconds. Today those resources are lost in navigation — and this is exactly the area where cities receive grants and funding.

04

The visuals feel dated

Small images, inconsistent spacing, old-style buttons, generic layouts. Perception matters — to residents, to visitors, and to businesses comparing Opp against Opelika or Enterprise.

More Than a Facelift

What a modern municipal site actually delivers

The visible redesign is the easy part. These are the points that matter to stakeholders, auditors, and grant applications:

ADA Accessible

Built to WCAG 2.1 AA — screen-reader friendly, keyboard navigable, proper contrast. Government sites are held to this standard, and non-compliance is a legal risk.

Mobile First

Most residents reach city services from a phone. Every page is designed for a phone screen first, desktop second.

Fast Loading

Lightweight pages that load in under two seconds — including on rural cell connections around Covington County.

Staff-Editable

City staff post news, alerts, and events themselves — no developer, no waiting. Emergency notices go up in minutes.
