Orders & Shipping

Free shipping on all orders $100+. We ship every order via UPS Ground.

Our products fall into two categories: In Stock and Made to Order. Whether you’re shopping for game day or planning ahead, here’s what to expect when placing your order!

In stock

Ready to ship in 5–7 business days

These items are already made and waiting in our warehouse, ready to head your way! You can find them in the In Stock section of your school’s page. Perfect for game week, last-minute gifts, or when you need something soon.

Made to order

Made just for you in 10–14 business days

Our tees, fleece, and Client Collections are made just for you after your order is placed. Each piece is printed or embroidered in our shop in Auburn, Alabama. Perfect for planning ahead, ordering for your group, or getting something made especially for you.

A little planning goes a long way! Be sure to check the shipping timeline for each item so your order arrives in time for game day.

Returns & Exchanges

Store credit or exchange, requested within 14 days of delivery. Holiday purchases through January 30.

We’d be happy to accept your return for store credit or exchange! Here’s how to start your return:

  1. Go to our Returns page (opens in a new tab) (also linked at the bottom of every page).
  2. Log in with your email and order number.
  3. Select your order and click Start a Return/Exchange.
  4. Choose the items you’re returning, add a note, and submit.
  5. Pick your return option: store credit or exchange, if available.

Standard return policy: Returns for eligible items must be requested within 14 days of delivery. Returns must be received back by the S&G team within 14 days of your return request.

Please note that any items used or with stains will not be accepted. You can track the progress of your return at any time through your account portal.

For complete details, please review our Return Policy (opens in a new tab).

Holiday return extension: For eligible holiday purchases made between November 1 and December 31, we’re happy to extend the return window! Holiday returns may be requested and sent back to our team through January 30.

If you selected Hassle Free Returns with Checkout+ at checkout, you are eligible for free returns and exchanges along with package protection.

If your order did not include Hassle Free Returns, once your return is approved, you’ll receive a prepaid label via email. The label cost will be deducted from your store credit (example: $50 return − $10 label = $40 credit).

Live nearby? Bring your item and your order number to our Auburn store to return it for store credit or exchange it in person, without the hassle of shipping it back.

Products & Sizing

Women’s XS–2XL and girls’ sizes from 12M.

We’re here to help you find your perfect fit! Each product page includes a detailed size guide in the product description to help you choose the size that’s right for you.

Need a little extra guidance? Check out our size guides (opens in a new tab).

Print-on-demand means your item is made just for you after you place your order. We don’t keep pre-printed or pre-embroidered products in stock.

Once your order is placed, we order the blank product from our distributor. When it arrives at our shop, we print or embroider your item, package it, and ship it directly to you.

This process allows us to offer a wider variety of sizes, colors, and styles without keeping large amounts of inventory on hand. The trade-off is a slightly longer production time than items that are already in stock.

Please allow 10–14 business days for your print-on-demand order to be produced and packaged.

Officially licensed products are approved by the appropriate university or licensing organization and meet the requirements of their licensing agreements.

With each purchase, a portion of the sale goes toward required licensing royalties, which help support university programs, including student scholarships.

We take our licensing responsibilities seriously and have a dedicated team to ensure our products meet licensing requirements and that all applicable royalties are paid.

Shop with confidence. Make an impact. When you purchase officially licensed merchandise, you’re helping support the universities and brands you love. Before purchasing collegiate products, look for official licensing language or a hologram sticker when applicable to help verify authenticity.
