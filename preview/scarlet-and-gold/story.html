Our story · 2013 to today

From a guest bedroom to Shug Jordan Parkway.

Scarlet & Gold started with one woman, one spare room and a love for the way a team brings people together. A lot has changed since then. The care we put into every order hasn’t.

  1. 2013

    Where it began

    A creative outlet in a spare room

    While working in pharmaceutical sales, Megan started Scarlet & Gold in her guest bedroom in Auburn. She packed every order herself and tucked a handwritten thank-you note into every box.

  2. The early years

    Finding our way

    From wall art to game day apparel

    We started with wall art, not clothes. Apparel came next, because Megan still couldn’t find game day pieces she loved wearing, for herself or the women around her.

  3. Going official

    Licensed & growing

    Two schools, then many more

    We became officially licensed, starting with two schools. Every licensed piece sends a portion back to the university to support student scholarships.

  4. For our minis

    The soft one

    A cheer dress she’ll keep on

    Most cheer dresses are stiff polyester, and by the second quarter she’s begging to change. So we made ours from a soft, stretchy, non-itchy fabric she can jump and cheer in all game long. It became our signature fabric.

  5. Opening our doors

    Auburn, Alabama

    A shop of our own

    We opened our retail shop on Shug Jordan Parkway, a place to try things on, meet our team and find gifts for every age and occasion.

  6. Today

    Still in Auburn

    An in-house team that designs every piece

    Our in-house team in Auburn designs every piece, and most orders ship from right here. You’ll also find us in boutiques and campus stores across the South.

Meet our founder

Hi, I’m Megan.

I grew up in Texas playing on all-boys teams and fighting for my spot, and I always wanted something that was both athletic and feminine.

Cheered at Auburn

Then I cheered at Auburn, and I learned how a team brings people together.

In 2013, while working in pharmaceutical sales, I started Scarlet & Gold in my guest bedroom as a creative outlet. I packed every order myself and wrote a thank-you note for every box. Years later, I still couldn’t find anything for my school I wanted to wear, for me or the women around me. So we made it: from wall art to apparel […]. Every piece is still designed by women, for women and girls.

Today my husband Blake, a basketball coach, and I are raising our four boys in Alabama, so my game days are usually a gym, a ballfield or a tailgate with a cooler full of snacks. My faith in Jesus shapes how I lead and how we treat every customer, which is why every order still matters to us.
