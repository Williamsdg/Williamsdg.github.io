Our blanks

We don't do scratchy.

The original souvenir was probably a thin, boxy shirt from a stall by the harbor. Ours isn't. Every product is printed on a named, premium blank we've worn and washed ourselves before it goes on the site — and we tell you exactly which one.

Sample-tested

We order, wash and wear every blank before it's listed. If it pills, shrinks or feels cheap, it's out.

Printed near you

Orders route to the closest print partner — US, EU, UK, Australia — so it arrives faster.

Soft-hand prints

Water-based ink printed into the fabric. No thick plastic transfer that cracks in the dryer.

Made right or remade

Misprint, defect or damage in transit? Send a photo and we reprint it free.

Questions

Before you go back.

Is this official merchandise from those places?

No — and that's on purpose. Every design is original and made in-house, inspired by the places themselves. We don't copy shop logos, resort branding or anything trademarked.

How long does it take?

2–4 business days to print, then shipping — see the table above. The product page shows an estimated arrival date for your order before you buy.

How do the shirts fit?

Each product names its exact blank with a size chart (tap “Size guide” on any apparel item). The Heavyweight Tee runs relaxed; the Everyday Soft Tee is a closer retail fit — size up if you like it roomy.

Can I put my own dates or names on it?

Yes — add a line of up to 28 characters on apparel, drinkware, totes and ornaments. It's free and you'll see it on the preview before you add it to your bag.

What if something's wrong?

Because every piece is made just for you, we can't take back items for size or change of mind — but anything misprinted, defective or damaged gets reprinted for free. Just send a photo within 30 days.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You have 2 hours after ordering to change or cancel from your confirmation email. After that it's usually already on the press.
