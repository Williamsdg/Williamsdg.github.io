Our blanks

We don't do scratchy.

The original souvenir was probably a thin, boxy shirt from a stall by the harbor. Ours isn't. Every product is printed on a named, premium blank we've worn and washed ourselves before it goes on the site — and we tell you exactly which one.

Sample-tested

We order, wash and wear every blank before it's listed. If it pills, shrinks or feels cheap, it's out.

Printed near you

Orders route to the closest print partner — US, EU, UK, Australia — so it arrives faster and ships less far.

Soft-hand prints

Water-based DTG ink on apparel. No thick plastic transfer that cracks in the dryer.

Made right or remade

Misprint, defect or damage in transit? Send a photo and we reprint it free.

Questions

Before you go back.

Is this official merchandise from those places?

No — and that's on purpose. Every design is original and made in-house, inspired by the places themselves. We don't copy shop logos, resort branding or anything trademarked. If you want the exact shirt from a specific shop, we'll point you to them if they sell online.

How long does it take?

Most items are printed in 2–5 business days, then shipped. US orders usually arrive in about a week total; international orders are routed to a print partner in or near your country.

How do the shirts fit?

Each product lists its exact blank (e.g. Comfort Colors 1717 or Bella+Canvas 3001) with a size chart. Comfort Colors runs relaxed; Bella+Canvas is a modern retail fit. If you liked a roomy vacation tee, size up in Bella+Canvas.

Can I put my own dates or names on it?

Yes — most apparel and drinkware can add a line like “Honeymoon · June 2026” at checkout. It's printed with everything else, no extra wait.

What if something's wrong?

Because every piece is made just for you, we can't take back items for size or change of mind — but anything misprinted, defective or damaged gets reprinted for free. Just send a photo.
