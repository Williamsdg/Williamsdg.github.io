Why people come back

Ten reasons to love Auburn and Opelika.

Eagles live here. And tigers too. We take our football seriously but know that no one can play golf without a sense of humor.

1

Rolling Toomer's Corner

Thousands of AU faithful celebrate Tiger victories by hurling tissue into the trees downtown.
2

Culinary scene

From Iron Chef and the James Beard Foundation to Garden & Gun and Wine Spectator, the world is figuring out what we already know.
3

RTJ Grand National

Robert Trent Jones called it the “single greatest site for a golf course” he had ever seen.
4

Toomer's lemonade

Number one in Esquire's “162 Reasons It's Good To Be An American.”
5

War Eagle

Almost 90,000 people rumbling in unison as an eagle free-flies around the stadium.
6

Local craft spirits

John Emerald's is the first legal whiskey distilled in Alabama since Prohibition.
7

Performing arts

Year-round arts experiences designed to entertain, educate, and unite.
8

The Spa at Grand National

An award-winning retreat that blends modern techniques with natural elements of Alabama.
9

The Chihuly chandelier

Amber Luster: more than 600 pieces of hand-blown glass at the Jule.
10

Winter wonderland

Carol concerts, tree lightings, and the most unique gingerbread village in the Southeast.
