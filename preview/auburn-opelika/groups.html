Sports planners

Auburn-Opelika is for sports.

Home of Auburn University's facilities, plus city venues for adult and youth tournament play in tennis, swimming, softball, soccer and more. Easily accessible lodging, diverse restaurants and plenty to do off the field.

Let our staff take the weight of event planning off your shoulders. From the kickoff to the 9th inning, we are here to make your event a winning experience.
