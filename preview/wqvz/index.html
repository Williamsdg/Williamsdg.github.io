Advertise & Broadcast

Put Your Message On The Air.

Reach listeners across Lee County, Chambers County, and the surrounding region through on-air advertising, digital promotion, sponsorships, and original programming.

01
Advertise on WQVZ
On-air spots across the broadcast day, plus digital placement on the website, stream, and station social channels.
02
Sponsor a Program
Attach your business to a specific show — billboards at open and close, host mentions, and a logo on that show's profile page.
03
Broadcast Your Church Service
Put your Sunday service on 1700 AM and the live stream, the way Newnan City Church already does each Sunday morning.
