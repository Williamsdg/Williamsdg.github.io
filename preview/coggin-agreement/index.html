Logo preparation
The existing "C" mark redrawn as scalable vector artwork in horizontal, stacked, reversed and one-colour versions.

Per-page titles and descriptions, clean URLs, structured data, XML sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs, analytics and Search Console setup.
Accessibility and performance
Built to WCAG 2.2 Level AA, targeting Core Web Vitals in the "good" range, with content-editing access for approved firm staff.

2.Content Accuracy & Client Approval

Williams Digital will not originate claims about the firm's credentials, judicial service, awards, ratings, case results, testimonials, fee arrangements or areas of practice. All such material is drawn from Client-supplied or Client-published sources and must be confirmed by the Client in writing before launch.

Testimonials, peer ratings, directory badges and awards will be published only where the Client confirms they are current, accurate and permitted for use in attorney advertising. Language implying a guaranteed outcome, and any firm-wide "no fee unless we win" statement, will not be used; contingency-fee wording will appear only where the Client confirms it applies.

The Client is responsible for the final accuracy of all legal, biographical and advertising content, and for any attorney-advertising disclosures required by the Alabama State Bar. Williams Digital does not provide legal advice.

3.Not Included

  • Photography. Attorney portraits, office exteriors and interiors, and any location photography are supplied by the Client or quoted separately as a photography engagement.
  • Secure client portal or document intake. Explicitly out of scope. The consultation form is designed to accept a general description only and is not a secure channel for case documents, medical records or other confidential material.
  • Legal review. Review and approval of the privacy, disclaimer, accessibility and attorney-advertising language rests with the Client or its counsel.
  • Ongoing article or blog writing beyond the launch content described in Section 1.
  • Paid advertising, directory management and review solicitation.
  • Domain registration. The Client owns and retains thecogginfirm.com. Williams Digital will configure DNS at launch; registration and renewal remain the Client's.
  • Email hosting. Existing firm email, including secretary@thecogginfirm.com, remains with the Client's current provider and is not migrated.
  • Third-party costs — premium fonts, licensed stock imagery, paid plugins or third-party services — are billed at cost and only with prior written approval.
