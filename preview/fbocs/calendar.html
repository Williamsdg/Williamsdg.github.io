The year in order

    How the dates work

    Mandatory meetings

    The Fall Orientation Meeting for both parents is held annually on the second Sunday in August. The Midyear Progress Meeting for the teaching parent is held annually on the third Thursday in January.

    Planning Conferences will be scheduled with the Administrator (K-8) and/or Principal (high school) during the months of January through April for each member family.

    Bimonthly reports

    Bimonthly Reports are due on the first day of every other month with a 10-day grace period built in to submit the report online.

    Year-end reports

    Year-End Reports must be submitted online by June 10 unless an exception is granted by the FBOCS Administrator or Principal.

    Registration materials

    Registration Materials are due by June 30 with late fees applying after that:

    • Annual Enrollment Application: submit online by June 30
    • Signed Handbook Verification: submit signed paper form by June 30
    • Church School Enrollment Forms for new students: submit signed paper forms by June 30
    • Tuition and Fees: pay by June 30

    New school year requirements

    These must be met to begin the year in good standing:

    • HSLDA membership or renewal: verify by July 15
    • Course of Study forms: submit online no later than July 15
