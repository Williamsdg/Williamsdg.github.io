    What membership asks of you

    Meetings

    • Both parents attend the fall orientation meeting in August
    • The teaching parent attends the midyear progress meeting in January

    Records

    • A course of study for each student
    • Bimonthly progress reports for each student
    • An annual attendance record, with at least 160 days of instruction
    • A report card for each student in grades K-8
    • Standardized test scores for students in grades 4 and 8

    What members receive

    • A legal covering for home educators, in accordance with Alabama Code §16-28-1(2)
    • Field trips and social events through the year, as volunteers host them
    • The FBOCS Resource Room: books, curriculum and other educational materials
    • Official high school transcripts for grades 9 – 12, sent to other institutions on request
    • A high school diploma, and a graduation ceremony at First Baptist Church

    About FBOCS

    Reports, grades and messages in one member area

    Member families sign in to file bimonthly reports, keep grades and attendance, track high school credits and write to the Administrator and Principal.
