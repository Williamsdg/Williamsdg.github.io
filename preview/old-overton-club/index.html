The Club

In the heart of Liberty Park

Old Overton Club is located in the heart of the Liberty Park Community, a welcoming neighborhood located just a few miles out of Birmingham. This private, nestled country club provides year-round, family-friendly recreation for an active and diverse membership.

Our extensive amenities include an 18-hole world-class golf course and a well-appointed practice facility, an impressive 20,000 square foot clubhouse with several dining options, a resort-style pool, and a state of the art tennis facility, plus a full calendar of social and recreational events.

Old Overton is an invitation only club.

Golf

A Fazio and Pate design

We are an invitation-only private golf club, which opened in 1993. The course was designed by professional golfer Jerry Pate and Tom Fazio. Old Overton Club has played host to a number of tournaments including the Annual Jerry Pate National Intercollegiate Tournament.

Old Overton Club's golf course was shaped from terrain to be the great golf course it is today. The mission of the developers and designers was to build a course that would transcend its surrounding residential development to become a world class golf club. They have succeeded.

Ranked as one of the top five private golf courses in Alabama.

Golf Digest, 2020. Golf Digest also rated Old Overton one of the best new private courses in 1994.

Clubhouse & Lifestyle

A comfortable home away from home

Overlooking the 18th green and fairway, the cabin-like clubhouse offers both a private and casual dining experience. Old Overton is where you build community with those around you.

Member Dining

Chef prepares everything from sophisticated options to laid-back fare. The Overton Grill, Gentlemen's Locker Room, and The Hillside Terrace are all a favorite among members.

The View

Spectacular views of the rolling landscapes and occasional wildlife sightings. Reservations are not required but highly suggested.

The Pool

Pool Season is from Memorial Day to Labor Day, with a full service pool bar and grill, locker rooms, and plenty of lounge seating on the deck.

Guests must be accompanied by a member of Old Overton to dine with us or to enter the pool.

Dress Code

Country club casual

In keeping with the private club nature of Old Overton Club, our members and guests are asked to observe the following guidelines. Management asks that you review these policies with your guests.

  1. Country club casual attire is permitted in all areas of the club.
  2. Denim is allowed but must be in good order. Denim is not allowed to be worn on the golf course.
  3. Men must wear a collared shirt, tucked in at all times, except for Tommy Bahama style and dress button down shirts.
  4. Tank tops for men are not permitted.
  5. Workout, fitness or tennis attire may not be worn in the Overlook Dining Room. It is permitted at The Overton Grill, The Hillside Terrace and outdoor areas.
  6. Hats are not to be worn inside the clubhouse. Hats are permitted for ladies only in the Overton Grill.

Contact & Directions

Finding the gate

  1. From Highway 459 coming from 280, take exit 23 toward Liberty Parkway.
  2. Follow Liberty Parkway past the Publix to the four way stop at On Tap Sports Cafe. This is the first resident's gate. There is not a guard at this gate.
  3. Continue past this gate, through the roundabout, to the gate across from the pond on the left. This is the Old Overton Gate, where a guard will let you in.
  4. Through the gate, follow the road for approximately 1.4 miles. Old Overton Club is on your right.
