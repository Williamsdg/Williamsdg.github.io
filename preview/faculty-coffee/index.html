Our Story

Small by design.

"A small, quality-focused coffee and tea shop in Birmingham's city centre."

Faculty is independent and proudly small-scale. We'd rather do a short menu beautifully than a long one indifferently — which is why the espresso is dialled in every morning, the filter is brewed by hand, and the bakery kitchen runs in-store, daily.

Tucked inside the Edwardian Piccadilly Arcade, we're built around a simple idea: a welcoming room, a close-knit team behind the bar, and coffee worth slowing down for. Brunch runs Thursday through Sunday, and there's always a seat that feels like it's yours.
