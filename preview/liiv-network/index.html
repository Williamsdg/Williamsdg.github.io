Our Story · Why Atlanta

It started
with a call.

Mayo and Kai Sowell knew Atlanta — and Atlanta knew them, in seasons they'd rather forget. But when God said "there's more," it meant planting a church in the very city where their story hurt most. They came back to build a church where everyone belongs and everyone has a part to play. One location became three. And the story is still being written.
