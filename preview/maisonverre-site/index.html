The Chef

A bistro built on three rules.

Chef Laurent Beaumont opened Maison Verre in 2021 after fifteen years cooking through Lyon, Paris, and New York. He came home to Birmingham with three rules: buy the best ingredient you can afford, cook it with respect, pour wine that makes the room feel taller.

The kitchen runs on classical French technique. The cellar leans Burgundy, but you'll find Alsace, Loire, Rhône — and a very deliberate handful of things from outside France that earned their spot on the list.

The room itself is small on purpose. Forty-eight seats, a bar, a private dining nook for parties of eight. There are no televisions. The music doesn't get loud.

— Chef Laurent BeaumontExecutive Chef · Owner
Private Events

A room of your own.

The private dining room seats up to sixteen behind a glass partition that closes you off from the floor without sealing you in.

We work with you on a custom prix-fixe — three or four courses, optional wine pairings — or we'll do à la carte if that's what the occasion calls for. Buyout of the full bistro is also available Mondays.
