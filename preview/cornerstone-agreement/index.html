01Scope of Work

Developer will design, build and launch a public website for Client, replacing the existing site at cornerstoneadj.com. This Agreement covers the public-facing website only.

The scope comprises the following pages:

  • Home — positioning, service overview, credentials, testimonial, service area and calls to action.
  • Services — vehicle, heavy equipment, truck and trailer, mechanical damage, fuel contamination, and total loss and value consulting.
  • Fleet & Commercial — direct-to-business and fleet services.
  • Claims Consulting — estimate and file review, repair quality and body shop disputes, subrogation review, post-repair inspection, dispute resolution and complex damage consulting.
  • About — firm background, personnel, credentials and testimonials.
  • Submit an Assignment — structured intake form.
  • Contact — contact details and enquiry form.
  • Adjuster Roster — adjuster application form.

The build additionally includes:

  • A single design system applied across all pages, including a mobile-specific layout and responsive behaviour tested across desktop, tablet and phone widths.
  • A structured assignment intake form capturing client type, assignment type, loss and unit details, with document and photograph upload.
  • An adjuster roster application form and a general contact form.
  • A regional service-area map covering Alabama, Florida, Georgia and Mississippi.
  • Form submissions routed to Client's designated inbox, with an automated confirmation email to the submitter.
  • Per-page titles and meta descriptions, and a redirect map from the existing website URLs.
  • Migration of existing content and repointing of DNS to the new site at launch.

Work not described in this Section or in Section 2 is out of scope and governed by Section 5.

02Domain & Email Configuration Review

As part of this engagement, Developer will review Client’s existing domain and email configuration and provide written recommendations for improvement. The review covers:

  • Domain configuration — where cornerstoneadj.com is registered, how DNS is hosted, nameserver and record setup, renewal and expiry status, domain lock and privacy settings, and SSL.
  • Email configuration — how Client's email is currently hosted and routed, MX record setup, and the authentication records that affect whether Client's email reaches its recipients (SPF, DKIM and DMARC).
  • Written recommendations — a plain-language summary of what is configured well, what is at risk, and what Developer recommends changing, in priority order.
What this review is and is not

This is an advisory review and a written set of recommendations, delivered once during the project. Implementing those recommendations — migrating email to a different provider, reconfiguring mailboxes, changing registrars, or ongoing email administration — is not included in this Agreement. Developer will quote that work separately if Client wishes to proceed. The only configuration change included here is pointing DNS at the new website at launch.

Client will provide the access necessary to perform the review, or the information required to complete it. Where access cannot be provided, Developer will review what is publicly verifiable and say so.

03Content Accuracy & Client Approval

Developer originates no factual claims about Client. All credentials, licences, certifications, arbitration history, professional background, years of experience, service areas and testimonials published on the website must be supplied or confirmed in writing by Client prior to launch.

This includes, without limitation: I-CAR certification levels and course counts, Farmers University certifications, Arbitration Forums service, state adjuster licensing and reciprocity, years of industry experience, and the professional histories of Client’s personnel.

Testimonials used on the website are reproduced from material Client has already published. Client confirms it has the right to use them and is responsible for their accuracy.

Developer will provide a written verification list and will not publish any unconfirmed item. Client is solely responsible for the accuracy of information it supplies and for ensuring its published claims comply with applicable state insurance-adjuster advertising rules. Developer will not invent testimonials, claim results, client names, carrier relationships or performance statistics.

The website collects information from third parties: assignment submissions may include contact details for insureds, owners and site personnel, and the adjuster roster form collects personal information and resumes from applicants.

Client owns and controls all data submitted through the website. Developer provides and maintains the mechanism only, and will not use, sell, share, or train any system on submitted data.

Developer will delete any individual submission, or all stored submissions, within ten (10) business days of a written request from Client. Client is responsible for how it uses submitted information and for any notice or consent its own practices require.
