01Scope of Work

Developer will design, build and launch a fourteen-page website for Client, replacing the existing Wix site at cornerstoneadj.com. The scope comprises:

  • Fourteen pages: Home, Expertise, Heavy Equipment & Machinery, Complex Auto Claims, Commercial & Fleet, Specialty & Disputed Claims, Mechanical & Fuel Claims, RV/Marine & Powersports, Services, About, Coverage, Join Our Roster, Submit an Assignment, and Contact.
  • A shared design system applied across all pages, including a mobile-specific layout.
  • An interactive coverage map reflecting Client’s licensed and serviced states.
  • A structured assignment intake form with document upload.
  • An adjuster roster application form with resume upload.
  • A general contact form.
  • Form routing to Client’s designated inbox, with confirmation emails to submitters.
  • Per-page titles, meta descriptions and a redirect map from existing Wix URLs.
  • Migration of existing content and repointing of DNS at launch.

Work not described above is out of scope and governed by Section 8.

02Content Accuracy & Client Approval

Developer originates no factual claims about Client. All credentials, licences, certifications, arbitration records, professional history, years of experience, coverage states, testimonials and service descriptions published on the site must be supplied or confirmed in writing by Client prior to launch.

This specifically includes, without limitation: I-CAR certification levels and course counts, Farmers University certifications, Arbitration Forums service and record, state adjuster licensing and reciprocity, years of industry experience, and the professional histories of Client’s personnel.

Developer will assemble a written verification list and will not publish any unconfirmed item. Client is solely responsible for the accuracy of information it supplies and for ensuring that its published claims comply with applicable state insurance-adjuster advertising rules.

Developer will not invent testimonials, claim results, client names, carrier relationships or performance statistics.

03Not Included

  • The assignment and adjuster-roster dashboard. Any internal system for managing assignments, adjuster records, statuses or reporting is expressly outside this Agreement and would be the subject of a separate written agreement.
  • Claim file handling. The site will not receive, store or process claim files, insured personal information, medical information, or carrier system data.
  • Photography. Client supplies images. Licensed stock, if requested, is handled under Section 4.
  • Copywriting from scratch beyond structuring and editing Client’s existing material and one recorded interview.
  • Migration of email, CRM or telephony systems.
  • Paid advertising, ongoing SEO retainer, or content production after launch.

04Investment & Payment Terms

The standard fee for the scope in Section 1 is $3,000. Developer is extending a founding-client adjustment of $1,500, making the total build fee $1,500.

The founding-client adjustment is granted in exchange for: (a) a written case study once the site is live; (b) permission for Developer to display the work publicly in its portfolio; and (c) willingness to serve as a reference. The adjustment applies to the build fee only and does not apply to the monthly fee in Section 5. Should Client decline (a), (b) and (c) after launch, Developer may invoice the difference.

  • $750 deposit, due on signature. Work begins on receipt.
  • $750 balance, due upon Client approval and prior to DNS being repointed.
  • Out-of-scope work is billed at $50 per hour, quoted and approved in writing in advance.
  • Third-party costs incurred at Client’s request — including licensed photography or a specific paid service — are billed at cost with prior written approval and no markup.
  • Domain registration for cornerstoneadj.com remains the Client’s own cost and is not billed through Developer.

05Ongoing Hosting, Form Automation & Maintenance

Following launch, Client will be billed $75 per month, beginning on the launch date, covering:

  • Website hosting and SSL certificate
  • Assignment form routing, processing and document storage
  • Roster application processing and resume storage
  • Automated confirmation emails to form submitters
  • Spam filtering on all forms
  • Daily backups and uptime monitoring
  • Security and dependency updates
  • Minor content edits (text and image swaps), up to one hour per month
  • DNS management

The monthly fee may be cancelled by either party with thirty (30) days’ written notice. On cancellation, Developer will provide Client with a complete copy of the site files and any stored form submissions, and will assist with transfer to a host of Client’s choosing. Domain control remains with Client at all times.

06Applicant & Submitter Data

The roster application collects personal information from third-party applicants, including resumes. The assignment form may collect contact details for insureds, owners or site personnel.

Client is the owner and controller of all data submitted through the site. Developer provides and maintains the mechanism only, and will not use, sell, share or train any system on submitted data.

Developer will delete any individual submission, or all stored submissions, within ten (10) business days of a written request from Client. Client is responsible for how it uses applicant and submitter information and for any notice or consent its own practices require.

07Ownership & Rights

On receipt of final payment, Client owns the finished website: all page files, styling, written content and any custom graphics produced for the project.

Client owns and retains cornerstoneadj.com at all times. Developer claims no interest in the domain.

Developer retains the right to display the work in its portfolio and marketing, consistent with Section 4. Developer retains ownership of any underlying reusable code libraries, frameworks or components not created specifically for Client.

Client warrants that any material it supplies — photographs, logos, text, credentials — is either owned by Client or properly licensed for this use.

08Revisions & Support

The build fee includes two rounds of revisions during the build phase. A round is a consolidated set of feedback delivered at one time.

Structural changes requested after approval — new pages, new functionality, or reworking an approved page’s structure — are out of scope and billed under Section 4.

Developer will correct any defect in delivered work at no charge for ninety (90) days following launch.

09Client Responsibilities

  • Provide written confirmation of all credentials, licensing and coverage states before launch (Section 2).
  • Participate in one recorded interview for content.
  • Supply photography, or approve licensed stock purchases.
  • Provide access to the domain registrar, or make the required DNS changes when instructed.
  • Provide consolidated feedback within five (5) business days of each review request.
  • Designate a single point of contact authorised to approve content and sign off on launch.

Timeline commitments are contingent on Client meeting these responsibilities. Delays in feedback or content extend the schedule accordingly.

10Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential any non-public business information disclosed by the other during this engagement, and will not disclose it to third parties except as required by law.

Developer will not request, and Client will not provide, claim files, insured personal information, carrier system credentials, or any material subject to a confidentiality obligation Client owes to a carrier or client of its own.

11Cancellation

Either party may cancel this Agreement with written notice.

If Client cancels after work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable and Client is invoiced for any approved out-of-scope work completed to date. Work completed to the point of cancellation is delivered to Client.

If Developer cancels, Client receives all work completed to date and a pro-rata refund of any amount paid for work not performed.
