3.Domain & Email Configuration Review

  • Domain configuration. Where cornerstoneadj.com is registered, how DNS is hosted, nameserver and record setup, renewal and expiry status, domain lock and privacy settings, and SSL.
  • Email configuration. How email is currently hosted and routed, MX record setup, and the authentication records that affect whether Cornerstone's email reaches its recipients — SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
  • Written recommendations. A plain-language summary of what is configured well, what is at risk, and what Williams Digital recommends changing, in priority order.

4.What the Review Is and Is Not

Advisory onlyThis is an advisory review and a written set of recommendations, delivered once during the project. Implementing those recommendations — migrating email to a different provider, reconfiguring mailboxes, changing registrars, or ongoing email administration — is not included in this agreement, and will be quoted separately if Cornerstone wishes to proceed. The only configuration change included here is pointing DNS at the new website at launch. Cornerstone will provide the access necessary to perform the review, or the information required to complete it; where access cannot be provided, Williams Digital will review what is publicly verifiable and say so.

5.The Dashboard Is a Separate Agreement

Not included in this agreementAny internal or client-facing dashboard — including assignment management, adjuster records, status tracking, reporting or any logged-in area — is expressly outside this agreement. It will be scoped, quoted and contracted separately. Nothing in this agreement commits either party to that work or to any particular price for it.

6.Content Accuracy & Client Approval

Williams Digital originates no factual claims about Cornerstone. All credentials, licences, certifications, arbitration history, professional background, years of experience, service areas and testimonials published on the website must be supplied or confirmed in writing by Cornerstone prior to launch.

This includes, without limitation: I-CAR certification levels and course counts, Farmers University certifications, Arbitration Forums service, state adjuster licensing and reciprocity, years of industry experience, and the professional histories of Cornerstone's personnel.

Testimonials used on the website are reproduced from material Cornerstone has already published. Cornerstone confirms it has the right to use them and is responsible for their accuracy.

Williams Digital will provide a written verification list and will not publish any unconfirmed item. Cornerstone is solely responsible for the accuracy of information it supplies and for ensuring its published claims comply with applicable state insurance-adjuster advertising rules. Williams Digital will not invent testimonials, claim results, client names, carrier relationships or performance statistics.

7.Not Included

  • Implementation of the domain or email recommendations beyond pointing DNS at the new site.
  • Claim file handling. The website will not receive, store or process claim files, insured personal information, medical information, or carrier system data.
  • Photography. Cornerstone supplies images, or licensed stock is purchased at cost with prior written approval.
  • Copywriting from scratch beyond structuring and editing Cornerstone's existing material and one recorded interview.
  • Migration of email, CRM or telephone systems.
  • Paid advertising, ongoing SEO retainer, or content production after launch.

10.Form Submissions & Applicant Data

The website collects information from third parties: assignment submissions may include contact details for insureds, owners and site personnel, and the adjuster roster form collects personal information and resumes from applicants.

Cornerstone owns and controls all data submitted through the website. Williams Digital provides and maintains the mechanism only, and will not use, sell, share, or train any system on submitted data.

Williams Digital will delete any individual submission, or all stored submissions, within ten (10) business days of a written request from Cornerstone. Cornerstone is responsible for how it uses submitted information and for any notice or consent its own practices require.

On receipt of final payment, Cornerstone owns the finished website: all page files, styling, written content and any custom graphics produced for this project.

Cornerstone owns and retains cornerstoneadj.com at all times. Williams Digital claims no interest in the domain.

12.Revisions & Support

The fee includes two rounds of revisions during the build phase. A round is a consolidated set of feedback delivered at one time.

Structural changes requested after approval — additional pages, new functionality, or reworking an approved page's structure — are out of scope and billed under the Fee & Payment Terms section.

Williams Digital will correct any defect in delivered work at no charge for ninety (90) days following launch.

14.Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential any non-public business information disclosed by the other during this engagement, and will not disclose it to third parties except as required by law.

Williams Digital will not request, and Cornerstone will not provide, claim files, insured personal information, carrier system credentials, or any material subject to a confidentiality obligation Cornerstone owes to a carrier or a client of its own.

Any credentials shared for the purposes of the domain and email review will be used only for that review and for launch, and Cornerstone may revoke them at any time.

15.Cancellation

Either party may cancel this agreement with written notice.

If Cornerstone cancels after work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable and Cornerstone is invoiced for any approved out-of-scope work completed to date. All work completed to the point of cancellation is delivered to Cornerstone.

If Williams Digital cancels, Cornerstone receives all work completed to date and a pro-rata refund of any amount paid for work not performed.
