3.Domain Name — Purchased & Owned by Rojo Painting

Rojo Painting will purchase its own domain name (for example, rojopainting.com, subject to availability) in Rojo Painting's name and on its own account. Williams Digital does not buy, hold or register the domain on the client's behalf, so that Rojo Painting owns and controls it permanently, whether or not it continues with Williams Digital.

The domain registration fee is paid by Rojo Painting directly to the registrar and is not included in the website fee or the monthly service fee. A .com domain typically costs roughly $10–$25 for the first year at GoDaddy; renewal pricing is set by GoDaddy and is usually higher than the first-year promotional price.

Once the domain is purchased, Rojo Painting grants Williams Digital delegate access (below) so Williams Digital can connect the domain to the website at launch. Williams Digital will only change the records needed to point the domain at the website, and will walk Rojo Painting through each step on request.

4.How to Buy the Domain on GoDaddy

Step 1
Search for the name
Go to godaddy.com and type the name you want into the search bar (for example, rojopainting.com). A .com is recommended. If it's taken, GoDaddy will suggest close alternatives. Send Williams Digital your top choices if you'd like a second opinion.
Step 2
Add it to your cart and skip the extras
Add the domain to your cart. Decline the add-ons GoDaddy offers: website builder, hosting, SSL certificates and extra security bundles are already covered by the Williams Digital monthly service. Business email (Microsoft 365) is optional and not required for the website.
Step 3
Create the account in the business's name
Create a GoDaddy account using an email address Rojo Painting controls, and list Rojo Painting as the registrant/organization. Keep the login somewhere safe. This account is proof of ownership.
Step 4
Choose the term and turn on auto-renew
Choose a 1-, 2- or 3-year registration and make sure auto-renew is on, so the domain, and the website with it, never lapses.
Step 5
Check out
Complete the purchase with Rojo Painting's payment method. GoDaddy will send a confirmation email; verify the registrant contact email if GoDaddy asks you to.
Step 6
Give Williams Digital delegate access
In GoDaddy, open your name at the top right → Account Settings → Delegate Access → Invite to Access. Enter Williams Digital and Dylan@williamsdigital.io, choose the Products & Domains access level, and send the invite. You never need to share your password, and you can remove access at any time.
