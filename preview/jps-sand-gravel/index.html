One addition we recommend

A material calculator. The customer enters length, width and depth and gets cubic yards and tons for the material they are looking at. It gives contractors a reason to keep the app installed and makes inquiries more specific.

Behind the app

The admin panel

Your team manages materials, categories, banners, reviews, testimonials, locations and customer enquiries from a web browser. No developer is needed for day-to-day changes.

About the content shown

This is a design and layout concept. The material list, weights, yard cities, ratings and reviews are samples so the screens have something to show. Your own materials, photographs, logo, addresses, team and company wording replace all of it. Forms in the concept do not send anything.
