The Chain Reaction

Sponsor one student.
Change a generation.

This isn't a statistic. It's a sequence — and it's happening right now in Santiago.

Step 01

You sponsor a student

A young person from poverty gains a champion — and a way forward.

Step 02

They attend the Career Institute

15 months of training in English, bookkeeping, computer technology, and executive skills.

Step 03

Their family gains opportunity

A first professional income lifts the whole household — and microloans launch new businesses.

Step 04

The community improves

Clean water, better schools, and local commerce raise the standard for everyone.

Step 05

The local church grows

Trained pastors and discipled students strengthen churches at the center of it all.

Step 06

Future leaders emerge

Leadership Academy graduates return to lead — and the ripple begins again.
