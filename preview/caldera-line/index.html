Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is the price per person or per car?

Per vehicle. Two guests or seven, the fare for your vehicle is the same. It includes luggage, waiting time, tolls and airport or port parking.

What if my flight or ferry is late?

We track it, so your driver changes his schedule to match. Waiting time is free: 60 minutes after landing at the airport and 90 minutes after the ferry docks at Athinios.

Can you get into Oia and Fira town centres?

Cars can’t enter the pedestrian lanes. Your driver will take you to the closest drop-off point and help carry bags to your hotel reception when it’s nearby.

How do I pay?

Pay online when you book, or pay your driver by card or in cash on the day. Every car has a card terminal.

Do you have child seats?

Yes. Infant, toddler and booster seats are free. Tick the box when you book and tell us the children’s ages.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Transfers are free to cancel up to 24 hours before pickup. Hourly tours and the Sunset Run can be cancelled free up to 48 hours before.
