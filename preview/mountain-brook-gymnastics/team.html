MBG Competitive Team

Levels 3 to 10. Xcel Silver to Diamond. One team.

Alabama USA Gymnastics’ 2024–2025 Compulsory Gym of the Year, and one of the largest compulsory competitive team programs in the state.

General information

How the program works.

Our Competitive Program includes Level 3 through Level 10 in the USAG program, as well as Xcel Silver through Xcel Diamond.

Groups train as few as 6 hours per week and as many as 25. Higher-level athletes typically train more hours, and training-group decisions are made by your coaches as your child progresses through the program.

Team tryouts are held every fall for all of our Advanced Rec students, as well as a select few coach-recommended lower-level Rec students.

Awards & accolades

Four seasons of results.

2024–25

  • Alabama USA Gymnastics 2024–2025 Compulsory Gym of the Year
  • 31 Individual & All-Around State Champions
  • 12 Region 8 Qualifiers
  • State team placements: Level 4 – 1st · Level 5 – 1st · Level 6 – 3rd · Level 7 – 2nd · Level 10 – 3rd
  • Class of 2025: Chloe Gann — University of California, Davis
  • Class of 2025: Avery Aaron-Brush — Rhode Island College

2023–24

  • 25 Individual & All-Around State Champions
  • 15 Region 8 Qualifiers, with 1 Regional Individual Event Champion
  • 2 Qualifiers to the Women’s Development Program Level 9 Eastern Championships
  • State team placements: Level 3 – 3rd · Level 4 – 1st · Level 5 – 3rd · Level 8 – 2nd · Level 9 – 3rd · Level 10 – 3rd

2022–23

  • 28 Individual & All-Around State Champions

  • 1 Qualifier to the Women’s Development Program National Championships
