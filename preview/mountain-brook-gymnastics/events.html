Summer

Summer Day Camp

When the summer hits, our day camp is the place to be! We have options for both preschoolers and school-aged boys and girls.

Camp typically runs Monday to Friday from the end of May through early August. Campers learn gymnastics skills, with scheduled breaks for craft, snack, game time and lunch throughout the day.
