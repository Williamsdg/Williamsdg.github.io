Three classes for our youngest gymnasts.

18 months – 3 years · 45 minutes

Tumble Buddy 2s

An introductory class in movement education. Children begin to develop physical strength and coordination, balance and agility at an early age — but the greatest part of this class is the interaction between buddies and their children.

Each student needs ONE active buddy — a parent or caregiver who isn’t already a buddy to another child in class. Additional babies, grown-ups or siblings aren’t allowed in the gym during class.

3 years · 45 minutes

3 All Me

The first class our students take all by themselves! Coaches promote physical fitness and introduce basic gymnastics skills on vault, bars, beam, floor and more.

Students discover turn-taking, manners and other early-childhood social skills while developing body awareness, coordination and cooperative learning.

4 & new 5-year-olds · 55 minutes

Fantastic 4s

The class that leads into our Recreational Program. Children are encouraged to master progressively more difficult gymnastics skills.

They also keep the fun going with Tumbl Trak jumping, loose-foam pit play and trench slide stations.

Recreational · Girls 5+

Three levels, all four Olympic apparatuses.

Students progress at their own pace. Each level lists the major skills required for promotion to the next — these include, but aren’t limited to, the skills below.

Beginner Rec

55 min · once a week

Exposes students to foundational gymnastics skills, body form and positions, strength and flexibility.

VaultStretch jump, stick it
BarsPullovers, casting, chin hangs, scissors
BeamRelevé walks, arabesque, stretch jumps
FloorForward & backward rolls, cartwheels, handstands

No prerequisite skills required. Students must be 5+ to join.

Intermediate Rec

90 min · once a week

Slightly increases the training intensity while students keep progressing at their own pace. New skills are introduced and skills mastered in Beginner are polished.

VaultMat-stack stretch jump, handstand tree fall
BarsChin-up pullovers, back hip circles, squat-ons
Beam¾ handstands, jumps, cartwheel dismounts
FloorVertical handstands, cartwheel step-ins, backward rolls to push-up, bridge kickovers

Completion of Beginner Rec or an evaluation is required.

Advanced Rec

105 min · once a week

Introduces students to the skills necessary for our Compulsory Team Program — without competition.

VaultHandstand flat backs
BarsCast back hip circles, shoot-throughs, mill circles, front hip circles
BeamHandstands, cartwheels, jumps, cartwheel ¼-turn dismounts
FloorHandstand forward roll, round-offs, backward rolls to push-up, back walkovers, intro to back handsprings

Completion of Intermediate Rec or an evaluation is required. Team tryouts are held every fall for Advanced Rec students.

How to register

A request first, then we confirm.

All registration is done in our Customer Portal with a debit or credit card. We don’t accept registrations by email or phone.

  1. Request your classPick a class in the Customer Portal. Online requests are not automatic enrollments.
  2. Check out at $0.00Your cart will say $0.00 — that’s expected. Keep going until the page says “Thank You For Your Purchase.”
  3. Watch for two emailsOne right away confirming your request, and another once we’ve processed it. Requests also show under your student in the portal.
  4. We approve and chargeSession tuition plus any registration fee is charged to your card on file when your request is approved.

No emails and nothing in your account? The request didn’t go through — please try again. If your first choice is full, request the waitlist.

All classes are non-refundable.
We do not offer makeup classes — this includes the rare event we have to close for weather.
Withdrawing for a credit

If you withdraw in writing before the session start date — Session 1: August 10; Session 2: October 7 (this may be before your actual first class day) — we’ll issue a credit on your account, good toward a future class session in the current gym year (Session 1, 2, 3, 4 or the Summer Session).

The credit is non-transferable, can’t be used for camps, privates or other special events, and expires when the new gym year starts in August.

Switching class times

Request the new time in the Customer Portal and add a note that you’re switching. Switching is free until the new session begins.

Once the session start date arrives — regardless of which day your class meets — a $10 change fee applies and you may only switch once. The fee doesn’t apply when moving off a waitlist or moving up or down a level.

Tumble Buddy 2s — who can be in the gym

Only the one adult buddy taking part in class may be out in the gym. No siblings or additional family, including holding infants or baby-wearing. A buddy can’t be the buddy of another student in the same class.

Minimum class size

We reserve the right to cancel a class with fewer than two students. If that happens we’ll help you find another open class, or issue a refund if no option works.

Preschool placement

All preschool classes are placed by birthdate.

Questions? Email info@mountainbrookgymnastics.com.
