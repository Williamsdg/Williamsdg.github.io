Employment opportunities

We hire people, not positions.

We’re always interested in hiring a qualified candidate, regardless of current openings. A fun, energetic and kind personality is a must — a gymnastics background is a plus, but isn’t required for many of our positions.

What we teach

Gymnastics, with a focus on building character.

We teach with a focus on character as well as strength, flexibility, cognitive and locomotor skills, and the overall fitness of all our gymnasts — from the Tumble Buddy 2s to Advanced Recreational and the Competitive Team.

Gymnastics Instructor

MBG instructors believe in teaching through positive reinforcement, with an emphasis on making a wonderful experience for each student. Instructors can teach Preschool or Recreational (school-age) classes, or a combination of both. Strong candidates have good time-management skills and can keep the class energized, fun and moving while making sure each child receives the individual attention needed to progress.

Advanced Rec Instructor / Competitive Team Coach

In addition to the qualities of a Gymnastics Instructor, these coaches have additional experience working with advanced gymnasts. Our competitive gymnasts compete at various levels within the USAG program in Alabama and across the Southeast. Familiarity with advanced skills, drills, conditioning exercises and technique is a definite plus.

Summer Camp Instructor

All the qualities of a Gymnastics Instructor, plus the ability to work with kids in an extended class format. Summer Camp typically runs Monday–Friday from the end of May through early August. Camp instructors teach gymnastics skills with scheduled breaks for craft, snack, game time and lunch, and should be comfortable working with a group of up to 10 children for 3–6 hours at a time.

Special Events Staff

Fun, energetic individuals who can safely lead children of varying skill levels through the gym — often for their very first introduction to gymnastics and MBG. You’ll keep your party group energized and moving, interact with parents, and set up, serve food and clean up at the end. Almost all of our special events are held on Saturdays and Sundays.

Many of our staff members wear multiple hats, or began in one position and transitioned to another. If you’re interested in multiple positions, please let us know. We welcome enthusiasm and a desire to learn, and we encourage individual as well as professional growth. We can’t wait to hear from you.
