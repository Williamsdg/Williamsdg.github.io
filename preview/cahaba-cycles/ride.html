Places to ride

A few of our favorite places in and around Birmingham, for every level of rider, from easy-going paths to challenging mountain trails. Questions about trails, gear or conditions? Stop by any shop.

Trails and paths

  • Oak Mountain State Park

    50+ miles of pro built single track, double track, gravity/downhill, pump track, beginner, intermediate, and advanced trails.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Red Mountain Park

    A 15-mile trail system that traverses exciting and diverse terrain. Includes scenic views. Great for beginners but has some advanced features as well

    Trail infoDirections

  • Tannehill State Park

    Tannehill has roughly 40 miles of trail winding through the hills surrounding a historic Civil War-era foundry. Machine-built fast-flowing cross-country trails.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Coldwater Mountain

    60 miles of premier, purpose-built singletrack, ranging from beginner to expert, including flowy, rocky, and jump-filled terrain. Rated as a Bronze Level Ride Center by IMBA.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Trussville Sports Complex

    Trussville City Park has approximately 5 miles of trail, mostly hand-built singletrack, bordering the Cahaba River. Much of the trail is rooty and technical, but there is plenty of fast flowing trail to mix it up.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Turkey Creek

    About 7 miles of easy to moderate difficulty multi-use trail in the Turkey Creek Nature Preserve.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Swayback

    12 miles of trails for hiking and biking near the historic Swayback Bridge.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Lake Lurleen

    More than 23 miles of multi-use trails ranging from easy to moderate in difficulty.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Duck River

    A 20 mile loop around the lake. Single track trail.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Munny Sokol

    About 11 miles of beginner to intermediate level single track trails.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Gardendale Urban Trail System

    A 3-mile trail that is easy to extend the mileage by the addition of Wild West and Peak Attack.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Lake Howard

    Sylaward Trails at Lake Howard in Sylacauga, AL, offer roughly 15 miles of fast, flowing, and mostly intermediate (blue) singletrack suitable for all skill levels.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Noccalula Falls Park

    The Black Creek Trail system in Gadsden at Noccalula Falls Park offers a scenic, multi-use, and singletrack mountain biking experience covering roughly 16 miles of machine-cut trails with approximately 110 ft of elevation change per mile. Ranging from easy to moderate, these trails are popular for cross-country riding, featuring rocky terrain, flowing sections, and challenging climbs.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Cahaba River Park

    Cahaba River Park offers roughly 17 miles of purpose-built, professionally constructed mountain biking trails featuring a mix of scenic, wooded singletrack with beginner as well as some technical rocky sections suitable for riders looking for a fun, intermediate challenge.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Black Creek Mountain

    New trail Fall 2019. Spanning 73 acres of up and down woodland terrain and offering 5 miles of trails.

    Trail infoDirections

  • RideBHM

    RideBHM is Alabama’s first downhill mountain biking park, where a day pass or membership gets you access to some of the top trails in Alabama. This park has trails for all skill levels, from beginner to advanced, including flowy XC trails as well.

    Trail infoDirections

  • McClellan Mountain Bike Trail

    15 Miles of purpose built singletrack and is perfect for beginner riders of all ages. It is the first specifically designed NICA trail in the state of Alabama. The system has a great variety of green and blue trail segments. Trail names capture the history of the military base, community figures and local heros. Equally fun in either direction, the trails twist, turn and roll around Sunset Hill and along the base of the Baltzell Mountains.

    Trail infoDirections

  • Double Oak Park

    Double Oak Park is the Birmingham area’s newest trail system, and was funded and developed by Shelby County. Phase 1 is complete with about 10 miles of trail machine-built trail, which is rocky and drains well. Much of Double Oak Park’s trail system is situated on top of a massive ridgeline, which requires an almost 2-mile long climb up from the main parking area, but once you’re over the ridge you’ll be rewarded with incredible views and chunky machine built descents with lots of alternate lines to choose from. Find a trail near you using the The Red Rock Trail System® and see which trails/paths are coming soon!

  • Black Creek Park Rails to Trails

    Trail follows an old rail line starting at Black Creek Park and runs under highway 31 and I65 for about 8 miles. It stays pretty close to Black Creek the entire time and is well maintained on smooth gravel. It passes the Children's Park as well as the Bark Park. There are benches about every mile with markers every half mile. A little over a mile in there is a picnic table in the woods and a couple of side trails that lead down to the creek in a couple of places.

Tour de Cahaba

The 2026 ride rolled out of our Homewood shop on Sunday, August 2. Fully supported with SAG vehicles, a rest stop at every Cahaba shop and a post-ride cookout. It’s not a race: expect every pace.

The slow roll is a guided family ride through Homewood with a popsicle stop, and a peel-off point if little legs get tired. $3 of every registration goes to BUMP.

Cahaba Gives

Because you shop with us, we can support the groups that build trails, teach kids and push for safer roads. Get involved with them too.

  • Bo Bikes Bama

    Every April since 2012, Bo Jackson has ridden across Alabama to raise money for natural-disaster relief. We’ve donated bikes and gear every year and recruited hundreds of riders.

  • IMBA

    The International Mountain Biking Association is behind many of the beautiful trails in Alabama today.

    $10 from every full-suspension mountain bike
  • League of American Bicyclists

    Safer roads, stronger communities and a bicycle-friendly America.

    $1 from every helmet
  • NICA

    We’ve helped build high school mountain bike teams in Alabama.

    $1 from every tire
  • BUMP

    Birmingham Urban Mountain Pedalers build, preserve, maintain and ride our local trails, and put on Bump n’ Grind every year.

    $3 from every Tour de Cahaba registration
  • Trips for Kids

    Trail rides and six-week lessons in bike safety, maintenance and repair, plus affordable refurbished bikes.

  • Birmingham Hammerfest

    We’re an official sponsor of the criterium weekend, with races for pros, amateurs and juniors and a kids’ zone.

  • Homewood Public Library

    For the past 10 years we’ve given away a bike each summer through the library’s reading program.

  • Redemptive Cycles

    Their mission, to get more people on bikes, is what we strive for every day.

  • Birmingham Bicycle Club

    Safe cycling for fitness and recreation, for people of all ages.

  • Vulcan Tri Club

    Open to anybody interested in swimming, cycling or running: fast, slow, or currently afraid of the water.
