You get so much more than just a bike.

We strive to be your local bike shop, whether you’re a roadie, a mountain bike junkie, a casual cruiser, or a parent shopping for a first bike.

Free bike sizing

When you buy one of our bikes, we’ll make sure it fits. Custom fittings are there when you want more.

Free 6-month tune-up

Just like a car, bikes need tune-ups. The first one is on us. Bring it in and we’ll fix it right up.

5-year parts, lifetime frame

Most manufacturers only offer a 1 or 2 year parts warranty. Basically, your bike is covered.

Trade in, trade up

Who buys just one bike over their lifetime? Trade yours in and put that money toward a brand new ride.

Cahaba Rewards

Earn on every purchase: 1% on bikes, 2% on accessories, 3% on service. Rewards of up to $100.

Ride now, pay later

Pay for bikes, parts and accessories over 12 months with deferred interest. Approval required.
