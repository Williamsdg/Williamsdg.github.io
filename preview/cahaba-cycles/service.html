New bike? Tune-ups are covered

Every new bike gets its first 6-month tune-up free. Add the Cahaba Plan for $200 and get unlimited 25-hour tune-ups for 3 years.

Need it back fast?

A day without a bike can be a painful day. Add a note when you book and we’ll follow up as soon as we can. Rush service is available on request; it doesn’t apply to warranty repairs or jobs waiting on shipped parts.

Do I have to be home?

Usually, yes, though there may be exceptions. Note it in your appointment if you can’t be home.

How much does mobile service cost?

Labor runs about 25% more than in-store, to cover gas, time and the van. The work has to total at least $90 in parts and labor.

How long does it take?

It depends on the job, but usually 1 to 1.5 hours per bike. We can do around 6 bikes in one trip.

What can be done from the van?

Most general maintenance: tune-ups, washes, parts installs. Some jobs need specialty parts or tools, so the bike comes back to the shop.

Does my bike need to be clean?

Yes, but if you don’t have time we’ll wash it first for $25. We may need a hose.

Where will the van park?

We need about 20 feet of curb or driveway that doesn’t block household parking.

How do I pay?

All credit cards, taken with the Square app.

Ride fast. Ride fit.

Tired of an aching back, sore knees or numb hands? Body Fit, Flex Fit and Aerobar Fit are guaranteed for 6 months, with free adjustments for 60 days and 15% off any installs your fit needs.

  • Bike sizingFreeAbout 15 minutes. Guarantees the correct frame size and saddle height. Free with a new bike; $30 for a bike not bought from us.
  • Body Fit$175Allow 1.5–2 hours. A comprehensive road fit for power, comfort and speed: interview, body analysis, then cleat, saddle, stack, reach and cockpit adjustments.
  • Flex Fit$295–$360Allow 3–4 hours. Road, tri/TT, mountain or cyclocross fit by John Karrasch, Licensed Physical Therapist Assistant, with a flexibility assessment and an off-bike exercise program.
  • Cleat and pedal analysis$50Allow 30–60 minutes. Cleats fitted and aligned to how your feet move, to prevent hot spots, numb feet and pinched nerves.
  • Aerobar fit$50Allow 30–60 minutes. Adjustments to your existing bars for the best upper-body position for triathlon.

Fittings are by appointment. Bring shorts, shoes and pedals. Parts aren’t included in fit prices, and extra labor may apply.
