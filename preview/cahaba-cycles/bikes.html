Buying a bike with us

Every new bike gets a free sizing, a free 6-month tune-up, a 5-year parts warranty and a lifetime frame warranty.

Trade in your old bike

  1. Bring your bike-shop-quality bike to any Cahaba Cycles shop.

  2. We evaluate it for trade-in credit, or cash paid by check within 5–7 business days.

  3. If you trade, pick out your new bike and get an instant credit.

  4. Leave your old bike and ride away on your new one, with a big smile.
