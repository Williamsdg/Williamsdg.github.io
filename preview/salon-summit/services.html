About the Brazilian treatment

Brazilian Blowout builds a protective protein layer around each strand to smooth the cuticle and eliminate frizz, for smooth, healthy hair with radiant shine.

There’s no down time. The minute you leave the salon you can wash and air dry your hair, work out, or put it in a ponytail or clip, and it stays sleek and smooth.
