How to donate

  1. Fill out the donor form

    Use the online hair donor form. Donate hair only, hair with a financial donation, or hair for a Girl Scout patch. You’ll get a personal ID number.

  2. Come in for your cut

    Bring your ID number. We cut off your braid or ponytail for free, as long as it’s at least 12 inches.

  3. We pack it up

    We put your number on the form, seal it in the envelope with your hair, and hand you an addressed envelope. Doing this paperwork helps Wigs for Kids keep its costs low.

  4. Mail it

    Have the envelope weighed, buy the postage, and send it to Wigs for Kids.

Want a finished look afterward? A structured or layered cut and style is $40. And we hope you’ll let us add your picture to our Wigs for Kids page.

About Wigs for Kids

For over 30 years, Wigs for Kids has provided hair replacements and support to children who have lost their hair to chemotherapy, alopecia, burns and other medical issues. Children receive their hair replacements at no charge.

Each one is made from donated human hair and custom-fit to the child’s head. Children choose everything: the color, the length, the texture and how it’s styled. Medical-grade adhesive keeps it in place through the most active days, so a child can look like themselves and live their life.
