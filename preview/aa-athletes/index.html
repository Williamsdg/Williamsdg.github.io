The argument

The pitch you make
to the next athlete.

An athlete choosing between agencies is comparing promises. Most agencies describe what they'll build. A&A can open a laptop and show the athlete their own site, their own media kit and their own inquiry inbox — before they sign.

That is what this platform is for. Not a directory of who A&A already represents, but the reason the next one says yes.

Their story, stats, film, partners, merch and appearances on their own domain — with business inquiries routed back to A&A, never to a personal inbox.

Why they don't match

Same engine.
Different athlete.

An athlete's site is a brand asset, not a directory listing. The system carries the structure; the athlete carries the identity.

Football

Heavy condensed type, stadium darkness, numbers that fill the screen. Performance and recruiting forward.

Gymnastics

Editorial serif, generous whitespace, lifestyle photography. Closer to a fashion portfolio than a sports page.

Boxing

Fight-poster scale, tale of the tape, bout-by-bout record. A promoter's aesthetic, not a college one.

One codebase, one database, one console. The presentation layer is configuration — colour, type, layout and section order per athlete. See the football build, then the gymnastics one.
