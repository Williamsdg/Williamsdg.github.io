The story
She competes like the room is empty.

Nia started gymnastics at six because her older sister did, and stayed because of the ten seconds before a beam routine — the part everyone else finds unbearable.

This is where the athlete's own voice goes: what they are building, who they are away from competition, and what they want a brand relationship to feel like. On a lifestyle athlete's site this section matters more than the scores do.

Partnerships

Categories that fit.

A gymnast's commercial value is not the same shape as a quarterback's. Her partnership categories are beauty, wellness and lifestyle before they are sport — and the site is built to say so.

Beauty

Wellness

Fashion, travel and home — the categories that outlast a competition career.
