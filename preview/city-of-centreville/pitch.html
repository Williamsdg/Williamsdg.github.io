Accessibility

A federal date worth planning around.

April 26, 2028

The ADA Title II web rule

The U.S. Department of Justice rule sets WCAG 2.1 Level AA as the standard for state and local government websites. For cities under 50,000 residents the compliance date is April 26, 2028, after a one-year extension issued in April 2026.

What that means for Centreville

There is time, and the work is easier done once during a rebuild than patched page by page. The concept is built to that standard from the first line: labeled forms, readable contrast, keyboard access, described images and documents that have a plain-text summary beside the PDF.

This is general information, not legal advice. The City Attorney is the right person to confirm how the rule applies.

Benefits for the City

Less time on the phone, fewer trips to the counter.

Fewer routine calls

"When is court?" "Do I need a permit?" "Where do I pay my water bill?" The answers are on the first screen, so City Hall's one phone line is free for the calls that need a person.

Reports arrive complete

A short form gets finished. Each report carries a type, a location and a callback number, and routes to the right department without being forwarded.

Staff can update it

Posting a notice, a meeting packet or a new council member is a form on a phone, not a call to a vendor. Nothing here depends on one person knowing how the site works.

Nothing goes stale

Meeting dates calculate themselves. Notices expire on their own. Dated items like a quote request with a past deadline drop off automatically.

Open records in one place

Minutes, audits, the water quality report and ordinances in one searchable list, which shortens records requests and shows residents the City's work.

Portable by design

The site is plain, standard code. It can run on WordPress or any system the City prefers, and the City owns the content and the domain.

Economic development

The website is the first look a business or a visitor gets.

Centreville has real assets to show: a Main Street Alabama designation since 2023, a historic uptown, the Cahaba River with a canoe launch built to Cahaba Blueway standards, and Stewart-Wheeler Memorial Park. The concept puts them to work.

Open a business in three steps

Zoning, license, permits, in the order an owner needs them, with the online license link one tap away.

A reason to stop

The river, the park and the uptown venues presented as a visit, with the town's 1823 founding story told in five dates.

Support for grant applications

A current, accessible site with public documents in order is a visible sign of capacity when the City applies for state and federal funding.
