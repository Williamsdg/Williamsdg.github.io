      Plain answers

      The questions City Hall gets most.

      Each answer is taken from the City's own pages and written the way a clerk would say it over the counter.

      Not sure? Call City Hall at (205) 713-8886.

      How do I pay a court fine?

      Mail your payment and plea, or bring cash to the Court Clerk at City Hall, 1270 Walnut Street. Cashier's checks and money orders should be made payable to Centreville Municipal Court.

      Personal checks are not accepted.

      When and where is Municipal Court?

      The first Monday of each month at 5:00 p.m., on the 2nd floor of the Bibb County Courthouse. If the first Monday is a national holiday, call the Court Clerk for the new date.

      The Court does not mail notices, so keeping up with your date is your responsibility.

      What if I can't make my court date, or I missed it?

      Only the Judge can excuse you, and it has to happen before your date. A hospital stay or a doctor's written excuse is the usual reason.

      If you missed a date by accident, call the Court Clerk at (205) 713-8886 right away to avoid a failure-to-appear warrant.

      Do I need a building permit?

      Yes for all residential or commercial construction, major renovations, remodeling, additions and swimming pools. Contractors must be licensed.

      Properties in the historic district follow a separate procedure before a permit is issued. Call the City Clerk at (205) 713-8886 to start.

      Do I need a business license?

      Yes. Anyone who does business inside the city limits needs one. You can buy or renew it online at rds.bizlicenseonline.com without a trip to City Hall.

      What should I check before I build or put up a shed?

      The Zoning Ordinance, in effect since June 1, 2014. Check it before new construction, opening or changing a business location, placing a mobile home, shed or garage, adding a pool, or adding on to a building.

      A structure that does not comply may have to be removed at the owner's expense.

      What are the rules for pets?

      Pets must be on a leash in public and wear a current, visible inoculation tag. They may not roam freely in the City.

      An animal without a tag can be picked up by the County Animal Control Officer.

      How do I speak at a City Council meeting?

      Ask to be placed on the agenda before the meeting. The Council meets the 1st and 3rd Tuesday of every month at 5:00 p.m. at City Hall, and regular meetings are open to the public.

      Visit Centreville

      Where the mountains end and the river runs.

      The rocky shoals north of the Walnut Street bridge mark the southern end of the Appalachian chain. The Cahaba is one of the most biologically diverse rivers in North America, and it runs through the middle of town.

      Cahaba River & walking trail

      A riverside walking trail, a canoe launch and a scenic lookout built to Cahaba Blueway standards, about 200 feet north of the Bibb County Chamber of Commerce.

      Adventure Shoals

      Stewart-Wheeler Memorial Park

      Thirty-nine acres of youth athletic fields at U.S. Highway 82 and Alabama 219, opened in 2014 on land donated to the City by the Wheeler family.

      Park details

      Venues & Uptown

      The Oaks, an 1835 home that hosts weddings, the Vance Ellison House bed and breakfast, and The Studio, a nonprofit music and dance center on Walnut Street.

      1823

      Sarah Willis Chotard receives the land grant and lays out a town of 265 lots on both banks of the Cahaba.

      1829

      Centreville becomes the permanent seat of Bibb County.

      1832

      The town is incorporated on January 21.

      1859

      The county's fourth courthouse is completed, a year after a vote to move the seat fails.

      1890

      Centreville re-incorporates and elects its first mayor and council.

      History from Rhoda Coleman Ellison, Bibb County Alabama, The First Hundred Years, 1818–1918, as published by the City.
