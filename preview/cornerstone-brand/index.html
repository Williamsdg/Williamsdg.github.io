Where you are today

What your current logo does well, and where it struggles.

A refresh should keep what people already recognize and fix what makes the logo harder to use.

  • Worth keeping: the red, the diamond shapes, and "Integrity · Experience · Results." People connect those with you.
  • Hard at small sizes: the C and three diamonds blur together as a browser-tab icon or social profile picture.
  • Wide, not square: the lettering sits beside the symbol, so there is no simple square version for LinkedIn, Facebook, email signatures or a truck door.
  • Two lettering styles: a serif name over a narrow sans-serif tagline makes the logo feel busier than it needs to.
