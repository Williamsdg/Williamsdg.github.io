Good to know

Helena Hollow is a family owned and operated farm with years of history and memories waiting to be made. Here's what to know before you come.

How does the pumpkin patch work?

A tractor-pulled hayride takes you from the park side out to the pumpkin patch. Pumpkins aren't included with admission. Buy yours on site at the marked Pumpkin Purchase by the exit.

When does the last hayride leave?

The last hayride to the pumpkin patch leaves the park side no later than one hour before closing each day, so plan to arrive early.

Do little kids need a ticket?

No. Children age 2 and under don't need a ticket.

Can I bring my dog?

Helena Hollow has a no-pets policy on all property and at all venues. Please leave pets at home.

Is smoking allowed?

No. Helena Hollow is smoke-free on all property and at all venues.

Is my ticket good for a specific date?

A Friday ticket works on any one Friday in October, and a weekend ticket works on any one Saturday or Sunday. Columbus Day tickets are for Monday, October 12 only.

When does the season end?

Closing day is Saturday, October 31. Check the calendar above for that day's hours.
