Atop the mountain, since 1948.

Nestled atop the mountains of Vestavia Hills and spanning over 176 acres, Vestavia Country Club offers a rich tradition of hospitality, recreation and family focus for all ages in a luxurious and welcoming atmosphere.

A riding club that became one of the finest clubs in the Southeast.

  1. 1948Organized as the Vestavia Hills Golf and Riding Club.
  2. Summer 1950Doors open to the membership, now named Vestavia Country Club.
  3. Par 3 courseOriginally designed by George Cobb, designer of The Par 3 Course at Augusta National.
  4. 2011The Par 3 is renovated with MiniVerde Ultra Dwarf Bermuda greens.
  5. 2015The Tennis Center is named Facility of the Year.
  6. TodayA newly renovated 18-hole course and views of the valley from every amenity.
About the club

Dining, with classic southern charm.

Our talented culinary team provides our Members with some of the most delicious dishes and dining events in the area. There is something for everyone, whether they prefer unique, upscale delicacies or good old-fashioned comfort food.

Members also enjoy a formal ballroom for weddings and other large events, as well as smaller areas for more intimate get-togethers.

Dining & events

The first country club childcare program in Birmingham.

One of our most popular amenities is the Childcare Center, for ages 4 months to 10 years, in a bright and playful activity room with beautiful views of the mountains. We are proud to offer childcare 7 days a week, so Members are able to enjoy club activities.
