Two courses, one mountain.

Stunning views, dynamic features, and multiple options. A newly renovated 18-hole course and a 9-hole Par 3 originally designed by George Cobb.

The 18-hole course

This course is not only beautiful, it is fun for all skill levels. Intriguing hole layouts, several tee boxes, multiple options of play, numerous elevation changes, and picturesque views make each new round a unique experience at VCC.

A practice range sits alongside the newly renovated course, and all of it boasts beautiful views of the valley and surrounding foothills of the Appalachian Mountains.

The 9-hole Par 3

Not in the mood for a full 18? The Par 3 course provides 9 spectacular holes that feature MiniVerde Ultra Dwarf Bermuda grass greens and three separate sets of tees.

George Cobb, designer of The Par 3 Course at Augusta National, originally designed the course. Renovated in 2011, the landscape and breathtaking views create a scenic and formidable adventure.
