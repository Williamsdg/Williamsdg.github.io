Home / About the Club

One of the finest clubs in the South.

Since 1948, our club has brought together Members and their guests to enjoy social and recreational experiences.

Welcome to Vestavia Country Club, one of the finest country clubs in the South. As a rule, we strive to enhance the lives of our Members, staff, and the Vestavia Hills community as a whole.

Some of the club's highlights include several dining rooms ranging from elegant to casual atmospheres, a premiere Childcare department, 3 pools that include a relaxing infinity-edged pool with poolside bar, 15 tennis courts at our 2015 Facility of the Year-awarded Tennis Center, a state-of-the-art Fitness facility with a bonus high-intensity training (HIT) room, a 9-hole Par 3 course, a practice range, and a newly renovated 18-hole course. All of these wonderful amenities boast beautiful views of the valley and surrounding foothills of the Appalachian Mountains.

History

Vestavia Country Club, nestled in the mountains of Vestavia Hills, spans over a 176 acre area. VCC was originally organized as the Vestavia Hills Golf and Riding Club in 1948, but the name was changed to Vestavia Country Club by the time the doors opened to the membership in the summer of 1950. It has long been regarded as one of the finest clubs in the Southeast.

From its inception, Vestavia Country Club has endeavored to offer to its members a friendly, family oriented atmosphere and to promote the kind feelings and culture of its members. It has been successful in this endeavor and has been a credit to Birmingham and the South.
