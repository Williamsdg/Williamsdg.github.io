Setup · 5 Minutes

Let's Get Your Website Address

Follow these steps to reserve the official name for your site. It's quick — you just need a card at the end.

legacysportshq.com
Start Here → Open GoDaddy

Tapping the button pre-fills the name for you.

Tap the gold button above

It opens GoDaddy and automatically searches for our name. If it asks you to search yourself, type this exactly:

legacysportshq.com

Look for “It's available!”

If it shows legacysportshq.com as available, tap “Add to Cart.”

If it's taken: grab legacysportshq.net or legacysportshq.co instead, then text Dylan which one you got.

Go to your cart

Tap the cart icon (top right) or the “Continue to Cart” button. Don't worry about anything else it shows you yet.

Skip the extras (important)

GoDaddy will try to add email, website builders, and protection plans. You don't need any of them. See the red box below for what to say no to.

Create an account & pay

Enter your email and a password to make a free GoDaddy account, then enter your card to check out. That's it — the name is yours.

Pick 1 year to keep the cost low (usually ~$12–20). You can renew later.

Send Dylan the confirmation

Screenshot the “Order Confirmed” page (or forward the email) to Dylan. He'll connect it to your website — you're done!

What to skip at checkout

  • Professional Email / Microsoft 365
  • Website Builder / Hosting
  • “Full Domain Protection” & add-on security plans
  • SSL certificates
  • Any “recommended” upgrade or bundle
  • Keep ONLY: the domain legacysportshq.com (1 year)

That's All You Have To Do

Once you send Dylan the confirmation, he handles the rest — pointing the name to your new Legacy Sports Media site.
