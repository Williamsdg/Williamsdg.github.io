Frequently Asked

Before you
step aboard.

Still wondering? Text us at (850) 424-7682 — a real human replies in under fifteen minutes during harbor hours.

What happens if the weather turns?

Our captains make the call by 7 AM the day of your trip. If we cancel for weather, you get a full refund or a free reschedule — your choice, no fight. We will never sail you in unsafe conditions and we don't keep deposits on weather-canceled days.

What's your refund and cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before departure for any reason. Inside 48 hours, your deposit converts to a transferable credit good for 12 months. Same-day cancellations are case-by-case (we've never said no to a sick kid).

What should we bring?

Swimsuits, a towel each, reef-safe sunscreen (we sell some at the slip if you forget), and a polarized pair of sunglasses if you've got them. We provide water, light snacks, life vests in every size, and dry bags for phones.

Where do we park?

Free parking is available in the HarborWalk Village garage on Harbor Boulevard — we'll send you a parking validation when you book. Slip 21 is a 4-minute walk past the lighthouse, all on the boardwalk.

Are there age limits?

Kids 4+ welcome on dolphin and sunset cruises. Private charters welcome infants with a parent. Kayak and paddleboard rentals require guests to be 8+ and able to swim. Adults must accompany anyone under 16.

How early should we arrive?

15 minutes before departure is perfect. Check in at the wood-shingle kiosk at the head of Slip 21 — Megan will have your waivers ready and point you at the boat. Captains push off on the minute.

Are pets allowed?

Well-behaved leashed dogs are welcome on private charters and morning dolphin cruises (please tell us at booking so we can plan towels and water). Sunset cruises with champagne service are 21+, no dogs. Service animals always welcome on every trip.
