Our Story · 故事

From Grandmother Lin's
Kitchen to Yours

In 1996, the Lin family opened a six-table noodle shop with two woks and one rule: nothing leaves the kitchen that wouldn't be served at our own family table. Three decades later, that rule still holds.

Every morning Chef Wei breaks down whole ducks for our roasted Peking duck, hand-folds dumplings by the hundred, and reduces master stocks that have simmered, in unbroken lineage, since the day we opened.
