The Studio A team

Studio A started with a single vision: to make our clients feel special.

Annette and her staff have over twenty-five years of experience working in the beauty industry. Through those years, they have structured Studio A to be a relaxing place where everyone can come in, feel comfortable, and get exactly what they desire.

Annette Hyche

Master esthetician, makeup artist, spray tan guru, owner

In the beauty business since 2000

My journey into the beauty business began with my love of photography, art, fashion and design. I tend to be what I call “visually OCD.”

My dream was to become a photographer, an artist, or a fashion designer. To capture beauty, freeze it and admire it. I somehow ended up on the other side of the lens, always finding a little something to fine tune.

My dream, it seems, was Studio A!

Creative influences

My mother and father! I am blessed to have inherited a thimbleful of their talents!

Most important tools

My sometimes annoying visual OCD! My hands!

  • Spray tan gun
