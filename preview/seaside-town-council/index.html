Quick answers

The rules, in plain English.

The questions owners, guests and rental agencies ask most, answered from the Town Council's own published policies. Each card links to the full document.

A day at the beach

  • Who can use the pavilions? The seven private beach pavilions and the beach chair service are for Seaside owners and authorized guests only.
  • Bringing your own chairs? Set up in the "gap" area at the foot of the pavilion, where no rental chairs are placed.
  • Tents go behind the line of rental chairs and may not be larger than 10 by 10. Small children's tents up to 5 feet long may go in the gap area.
  • Take it with you. Nothing may stay on the beach between one hour after dusk and one hour after sunrise.
  • Music and other audio may not disturb the people around you.
  • No glass on the beach, stay off the dunes and leave the sea oats alone. These are Walton County rules.
  • Wristbands. Owners, renters and guests are asked to wear or show one at the pavilions. Children under eight do not need one.
  • Hosting an event at a pavilion? Owners contact the Town Manager at least 24 hours ahead.
Read the Residential Beach Policy (PDF)

Dogs and other pets

  • On a leash, always, with the owner present, anywhere in the residential neighborhoods.
  • On the beach, pets need a leash and a Walton County permit.
  • Clean up. Doggie bag stations are placed throughout the residential areas.
  • Repeat leash violations can bring a fine of up to $100 per infraction.
Read the Pet Leash Policy (PDF)

Grills

  • No portable charcoal grills in the residential areas.
  • Gas grills are allowed with 4 feet of clearance to the sides and 10 feet overhead.
  • Never leave it. A grill must be attended until it is out and cool, with a hose or extinguisher within reach.
  • Fixed grills and fireplaces need SARC review and written approval.
Read the Portable Grills policy (PDF)

Yard crews

  • Leaf blowers may run only from 9 AM to noon on Tuesdays and Fridays.
  • Keep it brief and at low throttle, and never blow debris into the street, footpaths or a neighbor's yard.
  • Owners are responsible for telling their contractors. Fines start at $50.
Read the Leaf Blower Policy (PDF)

Renting your cottage

  • Cottage list due December 31. Each rental agency, or homeowner who rents, submits a cottage list to the Town Council before the end of the prior year.
  • Parking hang tags. Two are issued each year for every active rental cottage and are specific to that cottage. Activation is $35 per tag.
  • RFID wristbands. One set per active rental cottage each year, programmed for that cottage. Activation is $15 per wristband.
  • Orders and payment run through the SymLiv system, which adds its own admin fee.
  • Replacements are $150 each, then $250. A malfunctioning wristband is replaced free when it is turned in.
  • No hang tags for private driveways, including Ruskin Park and driveways on the south side of 30A.

Homeowner & board portal

One sign-in for all nine street associations.

Behind the public site, each homeowner signs in and sees their own street association. The office and the board see the other side of the same system. Payments stay in AppFolio, one tap away.

  • STC and street association documents, always the current version
  • Notices that reach every owner, including the ones who live out of state
  • Architectural review and maintenance requests with a status the owner can see
  • Hang tags and wristbands for rental guests, tracked by cottage
  • For the office: the owner roster and every open request in one place
