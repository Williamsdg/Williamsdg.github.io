Homeowner & board portal

One sign-in for all nine street associations.

Behind the public site, each homeowner signs in and sees their own street association. The office and the board see the other side of the same system. Payments stay in AppFolio, one tap away.

  • STC and street association documents, always the current version
  • Notices that reach every owner, including the ones who live out of state
  • Architectural review and maintenance requests with a status the owner can see
  • Hang tags and wristbands for rental guests, tracked by home
  • For the office: the owner roster and every open request in one place
