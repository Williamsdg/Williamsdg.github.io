About Senn Medicine

Eric R. Senn, DO

Born and raised in Opelika, Alabama. Undergraduate from Samford University; medical degree from Nova Southeastern University.

Over 20 years of Emergency Medicine experience across Florida, Georgia and Alabama, including 16 years with East Alabama Health. Chief Resident in Columbus, Georgia. Medical Director of the Paramedic/EMT program at Southern Union State Community College. Teaches medical students at VCOM-Auburn.

Outside of medicine, he is a former artist who enjoys time with his wife and four children, Auburn and LSA athletics, and contributing to collaborative wellness initiatives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Interested in Learning More?

Do you accept insurance?

Senn Medicine does not bill insurance for any care services. Members pay a flat monthly fee.

Can I still use my insurance?

Yes. Insurance is encouraged to be used for services outside the practice such as specialist visits, imaging, or hospital care.

Do you accept Medicare?

No. Medicare beneficiaries are not eligible for membership.

Can I cancel my membership?

Membership may be discontinued at any time with appropriate notice.

Will Dr. Senn be my doctor in the ER?

Membership does not cover emergency care or hospital services, but members may contact Dr. Senn for guidance when urgent concerns arise. When available, Dr. Senn can help determine the appropriate next steps and provide context to ensure informed decisions. True medical emergencies should always be directed to 911.

What happens next?

Upon membership, you will receive a welcome email within 24 hours. You'll be given direct access to Dr. Senn by phone, text, and secure email. We'll schedule your first visit (virtual or in-person). You can reach out anytime for questions, concerns, or urgent issues.
