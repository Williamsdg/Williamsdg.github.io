# Senn Medicine — Concept 2: what we need from you

Prepared by Williams Digital. This is a checklist, not homework — send what's
easy first and we'll keep building around it.

Concept 2 is deliberately built with **visible labeled placeholders** so you can
see exactly where your words and photos go. Every grey box on the page is a slot
waiting for something specific.

---

## 1. Photography (the biggest lift, and the biggest payoff)

Concept 2 is a photography-led design. Right now it's beautiful and empty. With
real images it becomes a genuinely premium brand.

The direction we'd push for is **business/lifestyle editorial**, not clinical
stock. Specifically **not** a posed shot with a stethoscope.

What the page has slots for:

| # | Slot | What works best |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero — lifestyle portrait** | You, relaxed, environmental. The single most important image on the page. |
| 2 | **Meet Your Doctor — editorial portrait** | Vertical, in the office or another good-looking space. |
| 3 | **Full-width lifestyle break** | Wide, atmospheric — Auburn/Opelika, the practice, or a real patient interaction. |
| 4 | **Auburn students** | Campus or student lifestyle. Tasteful — not a football graphic. |
| 5 | **Local / Auburn section** | The practice exterior/interior, or Auburn scenery. |

Other shots worth having if you do a session: you walking through Auburn, sitting
in a good-looking office, close-up detail shots, and any patient interaction you
have permission to photograph.

**If you'd rather not commission a shoot yet**, tell us — we can suggest a local
photographer, or adapt the layout to lean harder on typography.

## 4. Patient testimonials

The "Kind words from our patients" section is **built and styled** with three
empty slots. It is intentionally unpublished.

We will not write these. Invented patient testimonials for a medical practice are
an ethical and regulatory problem, not just a taste one. Send 3–5 real quotes
from patients who have given you written permission, and we'll drop them in.
