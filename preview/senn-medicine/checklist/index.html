Photography

The biggest lift — and the biggest payoff.

The design is photography-led. Right now it's elegant but empty; with real images it becomes a genuinely premium brand.

The direction we'd suggest is business / lifestyle editorial — relaxed, environmental, natural light. Deliberately not a posed clinical shot with a stethoscope.

If a photo session isn't something you want to take on right now, just say so — we can recommend a local photographer, or shift the layout to lean harder on typography instead.

Details we didn't want to guess

These are facts about your practice, so we've left them to you rather than assume.

A few specifics so parents and students know where they stand:

  • Does the $125 rate require proof of Auburn enrollment, or a student ID?
  • Are graduate and part-time students included?
  • What happens to the rate after graduation?
  • Can a parent purchase the membership on a student's behalf?

We added “Can I use my HSA or FSA?” to the FAQ because people reliably ask it. It isn't answered on your current site, and it's the kind of question where a wrong answer could cause a real problem for a patient — so we've left it blank for you.

Your current site lists the address and social links, but no hours, phone number, or email address. The new page has a proper place for all three — send whichever you'd like published.

Patient testimonials

06

Two things for your approval

Small decisions that are genuinely yours to make.

A new section we wrote — “Not a waiting room. A relationship.”

We added a short section between your biography and the pricing that describes what membership actually feels like. Each line is drawn from a promise already on your site, so nothing overstates what you offer:

Text your doctor. Direct communication by phone, text, or telemedicine.

Be seen when you need to be. Same-day or next-day appointments whenever possible.

Unhurried time together. A comprehensive, unhurried appointment reviewing your history, concerns, goals.

We could say something sharper here — naming how long a typical visit really runs, for instance. We didn't write that, because it's a claim about how you practice and it should be in your words, not ours. If you're comfortable with a stronger version, send the wording.
