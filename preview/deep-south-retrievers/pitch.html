The Game-Changer

Weekly owner updates, sent automatically.

The single biggest reason owners leave a trainer is silence. Every Friday at 4pm, the console takes the week's session notes, milestone progress, and the best 2–3 photos or videos and packages them into a polished update — text + email — for every owner in the program.
