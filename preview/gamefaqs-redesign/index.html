Limgrave — Early Game

From the Stranded Graveyard, follow the path up to the First Step site of grace. Your first real objective is the Gatefront Ruins, where you'll pick up the Map: Limgrave, West and meet Melina.

◆ Tip: Grab Torrent before fighting the Tree Sentinel. You do not need to beat him now — ride past and come back at level 25+.

Recommended early route to snowball your runes:

  1. Gatefront Ruins ........... Map fragment + Whetstone
  2. Agheel Lake (dragon) ...... ~12,000 runes, optional
  3. Murkwater Cave ............ Bloody Finger / Reduvia
  4. Stormhill Shack ........... Roundtable Hold access

Why redesign GameFAQs?

GameFAQs is one of the most useful archives ever built — millions of player-written guides going back to the PlayStation 1 era. The knowledge is irreplaceable. The interface stopped evolving a long time ago.

This concept keeps everything that makes it sacred — the contributor culture, the plaintext FAQs, the boards, Game of the Day — and wraps it in something fast, mobile-first, and genuinely pleasant to read.

  • ✓Preserve the archive. Every legacy guide still works — just rendered properly.
  • ✓Respect the reader. No layout shift, no pop-overs on the strategy you need.
  • ✓Honor the community. Boards, Answers, and contributor ranks stay front and center.
  • ✓Mobile-first. Most players read guides on a phone next to a console now.
