Our Story

A hybrid café-bar, the European way

Inspired by the European café tradition, Margaux blends specialty coffee, handcrafted cocktails, and warm hospitality into a single, light-filled room.

It's the kind of place where a morning espresso and an evening negroni belong to the same address. Sunlight floods the room by day; by night the same space softens into a candle-lit lounge.

East Liberty's newest café and bar — built for connection, lingering conversations, and the in-between hours.
