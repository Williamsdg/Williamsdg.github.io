    How it works

    Book. Sign.
    Suit up. Smash.

    Your time block covers check-in, rules, gear and smashing, so the earlier you arrive, the more you get to break.

    1. Book online

      Reserve your slot any day between 12 PM and 10 PM. Staff aren't on site every hour, so booking ahead holds your spot.

    2. Sign the waiver

      Everyone signs, spectators included. Do it on your phone before you leave home.

    3. Suit up

      We provide gloves, face and eye protection and a body suit, sized S to 5XL and cleaned between every use.

      Pair your phone, play your own music, film it all. Then walk out and leave the mess to us.

    Donate breakables

    Your junk is
    our inventory

    We never buy new items to smash. Drop yours under the Donations arrow by the front door any time, or call for a pickup. Our trucks have lift gates for the big stuff.

    Bars, restaurants and thrift stores: we'll leave a labeled bin and swap it as often as you need. After the smashing, everything is sorted by material and recycled.

    We'll take it

    Flat screen TVs and monitors, computers, printers, scanners, cameras, old toys and electronics, VHS tapes, CDs and DVDs, glasses, bottles, dishware, furniture, speakers, pictures, old trophies, file cabinets, tools and general home decor.

    We can't take it

    CRT TVs, microwaves, batteries, light bulbs, smoke alarms, food, anything with liquid, anything pressurized or flammable, compressors and aerosol cans, items with mold or mildew, chemical containers, medical supplies, guns or ammunition.

    FAQ

    Good questions

    Type a word like "age", "shoes" or "parking" to jump straight to the answer.

    What is a rage room?

    A reinforced room built for smashing things. You suit up in safety gear, pick from the tools we provide, and break an array of items. It relieves stress, it's a lot of fun, and you don't clean up afterward.

    What is the age limit for each activity?

    Rage rooms and car smashing are 13 and up with a parent or guardian present. Axe throwing is 10 and up. The splatter room is all ages. A parent or guardian must sign the waiver and accompany any minor.

    How much time do I get in the rage room?

    Groups of 1 to 4 get a 30 minute block and groups of 5 or 6 get a 60 minute block. The block includes check-in, rules, gear and smashing, so arrive early to get the most out of it.

    How many people can go at once?

    Rage rooms hold 6 per room and we have two, so 12 at a time. The splatter room holds 12. Axe throwing takes 20 as standard, 5 per target, and we can accommodate more if you call. For parties and large gatherings, call us about event packages.

    What should I wear and bring?

    A photo ID and closed-toe shoes are required. Wear comfortable clothing you can move in that won't interfere with the safety gear.

    Do I have to wear safety gear?

    Yes. Nobody participates without it under any circumstances. Our gear generally fits S to 5XL. If you think you fall outside that range, come by before your booking to check the fit or bring your own for staff to approve.

    What do we use to break stuff?

    Metal bats, hammers, mallets and hand sledges. Nothing with a sharp edge.

    Can we bring our own stuff to break?

    Yes. BYOB means bring your own breakables. Items have to follow our policy on what's allowed, and staff will inspect them and can refuse anything unsafe.

    Where do I park?

    There's no parking lot, but 7th Avenue has plenty of free street parking on both sides of the road right outside the building.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Walk-ins and same day bookings are welcome when we have availability. Look for the "walk-ins welcome" sign on the sidewalk, and call ahead to confirm.

    What are your rules on alcohol?

    Alcohol is not allowed on the property and nobody may participate under the influence. A safe rule to remember: if you can drive, you can smash.

    Can we have spectators at axe throwing?

    Yes. Spectators of all ages are welcome to hang out and watch. They need to fill out a waiver and can't throw.

    Can we bring food?

    You're welcome to bring your own food to axe throwing. Just let our staff know ahead of time.

    Is there somewhere to keep my stuff?

    Yes. We have camera-monitored lockers for your group. We aren't responsible for lost or stolen items.

    Can we listen to music and take photos?

    Yes to both. Every activity lets you pair your phone and play your own music, and we want you to show your friends how much fun you're having.

    Do you recycle?

    Yes. We only use end of life items, and once they've been smashed the parts are separated by type and taken to the proper centers for disposal and reuse.

    I'm having trouble booking online. Can I book by phone?

    Yes. We can book and take payment over the phone at (205) 701-0001. We encourage calling for large events, custom or combo bookings.

    What if I want to keep an item instead of breaking it?

    You can purchase it up front and swap it for another breakable. The same goes for anything in our stock room.
