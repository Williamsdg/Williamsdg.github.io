Why Sunridge

The summer your kid will never stop talking about.

Forty-three summers of doing one thing well: helping kids try things they didn't think they could, in a place where it's okay to be exactly who you are.

Safety First, Always

Every counselor is lifeguard-trained, background-checked, and CPR/first-aid certified. On-site nurse and infirmary staffed 24/7 during sessions.

Real Counselors

Average counselor age is 22. 68% of our staff came up as Sunridge campers themselves. They know the songs, the traditions, and your kid's first name by Day 2.

A Real Break

Phone-free cabins. Daily photo updates for parents through our app. Your camper unplugs from screens; you stay connected to their week.

What We Do

Eighteen activities. Pick a few. Try them all.

Campers rotate through morning skill blocks and afternoon free-choice. By the end of the week, most have tried something brand new — and a lot of them keep coming back to it.

🏊

Lake & Swimming

Spring-fed swim lake with sectioned shallow areas, a floating dock, and the world's most beloved zipline-into-the-water. Daily swim instruction at every level.

4 lifeguards on duty Red Cross levels 1-6
🏹

Archery & Riflery

USA Archery and NRA-certified ranges. Beginners welcome. Earn arrow patches and target medals throughout the session.

Ages 8+ Skill progression patches
🧗

High Ropes & Climbing

40-foot climbing tower, two zip lines, and a multi-element ropes course. ACCT-inspected annually. Belay certified staff for every climb.

12 elements Ages 9+
🎨

Arts & Crafts Barn

Pottery wheels, screen printing, friendship bracelets, tie-dye, painting, and the legendary Wednesday Wood Shop where campers build whatever they can dream up.

14 mediums All abilities

Drama & Music

Each session ends with a camper-led variety show on the open-air stage. Drama, sketch comedy, songwriting, and our acoustic guitar circle around the campfire.

No experience needed Friday showcase

Horseback Riding

Twelve gentle, well-loved school horses. Lessons in our covered ring plus trail rides through the back pastures for intermediate riders.
