Where they meet

Selling a house that belonged to someone who has died?

It is the most common reason a sale stalls. Title has to pass through the estate before it can pass to a buyer, and the two jobs go faster when one office does both.

Ask about an estate property

  1. First

    Find out who holds title

    How the deed was worded decides whether probate is needed at all.

  2. Then

    Open the estate if required

    The probate court gives a personal representative the authority to sign.

  3. Finally

    Close the sale

    With authority in place, the sale closes like any other.
