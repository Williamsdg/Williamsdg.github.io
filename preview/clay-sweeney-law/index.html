What a closing attorney does

Four jobs, done before you sit down.

Most of a closing happens out of sight. By the time you are handed a pen, this work is finished.

Examine the title

The public record is searched for liens, judgments, unpaid taxes and anything else that would follow the property to a new owner.

Prepare the documents

The deed, the settlement figures and the lender's package are drawn up and checked against the contract.

Run the closing

Everyone signs, each document is explained in plain English, and funds are collected and paid out.

Record and insure

The deed and mortgage are recorded with the county probate office and the title policies are issued.
