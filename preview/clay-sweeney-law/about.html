Clayton T. Sweeney, Attorney at Law

Close to four decades at the closing table.

Clay Sweeney graduated from Cumberland School of Law at Samford University in 1987 and was admitted to the Alabama State Bar the same year. He established his own practice in Birmingham in 1992.

Residential real estate is the center of that practice: purchases, sales and refinances, closed at his office on Highway 280. He also handles business, probate, zoning and commercial transaction matters.
