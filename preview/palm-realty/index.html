Home Watch

Your place, watched like it's ours.

Own from Birmingham, Nashville, Atlanta or farther away? Home Watch means someone local walks your property on a schedule, checks again after every storm, and sends you a report with photos you can actually read.

  • Scheduled walk-throughs on a schedule you choose
  • After-storm checks for wind, water and power
  • HVAC, humidity, leaks, doors and windows on every visit
  • A dated photo report on your phone after each visit
  • Trusted local vendors when something needs fixing

Know the coast

Every beach town is different.

Rental rules, HOA culture, flood zones and the drive to the sand all change from one town to the next. Here's how we'd explain each one.

    Father & son

    Two agents.
    One family business.

    In 2020 Casey and Bill packed up in Birmingham and made a leap of faith to the coast. Casey had owned a landscaping company. Bill had spent decades in another career and got his license at 52. Clients like to joke that they get two agents for the price of one. Each deal really does get both of them: one leads the conversation, the other refines the strategy, and both see it to the closing table.
