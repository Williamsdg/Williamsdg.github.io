Our story

Birmingham’s neighborhood jeweler.

Cummings Jewelry Design has been providing beautiful jewelry to Birmingham since 1978. We’re family-owned, and we’re proud to have been your jewelers for so many years.

We’ve built a reputation as a place you can depend on for trustworthy, individualized service — a jewelry store where you can feel comfortable asking questions and taking the time you need.
