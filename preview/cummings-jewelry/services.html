01 · Repair & restoration

Heirlooms, rebuilt.

02 · Appraisals

Know what you have.

Inherited jewelry, a gift, a piece you’re insuring — a careful appraisal tells you exactly what it is and what it’s worth.

Our written appraisals list stone measurements, color grading and carat weights. You receive two copies: one for your records and one to send to your insurance agent. Every piece you purchase from us comes with one.

Appraised years ago? Precious metal prices have climbed, and older appraisals can leave jewelry underinsured. We recommend a reappraisal every few years.

03 · Sell your gold

From the drawer to a fair offer.

Broken chains, single earrings, cufflinks you haven’t worn in years, or a ring from a relationship that’s over. Bring them to the neighborhood jeweler you already trust.

We’ll assess how much gold you have — its karat purity and weight, against the current market price — and make you an offer. You decide whether to sell or keep it.

Please bring a photo ID. Regulations require us to document every purchase, and we pay by check rather than cash.
