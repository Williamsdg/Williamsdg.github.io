How it works

A conversation first, then a drawing.

  1. We meet

    Come in and tell us what you’re imagining — or bring a photo. We’ll answer questions and offer our own creative suggestions.

  2. We design

    Start from scratch with stones of your choice, or build from jewelry you already own. We refine the design with you until it’s right.

  3. We make it

    From model to metal to the final setting, your piece is made for you — one of one.
