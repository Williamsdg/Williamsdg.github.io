One Roof, Four Pillars

A different room for every part of the day.

Iron Oak isn't a coffee shop. It isn't a restaurant. It isn't a brewery. It's the place where all three live together — built for the regular who comes in for an oat latte at 7am and walks back through the door at 8pm for a pint and a set from a local band.

Our Story

Built by neighbors. For neighbors.

Iron Oak started in 2019 as a five-table coffee bar inside the front of an old hardware store. The brewery came next, then the kitchen, then a stage we kept saying we'd "eventually build." Six years and a lot of saturday mornings later, this is what it became.

We're independent, family-owned, and run by people who actually live in the neighborhood. Every cup, plate, and pour is a small thank-you to the folks who made this place what it is.

"We didn't set out to build a coffee shop, a restaurant, or a brewery. We set out to build a room where the people we love already wanted to be." — Eli Rourke, Co-founder
