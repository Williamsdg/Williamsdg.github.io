Why Choose Us

Why Choose North Carolina Frenchies?

We are committed to upholding the highest standards of the French Bulldog breed. Our ethical breeding program goes well beyond the AKC requirements to produce healthy, beautiful pups who will live long, fulfilling lives.

  • ✓ Intentional pairings selected for lineage, structure, temperament & genetic compatibility
  • ✓ Among less than 1% of breeders implementing both OFA orthopedic and full genetic testing
  • ✓ Low-volume program with puppies hand-raised in our home
  • ✓ 30 days medical coverage and a 1-year health guarantee
  • ✓ Ongoing support for the life of your Frenchie
  • ✓ AKC registered & FBDFA Verified Breeder
