    French West Indies-inspired pool on the west side of town, south of 30A, with a flowing fountain and shaded veranda.

    Sky Pool

    Daily 6 am to 10 pm

    Year-round swimming under a motorized roof that opens or closes with the weather.

    Owners Club and Pavilion

    For owners: a regulation bocce court, a large heated pool with quiet time days, private cabana loungers and a spacious upper veranda.

    Racquet Club

    Eight green clay (Har-Tru) courts, instruction, a viewing deck, restrooms and showers, and a fully stocked Pro Shop.

    Fitness Center

    9 East Water Street, a short distance from the Sky Pool. Recently renovated, with strength and cardio equipment.

    Fitness Trail

    A 2.3-mile trail and walking tour on sand paths and boardwalks, with four fitness stations along the way.

    Town Hall

    White stucco walls and a Dutch West Indies parapet roof. A gathering place for weddings, special events and the community.

    Post Office

    A fully functioning post office. Hours of operation vary by season.

    Butterfly Garden

    In the northwest corner of town at West Kingston Road, planted to showcase the native landscape.

    Greens and parks

    East and West Long Green, North and South Barrett Square, St. Augustine Park, Playground Park and the pocket parks between them.

    The beach

    A quarter mile of white sand. Chairs, umbrellas, watercraft rentals and bonfires through RBPOA Beach Service.

    Our history

    It began with a carriage house.

    A walkable town on 107 acres along Scenic Highway 30-A, planned by Andres Duany and Elizabeth Plater-Zyberk of DPZ on the principles of New Urbanism.

    • 1995The Rosemary Beach Land Company and President Patrick Bienvenue set out the vision. The first design meeting starts from a small prototype: a carriage house.
    • 1997The plan expands north of 30-A, and the East and West Long Green Parks are created. The first two family residences are completed.
    • 1999The Town Hall is completed at the center of the community, with its white stucco and Dutch West Indies parapet roof.
    • TodayTogether with its owners, the POA maintains the parameters set by the original founders, more than 25 years on.
