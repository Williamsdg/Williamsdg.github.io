Our Story

Eighty-two years. Four generations. One family.

Bill Mercier planted the first orchard in 1943 with apple trees brought from Quebec. His son Tim and grandson Tim Jr. expanded into peaches, hard cider, and the bakery you can smell from the parking lot.

Today, the fourth generation of Merciers — and a team of 120 year-round employees — keeps the trees pruned, the donuts hot, and the doors open seven days a week. Veteran-owned. Family-run.

Four Generations

Eighty-two years on this hillside.

From a hundred apple trees to a 300-acre destination — every milestone shaped by family, season, and stubborn North Georgia weather.

1943
First Generation · Bill & Adele Mercier

The first hundred trees

Bill brings apple cuttings down from his family farm in Quebec and plants the first orchard on a sloping hillside outside Blue Ridge. Adele runs the roadside stand from a folding table and a coffee can.

1972
Second Generation · Tim Sr. takes over

From farm stand to market

Tim Sr. expands to peaches and blueberries, builds the first packing house, and opens the original 800-square-foot market with his mother's apple-butter recipe as the bestseller — a recipe still on shelves today.

1998
Third Generation · Tim Jr. & Trish

Bakery, donuts, and the 90 acres

Tim Jr. takes the reins, adds 90 acres, opens the bakery, and starts hand-rolling cider donuts every twenty minutes. Customer count crosses 100,000 a year for the first time.

2012
Hard cider & winery

Estate-pressed, federally licensed

The cidery and tasting room open with three flagship hard ciders. Within five years, distribution covers four states. Today, the tasting room serves 60,000 flights a year.

2026
Fourth Generation · The next chapter

300 acres and growing

The fourth generation joins the farm full-time. New plantings of late-season varieties, expansion of school programs to 40,000 students a year, and a renewed focus on regenerative practices. Veteran-owned. Family-run.

Before You Come

Frequently asked questions.

If you don't find your answer, give us a call at (706) 632-3411 or text the team.

Do I need a reservation to u-pick?
During peak season (mid-Sept through October), reservations are strongly recommended — especially weekends, which sell out 5–7 days in advance. We do hold a small number of walk-up slots, but you may wait at the gate. Reserve online to skip the line and pick up your bag at the express counter.
Can I bring my dog?
Leashed dogs are welcome on the orchard paths and outdoor areas. They are not permitted inside the bakery, market, or cidery for health-code reasons. Water bowls and shaded benches are scattered throughout the grounds.
What if it rains?
U-Pick continues in light rain — apples taste better wet, anyway. In heavy rain or lightning, we close the orchard temporarily but keep the market, bakery, and cidery open. Reservations affected by closures get a free reschedule or full refund — we'll text you within 30 minutes.
Is the property accessible?
The market, bakery, cidery, and tasting room are fully wheelchair accessible. The orchard's main loop is gravel and stroller/wheelchair friendly. The tractor tours can accommodate wheelchairs with advance notice — call us 24 hours ahead and we'll have the lift-equipped trailer ready.
How long does u-pick take?
Most families spend about 90 minutes to 2 hours from gate-in to gate-out — that includes browsing the market and grabbing donuts. The actual picking part is 30–45 minutes. Reservation slots are 3 hours wide, so you have plenty of time.
Can I ship products home?
Yes — we ship bakery, jams, butters, and cider nationwide. Orders placed by 2pm Wednesday ship that week. Cider ships only to states where direct-to-consumer is legal (currently 32 states + DC). Members of the Cider Club get free shipping on orders over $50.
What's your refund policy?
U-Pick reservations: free reschedule up to 24 hours before, or full refund if we close due to weather. Tour bookings: 7-day full refund window, after that we'll reschedule for any reason within 12 months. Online shop: 30-day return on unopened products.
