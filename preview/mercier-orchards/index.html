Our Story

Eighty-two years. Four generations. One family.

Bill Mercier planted the first orchard in 1943 with apple trees brought from Quebec. His son Tim and grandson Tim Jr. expanded into peaches, hard cider, and the bakery you can smell from the parking lot.

Today, the fourth generation of Merciers — and a team of 120 year-round employees — keeps the trees pruned, the donuts hot, and the doors open seven days a week. Veteran-owned. Family-run.
