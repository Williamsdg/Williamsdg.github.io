3.Decisions only you can make

Before launch

No files needed — just your answer.

Partnership categories for each athlete

Every athlete profile has a "Partnership categories" panel — the kinds of brands that athlete would genuinely work with. I've deliberately left these blank rather than guessing on an athlete's behalf. Tell me what each athlete is open to, or say the word and I'll remove the panel entirely.

Whether to publish follower counts

Your signing graphics list audience figures — 142K for Ginni, 8,000 for Betty, and so on — and I've carried those onto the profiles because brands look for them. They do go stale, though. Happy to keep them, drop them, or show them only where they're strong.

Consent for any athlete under 18

The athlete application form collects parent and guardian details and asks for written consent. For athletes already on the roster, confirm you hold permission to publish their name, image and statistics — particularly for anyone who is a minor.

This protects both of us, and it's in the agreement.

Review the Privacy Policy and Terms

I've drafted both pages so the site launches complete, but they're a starting point rather than legal advice. Given how much of your business rests on doing things by the book, these are worth your own read — especially the sections on minors and athlete data.
