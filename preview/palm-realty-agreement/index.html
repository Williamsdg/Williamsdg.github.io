3.MLS & IDX Property Search

Live listing search requires an IDX agreement between Palm Realty's Broker, the MLS and an IDX provider. Palm Realty is responsible for its MLS membership, the IDX agreement and the provider account, and pays the provider and any MLS fees directly. Williams Digital adds no markup and will help Palm Realty choose a provider.

Williams Digital will connect one IDX provider to the website and style it to match. Search features such as saved homes and listing alerts depend on what the chosen provider offers.

If MLS or provider approval is not complete when the rest of the website is ready, the website launches with featured listings entered by hand, and Williams Digital will connect the IDX search at no additional charge once approval is in place, provided that happens within ninety (90) days of launch.

Palm Realty is responsible for the accuracy of listing information and for meeting MLS display rules, real estate licensing disclosures and fair housing requirements. Williams Digital will display the attribution and disclaimers that Palm Realty or its provider supplies.

5.Not Included

  • The Home Watch owner portal and agent tools. See the Future Phase section.
  • IDX provider and MLS fees. These are paid by Palm Realty directly to the provider and the MLS.
  • Photography, copywriting or logo design. The site uses Palm Realty's existing logo and the photos and wording Palm Realty supplies.
  • Domain registration and business email. Palm Realty registers and owns its domain; Williams Digital connects the website to it.
  • Paid advertising or an ongoing search-marketing retainer.
  • A native app in the App Store or Google Play.

6.Hosting & Third-Party Services

Palm Realty registers and owns its domain name, and Williams Digital will connect the new website to it at launch. Where the website is hosted, and any hosting cost, will be confirmed in writing before launch; no hosting fee is included in or added by this agreement.

Any outside service the website depends on, including the IDX provider, is opened in Palm Realty's name so that Palm Realty owns the account. Each provider bills Palm Realty directly. Providers may change their pricing; that is outside Williams Digital's control.

Ongoing maintenance after the support period in this agreement is not included and can be quoted separately.

9.Customer Data

The website's forms collect names, phone numbers, email addresses and property addresses from Palm Realty's clients and prospective agents.

Palm Realty owns and controls all data collected through the website. Williams Digital provides and maintains the mechanism only, and will not use, sell, share, or train any system on that data.

Williams Digital will delete any individual record, or all stored records, within ten (10) business days of a written request, and will provide a complete export of the data at any time on request.

10.Ownership & Rights

On receipt of final payment, Palm Realty owns the finished website: all page files, styling, the investor calculator and any custom graphics produced for this project.

Palm Realty owns and retains its domain name, its logo, its photos, its client reviews and all content it publishes. Williams Digital claims no interest in any of them.

Williams Digital retains ownership of any underlying reusable code, libraries or components not created specifically for Palm Realty, and retains the right to display the finished work in its portfolio and marketing. Client data is never shown.

11.Revisions & Support

The fee includes two rounds of revisions during the build phase. A round is a consolidated set of feedback delivered at one time.

Structural changes requested after approval (additional pages, new functionality, or reworking an approved section's structure) are out of scope and billed under the Fee & Payment Terms section.

Williams Digital will correct any defect in delivered work at no charge for ninety (90) days following launch.

13.Investor Calculator

The investor calculator produces illustrations from numbers a visitor enters and starting values that Palm Realty supplies. It is not financial, tax or investment advice, and the website will say so. Palm Realty is responsible for the starting values and for any statements about rental income made on the website.

14.Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential any non-public business information disclosed by the other during this engagement, including the pricing in this agreement, and will not disclose it to third parties except as required by law.

Any credentials shared for launch, such as domain or account access, will be used only for that purpose, and Palm Realty may revoke them at any time.

15.Cancellation

Either party may cancel this agreement with written notice.

If Palm Realty cancels after work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable and Palm Realty is invoiced for any approved out-of-scope work completed to date. All work completed to the point of cancellation is delivered to Palm Realty.

If Williams Digital cancels, Palm Realty receives all work completed to date and a pro-rata refund of any amount paid for work not performed.

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Alabama. Any dispute arising under it will be brought in the state or federal courts located in Jefferson County, Alabama. The parties agree to attempt good-faith resolution directly before pursuing any formal proceeding.

This agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the website and supersedes any prior discussions, proposals or representations. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
