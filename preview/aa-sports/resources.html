Compliance

Where A&A
stands on record.

LicenceAlabama Licensed Athlete Agent — UN-1670, valid through February 2028.
RegistrationRegistered under the Alabama Uniform Athlete Agents Act (§ 8-26B) with the Secretary of State.
72-hour ruleAn athletic director must be notified within 72 hours of a student athlete signing.
14-day cancellationA student athlete may void a representation contract within 14 days of signing.
Fee capsAgent fees are capped at 3% in the NFL and 4% in the NBA.
NILStudent athletes may earn from name, image and likeness under NCAA policy and Alabama law.
