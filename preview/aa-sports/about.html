Founder

Alander Pulliam

A&A was built on a straightforward idea: an athlete should always know what is being negotiated on their behalf, and why. That means transparency about terms, accessibility when it matters, and compliance treated as part of the service rather than paperwork filed after the fact.

Founded in 2010 and licensed in Alabama, A&A represents athletes across professional and college sport from Birmingham.

How we work

What athlete-first
actually means.

Transparent

Terms explained in plain language before signing, not after. An athlete should be able to repeat their own contract back in their own words.

Accessible

A named agent who answers. Representation is a relationship, not a ticketing queue.

Compliant

Licensing, disclosure and eligibility handled properly — because the cost of getting it wrong lands on the athlete, not the agency.

Family-first

The people around an athlete are part of the decision. A&A treats them that way.
