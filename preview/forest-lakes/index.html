Santa Rosa Beach, Florida

Forest Lakes is home.

There's something about the way the roads seem as if they've always been there, part of nature. It's something in the way the lakes are still and smooth like glass at sunset. It's in the fresh gulf air, and the uninhibited laughter of children echoing in the towering pines.

I've always been waiting to find this place. Or did it find me?

Community amenities

Around the lake, under the pines

Boardwalks and gazebo

Boardwalks and gazebo allow for a stroll over the lake.

Pool

Our pool is located in the center of the community; the pool area also includes a kiddie pool, bathrooms and outdoor showers.

Pavilion

For resident and guest use: fireplace, gathering area, grill, foosball and table tennis.

Walking trails

Walking trails are around the lake for viewing of wildlife.

Playground

Children will enjoy playing on our playground; a picnic table is also available.

Green space

Our green space is conveniently located by the lake.

Entrance gate

Installed in 2017. For access devices or stickers, contact Barbara Rouse: brouse@cmacommunities.com
