About the store

It started with a handful of artisans.

Our store began as a marketplace in 2017 with a handful of artisans and vendors. Now The Mercantile has grown to have over 50 vendors.

Many of our vendors are artists who create original, handmade and one of a kind pieces, as well as gifts on the go for busy lifestyles. And because we want to make it easy for you to create your perfect space, we have designers available to help bring it all to life.

  • Antique and new furniture
  • Floral arrangements
  • Lamps and home decor
  • Pottery
  • Original art
  • Handmade jewelry
  • Ladies and juvenile clothing
  • Candles and fragrance
