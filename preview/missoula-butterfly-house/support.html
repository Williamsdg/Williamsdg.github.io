Join the Flight Crew

Aside from staff salaries, keeping the butterfly house filled with beautiful butterflies from around the world is our second largest annual expense.

Tax-deductible monthly gifts from the Flight Crew go directly to buying butterflies. One-time gifts support education programs, the exhibits, or your favorite Bug Ambassador.

By mail: checks payable to Missoula Butterfly House and Insectarium, 1075 South Ave W, Ste 2, Missoula, MT 59801.

Volunteer

UM students, retirees, working parents: no PhD in entomology needed, just a passion for what the Butterfly House does and a willingness to learn.

The biggest need is people on the museum floor: welcoming visitors to the Butterfly House, wrangling butterflies so they stay safely inside, and helping at the Animal Encounter Area.

Floor shifts: Tuesday to Saturday 10 to 12:30, 12:30 to 3 and 3 to 5. Sunday noon to 2 and 2 to 4.

Apply to volunteer
  • Museum floor. Greet visitors, wrangle butterflies, interpret the living exhibits.
  • Education. Help with field trips, camps and community appearances. Play with kids and bugs.
  • Animal care. For people with a biology, veterinary or animal care background. Training plus one 4 to 5 hour shift a week for three months.
  • Behind the scenes. Social media, envelope stuffing, woodworking: special projects come up often.
