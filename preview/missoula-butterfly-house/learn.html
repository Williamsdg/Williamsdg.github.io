Find your grade's program

9 programs

Kindergarten

Creative Creatures

Students practice observation on bugs from around the world that are experts at camouflage, then design a brand-new insect species using leaves and natural materials from their own schoolyard.

Standard: K-LS1-1

1st grade

Bug Biomimicry

Students discover inventions engineers created by studying arthropods, then work in small groups to design and test their own bug-inspired prototype.

Standard: 1-LS1-1

2nd grade

Powerful Pollinators

Second graders meet live Bug Ambassadors and investigate the body structures pollinators use to move pollen, practicing how to observe, ask questions and argue from evidence.

Standard: 2-LS2-2

3rd grade

Beetlemania

Beetle biodiversity, habitat adaptations and life cycles, with living beetle Bug Ambassadors and new beetles invented from students' imaginations.

Standard: 3-LS1-1, 3-LS4-3

3rd grade

Amazing Aquatic Arthropods

Students pour through bins of river water to sort the nymphs of caddisflies, stoneflies and mayflies. Early fall and late spring only, river and weather permitting.

Standard: 3-LS1-1

4th grade

Attention on Antennae

An experiment with habitat choice chambers to find out what roly polys prefer, with a strong focus on hypotheses and good experiment design.

Standard: 4-LS1-2

5th grade

Food Web Invaders

Case studies of food webs changed by invasive arthropods: what happened, and what might restore the balance.

Standard: 5-LS2-1

All grades

Survival Skills

An active program on the adaptations arthropods use to survive, with Bug Ambassadors, movement games and a craft. Perfect for high-energy groups and wide age ranges.

All grades

Super Spiders

Easily one of the most popular: spider anatomy, feeding and defense, a wide range of spiders to meet, and a trivia game that busts spider myths.
