Know before you go

The Butterfly House is a USDA APHIS regulated facility. Its permit to keep exotic butterflies and arthropods is why some of these rules exist.

What can't I bring in?

Food, drinks, bags over 12 by 12 inches, large camera bags, backpacks, diaper bags, car seat carriers, strollers, coats, and tripods or monopods. Free lockers and coat hooks are provided.

When is the best time to visit?

Field trips run Tuesday to Friday during the school year, so classes may be exploring while you're there. Busier times are usually 10 to 10:30 a.m. and 12:45 to 1:30 p.m. Call 406-317-1211 to ask when field trips are scheduled on your day.

Is it accessible?

Yes. The Butterfly House and Insectarium are fully wheelchair accessible. Mobility devices are welcome; skateboards, scooters and wheeled shoes are not.

Can I take photos?

Photos and video for personal use are welcome; tripods and monopods are not. Commercial photography needs written permission, or book a photography reservation.

Are there quiet or sensory-friendly times?

Yes. "Adult Swim" quiet hours give grown-ups a child-free museum, and sensory-friendly hours turn off the fans, heaters and foggers. Noise-canceling headphones are welcome any time. Check the calendar for dates.

Can I bring my dog?

Service animals are welcome, but no pets. Emotional support animals aren't recognized as service animals and can't come in. Service animals stay leashed or harnessed and attended at all times.

Butterfly House etiquette
  • Stay on the paths, and please don't run.
  • Leave flowers, plants and animals for everyone. Taking butterflies or moths, alive or dead, violates USDA regulations.
  • Supervise children at all times: one adult for every five children.
  • The building is owned by Missoula County, so concealed weapons aren't allowed, even with a permit.
  • The property is tobacco and cannabis-free, including vaping.

Driving and parking

Park in lot C or lot A on the Fairgrounds, off South Avenue West. The building is right next to lot C.

Parking map

By bus

Mountain Line, Missoula's zero-fare bus service, has several routes with a stop right next to the building. Free, and no parking to find.

By bike

Missoula has over 22 miles of off-street trails and 40 miles of bike lanes, and several bike-friendly routes lead here. Bike parking is by the main entrance.
