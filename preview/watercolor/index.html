Amenities

Three pools on the Gulf, a lazy river and clay courts under the pines

Hours change with the season. The row that applies today is highlighted on each card.

WaterColor Beach Club

Three pools with seating on lower and upper decks and lounge areas, all with unobstructed views of the Gulf. Until the water is consistently warm enough on its own, the pools are kept at around 82 degrees.

  • Pools8:00 a.m. to sunset
  • Gates, March 1 to November 18:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., daily
  • Gates, fall time change to March 18:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.

Homeowners only from 8:00 to 8:30 a.m. Rental guests from 8:30 a.m. Guests with a cabana reservation may go to their cabana at 8:00 a.m.

Camp WaterColor

A zero-entry pool, a tower slide and a lazy river, all with lifeguard supervision. The Canteen Restaurant & Bar is inside Camp, and a half basketball court and a children's playground are just outside. Towel service is offered.

  • Labor Day to fall time change9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
  • Fall time change to March 19:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
  • March 1 to spring time change9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
  • Spring time change to Labor Day9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.

Homeowners have exclusive access from 9:00 to 9:30 a.m. The main slide may close in bad weather or high wind.

Wristbands and pool deck etiquette
  • Guests 5 and older must wear a guest wristband to enter the Beach Club or Camp WaterColor. Children under five are given a paper wristband.
  • Homeowners should wear a homeowner wristband and may bring up to two guests.
  • Homeowners and guests check in at the Beach Club and Camp WaterColor with their wristbands.
  • Coolers, glasses and any outside food or beverage are strictly prohibited.
  • Music or video from personal speakers or devices is not permitted inside WaterColor's facilities.
  • Lounge chairs are first come, first served. Items left on an unattended lounger for more than 60 minutes are moved to secure storage; see a concierge to collect them.

Parking & trolley

Getting around WaterColor

Rental guests follow the same parking rules as homeowners. The rules are set by the WaterColor Community Association Board of Directors.

Where can I park?

Allowed
Private driveways, parallel parking pads and marked parking spaces throughout WaterColor.
Not allowed
On the street, in pine straw, on sidewalks or on pathways.
Credential required
Buttercup Street, Mystic Cobalt, the Viridian loop, and overnight parking anywhere but a private driveway.
Town Center
Rental guests may use the 2-hour spaces. The lots behind the Town Center condos are for those buildings' homeowners and guests, with a lot credential, year-round.
Beach Club, Camp WaterColor and WaterColor Boulevard South
Rental guests may park in spaces marked with Passport Parking signs. A paid session in the Passport Parking app is required from March 1 to October 31.
Accessible parking
Designated accessible spaces are in all lots and on WaterColor Boulevard South.

Vehicles that do not comply could be subject to immobilization and a service fee.

WaterColor Trolley

A free, on-demand service for homeowners and rental guests, to and from any location in WaterColor.

  1. Download the WaterColor Trolley app.
  2. Create an account in the app.
  3. Request a ride and get an ETA.

Riders must wear seatbelts. A child under five needs a properly secured child restraint that you bring. Car seats can be left with reception at the Beach Club and Camp WaterColor.
