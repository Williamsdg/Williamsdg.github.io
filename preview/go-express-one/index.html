    Questions

    Before you fly.

    Straight answers on pickups, delays, kids, pets and cancellations.

    Still have a question?

    A real person answers — text anytime, 24 hours.

    Text 239-464-7577
    How will I know my car has arrived?

    Give us your mobile number and you'll get an SMS when your driver is on the way, and again when they arrive at your pickup. The driver's direct number is right in the text.

    What if my flight is early or delayed?

    We track every incoming flight, so whenever you land, we're there. Domestic arrivals get 40 minutes free and international arrivals get 60 minutes free, counted from the airline's scheduled arrival. After that, wait time is charged.

    Let us know ahead if your departure changes. We can't be responsible for diversions, customs or baggage delays.

    Where do I meet my driver?

    Pickup is curbside. Your car waits at the RSW Cell Phone Lot — once you've claimed your bags, call us and we pull right up. We don't currently offer inside meet-and-greet.

    Do I need to bring a child seat?

    No — reserve one when you book, at no additional fee. To make sure it's installed properly, your driver may ask for a hand with it.

    Can I bring my pet?

    Yes, as long as they ride in a crate for the whole trip. Service dogs are always welcome without one.

    Can we stop for groceries on the way?

    Unfortunately not — our insurance policy doesn't allow grocery stops during the trip.

    Is there a deposit? What's the cancellation policy?

    Reservations made less than 6 hours ahead require a 35% deposit at booking. It's non-refundable and non-transferable.

    To cancel, give us 12 hours' notice by email to GoExpressOne@Gmail.com or text to 239-464-7577, from the person who made the reservation.

    Are there any extra fees?

    Your estimated total is sent upfront by email or text and includes all charges. An after-hours surcharge of $5–$10 applies between 11:00pm and 5:30am. Gratuity isn't included — 20–30% is customary.
