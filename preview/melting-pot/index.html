    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    How long does a meal take?

    For parties of four or fewer, allow at least 90 minutes for the full four-course experience. If timing matters, say so in the Special Requests field when you reserve, or tell your server when you arrive.

    Can I order only cheese and chocolate fondue?

    Yes. You are not required to order all four courses. There is a full à la carte menu, so you can order just the courses you want.

    Is there a dress code?

    No. Guests wear everything from casual to more formal depending on the occasion. The only ask is family-friendly clothing.

    Is Melting Pot good for kids?

    Yes. It is interactive, kids get to cook their own food, and each restaurant has a kids menu on its Menus page.

    Do you have vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options?

    Yes. There are many gluten-free options with substitutions when needed, and a dedicated vegan menu. Tell your server about any allergy before ordering, and see the Allergens & Sensitivities page.

    Do I need a reservation?

    Walk-ins are welcome, but reservations are highly recommended for the main dining room, and often required on major holidays. You can usually book at least 30 days ahead.

    Do gift cards expire?

    No. Melting Pot gift cards have no expiration date. They can be used for food and drinks in a Melting Pot restaurant, not for online purchases.

    Our story

    It started with four tables.

    1975

    The first Melting Pot opens in Maitland, Florida, outside Orlando. Four tables and three things on the menu: cheese, beef and chocolate fondue.

    1985

    The Johnston brothers buy the rights to the restaurant and found The Melting Pot Restaurants, Inc.

    2005

    Melting Pot turns 30 and opens its 100th restaurant, in Pasadena, California.

    2015

    Named the No. 1 casual dining restaurant in the Nation's Restaurant News Consumer Picks report.

    $18 million+

    raised for St. Jude Children's Research Hospital by Melting Pot guests since 2003.
