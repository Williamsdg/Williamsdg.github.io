Do I need to buy tickets in advance?

No, but it’s faster. Order online and your armbands will be ready at the Ticket Counter. Just show the QR code from your email.

What’s the difference between weekday and weekend prices?

Kids armbands are $15 Monday through Friday and $20 on Saturday and Sunday. Adult armbands are $10 every day.

When is last admission?

Last admission is 5:30 PM. We’re usually open for a while after that.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes! Your dogs are always welcome at The Great Pumpkin Patch.

Can we bring our own food?

Absolutely. Bring a picnic lunch, or buy lunch from the Pumpkin Patch Kitchen. There are picnic tables all over the farm.

Is a pumpkin included?

Yes. Every kid and adult armband includes a covered wagon ride and your choice of a pumpkin at the patch. Smaller, larger and extra pumpkins are for sale at the entrance.
