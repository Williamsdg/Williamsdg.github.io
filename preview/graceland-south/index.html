    How it works

    You ask. Susan answers.

    Send a request

    Pick a weekend and tell us who is coming. It takes about two minutes and costs nothing.

    Hear back from Susan

    Susan reads every request herself and replies personally, usually with a question or two.

    Confirm and pay

    Once Susan says yes, she sends the rate and how to pay. The weekend is yours when payment arrives.

    Nothing is booked until Susan says yes. There is no instant booking on this site, and no card is taken here.
