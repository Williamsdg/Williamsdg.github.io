FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does your service cost?

Plans start at $699 and go up depending on the materials and design of the job. Our average client spends between $750 and $1,200.

What does your service include?

We are an all-inclusive service. Every plan comes with design, commercial-grade materials, installation, warranty, removal in January, and storage until the following year.

How are your lights different from the ones at a box store?

A lot. Most box store lights are made to be sold at a lower price, with the idea of buying more later. They are not as bright and do not hold up well when exposed to the elements. They also can't be customized, which leaves you with too many lights or not enough for the area.

How much is the discount the second year?

Your first year with us will always be more expensive than the following years. Discounts are 15%–25% based on the plan you choose.

Do you warranty your products?

Yes. We offer a full warranty on anything we provide, as long as it isn't vandalism or theft. It is our job to make sure your home is glowing from the moment we hang the lights.

Do you have any discounts?

Yes. We offer discounts on following-year services as well as referral discounts. See all four ways to save.

What happens if I don't renew the following year?

We hate to see people not renew with us, but we understand that life can get in the way. We maintain full ownership of all materials used in the decoration of your home.

What areas do you serve?

We cover several areas: Mountain Brook, Hoover, Greystone, Sylacauga, Chelsea, Trussville, Odenville, Leeds, Helena and Pelham, just to name a few.
