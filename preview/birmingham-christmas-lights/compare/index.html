// 07 · Taking payments

Four ways to do it

I recommend Stripe. If you already use something you like, I can connect that instead.

Recommended

Stripe

Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and bank transfer. Handles Easy Pay monthly plans and sends pay links by text or email. No monthly fee.

2.9% + 30¢ card · 0.8% bank, $5 cap

Square

The better pick if your crew already takes cards in person on a Square reader. Online and in-person sales land in one account.

About 3% online

PayPal + Venmo

Customers know it and many already have a balance. Fees run higher and monthly plans are clumsy.

About 3.5% + 49¢

Keep Markate

Your quote form already runs on Markate. If you invoice there today, the new site can hand customers off to it with no change to your process.

Your current rate

On a $1,500 install, a card payment through Stripe costs about $44 and a bank transfer costs $5. Your cash discount gives up $150 on the same job. Rates are each provider's published standard pricing and should be confirmed at signup.
