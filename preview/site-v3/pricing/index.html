Pricing FAQ

The fine print, in plain English.

Why do you show prices?

Because you should know roughly what something costs before you spend time on a call. Your quote will be fixed and in writing.

What is included in a care plan?

Hosting, SSL, backups, uptime monitoring, security updates, and a set amount of content changes each month. Plans are $50, $100, or $250 a month depending on whether you have a website, a website with a dashboard, or a full platform.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes — typically half to start and half at launch. Larger projects can be split into milestones.

Are there hidden fees?

No. Third-party costs like your domain name or Stripe processing fees are paid directly to those providers, and we tell you about them up front.

Can I start small and grow?

Yes, and we recommend it. Many clients start with a website and add a dashboard, portal, or store later — it is built to connect.
