FAQ

Ecommerce: common questions

Do I need Shopify?

Not necessarily. For a focused catalog, a custom Stripe storefront is faster and has no monthly platform fee. For large catalogs or complex inventory, Shopify can be the right call — and we can design around it.

Can I keep my existing store?

Yes. For Steve R. Skipper Studio we redesigned the site and kept the existing print store for checkout.

What does Stripe cost?

Stripe charges a per-transaction processing fee and no monthly fee. You are paid directly into your bank account.
