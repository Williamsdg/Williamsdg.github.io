FAQ

Municipal & community: common questions

Do you build websites for small towns?

Yes. We focus on small and mid-size Alabama municipalities and community associations that need a modern, accessible site without an enterprise contract.

Are your websites ADA compliant?

We build toward WCAG 2.1 AA and test keyboard navigation, contrast, and screen-reader behavior. Accessibility is ongoing, so our care plans include keeping new content accessible too.

Can our staff update the site?

Yes. Staff can publish events, news, and documents without touching code.
