FAQ

Client portals: common questions

What businesses need a client portal?

Builders and contractors, law and accounting firms, agencies, property managers, healthcare administration, and any business where customers regularly ask “where are we on this?”

Is a client portal secure?

Yes. Each customer signs in, and database-level security rules ensure they can only read their own records. All traffic is encrypted.

Can customers pay invoices in the portal?

Yes. We integrate Stripe so customers can pay by card or bank transfer, and payments are recorded automatically.
