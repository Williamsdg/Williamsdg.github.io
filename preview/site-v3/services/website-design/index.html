FAQ

Website design: common questions

How much does a small business website cost?

Most custom business websites we build fall between $1,000 and $2,500, depending on the number of pages and features. A one-page launch site can start lower. Every project gets a written, fixed quote before work begins.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most sites go live in two to six weeks. You see a visual preview early, so you can steer the direction before the full build.

Do you use WordPress or Wix?

We hand-code most sites, which makes them faster and harder to break. If you need to edit content yourself, we can add a simple editor or build on a CMS you already use.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site ships with technical SEO in place: clean titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap, fast load times, and mobile readiness. Ranking also depends on reviews and your Google Business Profile, and we help with those too.
