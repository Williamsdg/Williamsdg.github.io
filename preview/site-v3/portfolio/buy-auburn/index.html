Buy Auburn (the Auburn Business Network) connects the Auburn family with Auburn-owned restaurants, shops, and services across the country.

The problem

The idea was simple — help Auburn people find and support Auburn-owned businesses wherever they travel — but running it meant handling applications, vetting, and payments without a staff.

What we built

A searchable directory filtered by state, city, and industry, with a near-me view. Businesses apply online, an admin approves them from a dashboard, and listings are billed automatically through Stripe subscriptions.

How it is built

Supabase for authentication and data, Stripe for recurring billing, Resend for transactional email, hosted on Vercel.
