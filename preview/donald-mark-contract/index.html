01

Parties

This Website Development Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between:

Service Provider

Williams Digital, a digital services and web development firm specializing in website design, development, hosting setup, and related digital solutions.

Contact: Dylan@williamsdigital.io

Client

Donald Mark (the "Client"), the owner and authorized representative of the restaurant business for which the website described in this Agreement is being built.

Prior to commencement of work, the Client shall provide in writing: the legal business name of the restaurant, the physical business address, a direct phone number, and the domain name to be used for the website, which must be registered in the Client's own name per Section 3. This Agreement is not effective until this information has been received and confirmed by the Service Provider.

02

Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to design, develop, and deploy a custom restaurant website for the Client. The project includes the following deliverables:

  • Ten (10) Pages — Home, About Us, Menu, Banquet Facility, Gallery, Catering, Contact Us, Visiting Hours, Online Booking, and Menu Kit.
  • Responsive Design — All pages built to display properly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices, with particular emphasis on the mobile dining-decision experience.
  • Content Management — An easy-to-use backend allowing the Client to update menus, photos, hours, and other content without developer assistance, plus a training walkthrough at launch.
  • Inquiry Forms — Contact, catering, and banquet inquiry forms with email delivery to the Client.
  • Online Booking — Integration of an online reservation/booking system.
  • Image Gallery — A photo gallery showcasing the restaurant, food, and banquet facility.
  • Google Maps & Social Integration — Location map embed and links to the Client's social media profiles.
  • SEO Foundations — Page titles, meta descriptions, and basic on-page search-engine optimization.
  • SSL & Security — HTTPS/SSL configuration and standard security hardening.
  • Domain Connection & Launch — Connection of the Client's own domain (purchased and registered by the Client per Section 3) and deployment of the live website.
  • First-Year Hosting — Twelve (12) months of website hosting included in the build fee, beginning at launch.

Client Content & Materials

All written content, menu information, logos, artwork, and photography are to be supplied by the Client (or the Client's designated representative) at no cost to the Service Provider. The domain name must be purchased by the Client directly from a domain service provider and registered in the Client's own name, as detailed in Section 3 (Domain Purchase — Client Responsibility). The Service Provider shall not be required to pay, reimburse, or compensate any person or entity — including any employee, project manager, content provider, or contractor of the Client — in order to receive these materials. Any fee owed to any such person is the sole responsibility of the Client and is entirely outside this Agreement.

All required content and materials must be delivered within seven (7) days of the deposit being received. The project timeline does not begin until all materials have been received.

Revision Policy

The project includes up to three (3) rounds of revisions during the design and development phase. A revision round consists of a consolidated set of feedback submitted at one time. Additional revision rounds beyond the included three will be billed at the Service Provider's hourly rate of $50/hour. Revisions must be requested within seven (7) days of receiving a design proof or deliverable; otherwise, the deliverable will be considered approved.

Change Orders & Scope Changes

Any work requested by the Client that falls outside the scope described above — including additional pages, custom integrations, online ordering, or new features — will require a written Change Order. The Service Provider will provide a quote for the additional work, and no work will commence until the Client approves the Change Order in writing. Change Orders may affect the project timeline and will be billed separately from the build fee.

03

Fees and Payment

In consideration of the services provided, the Client agrees to compensate the Service Provider as follows:

ItemAmount
Deposit — 50%, due upon acceptance of this Agreement. Work begins after the deposit has been received and has fully settled.$750.00
Final Payment — 50%, due upon Client approval of the completed website, prior to launch.$750.00
Total Build Fee$1,500.00 USD

Build Fee — $1,500.00 USD

A one-time build fee of $1,500.00 USD covers the complete website design, development, all ten pages, forms, booking integration, SEO foundations, SSL, domain connection, launch, and the first twelve (12) months of hosting, as described in Section 2. This fee is split into two equal installments of $750.00: a 50% deposit due upon acceptance of this Agreement, and the remaining 50% due upon approval of the completed website, prior to launch.

Hosting After Year One — $50.00 USD / month

Beginning twelve (12) months after launch, a recurring monthly fee of $50.00 USD covers website hosting, SSL certification, form and booking functionality, security and software updates, uptime monitoring, and backups. The monthly fee continues until cancelled by either party with thirty (30) days written notice. The domain remains owned by and registered to the Client at all times.

Payment Methods & Settlement

All payments shall be made exclusively through the secure electronic payment link (credit/debit card invoice) provided by the Service Provider. The Service Provider does not accept payment by paper check, cashier's check, money order, wire transfer, mobile check deposit, gift card, or cryptocurrency, and no such instrument will be treated as payment under this Agreement.

Work commences only after the deposit has been received and has fully settled with the Service Provider's payment processor. A payment that is later reversed, disputed, or returned is deemed never to have been made.

No Third-Party Disbursements

The Service Provider will never disburse, forward, transfer, or refund any funds to any person or account other than the Client's original payment method. Under no circumstances will the Service Provider pay any portion of any amount received to the Client's employees, project managers, content providers, designers, or any other third party, regardless of instruction. Any request that the Service Provider make such a payment shall be void and of no effect under this Agreement.

Overpayment

If any payment received exceeds the amount invoiced, the excess will be refunded only to the original payment method, only after the original payment has fully settled and cleared any applicable dispute window, and less any non-recoverable processing fees. Overpayments will not be applied to third parties, alternative accounts, or alternative payment channels under any circumstances.

Chargebacks & Payment Reversals

Any chargeback, payment dispute, or reversal initiated on a settled payment constitutes a material breach of this Agreement. Upon such an event, all work ceases immediately, all licenses and rights granted to the Client under this Agreement are revoked, any website or preview materials may be taken offline, and the full unpaid balance for work performed becomes immediately due, together with any processing or dispute fees incurred by the Service Provider.

Domain Purchase — Client Responsibility

The Client shall purchase and register the website domain name directly from a recognized domain service provider (e.g., GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google/Squarespace Domains, or Cloudflare Registrar) using the Client's own account, name, and payment method, at the Client's own cost. The annual domain registration fee (typically $15–$25 per year) is paid by the Client directly to the registrar and is not included in the build fee or hosting fees under this Agreement.

The Service Provider will not purchase the domain on the Client's behalf, will not reimburse any party for a domain purchase, and will not accept a domain "provided" or transferred by any third party, including the Client's designated representative. The Service Provider's role is limited to connecting the Client-owned domain to the completed website via DNS configuration. Registrar account access sufficient for DNS setup (or the ability to enter DNS records as directed) must be provided by the Client prior to launch.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Should the project be cancelled by the Client after work has commenced, the $750.00 deposit is non-refundable, as it covers discovery, planning, design, and development work already performed. If the project is cancelled prior to launch, no final build payment shall be owed. If the Service Provider cancels before any work has been performed, the full deposit will be refunded to the original payment method.

Project Timeline

The Service Provider will aim to complete the project within approximately fourteen to twenty-one (14–21) days from the later of: (a) settlement of the deposit, and (b) receipt of all final content, branding materials, images, menu information, and domain access:

  • Days 1–5 — Design direction, homepage concept, and structure of all ten pages.
  • Days 6–12 — Full build-out, content loading, forms, gallery, and booking integration.
  • Days 13–17 — Client review, revision rounds, SEO configuration, and mobile refinement.
  • Days 18–21 — Final approval, final payment, domain connection, launch, and backend training walkthrough.

This timeline is a good-faith estimate. Delays in providing content, feedback, approvals, or domain access may extend the schedule accordingly, and the Service Provider shall not be held liable for such delays. A private preview link will be provided throughout development for review.

Client Responsibilities & Support

To facilitate the effective delivery of services, the Client agrees to:

  • Provide the business identification details described in Section 1 prior to commencement.
  • Provide timely, accurate, and complete content, including menu information, logos, artwork, and photography.
  • Purchase and register the website domain in the Client's own name and registrar account per Section 3, and provide DNS access for launch.
  • Confirm the accuracy of all menu items, prices, hours, and business details prior to launch.
  • Respond to communications, design proofs, and requests for approval within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Warrant that all materials provided are owned by the Client or properly licensed.

Post-Launch Support

The Service Provider will provide thirty (30) days of complimentary post-launch support beginning on the date the website goes live. This support covers bug fixes and configuration questions, in addition to the backend training walkthrough provided at launch.

Project Pause & Inactivity

If the Client (or the Client's representative) becomes unresponsive or fails to provide requested materials, feedback, or approvals for a period of fourteen (14) consecutive days, the project may be placed on hold. Projects paused for more than sixty (60) days may be treated as cancelled under the Cancellation & Refund Policy outlined in Section 3.

Launch Sign-Off

Prior to the public launch of the website, the Service Provider will present the completed site to the Client for final review and approval. The Client shall have five (5) business days to review and provide written approval or submit final revision requests. Upon approval, the final build payment of $750.00 becomes due, and the website will be launched once that payment has settled. If the Client does not respond within this period, the website will be deemed approved.

Term and Termination

This Agreement shall become effective upon acceptance by the Client and receipt of the information required under Section 1, and shall remain in effect until cancelled as provided below.

Termination by Either Party

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing thirty (30) days written notice to the other party. Upon termination and settlement of all outstanding balances, the Service Provider will provide the Client with all completed and paid-for deliverables and assist with a reasonable transition of hosting.

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision and fails to cure such breach within five (5) days of receiving written notice. A chargeback or payment reversal under Section 3 is a material breach not subject to cure.

Effect of Termination

Upon termination, the Client shall pay the Service Provider for all services rendered up to the effective date of termination. Deliverables transfer only upon settlement of all amounts owed.
