1.

Scope of Work

Williams Digital agrees to design, develop and deploy a complete rebuild of the JE Holdings website, deployed to the thejeh.com domain.

The design direction, homepage and Our Principal page are already built and available for review at the preview link above.

Homepage
The full custom build shown in the preview, including The Safe — a scroll-controlled vault sequence built specifically for JE Holdings — the exposure profiles, the compartment wall, and the closing sequence.
Five Service Pages
Asset Protection, Estate & Legacy, Business Structures, Onshore + Offshore, and Athletes + Entertainers, each built out from its compartment in The Safe.
Our Principal
A dedicated page for Rupert Francisco carrying the full biography, chronology, recognition and memberships. Built and included in the preview.
Contact & Legal
Contact page with office details and enquiry form, plus Legal Notice and Privacy Policy pages.
Mobile & Tablet
Every page laid out for phone and tablet as its own composition, not a scaled desktop view. The Safe runs on mobile at reduced fidelity so the door still opens.
Accessibility
Keyboard navigation and a full reduced-motion mode, plus a static fallback for browsers without WebGL so the site is never dependent on the animation.
Content Migration
Existing copy carried across from the current thejeh.com and set into the new design.
Hosting & Deployment
Domain configuration, SSL, and launch to thejeh.com.
Revisions & Support
Two rounds of revisions during design, and 30 days of post-launch support.
2.

Not Included

The following fall outside this agreement and will be quoted separately if wanted:

  • Photography and videography. The Our Principal page currently uses a monogram placeholder where Rupert’s portrait belongs. A professional headshot is needed from the Client; retouching and colour-grading of Client-supplied images is included at no additional cost.
  • Client portals, intake forms or payment processing beyond a standard contact enquiry form. The existing Payment page is carried across as content, not rebuilt as a transaction system.
  • Copywriting beyond migrating and lightly editing existing content.
  • Legal review. Legal Notice and Privacy Policy content is migrated as supplied; Williams Digital does not draft or review legal language.
  • Domain registration, premium fonts and third-party licences, which are billed at cost if required.
