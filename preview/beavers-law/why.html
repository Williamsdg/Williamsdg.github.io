// What makes this version better

Eight specific changes.

Each one is something I found on the current site, and what the concept does about it.

Area
Current site
Concept
Practice areas
Current siteThree headings with no description under them.
ConceptEach area is explained, using the detail that is already written in Charlie's bio.
Credentials
Current siteChambers USA, Best Lawyers and the AV Preeminent rating sit in the last paragraph of the About page.
ConceptThey are the first thing under the headline on the homepage.
Experience
Current siteThe project types Charlie has handled are packed into one long sentence.
ConceptEight entries a developer or property owner can scan in a few seconds.
Calling
Current siteThe number is plain text, and on a phone it is not in the header at all.
ConceptA call button in the header and a call bar at the bottom of every phone screen.
Inquiries
Current siteNo contact form and no email link. A visitor has to copy the address by hand.
ConceptTap-to-email, directions to the office, and a short form that sorts the inquiry by type of matter.
Google
Current siteThe homepage has no search description, and its title does not mention real estate or Birmingham.
ConceptA written title and description, plus the law-firm data search engines read for name, address and phone.
Polish
Current siteThe browser tab shows the default Squarespace icon, and the About page has an empty image block.
ConceptThe firm's own monogram in the tab and no empty blocks.
Leftovers
Current siteA second copy of the homepage is still live at the address /contact-harris.
ConceptOne homepage. Old addresses forward to the right page.

Observations taken from the firm's public website on October 7, 2026. Nothing about the firm on the concept is invented, and the State Bar disclaimer is kept word for word.
