Charlie has been representing clients with respect to real estate law matters for over 40 years.

His primary areas of law include the acquisition, sale and development of real properties, including rezonings, subdivisions and other land use matters before elected governing councils and commissions and before appointed boards and committees. He also regularly represents property owners in condemnation proceedings and is experienced in municipal annexations.

Charlie has served as the Chairman of the Real Property, Probate and Trust Section of the Alabama State Bar Association. He has taught real estate law as an adjunct professor at The University of Alabama School of Law and has made presentations on many occasions at continuing legal education seminars.

Charlie is listed in Chambers USA and The Best Lawyers in America, and has an AV Preeminent Rating with Martindale-Hubbell.
