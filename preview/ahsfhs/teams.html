How to help

The archive is built by its readers

A yearbook in an attic, a clipping in a scrapbook, a program from 1954 — most of what is in here arrived that way. Nothing is too small to send.

Step one

Find the gap

Search for your school above. If a season is missing scores, or the status is not Complete, that is where help is needed.

Step two

Send what you have

Scores, rosters, coaches, stadium names, colors, mascots, helmet designs. Photographs of the source are ideal — we verify everything.

Step three

We check it

Every submission is matched against a second source before it is published, and contributors are credited on the contributors page.

Submit scores or a correction

We read every one.

Join the Society

Membership is free. Members get the forums, the research queue and a say in where the archive goes next — this group is run by consensus, and every member has a right to voice an opinion about the site and its contents.

Fair warning, in the words of the join page: this will become addictive.
