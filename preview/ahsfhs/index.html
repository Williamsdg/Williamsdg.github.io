About the Society

“We are a research group and pride ourselves in producing quality and accurate information. This site will forever be a free site with access to everyone.”

AHSFHS.org · founded June 2007

Research began about twenty years before the site existed. AHSFHS.org is a non-profit group, unaffiliated with the AHSAA or any government entity, run by consensus of its members. In 2012 basketball, baseball, softball, volleyball and soccer joined football in the archive.
