About the Firm

Discipline meets relationships.

Founded in 2020, McBride Real Estate has grown into one of the most trusted boutique commercial brokerages in the Southeast. We are not a transactional firm — we build long-term partnerships with landlords, retailers, and investors who value precision, candor, and follow-through.

Our clients return because we treat their portfolios the way we'd treat our own. Every site visit, every lease negotiation, every reconciliation reflects a standard of care more often associated with private wealth than commercial real estate.

What We Do

Three disciplines, one standard.

Whether you are leasing a shopping center, expanding a national footprint, or stewarding a portfolio of stabilized assets — our work begins and ends with a fiduciary standard.

01 — Landlord

Shopping Center Leasing

Strategic marketing, qualified tenant procurement, and disciplined negotiation that protects long-term asset value while filling vacancy on landlord-favorable terms.

  • Market analysis & rent benchmarking
  • Co-broker network & ICSC presence
  • LOI & lease negotiation
  • Custom marketing flyers & site plans
02 — Retailer

Tenant Representation

Site selection, expansion strategy, and lease negotiation for national and regional retailers entering or growing across the Southeast.

  • Trade area & demographic analysis
  • Multi-market roll-out planning
  • Lease economics & concession recovery
  • Renewal & renegotiation strategy
03 — Owner

Property Management

Transparent, flat-fee management with proprietary owner reporting, automated CAM reconciliation, and tenant-portal experience that reflects your asset's quality.

  • Flat-fee, transparent pricing
  • CAM & tax reconciliation
  • COI & lease compliance tracking
  • Branded owner & tenant portals
