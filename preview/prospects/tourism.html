The one number that sells this

A charter running 1,500 trips a year at a $150 average through Viator at 25% hands the platform $56,000 a year. A $3,000 direct-booking site pays for itself in weeks. Do this math with THEIR numbers on the call — ask what the platform takes, go quiet, let it land.

How to work this list

2. Click a card → call script, questions and pricing are in the drawer. Log every touch with the status buttons and write what they want in My notes (it saves automatically to this browser and shows on the card).
