Ground rules

The goal of every call is permission to send a free mockup and the best email to send it to. That's a win. You are not closing a sale on the phone.
Read the card first. Every prospect on the pipeline has notes, website status, and a suggested message. Mention something specific ("I saw your u-pick opens in June") — it kills the robocall vibe instantly.
Who we are: Williams Digital, a small web studio in Birmingham, AL run by Dylan Williams. We build websites and custom tools businesses own — one-time build plus a low flat monthly, no per-user SaaS creep.
Never quote a hard price. Give the anchor range if pushed, then: "the free mockup comes with an exact quote — no obligation."
Proof: if they want to see work, send the portfolio link (button above) or tell them to check williamsdigital.io. Don't describe it — send it.
After every call, note the outcome (email captured / callback time / not interested / gatekeeper name) so Dylan can update the pipeline. Anything you promise, cc Dylan@williamsdigital.io.

Call flow — 5 steps, under 4 minutes

Opener — earn 20 seconds. Confirm the business, say who you are, one specific observation. "Hi, is this [business]? This is [your name] with Williams Digital — we're a small web studio out of Birmingham. Quick one: I was looking you up and noticed [no website / only the Instagram page / the site doesn't work great on a phone]… do you have 60 seconds?"
Right person? If not the owner/decision maker, get a name and a time — politely. "Who handles the website side of things? … When's a good time to catch them — mornings or afternoons?"
Discover — ask 2–3 questions from their business type below. Let them talk. You're listening for the pain the pitch solves.
Pitch in one line. Match it to what they just said, then offer the mockup. "That's exactly what we fix. Dylan builds a free mockup of what your [site / ordering page / booking page] could look like — no cost, no obligation. If you love it, we talk. If not, you keep the idea."
Close — get the email. Confirm spelling, set the expectation, done. "What's the best email to send it to? … Perfect — you'll have it within a few days from Dylan@williamsdigital.io. Keep an eye out for it."
Voicemail (15 sec): "Hi, this is [name] with Williams Digital in Birmingham. I put together some ideas for a [business type] website for [business name] and wanted to send them over — no charge, no strings. Call or text me at [number], or email Dylan@williamsdigital.io. Thanks!"
