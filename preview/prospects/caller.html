Memorize this — the backbone of every call, email, and proposal

"Williams Digital builds websites, portals, dashboards, and customer experiences that businesses actually own. Instead of paying endless monthly software subscriptions, our clients get a custom solution designed around their workflow with simple, predictable support costs."

Rule #1: Don't sell websites. Sell solutions.

Nobody wakes up wanting a website. Always start with the business problem. They want:
↗More leads
📅More bookings
📄Less paperwork
❤Better customer experience
💸Lower software costs
🛠Better internal organization
The goal of every call is permission to send a free mockup and the best email to send it to. That's a win. You are not closing a sale on the phone.
Read the card first. Every prospect on the pipeline has a score, notes, and website status. Mention something specific — it kills the robocall vibe instantly.
Never quote a hard price. Give the range if pushed, then: "the free mockup comes with an exact quote — no obligation."
After every call, set the status in the pipeline (Contacted / Follow up / Hot) and note the outcome. Anything you promise, cc Dylan@williamsdigital.io.

Call flow — 5 steps, under 4 minutes

Opener — earn 20 seconds. Confirm the business, say who you are, one specific observation. "Hi, is this [business]? This is [your name] with Williams Digital — we're a small web studio out of Birmingham. Quick one: I was looking you up and noticed [no website / only the Instagram page / the site doesn't work great on a phone]… do you have 60 seconds?"
Right person? If not the owner/decision maker, get a name and a time — politely. "Who handles the website side of things? … When's a good time to catch them — mornings or afternoons?"
Discover — ask 2–3 questions from their vertical below. Let them talk. You're listening for the pain the pitch solves.
Pitch in one line. Use the vertical's pitch line, tied to what they just said, then offer the mockup. "That's exactly what we fix. Dylan builds a free mockup of what your [site / ordering page / booking page / dashboard] could look like — no cost, no obligation. If you love it, we talk. If not, you keep the idea."
Close — get the email. Confirm spelling, set the expectation, done. "What's the best email to send it to? … Perfect — you'll have it within a few days from Dylan@williamsdigital.io. Keep an eye out for it."
Voicemail (15 sec): "Hi, this is [name] with Williams Digital in Birmingham. I put together some ideas for a [business type] website for [business name] and wanted to send them over — no charge, no strings. Call or text me at [number], or email Dylan@williamsdigital.io. Thanks!"

Universal discovery questions — work on any business

Website

  • Who updates your website?
  • When was it last redesigned?
  • Do you actually get leads from it?

Operations

  • What software do you currently use — and what does it cost you?
  • What's your biggest administrative headache?
  • What takes the most time every week?

Growth

  • What's your biggest goal this year?
  • What would make the biggest difference to your business?

Qualification scale — grade every call

🟢 Green light — push for the mockup NOW

  • Owner involved in the call
  • Actively growing
  • Frustrated with current tools
  • Has budget

🟡 Yellow light — mockup + scheduled follow-up

  • Interested but not urgent
  • Needs approval (board, partner, council)
  • Small budget

🔴 Red light — polite exit, log it, move on

  • No budget
  • Happy with current setup
  • No decision authority & won't route you
