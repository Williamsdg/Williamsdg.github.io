Pitch angles by website status

No website (landscapers, coffee, farms) → "You're advertising / open for business with nowhere to send people." Build a site.
Weak/outdated (museums, some farms, Shopify) → redesign + the right tool (ticketing / ordering / CRO).
Has a site, multi-location (construction, multi-crew landscapers) → ops dashboard, not a website.
On a SaaS tool (Fryar/Foundation on Buildertrend) → displacement: "own it instead of renting forever."

Municipality procurement (read before pitching towns)

Website builds are service contracts — below ~$5k–10k, most towns can hire directly, no formal RFP. But even tiny towns need a select-board / council vote to spend. So: pitch the clerk or mayor → offer a free sample homepage → they take it to the board. Keep the first ask under the no-RFP threshold. Lead with "a site the town owns, no yearly vendor license."
