How it works

Your crown, in three steps.

Whether you’re navigating hair loss or just switching up your look, it starts the same way — with people, not a product page.

A private, unhurried consultation.

Tell us what you’re looking for and try pieces on. Bring someone along if that feels better — no question is too small.

  • Measured for the right cap size
  • Try different caps, lengths and colours
  • No pressure to decide on the day
Book a consultation →

Ready-to-wear or custom.

A ready-to-wear piece or one made for you — matched to how you’ll actually wear it, not just how it looks on the shelf.

  • Take the two-minute wig finder
  • Explore cap styles in the Wig Bible
  • Or design a custom piece together
Take the wig finder →

Fitted, styled, cared for.

We fit, customise and style your piece — and show you how to care for it at home, so it keeps looking like you.

  • Install, cut and style
  • A care walkthrough before you leave
  • Repairs and refreshes when you need them

Private consultations

Come let us take care of you.

If you’re navigating hair loss — from a medical condition, alopecia, hormonal change, treatment or simply time — the first step is a conversation, not a checkout.

“It’s about walking out of the door feeling lighter, bolder, and more you than you were when you came in.”

Request a private appointment →
  1. 1
    Reach out, quietly

    Message, email or book online. Share as much or as little as you like.

  2. 2
    Sit down together

    Private, one-to-one and unhurried. Bring someone along if that helps.

  3. 3
    Try, talk, take your time

    Feel different caps, lengths and colours. Ask anything.

  4. 4
    Leave with a plan

    A clear next step and what it would cost. No pressure to decide.
