Sword & Crown

Beautiful hair. Personal attention. A little room to have fun. Sword & Crown brings luxury wigs, expert customization, and thoughtful salon care together for women with alternative hair needs. Whether you’re navigating hair loss or exploring a new look, we’ll help you find something that feels like you.

And because personal style goes beyond your hair, our boutique is filled with clothes, jewelry, accessories, and beauty favorites chosen to help you make it your own.

Hair loss

You don't have to figure this out alone.

Hair loss changes more than your hair. Finding the right solution can feel overwhelming — and that's exactly why Sword & Crown exists.

We offer private consultations, thoughtful guidance and beautiful alternative-hair solutions for women experiencing alopecia, chemotherapy-related hair loss, hormonal changes, thinning and other forms of hair loss.

Meet the owner

Hi, I'm Elana.

I’m a wife, a young mother in my thirties, and a woman who believes that something beautiful can grow from the hardest parts of our stories. Sword & Crown is mine.

After more than twenty years of wearing wigs, I’ve learned a lot about finding, fitting, styling, and caring for alternative hair. I’ve also learned how much it matters to have someone beside you who understands. That’s why I created Sword & Crown.
