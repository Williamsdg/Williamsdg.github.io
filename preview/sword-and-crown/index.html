Private consultations

Come let us take care of you.

If you’re navigating hair loss — from a medical condition, alopecia, hormonal change, treatment or simply time — the first step is a conversation, not a checkout.

“It’s about walking out of the door feeling lighter, bolder, and more you than you were when you came in.”Sword & Crown
Request a private appointment →
  1. i

    Reach out, quietly

    Message, email or book online. Tell us as much or as little as you like.

  2. ii

    Sit down together

    Private, one-to-one and unhurried. Bring someone along if that feels better.

  3. iii

    Try, talk, take your time

    Feel different caps, lengths and colours. Ask anything — no question is too small.

  4. iv

    Leave with a plan

    A clear next step and what it would cost. No pressure to decide on the day.
