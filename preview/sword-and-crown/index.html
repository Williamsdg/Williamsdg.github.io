Sword & Crown

This isn't your grandmother's wig shop.

Sword & Crown is a new kind of salon experience built for women with alternative hair needs — combining luxury human hair wigs, expert customization, restorative beauty services and damn good hair.

Whether you're navigating hair loss, looking for a little more volume, or simply ready to change things up, we're here to help you find a crown that feels completely, unapologetically you.
