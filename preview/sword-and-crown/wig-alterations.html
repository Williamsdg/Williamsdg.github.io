Made To Fit

Wig Alteration Services

Small adjustments can make a meaningful difference in how your wig fits and feels. Each alteration begins with an assessment of your cap’s construction and your preferences. Prices include materials and installation.

Adjustable Wig Elastic Strap

Timing confirmed after assessment

Installation of an adjustable interior elastic strap to help personalize the fit and improve stability. Placement and tension are tailored to your cap and comfort, with care to avoid unnecessary pressure.

From $45Request this service

Structural Ear Tabs

Timing confirmed after assessment

Addition of reinforced, shapeable ear tabs to help the wig sit more consistently at the temples and support a more defined fit around the ears. Tabs are positioned to suit your hairline, cap construction, and comfort.

From $65per pairRequest this service

Velvet Liner

Timing confirmed after assessment

Addition of a soft velvet lining along selected interior areas of the cap to provide a cushioned contact surface and gentle grip. Placement is customized to improve comfort and help reduce shifting without adding unnecessary bulk.

From $55Request this service

Silicone Liner

Timing confirmed after assessment

Addition of silicone lining or grip sections in selected areas of the cap to help improve traction against the skin and reduce movement. Placement is tailored to your needs, with consideration for exposed scalp, existing hair, and skin sensitivity.

From $75Request this service

A note on pricing

All listed prices are starting rates. Your final price depends on the length, density, condition, construction, and customization needs of your hair or piece. Any additional charges will be discussed before work begins.

Please bring wigs and toppers clean and detangled for cutting or alteration appointments unless a cleansing service is included in your booking. Pieces requiring additional cleansing, detangling, or corrective work will be assessed and quoted before proceeding.

Hairpiece acceptance & outside purchase policy

Sword & Crown Salon reserves the right to decline service on any wig, topper, or hairpiece based on its quality, condition, construction, or suitability for the requested service. Our team will assess whether the piece can withstand the proposed work and whether we have the tools and expertise needed to achieve the desired result.

Any wig, topper, or hairpiece not purchased from Sword & Crown or its affiliated brands, Maven Mane Co. or Milk and Honey Co., is subject to a $75 outside-piece fee, in addition to applicable service charges.

A consultation is required before service is accepted on an outside piece. During this appointment, we will assess the hairpiece, discuss your goals, determine the work required, and confirm whether our team is equipped to carry out your requests. A consultation does not guarantee acceptance for service; any limitations and proposed charges will be discussed before work begins.
