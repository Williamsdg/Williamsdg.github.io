The Salon · wig maintenance, styling, cutting, colour & alterations

Your hair. Your rules. Our specialty.

From refreshing your favorite wig to finding a cut that works with your changing hair, our salon services bring thoughtful technique and personal attention to every appointment. We specialize in alternative hair and restorative hairstyling, with compassionate care for wherever you are in your hair journey.

For generations, the beauty shop has been a place to catch up, feel cared for, and leave with a little more confidence. Women with hair loss and alternative hair needs deserve that experience, too—and that’s the salon we’re building at Sword & Crown.

Your appointment is with Jennifer Ryder, Briana Sims, or owner and wig specialist Elana Brooks-Giglio. Meet the team →

All salon services require an intake appointment because the client needs to be present when we receive and assess the piece. Maintenance, repairs, and alterations are generally drop-off services after that appointment. Turnaround depends on our schedule, the scope of work, and the client’s needs. Expedited work may be possible case by case.

Appointment requests and drop-off services

Submitting a request form does not confirm an appointment. We confirm the service and time with you. Maintenance, alterations, and repairs require an intake appointment and are generally completed as drop-off work afterward. We assess your piece, discuss the scope and price, and provide an estimated pickup time. Turnaround varies with workload, condition, and complexity. Expedited work is subject to availability and a separately agreed fee. Please wait for our ready-for-pickup confirmation before traveling to the salon.

A note on pricing

All listed prices are starting rates. Your final price depends on the length, density, condition, construction, and customization needs of your hair or piece. Any additional charges will be discussed before work begins.

Please bring wigs and toppers clean and detangled for cutting or alteration appointments unless a cleansing service is included in your booking. Pieces requiring additional cleansing, detangling, or corrective work will be assessed and quoted before proceeding.

Hairpiece acceptance & outside purchase policy

Sword & Crown Salon reserves the right to decline service on any wig, topper, or hairpiece based on its quality, condition, construction, or suitability for the requested service. Our team will assess whether the piece can withstand the proposed work and whether we have the tools and expertise needed to achieve the desired result.

Any wig, topper, or hairpiece not purchased from Sword & Crown or its affiliated brands, Maven Mane Co. or Milk and Honey Co., is subject to a $75 outside-piece fee, in addition to applicable service charges.

A consultation is required before service is accepted on an outside piece. During this appointment, we will assess the hairpiece, discuss your goals, determine the work required, and confirm whether our team is equipped to carry out your requests. A consultation does not guarantee acceptance for service; any limitations and proposed charges will be discussed before work begins.
