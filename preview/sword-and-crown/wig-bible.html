Glossary

Every term, A to Z.

The same definitions appear in place when you tap a term inside any guide. Use the search above to filter.

#ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ

#

360 laceCap construction
Lace around the whole perimeter of the cap, so hair can be pulled back into a ponytail or high bun without the cap showing.

B

Baby hairsHairline
Short, fine hairs along the front hairline that soften the edge so it looks like natural growth.
Bleached knotsHairline
The tiny knots where hair is tied into lace, lightened so they are less visible against the scalp.

C

Cap sizeFit
Your head circumference, measured around the hairline and nape. Most caps come in petite, average and large.
ClosureCap construction
A small lace panel at the top of the head — often 4×4 or 5×5 inches — that gives a natural-looking part in one area.

D

DensityFit & fullness
How much hair is on the cap, shown as a percentage. Lower densities look more natural; higher densities look fuller.

F

FrontalCap construction
A lace panel running ear to ear across the front, such as 13×4, allowing a natural hairline and more parting options than a closure.
Full laceCap construction
A cap made entirely of lace with hand-tied hair — part it anywhere and wear it up. The most versatile, and the most delicate.

G

GluelessCap construction
A wig designed to stay secure with combs, straps or an adjustable band instead of adhesive.

H

Hand-tiedCap construction
Hairs knotted into the cap one at a time by hand, so they move freely and can be parted and brushed any way.
HD laceLace
A very thin, fine lace designed to blend with the skin. It looks the most invisible and is also the most delicate.
Human hairHair type
Real hair that can be heat-styled and coloured like your own. It needs more upkeep than synthetic fibre and is restyled after washing.

L

Lace frontCap construction
A sheer lace panel along the front hairline, knotted by hand, so the hair appears to grow straight from the scalp.

M

MonofilamentCap construction
A fine mesh top where each hair is tied individually. The part looks like scalp and can be brushed any direction.

P

Pre-plucked hairlineHairline
A hairline thinned out before sale so it looks less dense and more natural straight out of the box.

R

RemyHair type
Human hair collected with the cuticles kept intact and facing one direction, which helps reduce tangling.

S

SyntheticHair type
Man-made fibre that holds its style after washing. Most can’t take heat unless labelled heat-friendly.

T

TopperHair pieces
A partial piece that clips into your own hair to add coverage on top, rather than replacing all of it.

W

WeftsCap construction
Strips of hair sewn onto the cap in rows. Quick to make and airy to wear, but they can’t be parted in any direction.
Wig gripWearing
A soft band worn under a wig for comfort and to help it stay in place.
