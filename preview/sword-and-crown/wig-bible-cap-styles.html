Caps & laceBeginnerGuide II of V

Cap styles, explained.

Lace front, glueless, 360, monofilament, full lace and toppers — what each one actually changes, and where to start.

7 min readReviewed by stylist nameLast reviewed date

On this page

  1. Why the cap matters
  2. The six you’ll hear about

Key takeaways

  • The cap decides the hairline, the part and how it feels by evening — not just how it looks on the shelf.
  • Glueless and lace front caps are the most common starting points.
  • Monofilament tops are about the part and comfort; 360 and full lace are about styling freedom.
  • If you’re thinning on top rather than all over, a topper may be all you need.

01Why the cap matters

Two wigs can use the same hair and look completely different on you. The difference is the cap — the base the hair is attached to.

Some caps are built from , rows of hair sewn on in strips. Others are , knotted strand by strand into lace or mesh. Where that lace sits decides where a wig looks most natural — and every term here opens a definition if you tap it.

Good to knowPeople notice the hairline and the part first. If you’re choosing where to spend, start there.

02The six you’ll hear about

Lace front

Sheer lace along the front hairline, with the rest built from wefts.

Best for · a natural hairline
Glueless

Secures with combs and an adjustable band — no adhesive.

Best for · first-time wearers
360 lace

Lace around the whole perimeter, not just the front.

Best for · ponytails & updos
Monofilament top

Hand-tied mesh on top, so the part looks like scalp.

Best for · parting freely
Full lace

All lace, all hand-tied. Part it anywhere, wear it up.

Best for · maximum versatility
Topper

A partial piece that clips into your own hair on top.

Best for · thinning at part or crown

04Where to start

If you want the simplest everyday routine, a lace front is where most people begin. If parting matters most, look at a top. If you live in ponytails, consider or .

TipGet measured before you shop. The right matters more than almost any other choice for comfort and security — we measure at every consultation.
Watch outHold off trimming the lace on a new piece until you’re certain. Trimmed lace can’t be undone — ask us to customise it for you.

05Questions to ask at a fitting

  1. What cap size am I, and how was it measured?
  2. Where does the lace sit, and how is it secured?
  3. What suits my face and my own hair?
  4. How do I wash it, and how long should it last with my routine?
  5. Can the hairline, the part or the cut be customised?
