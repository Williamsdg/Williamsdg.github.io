Scalp care

Assess. Treat. Restore.

Sword & Crown offers a clinical approach to scalp and hair wellness designed around individualized assessment, advanced scalp therapies, and purposeful treatment. Every service is customized according to the condition and needs of your scalp and hair.

Your scalp is not treated with a one-size-fits-all protocol. Every Sword & Crown treatment begins with observation and assessment, allowing your service to be tailored to what your scalp and hair actually need. Our goal is not simply beautiful hair. Our goal is a healthier scalp environment, stronger-looking hair, and a treatment plan created specifically for you.

The Root of It All

Comprehensive Scalp & Hair Consultation
45 minutes$95
Details

Great hair care starts with getting to know your roots. This personalized consultation explores your scalp condition, hair quality, current routine, and concerns—including dryness, excess oil, buildup, sensitivity, shedding, or changes in fullness.

Securing your appointment

A valid credit card is required to secure a spa appointment. Your card is stored securely by our payment processor and is not charged when you book — it is kept on file for any charge you authorize under the cancellation policy below.

Before you confirm, you’ll see:

  • Your selected service and enhancements
  • The total appointment price
  • The appointment date, start time, and expected duration
  • The cancellation policy, and an acknowledgment authorizing applicable cancellation or no-show charges to the card on file

Spa cancellations and rescheduling

For spa services, please provide at least 24 hours’ notice. Cancellations or rescheduling 24 hours or more before the appointment carry no charge. Changes from 12 hours to less than 24 hours before the appointment may be charged 50% of the booked total. Changes less than 12 hours before the appointment, or a no-show, may be charged 100%. The booked total includes selected enhancements. Exactly 24 hours carries no fee; exactly 12 hours falls within the 50% window. Card-on-file charges require acceptance of the applicable policy at booking.

Before you book

Spa services focus on scalp care, beauty and comfort. They do not diagnose or treat the underlying medical causes of hair loss. Please tell us about any allergies, sensitivities or scalp conditions when you arrive.
