Hair loss, surgery, and changes to your skin can reshape the way you see yourself. Our Restorative Artistry services offer a compassionate, personalized approach to recreating familiar details or creating something entirely new. Every experience begins with a conversation about your story, your goals, and what feels right for you.

Our resident artist, Natalie Henderson of Natalie Henderson Ink, brings a fine artist’s eye to detailed, natural-looking work. With a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Drawing from the University of Montevallo and two years of tattooing experience, Natalie approaches each appointment with precision and a design tailored to you.

Natalie also works from her private tattoo studio at The Orchard House in Avondale. She joins us at Sword & Crown for consultations on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and on Wednesday and Friday mornings, with treatment appointments planned with you at your consultation.

Reconstructive Nano Brow

Fine detail. Thoughtful shape. Beautifully yours.3.5-hour session · booked after your consultation

Designed for clients experiencing partial or complete eyebrow hair loss, Reconstructive Nano Brows use precise machine-applied hairstrokes to create the appearance of natural brow hair.

Your artist customizes the shape, placement, and pigment selection to complement your features and personal preferences. Whether you’re looking to recreate a familiar brow or discover a new shape, the focus is a considered, balanced result that feels like you.

Final pricing is determined during your consultation based on the extent of hair loss and reconstruction required.

A $250 nonrefundable deposit is required to book your treatment appointment and will be applied toward the total service cost.

Restorative Tattooing

Artistry with purpose. Care in every detail.4-hour session · booked after your consultation

Restorative Tattooing brings thoughtful pigment work to areas affected by surgery, scarring, hair loss, or other changes in appearance. Each service is individually planned around your skin, the treatment area, and your desired outcome, with an honest conversation about what tattooing can achieve.

Areola Tattooing: Customized pigment work to recreate or enhance the appearance of the areola and nipple, including dimensional shading where appropriate.

Scar Camouflage & Cover-Ups: Skin-tone camouflage or artistic tattoo designs intended to soften the visual prominence of suitable, fully healed scars.

Scalp Micropigmentation: Carefully placed pigment impressions that create the appearance of closely cropped follicles or help reduce visible contrast between the scalp and existing hair.

Other Paramedical Tattooing: Individualized restorative pigment work, with suitability and treatment options discussed during consultation.

Your artist will review the proposed design, pigment approach, anticipated appointment needs, and aftercare before treatment is scheduled. Final pricing is quoted at your consultation.

A $150 deposit is required to book your treatment appointment and will be applied toward the total service cost.

Restorative Tattooing is individually planned around your needs. To learn more, call or text 205-259-6911 to discuss your next step.
