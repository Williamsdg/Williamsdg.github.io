Nano brows & reconstructive tattooing

The smallest details.

Sometimes, the smallest details make the biggest difference in feeling like yourself. Our nano brow and restorative tattooing services offer a personal approach to thinning or missing brows, realistic areola tattoos, and scar coverups—with thoughtful attention to your features, your goals, and your story.

Our resident artist, Natalie Henderson of Natalie Henderson Ink, brings a fine artist’s eye to detailed, natural-looking work. With a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Drawing from the University of Montevallo and two years of tattooing experience, Natalie approaches each appointment with precision and a design tailored to you.

Natalie also works from her private tattoo studio at The Orchard House in Avondale. She joins us at Sword & Crown Salon on Tuesdays and Thursdays, with special appointments available by arrangement.

The menu

Consultation

30 minutes

A private conversation with Natalie to explore your goals, review the area, and discuss design, suitability, and what to expect from the process. Designed for clients navigating thinning or missing brows, changes after breast surgery, or healed scars who want to understand their options before scheduling a tattoo session.

Book

Nano Brow Session

3.5 hours

Designed for clients with thinning, sparse, or missing brows, this appointment uses fine, hair-like tattoo strokes to create natural-looking definition. Natalie will work with you on shape and pigment selection before tattooing, then explain healing and aftercare.

Book

Restorative Tattoo Session

3.5 hours

For clients seeking realistic areola tattoos following breast surgery or tattoo designs to cover or soften the appearance of healed scars. Your appointment includes reviewing the area, planning placement and color, completing the agreed tattoo work where suitable, and discussing aftercare; the scope and any additional sessions depend on your individual needs.

Book

Touch Up Session

2 hours

A follow-up appointment for clients whose nano brows or restorative tattoos have healed and need refinement. Natalie will assess the healed result and address areas that may benefit from additional pigment or detail, with timing and suitability determined individually.

Before you book, please read this.

Tattoo services are available only to clients over the age of 18. We do not perform tattoo services on anyone actively undergoing chemotherapy or radiation. We do not recommend tattooing for clients with lupus without approval from their doctor; medical approval does not replace Natalie’s assessment of suitability.
