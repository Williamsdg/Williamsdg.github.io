The small print

Policies.

Written plainly, so nobody has to guess.

Boutique and retail returns

Returns for products, apparel, accessories, tools, and equipment must be initiated within 14 days of delivery or in-store purchase.

Items must be in their original condition, unaltered and unwashed, with original tags, labels, packaging, and included components. Items marked Final Sale are nonrefundable. Shipping charges are nonrefundable for ordinary returns.

Wig returns

Return requests must be initiated within 5 days — counted from delivery for online orders, or from purchase for in-store.

To qualify, the wig must retain its original quality and integrity:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and unaltered
  • Lace uncut
  • Hair not styled, cut, colored, or otherwise changed
  • No makeup stains, styling products, adhesive residue, odors, or other evidence of wear
  • All original accessories and packaging included

Approved returns are subject to a $250 restocking fee per wig, deducted from the refund, along with shipping costs. Returns are inspected before a refund is approved.

Please note: altering a wig — including cutting its lace — makes it ineligible for a change-of-mind return.

Wig consultations

A $250 nonrefundable consultation deposit reserves a 90-minute appointment. The deposit is applied toward the cost of a wig purchased during that consultation.

The appointment includes a private consultation, a wig fitting, and a face-framing cut or bangs for the wig purchased.

More involved styling, coloring, and customizations to fit, hairlines, ear tabs, silicone, or other structural elements cost extra. Additional work and pricing will be discussed before proceeding.

Consultations are available Monday–Friday, with the latest appointment starting at 4:00 p.m. Central Time, subject to availability.

Booking and appointment requests

A contact-form submission is an appointment request, not a confirmed booking. Clients receive confirmation after our team agrees on the service and appointment time.

Service durations vary. Some appointments are completed the same day; repairs, restorations, and more involved alterations may require drop-off and pickup.

Spa services — securing your appointment

A valid credit card is required to secure a spa appointment. The card is stored securely by our payment processor and is not charged when you book; it is kept on file for charges authorized under the spa cancellation policy. Before confirming, you will see your selected service and add-ons, the total appointment price, the date, start time and expected duration, the cancellation policy, and an acknowledgment authorizing applicable charges to the card on file.

Spa appointments are available online on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, starting at 9:00 a.m., 11:00 a.m., 1:00 p.m. or 4:00 p.m. Central Time. For Tuesday or Thursday, or a time you don’t see online, call or text 205-259-6911.

Spa cancellation policy

We understand that plans can change. Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice when canceling your appointment.

  • 24 hours or more before your appointment: No cancellation charge.
  • 12 hours to less than 24 hours before your appointment: A cancellation charge equal to 50% of the booked appointment total.
  • Less than 12 hours before your appointment, or failure to attend: A charge equal to 100% of the booked appointment total.

Applicable charges will be made to the card provided at booking. The booked appointment total includes your selected service and add-ons. A cancellation made exactly 24 hours before the appointment carries no charge; one made exactly 12 hours before carries the 50% charge.

Restorative Artistry — consultation fee and deposits

The $75 consultation fee is paid when you book and is applied toward the cost of services rendered. Your card is securely saved for future authorized charges associated with your services; any future service charges or deposits are communicated and authorized before processing.

Treatment appointments are planned at your consultation and are not booked online. A $250 nonrefundable deposit secures a Reconstructive Nano Brow appointment and a $150 deposit secures a Restorative Tattooing appointment; each is applied toward the total service cost.
