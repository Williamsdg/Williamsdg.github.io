The small print

Policies.

Written plainly, so nobody has to guess. Everything below marked In review is still being finalised and is not yet in force.

Wig returns

Return requests must be initiated within 5 days — counted from delivery for online orders, or from purchase for in-store.

To qualify, the wig must retain its original quality and integrity:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and unaltered
  • Lace uncut
  • Hair not styled, cut, colored, or otherwise changed
  • No makeup stains, styling products, adhesive residue, odors, or other evidence of wear
  • All original accessories and packaging included

Approved returns are subject to a $250 restocking fee per wig, deducted from the refund, along with shipping costs. Returns are inspected before a refund is approved.

Please note: altering a wig — including cutting its lace — makes it ineligible for a change-of-mind return.

Boutique and retail returns

Returns for products, apparel, accessories, tools, and equipment must be initiated within 14 days of delivery or in-store purchase.

Items must be in their original condition, unaltered and unwashed, with original tags, labels, packaging, and included components. Items marked Final Sale are nonrefundable. Shipping charges are nonrefundable for ordinary returns.

Wig consultations

A $250 nonrefundable consultation deposit reserves a 90-minute appointment. The deposit is applied toward the cost of a wig purchased during that consultation.

The appointment includes a private consultation, a wig fitting, and a face-framing cut or bangs for the wig purchased.

More involved styling, coloring, and customizations to fit, hairlines, ear tabs, silicone, or other structural elements cost extra. Additional work and pricing will be discussed before proceeding.

Consultations are available Monday–Friday, with the latest appointment starting at 4:00 p.m. Central Time, subject to availability.

Booking and appointment requests

A contact-form submission is an appointment request, not a confirmed booking. Clients receive confirmation after our team agrees on the service and appointment time.

Service durations vary. Some appointments are completed the same day; repairs, restorations, and more involved alterations may require drop-off and pickup.

The policies below are in review. Elana is still finalising these terms, so they are shown here for review only and are not yet in force. The four policies above are established and current.

Cancellation and rescheduling In review

Please give at least 48 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule.

For wig consultations, one reschedule is allowed with at least 48 hours’ notice, transferring the existing deposit to the new appointment. The deposit remains nonrefundable. Late cancellations, late rescheduling, and missed appointments forfeit the deposit; a new deposit is required to rebook.

For other salon and head spa services: at least 48 hours’ notice, no cancellation charge. Less than 48 hours, 50% of the booked service price. No-show, 100% of the booked service price.

Any service deposit retained counts toward the cancellation charge rather than being charged in addition to it. Fees are disclosed and accepted before booking and are not applied retroactively.

If Sword & Crown cancels an appointment and cannot offer a suitable alternative, the client may choose a refund of the payment for the canceled appointment.

Tattoo-specific deposits and cancellation figures will be published with Natalie’s final menu.

Late arrivals In review

Please arrive on time. Clients arriving more than 15 minutes late may need a shortened appointment or rescheduling, depending on the service and remaining time.

If the service cannot safely or reasonably be completed, the late-cancellation policy may apply. We will explain any changes before proceeding.

Return process and refund timing In review

Contact us before sending a return and include your order number or proof of purchase. We will provide return instructions.

Send an approved return within 7 calendar days of authorization. Customers pay return shipping for change-of-mind returns. Please use protective packaging and a tracked shipping service.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of receiving and inspecting the return. Banks and payment providers may take additional time to post the credit.

Exchanges depend on availability and follow the same eligibility requirements.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect orders In review

Please inspect your order on arrival and contact us promptly if something is damaged, defective, missing, or incorrect. Include your order number and photographs where helpful.

We ask for notification within 48 hours of delivery so we can investigate promptly. This is not a cutoff for reporting a defect that could not reasonably be identified immediately.

Confirmed fulfillment errors or defective merchandise are handled separately from change-of-mind returns. Applicable remedies do not carry the ordinary restocking fee, and we arrange the necessary return shipping.

Custom and altered merchandise In review

Made-to-order wigs, personalized pieces, and items altered specifically for a client are final sale for change-of-mind purposes, where this is disclosed before purchase.

Before custom work begins we confirm the scope, price, required payment, and estimated completion time. Additional work requires your approval.

A discount alone does not make an item final sale; final-sale status is clearly identified.

Service concerns and adjustments In review

Please contact us within 7 calendar days if you have a concern about a completed salon service so we can assess it and discuss an appropriate solution.

An adjustment to the original agreed service is different from requesting a new look or additional work. New requests may incur additional charges.

Completed services are not automatically refundable because of a change of preference. Service errors, damage, and other legitimate concerns are assessed individually. This reporting request does not remove your rights concerning issues discovered later.

Tattoo healing, follow-up appointments, and touch-up pricing follow Natalie’s separately published terms.

Shipping and order changes In review

Shipping charges and estimated processing times appear before checkout. Custom pieces and preorders carry their own stated timelines.

Order-processing time is separate from carrier transit time. If we cannot ship within the promised timeframe we will contact you and offer the choice of consenting to the delay or cancelling for a refund.

Please contact us immediately about an address correction or cancellation. Changes cannot be guaranteed once fulfillment or approved customization has begun.

If a package goes missing, contact us and we will investigate with the carrier.

Privacy, photography, and health information In review

Client photographs, videos, and personal stories require separate permission before marketing use. Receiving a service does not authorize promotional photography.

Please disclose relevant allergies, sensitivities, scalp conditions, and other information needed for your service. Sensitive medical details should not be sent through the general appointment form.

Head spa services focus on scalp care, beauty, and comfort. They do not diagnose or treat the underlying medical causes of hair loss.

Tattoo eligibility. Tattoo services are available only to clients over the age of 18. We do not perform tattoo services on anyone actively undergoing chemotherapy or radiation. We do not recommend tattooing for clients with lupus without approval from their doctor; medical approval does not replace Natalie’s assessment of suitability.
