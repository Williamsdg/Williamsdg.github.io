The route at Alabama Adventure
Your night, start to finish

Drive the lights. Then stay a while.

1
Show your ticket at the gate

Scan from your phone. One price covers everyone in the car.

2
Drive the route

A 32-foot waving Barbie, a prehistoric Christmas with life-size dinosaurs, the BIGFOOT monster truck and two million lights. Plan on 20 to 45 minutes, longer on busy weekends.

Birmingham FAQ

Good to know before you go.

When can I use my ticket?

Any time after you buy it, as long as you visit in that ticket's window. Weekday tickets are good Monday to Thursday. Any Day tickets work every night. November tickets are good through November 30 only.

Is Sunday a weekday?

No. Weekday tickets cover Monday to Thursday. For a Friday, Saturday or Sunday visit you need an Any Day ticket, a November ticket (in November) or a season pass.

Can I buy at the gate?

Yes, once the show is open. Gate prices are higher than online, and the Santa Saver and Weekday tickets are online only.

Who gets the $5 heroes discount?

Military members, first responders, nurses, teachers and seniors, off the gate price on Monday to Thursday nights.

Is it one price per car?

Yes. One carload, one price. Limos and party buses have their own ticket.

Where is it?

At Alabama Adventure, 4599 Splash Adventure Parkway in Bessemer. Directions are below.
