What we noticed

Three things on the Birmingham page today

Checked on October 2, 2026, about seven weeks before opening night. None of this is a knock on anyone. A national template just can't know about Bessemer.

Fix this week

The Facebook feed is South Carolina's

The "About the Venue" box on the Birmingham page embeds Magic of Lights – Hollywild, the Upstate SC show. Right now it shows a "Now Closed! Thank you for a magical season" post brightened by FOX Carolina. Birmingham's own page is MagicofLightsBirmingham. The Directions heading under it is also empty.

Costing ticket sales

Five tickets, no help choosing

November Only, Santa Saver, Weekday, Season Pass and Limo each have different day rules and deadlines. A family planning a December Saturday has to read all five to work out that the Santa Saver is their ticket, and only until November 19. The concept's calendar answers it in one tap.

Unsold space

Birmingham's sponsor and beneficiary sections are switched off

The page template has "Event Sponsors", "Event Beneficiaries" and "Event-Specific FAQ" sections, but they're hidden on the Birmingham page. Apart from the WBRC logo, there is nowhere to show a local sponsor or a charity partner. That's room for local businesses who would pay to be in front of every family that drives through.

What we'd build

A Birmingham home for the show

It can live on alabamaadventure.com, for example at /lights, so it builds the park's own search traffic, while tickets keep going through the same Tixr link.

01 · Plan-your-night

Ticket finder and calendar

Every open night colored by its best ticket, with that night's hours, gate price and the hometown-heroes discount.

  • Fewer "which ticket do I need?" messages
  • Pushes November nights and weeknights
02 · Tonight

A status bar the team updates from a phone

Open, weather call, or "long line, try after 8". You post it from a simple dashboard and it shows on the page.

  • No developer needed on a cold Saturday night
  • One place for weather closures
03 · Sponsors

Local sponsor program

Named spots to sell: a route scene, the Holiday Village, the heroes nights. Each comes with a sponsor page and a one-page leave-behind your team can email.

  • Fills the empty sponsor section
  • Turns logos into revenue
04 · Groups

Group and private-event requests

Churches, offices and teams send a request with headcount, cars and dates. It lands in an inbox and a list, not a generic contact form.

  • Easier to quote
  • Easy to follow up next year
