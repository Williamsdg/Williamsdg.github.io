How ordering works

Two easy ways to order

Shop the Collection

For ready-to-personalize retail pieces

  1. Pick your piece from the current lineup.
  2. Choose your options — size, color, placement, and personalization.
  3. Check out securely — payment handled by a trusted processor like Stripe or Square.
  4. We stitch and ship (or arrange local pickup in Leeds).
Start Shopping

Start a Custom Order

For logos, teams, businesses, and bulk runs

  1. Tell us about the job — garments, quantity, deadline — and upload your artwork.
  2. Get a personal quote, usually within one business day.
  3. Approve your proof before a single stitch runs.
  4. Track your order — every job gets an order number like WAL-1024.
