The Case for Action

Record momentum, unmanaged risk

The College is growing faster than the technology organization that supports it. Four realities make this the moment to act.

Growth engines, all technology-dependent

  • 59% of students touch online learning — 13% fully online, 46% partly online.
  • The Auburn aviation A&P partnership opened its hangar April 2026, projected to grow from 24 to 120+ students by Fall 2026.
  • Health-science simulation, dual enrollment on high-school campuses, and advanced-manufacturing pipelines all run on tech.
  • $3M City of Opelika gift plus new residence hall and science-lab plans create capital-project windows this plan must be ready for.

The foundation hasn't kept pace

  • ≈ 4–5 IT staff for 5,000+ students across 3 campuses — no CIO or director-level leader, no IT seat on executive council.
  • No technology goal in the 2026–2029 institutional strategic plan; prior 3-year replacement cycles have lapsed.
  • Hardened compliance: GLBA tested every Title IV audit, ADA deadline April 2027, an aging endpoint fleet on unsupported operating systems.
  • The 2026 Canvas/Instructure breach (thousands of institutions, Alabama colleges included) shows system platforms carry local risk.
Current Technology Environment

Strong platforms, thin foundations

The College rides solid System-level platforms — the gaps are in local leadership, security discipline, and standardization.

Working in our favor

  • OneACCS / Ellucian Banner — ERP run at the System level; local dollars free for differentiation.
  • Canvas on the shared statewide ACCS platform.
  • Microsoft 365 migration done (2024) with MFA and unified identity — the launchpad for zero trust, Apple Business Manager + Jamf device management, Teams Phone.
  • Dedicated distance-education / instructional-design leadership.
  • Favorable ACCS funding climate and local capital investment.

Gaps carrying real risk

  • No IT leadership role; ≈ 4–5 FTE vs. sector norm of ~4% of institutional staff.
  • GLBA program elements (risk assessment, IR plan, annual report) not evidenced — audit exposure.
  • Room-by-room AV with no standard, no remote monitoring — every trouble call is a campus drive.
  • No lecture-capture platform or video storage policy; recordings drift into SharePoint/OneDrive.
  • No device standard or refresh calendar; legacy CMS/ColdFusion web estate; no WCAG program.

Initiative 3 · Spotlight

One standard classroom, built for a small team

One touch to teach, zero touch to record, remote-first to fix — the same cloned Q-SYS template in every room on all three campuses.

The Q-SYS standard room

Standardize, then scale

  • Q-SYS Core (≈ $3.3K) runs audio, video routing, and control on one network processor — the same cloned template in every room, est. $12–25K/room.
  • Reflect Enterprise Manager (free base; Plus ≈ $21/room/mo): the helpdesk sees every room's health and operates any touch panel from a browser — most calls fixed without a campus drive.
  • Stream Deck + Bitfocus Companion ($80–250 vs. $1,500–3,500 touch panels): one-button presets over Q-SYS's open QRC protocol.
  • WolfVision Cynap Pure/Pure Pro (≈ $0.9–2.1K): app-free wireless BYOD sharing; Core Pro adds native Panopto auto-upload.
  • Free Q-SYS Level 1/2 certification for every technician — no proprietary programmer dependency.

Rollout & offsets

FY27 — Pilot6 rooms (2 per campus) + Reflect monitoring; measure the trouble-call delta.
FY28–31 — Scale10–12 rooms/year to the standard; ≥ 45 rooms by FY31.
New constructionOpelika expansion, aviation, Valley get the standard in the building spec — never retrofit.

Implementation Roadmap

Three phases, five years

Foundation first, then standardize and scale, then optimize and extend — each phase gated on the prior one's evidence.

Phase 1 — Foundation

FY 2026–27
  • Hire IT Director/CIO; charter governance committee
  • GLBA formalized; EDR/MDR live; Mac fleet standardized
  • Q-SYS pilot — 6 rooms (2/campus) + Reflect
  • Video CMS RFP; accessibility audit; AI policy
  • Network assessment; device standards published

Phase 2 — Standardize & Scale

FY 2027–28 → 2028–29
  • ADA / WCAG 2.1 AA compliance (Apr 2027)
  • 10–12 rooms/yr + tracking cameras; Cynap BYOD
  • Wi-Fi 6E refresh + segmentation; Teams Phone
  • Jamf + ABM zero-touch; ITSM SLAs; +2 staff
  • CRM/chatbot pilot → production; Power BI layer

FY 2029–30 → 2030–31
  • VisionSuite AI tracking in flagship halls
  • Capture standard in all high-enrollment rooms

  • Refresh cycles running on calendar
  • Day-one fit-out of new construction; FY32–36 plan

Funding Offsets to Pursue

Grants and gifts that reduce the ask

The incremental cost is real, but not all of it lands on the general fund. Six offset lanes carry the load:

  • Perkins V — CTE equipment: cameras, sim/VR, lab workstations, aviation tech (via ACCS).
  • NSF ATE — technician education (mechatronics, aviation, CNC).
  • Title III Part A — student-success technology (CRM, advising).
  • Alabama SLCGP — cyber grant subawards (verify eligibility with Alabama OIT).
  • City of Opelika $3M gift — fold AV/network into construction budgets.
  • ACCS leverage — system pricing, shared security services.
Assumptions & Items to Verify
  • Exact IT staffing, reporting line, and current operating budget (assumed ≈ 4–5 FTE).
  • Endpoint fleet count (≈ 400–450 devices) and Mac standardization / macOS version status.
  • Network/wireless inventory, AREN/ISP contracts, and PBX age for the Teams Phone timeline.
  • ADA Title II deadline classification for ACCS colleges — confirm April 2027 with counsel.
  • Whether SUSCC data was in scope of the 2026 Instructure/Canvas breach.
  • Alabama SLCGP eligibility and current Title III competition status.
