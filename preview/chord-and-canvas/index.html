Why We Gather

Turning creativity into healing

Chord & Canvas was created to unite artists, musicians, and community members around a shared purpose: making a meaningful difference through creativity and generosity.

Founded by coastal artist and educator Shannon Harris, Chord & Canvas is a nonprofit dedicated to art and music therapy. It grew from a simple belief — that creativity is one of God's greatest gifts and a powerful tool for healing.

Through live experiences and hands-on workshops, Chord & Canvas brings creative healing to individuals facing real obstacles — restoring confidence, purpose, and connection one story at a time. Every ticket, sponsorship, and donation goes directly toward that work.
