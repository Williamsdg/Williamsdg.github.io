We believe every person has a story worth telling.

Sometimes music helps us hear it, and art helps us share it.

Chord & Canvas is a nonprofit organization dedicated to enriching lives through art, music, and meaningful human connection. We create inclusive experiences for individuals with disabilities, those in recovery, people on the autism spectrum, veterans, seniors, schools, and community organizations. Whether accompanied by live musicians or thoughtfully curated playlists from our music partners, every Chord & Canvas experience is designed to inspire creativity, build confidence, and foster community.

Our Story

Where music, art & community come together.

Inspired by music. Created with purpose.

Chord & Canvas was founded by coastal artist and educator Shannon Harris, who has spent more than 25 years helping people discover the creativity already inside them. After a macular degeneration diagnosis at 49, Shannon turned a setback into a calling — a belief that creativity is one of God's greatest gifts and a powerful tool for healing.

What began with a paintbrush and a song has grown into something bigger: intentional environments where music and art work together to promote connection, creativity, and well-being. Our goal is simple — create spaces where people can slow down, create, and reconnect, with themselves and with one another.

Our Programs

Whether accompanied by a live performance or a thoughtfully curated playlist from one of our music partners, every experience is designed to inspire creativity, conversation, and connection.

Studio Sessions

Relax in a beautifully designed studio while painting alongside a carefully curated playlist featuring music from our artist partners — or a live guest musician.

Live Music + Live Painting

An immersive experience where musicians perform while artists create in real time — transforming music into visual art at festivals, fundraisers, and celebrations.

Community Outreach

Taking Chord & Canvas into schools, recovery centers, disability organizations, hospitals, senior living communities, churches, and nonprofit partners.

Corporate & Team Experiences

Creative team-building events for businesses and organizations — slow down, make something together, and reconnect as a team.
