— 01

A family-owned operator with a quarter-century of stewardship.

Tri-Cities, WA

Founded in 1997 by Vijay and Mita Patel, A-1 Hospitality Group has grown from a single property into a multi-brand operator across the Pacific Northwest, Idaho, Montana, and Texas.

We are family owned and operated, and we pride ourselves on our customer service across a wide range of hotel brands. Our properties consistently rank highly in guest ratings within the regions we serve — the result of disciplined operations, a long-term outlook, and a team that has stayed with us for decades.

The success of our mission depends upon our employees, so we strive to create a healthy, professional environment where they can build careers, not just hold jobs.

— 04

Three services. One operating philosophy.

01 / Development

Development

We have a track record of success in developing and managing hotel properties in the Pacific Northwest. Unparalleled guest service is our cornerstone — and the reason our properties consistently rank highly within the regions they serve.

  • Site selection & feasibility
  • Brand & flag negotiation
  • Construction oversight
  • Pre-opening & ramp-up
02 / Investment

Investment

We welcome investor inquiries from respected companies, institutions, and individuals who share our long-term vision for success. Hotels that distinguish themselves with excellent service and a clean, comfortable experience see the highest occupancy.

  • Direct booking & rate strategy
  • Guest retention & repeat bookings
  • Service, location, security, amenities
  • Long-term partnerships
03 / Management

Management

Hotel management built on more than two decades of operational experience. We seek partners that wish to bring our proven system of hospitality management to their properties — staff development, guest-focused operations, accountable reporting.

  • Full-service property management
  • Revenue strategy & reporting
  • Sales, marketing, F&B
  • HR, training & retention
