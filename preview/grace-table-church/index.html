about us Who we are

A church for people who aren't sure about church.

We started in a living room in 2018 with twelve people, a Bible, and a French press. We've grown into a community of about 400 — and somehow it still feels like a living room.

We believe Sunday should feel less like a performance and more like a family meal. So we gather around the same idea Jesus gathered around: a table. Bread, wine, story, stranger, friend. Everyone fed. Nobody turned away.

  • Non-denominational, gospel-centered, and unapologetically rooted in Scripture.
  • Casual on the outside. Serious about Jesus on the inside.
  • Led by a team of pastors, not a single celebrity.
  • You don't have to believe yet. You just have to come.

What We Believe

Simple. Scriptural. Centered on Jesus.

We're not the first to say these things, and we won't be the last. They're the historic beliefs of the Christian faith, written down across two thousand years of saints and sinners — and just as true on a Tuesday as a Sunday.

i.

The Bible is true.

God-breathed, trustworthy, and sufficient. We read it together — verse by verse, book by book — because the Word doesn't return void.

2 Timothy 3:16–17
ii.

Jesus is the center.

Fully God, fully man. Crucified, buried, and raised. The only way home and worth every hymn ever written.

John 14:6 · Colossians 1:15–20
iii.

Grace is the floor.

You can't earn your way in. You also can't earn your way out. The cross says enough — for the addict, the doubter, the deacon, and the prodigal.

Ephesians 2:8–9
iv.

The Spirit is alive in us.

We aren't trying harder; we're being made new. The Holy Spirit comforts, convicts, and carries us further than willpower ever could.

John 14:26 · Galatians 5:22–23
v.

The Church is family.

Not a building. Not a brand. A blood-bought, multi-generational, every-tribe family that eats together, weeps together, and waits for the King together.

Acts 2:42–47
vi.

Hope wins.

Christ is coming back. Every tear, every grave, every aching prayer — answered. We worship like the end of the story is already written, because it is.

Revelation 21:1–5
