The signature

Ten thousand volts, one piece at a time.

Fractal burning sends high voltage across damp hardwood and lets the current find its own way through the grain. What it leaves behind looks like lightning caught mid-strike — branching, forked, and impossible to draw by hand.

The wood decides the pattern. That is the whole point: the piece on your counter cannot be reproduced, not by us and not by anyone.

The makers

“We create handmade home decor that celebrates your personal style.”

Spritz and Pyro is two people. Cait and Phil design it, burn it, print it, weave it, pack it, and answer the email you send about it — from a workshop in South Florida.

That is the trade-off, and it is the good kind: nothing here ships the same afternoon, and nothing here is a copy of something else.
