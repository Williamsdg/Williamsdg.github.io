Report a problem

Emergencies — a main break, a downed line, a fire — call (601) 635-2761 or 911. Do not use this form.

Where this goes. Your request lands in the Town Hall dashboard the moment you submit it, gets a number, and gets assigned to Public Works or the Police Department. You get a text at every step. Nothing sits in a Facebook comment thread hoping somebody scrolls past it.

Request a public record

Mississippi's Public Records Act gives you the right to inspect and copy the Town's records. The Town responds within seven working days under the policy the Board has adopted, and must tell you in writing if it is denying any part of your request and why.

The Town may charge the actual cost of copying and staff time. You will get a written estimate before any work begins.
