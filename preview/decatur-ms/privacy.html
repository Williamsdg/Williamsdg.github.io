Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The Town of Decatur (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates this website. This policy explains what information the website collects and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

This website does not require an account, does not set advertising or analytics cookies, and does not track you across other sites. Our web host records standard server logs (the address of the page requested, the time, your IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running. Those logs are kept for a limited time and are not used to identify individual visitors.

If you contact us by email, by phone, or through the feedback form (which opens a message in your own email program), we receive the information you choose to send.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate and maintain this website
  • To respond to your questions, requests and feedback
  • To comply with applicable law

3. Payments

Water bills are paid through NexBillPay and court fines through GovPayNow. Those services are operated by their own companies under their own privacy policies; this website does not collect or store payment details.

4. How we share your information

We share information only with the service providers that host this website and deliver our email, and only so that they can provide those services. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.

5. Government records

This website publishes government records, such as ordinances, that may reference individuals by name. Those records are published under Mississippi’s public records and open meetings laws, and the rights described in this policy do not apply to information in public government records.

6. Your rights

You may ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to the record-keeping obligations of a Mississippi municipality. Contact Town Hall at the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
