Volunteer Fire Department

Dial 911 for emergencies. Non-emergency calls: (601) 635-2761.

  • Police Department
  • Volunteer Fire Department
  • Public Works

Decatur Volunteer Fire Department currently has 22 members, 5 emergency medical technicians, 3 paramedics and 2 firefighters 1,001 certified.

We are fully equipped with 3 Class A Pumpers, Rescue Equipment (Jaws), 1 Wildland Fire Vehicle, 1 Quick Response Vehicle and 1 Off Road Rescue Vehicle with a response area of 65 sq. miles.

Emergency: dial 911.

Non-emergency calls: (601) 635-2761.
