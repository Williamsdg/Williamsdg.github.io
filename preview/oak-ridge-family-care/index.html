Common Questions

Quick answers

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — all three providers are accepting new patients, and most new-patient appointments are available within the same week. Book online or call us.

Do you offer same-day sick visits?

We hold same-day slots every morning for established patients. Call before 10 a.m. or message us through the portal.

How do prescription refills work?

Request refills through the Patient Portal or have your pharmacy send the request. Most refills are processed the same business day.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your insurance card, photo ID, a list of current medications, and any recent lab work. Better yet — complete your forms in the portal and just bring yourself.

Do you see patients via telehealth?

Yes. Follow-ups, medication checks, and many minor illnesses can be handled over a secure video visit booked directly from the portal.
