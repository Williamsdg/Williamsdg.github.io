Pricing Notes

Assumptions & clarifications

  • Independent third-party VPAT assessment expenses are excluded from the proposed project price in accordance with the County's published clarification; Williams Digital's support of the VPAT process and remediation of applicable implementation issues is included.
  • Translated content will be supplied or approved by Olmsted County/OVG unless professional translation services are separately added to the project scope; the multilingual content architecture is included in the base price.
  • Photography, video, and brand assets will be supplied by Olmsted County and OVG.
  • Fees or subscription costs charged by third-party platforms selected by Olmsted County or OVG (ticketing, CRM, consent management, and similar) are not included.
  • Additional major feature development beyond the proposed scope is available and will be quoted prior to any work.
