The Construction Story

Something big is taking shape.

Follow the journey from state funding to opening day — and be there when the doors open.

2023

State funding secured

$8 million in State of Minnesota funding kick-starts the region's next great gathering place.

July 2025

Groundbreaking

Construction begins on the 80,000-square-foot exhibition center at Graham Park.

Spring 2026

Final structural beam raised

The building tops out as the structure takes its full shape over the fairgrounds.

Summer 2026

Outdoor program areas open in time for the Olmsted County Fair.
