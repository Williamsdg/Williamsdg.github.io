Executive Summary

A sales-ready, secure, accessible platform — live January 1, 2027

Williams Digital proposes a modern digital platform for Graham Park Exhibition Center designed around the County's two stated objectives: public awareness and venue sales.

The proposed website is a premier venue destination experience — not a traditional municipal website — organized around two visitor journeys: Attend an Event and Plan an Event. It is built on a modern, security-conscious architecture that directly satisfies the County's published requirements: Microsoft Entra ID authentication with MFA, role-based administration, audit logging, WAF-protected managed hosting with a 99.9% availability commitment, multilingual capability at launch, WCAG-conformant development with VPAT support, a CMS supporting recurring events and daily updates by approximately three OVG staff, and a fully operational launch by January 1, 2027.

Williams Digital is a principal-led firm: the person who signs this proposal is the person who designs, builds, deploys, and supports the platform. Proposed pricing — provided in the separately submitted Williams Digital – Cost Proposal — falls within Olmsted County's published implementation budget, and the proposed annual managed-hosting cost aligns with the County's stated annual website budget.

An interactive preliminary concept accompanies this proposal at williamsdigital.io/preview/graham-park/.

Understanding of Graham Park

A new destination — and what its website must do

Graham Park Exhibition Center is more than a new building. It is a new destination for exhibitions, agricultural events, entertainment, community gatherings, sporting events, ticketed experiences, meetings, and regional tourism.

Williams Digital understands the website's mission as defined by the County and OVG: a sales tool and public information destination serving attendees, exhibitors and vendors, sponsors, 4-H and fair participants, meeting planners, show organizers, ticket purchasers, and the general public — supporting 40,000+ average monthly visits with event-driven traffic spikes, daily content changes by a small OVG team, and a site that will continue evolving as the facility approaches and passes its opening.

The website will be designed around two primary visitor journeys:

Attend an Event

Quickly discover upcoming events, obtain tickets, review event details, find parking information, understand policies, receive ticket alerts, and prepare for a visit.

Plan an Event

Explore the facility, understand available spaces and capabilities, review planning information, and begin the process of bringing an event to Graham Park.

The result will be a scalable, accessible, marketing-ready website capable of evolving alongside the Exhibition Center after its opening.

Accessibility · WCAG · VPAT

Accessibility by design

Accessibility will not be treated as a plugin, overlay, or final-stage addition.

The website will be designed and developed to conform with applicable federal accessibility requirements for local government websites and, at minimum, WCAG 2.1 Level AA or the applicable standard required by law at the time of launch.

  • Semantic HTML & logical heading structure
  • Keyboard navigation & visible focus states
  • Skip navigation & descriptive link text
  • Accessible forms & error messaging
  • Appropriate ARIA usage
  • Adequate contrast & scalable typography
  • Image alternative text
  • Media captions and transcripts where applicable
  • Zoom support
  • Reduced-motion support
  • Accessible modal and announcement behavior
  • Screen-reader consideration
  • Automated accessibility testing
  • Manual keyboard testing
  • Device and browser review
  • Pre-launch remediation

Williams Digital will support the post-development VPAT process and address applicable technical accessibility issues identified in the website implementation. Independent third-party VPAT expenses are excluded where permitted by the RFP clarification.

Public accessibility page — Graham Park is for everyone.

The public website will include a dedicated accessibility section covering website accessibility, venue accessibility, accessible parking and seating, entrances, accommodations, service animals, and accessibility contacts — including a Report an Accessibility Issue pathway.

Security · Entra ID · MFA · Audit Logging

Enterprise authentication and security-conscious development

Microsoft Entra ID

Administrative authentication will integrate with Microsoft Entra ID. Backend access will support Microsoft Single Sign-On, Multi-Factor Authentication, Microsoft Authenticator where applicable, role-based permissions, and secure administrative access.

The initial permission model will support Administrator and Marketing-oriented roles, with additional granular roles available as needed. Final access roles will be developed collaboratively with Olmsted County and OVG.

Audit logging

Administrative activity will be logged. At minimum, relevant records will include user, timestamp, and action. Where practical, audit data will be structured to facilitate ingestion or review through County security/SIEM workflows.

Password-protected content

The website will support restricted pages for audiences including media, promoters, event partners, private planning resources, and nonpublic event information. Access will be implemented using appropriate authentication or password protection based on the specific use case.

Security controls

Security will be incorporated throughout development and hosting. Controls will include, as applicable: WAF protection, secure HTTPS and modern TLS, Entra ID authentication with MFA, role-based access, audit logging, secure APIs, dependency monitoring, vulnerability scanning, least-privilege access, environment separation, security headers, OWASP-aligned development practices, and controlled production deployments.

Williams Digital will support and cooperate with all required pre-launch penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, and security review activities and will remediate identified issues within Williams Digital's implementation that are necessary to satisfy the agreed launch requirements.

Performance & scalability

The architecture will be designed around the County's expected traffic profile — 40,000+ average monthly visits with event-driven spikes around on-sales, major events, and the facility's opening. Performance measures will include global CDN caching, image optimization, code splitting and application-level caching where appropriate, continuous performance monitoring, and scalable cloud infrastructure that absorbs traffic surges without manual intervention.

Backup & disaster recovery

The hosting environment will include automated backups of site content and configuration, separation of production and staging environments, documented restoration procedures, and defined incident-recovery practices. In the event of a service disruption, Williams Digital will restore service from the most recent viable backup and communicate status directly to designated County/OVG contacts. Backup frequency and retention specifics will be confirmed with the County during discovery and reflected in the executed agreement.

Analytics · Marketing · Privacy

A marketing-ready platform

Marketing & analytics

The website will be structured to support Graham Park's planned digital advertising strategy across Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok.

Privacy & consent architecture

Because the County and OVG have not yet selected a first-party consent-management platform, marketing and advertising tags will be implemented through a structured tag-management layer designed to accommodate the eventual consent-management solution selected by the County and OVG — rather than hard-coding tracking that would later need to be rebuilt. All tracking implementations will be configured in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

SEO

Technical SEO will be incorporated throughout development: clean URLs, editable titles and meta descriptions, XML sitemap, robots controls, canonical URLs where appropriate, Open Graph and social sharing metadata, structured heading hierarchy, optimized images, performance optimization, and event schema markup. Structured event data will be implemented where practical to improve event discoverability through search engines.

Integrations & Future Architecture

Integration-ready, not integration-dependent

Ticketing

Because OVG's ticketing provider has not yet been publicly identified, Williams Digital proposes a two-stage approach:

Launch capability. Event pages support direct outbound ticket purchasing links.

Future capability. The architecture will support an embedded or API-based ticketing integration once the selected ticketing provider, documentation, and implementation requirements are available. A not-to-exceed integration allowance is identified in the separately submitted Williams Digital – Cost Proposal.

Future API capability

The architecture will accommodate future communication with external systems — ticketing, CRM, email marketing, SMS, mobile applications, catering, venue-management systems, RFP systems, and analytics platforms. The objective is to create an integration-ready platform without making the launch dependent upon systems that have not yet been selected.

Project Methodology

How we'll work together

The project will run as a direct, collaborative engagement between Williams Digital and the County/OVG project team. Work proceeds through structured phases — discovery, information architecture, design, development, content population, QA, and launch — with regular check-ins and a standing staging environment where stakeholders review real, working pages throughout development rather than waiting for a single big reveal.

  • Scheduled progress check-ins with the County/OVG project team
  • A staging environment available to stakeholders for the duration of the project
  • Design and functionality approvals at defined milestones, aligned to the project schedule
  • A shared, running punch list so open items are always visible
  • Direct communication with the developer — questions answered by the person doing the work

Browser & device testing

The website will be tested against current mainstream releases of Chrome, Safari, Edge, and Firefox, with responsive testing across representative mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports — consistent with the County's confirmation that standard responsive-design compatibility is the requirement.
