Project Vision

A digital platform for Minnesota's newest gathering place

Graham Park Exhibition Center is more than a new building. It is a new destination for exhibitions, agricultural events, entertainment, community gatherings, sporting events, ticketed experiences, meetings, and regional tourism.

Williams Digital proposes a modern digital platform designed to serve as both a sales tool and public information destination for Graham Park Exhibition Center. The website will be designed around two primary visitor journeys:

Attend an Event

Quickly discover upcoming events, obtain tickets, review event details, find parking information, understand policies, receive ticket alerts, and prepare for a visit.

Plan an Event

Explore the facility, understand available spaces and capabilities, review planning information, and begin the process of bringing an event to Graham Park.

The result will be a scalable, accessible, marketing-ready website capable of evolving alongside the Exhibition Center after its opening.

This intentionally aligns the homepage with the two major business objectives identified by Olmsted County and OVG: public awareness and venue sales.

Ticket alerts may link to an internal Graham Park event page, an external ticket purchasing page, or a future embedded / API-based ticketing experience.

Reusable countdown component

Countdown functionality will not be limited to the facility opening. Staff can create countdowns for ticket on-sale dates, event openings, registration deadlines, doors opening, special announcements, and venue milestones. Each countdown can be activated, scheduled, edited, or removed from the CMS.

Announcement / splash system

OVG staff will have the ability to activate a prominent announcement overlay or banner with enable/disable control, headline, message, CTA label and destination, and optional start and expiration times. The implementation will maintain keyboard accessibility and appropriate screen-reader behavior.

Venue Experience

One space. Thousands of possibilities.

The website will visually demonstrate the versatility of Graham Park Exhibition Center across potential event categories:

EquestrianAgricultureExhibitionsSporting EventsEntertainmentCommunityMeetingsPrivate Events

Each experience can lead visitors directly into venue information or an event inquiry.

Interactive venue explorer — Find your space.

A visual facility experience may allow users to explore available spaces — Main Arena, practice/warm-up areas, spectator areas, Activity Pad, Support Plaza, and meeting/event spaces. Selecting a space can reveal dimensions, capacities, potential configurations, event types, downloadable information, relevant photography, and an inquiry CTA.

The final facility map and specifications will be based on information supplied by Olmsted County and OVG.

Plan your event — Bring your vision here.

The venue sales experience will be designed around event planners, show organizers, promoters, exhibitors, sponsors, and potential clients. The inquiry process can collect organization and contact information, event type and name, estimated attendance, preferred and alternative dates, requested space, event requirements, food and beverage needs, production requirements, and additional information.

The form architecture will allow future routing into OVG's selected CRM, venue-management, or RFP system.

Food & beverage

A dedicated Food & Beverage section can provide catering information, menus, policies, planning guidance, contact information, and links to future catering platforms. The initial implementation will remain flexible so a more advanced catering integration can be introduced later without rebuilding the website.

Ticketing integration

Because OVG's ticketing provider has not yet been publicly identified, Williams Digital proposes a two-stage approach:

Launch capability. Event pages support direct outbound ticket purchasing links.

Future capability. The architecture will support an embedded or API-based ticketing integration once the selected ticketing provider, documentation, and implementation requirements are available. A not-to-exceed integration allowance is identified in the separately submitted Williams Digital – Cost Proposal.

Content Management

Built to be managed.

The content-management system will be designed for nontechnical Graham Park staff. Approximately three initial administrative users are anticipated, primarily Graham Park General Management and Marketing personnel.

Staff will be able to manage:

  • Pages & homepage features
  • Events, recurring events & event status
  • Announcements & countdowns
  • Ticket links
  • Venue information
  • FAQs & policies
  • News
  • Documents
  • Sponsors
  • Promotional content
  • SEO metadata

Approximately 20 pages and three principal page templates are anticipated for the initial launch, while the system will remain scalable as the facility grows.

Microsoft Entra ID

Administrative authentication will integrate with Microsoft Entra ID. Backend access will support Microsoft Single Sign-On, Multi-Factor Authentication, Microsoft Authenticator where applicable, role-based permissions, and secure administrative access.

The initial permission model will support Administrator and Marketing-oriented roles, with additional granular roles available as needed. Final access roles will be developed collaboratively with Olmsted County and OVG.

Audit logging

Administrative activity will be logged. At minimum, relevant records will include user, timestamp, and action. Where practical, audit data will be structured to facilitate ingestion or review through County security/SIEM workflows.

Password-protected content

The website will support restricted pages for audiences including media, promoters, event partners, private planning resources, and nonpublic event information. Access will be implemented using appropriate authentication or password protection based on the specific use case.

Multilingual capability

Multilingual functionality will be available at launch. The content architecture will allow equivalent page and content structures to support additional languages without rebuilding the website. The specific languages and translation workflow will be finalized with Olmsted County and OVG during discovery. Translation services and translated copy will be supplied or approved by OVG unless otherwise added to the project scope.

Accessibility

Accessibility by design

Accessibility will not be treated as a plugin, overlay, or final-stage addition.

The website will be designed and developed to conform with applicable federal accessibility requirements for local government websites and, at minimum, WCAG 2.1 Level AA or the applicable standard required by law at the time of launch.

  • Semantic HTML & logical heading structure
  • Keyboard navigation & visible focus states
  • Skip navigation & descriptive link text
  • Accessible forms & error messaging
  • Appropriate ARIA usage
  • Adequate contrast & scalable typography
  • Image alternative text
  • Media captions and transcripts where applicable
  • Zoom support
  • Reduced-motion support
  • Accessible modal and announcement behavior
  • Screen-reader consideration
  • Automated accessibility testing
  • Manual keyboard testing
  • Device and browser review
  • Pre-launch remediation

Williams Digital will support the post-development VPAT process and address applicable technical accessibility issues identified in the website implementation. Independent third-party VPAT expenses are excluded where permitted by the RFP clarification.

Public accessibility page — Graham Park is for everyone.

The public website will include a dedicated accessibility section covering website accessibility, venue accessibility, accessible parking and seating, entrances, accommodations, service animals, and accessibility contacts — including a Report an Accessibility Issue pathway.

Marketing, SEO & Integrations

A marketing-ready, integration-ready platform

Marketing & analytics

The website will be structured to support Graham Park's planned digital advertising strategy across Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok. Tracking implementations will be configured in accordance with applicable privacy requirements and the consent-management strategy selected by Olmsted County and OVG.

SEO

Technical SEO will be incorporated throughout development: clean URLs, editable titles and meta descriptions, XML sitemap, robots controls, canonical URLs where appropriate, Open Graph and social sharing metadata, structured heading hierarchy, optimized images, performance optimization, and event schema markup. Structured event data will be implemented where practical to improve event discoverability through search engines.

Future API capability

The architecture will accommodate future communication with external systems — ticketing, CRM, email marketing, SMS, mobile applications, catering, venue-management systems, RFP systems, and analytics platforms.

The objective is to create an integration-ready platform without making the launch dependent upon systems that have not yet been selected.

Project Risk Management

Anticipating what could go wrong — and planning for it

Williams Digital has identified the three most significant risks to a January 1, 2027 launch and structured the project to mitigate each:

RiskMitigation
Compressed scheduleMitigated through overlapping design/development phases and scheduled stakeholder review windows.
Late content or brandingMitigated using reusable page templates, component-based architecture, staging environments, and phased content population.
Unselected third-party platformsMitigated through API-ready architecture and outbound-link functionality so the January 1 launch is not dependent on ticketing or CRM vendor selection.
