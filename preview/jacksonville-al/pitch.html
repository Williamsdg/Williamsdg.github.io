The Opportunity

Four things holding the current site back

01

The homepage is a link farm

Menus, notices, and department pages compete with no hierarchy. Residents come to do one thing — pay a bill, report an issue — and have to hunt for it.

02

The college-town story is invisible

JSU brings 9,200 students, faculty recruits, and thousands of game-day visitors — and the city's site gives them nothing. The single biggest brand asset in Calhoun County goes unused.

03

Economic development is buried

A business or family comparing Jacksonville against Oxford or Anniston should see growth, workforce, and opportunity in seconds. Today that case is scattered or missing — and it's exactly where cities win grants.

04

The visuals feel dated

Small images, inconsistent spacing, old-style layouts. Perception matters — to students deciding to stay after graduation, to families relocating, and to businesses choosing a storefront.

More Than a Facelift

What a modern municipal site actually delivers

The visible redesign is the easy part. These are the points that matter to stakeholders, auditors, and grant applications:

ADA Accessible

Built to WCAG 2.1 AA — screen-reader friendly, keyboard navigable, proper contrast. Government sites are held to this standard, and non-compliance is a legal risk.

Mobile First

Students and residents reach city services from a phone. Every page is designed for a phone screen first, desktop second.

Fast Loading

Lightweight pages that load in under two seconds — on campus Wi-Fi and rural Calhoun County connections alike.

Staff-Editable

City staff post news, alerts, and events themselves — no developer, no waiting. Game-day traffic notices go up in minutes.

The Concept

What we'd build — see it live

We've already designed a working homepage concept for Jacksonville, built around the community-hub model:

  • Game-day alert banner — traffic, closures, shuttle info
  • Hero with site-wide service search and city stats
  • Six "I Want To…" quick actions: utilities, concerns, jobs, permits, council, contact
  • "Happening This Week" live feed — JSU events, city events, road closures, new businesses, announcements
  • A dedicated JSU section — game days, campus events, move-in info, parents guide, student resources
  • "Life Around JSU" student-life guide and "Why People Choose Jacksonville" for newcomers and site selectors
  • Jacksonville Pulse — six live cards: JSU events, community events, road projects, development, new businesses, parks
  • "Meet Jacksonville" — 30-second video stories (business owner, student, teacher, family, city leader), rotated monthly, produced in-house with a broadcast background
  • Council meetings, departments, and emergency contacts

The Payoff

Benefits for the City

Beyond the new look, the redesign changes how the city operates day to day:

  • Mobile-friendly for residents and students — services work from any phone
  • Faster access to city services — pay, report, and apply in fewer clicks
  • Easier navigation for permits and forms — less counter traffic, fewer calls to City Hall
  • ADA-conscious design — accessible to every resident, and defensible
  • The JSU relationship finally visible — one front door for town and gown
  • Better economic development presentation — families and businesses see a city growing
  • Improved community engagement — events, alerts, and council business residents actually see
