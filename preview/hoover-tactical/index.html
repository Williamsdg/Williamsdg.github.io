    02 / Know before you go

    Six things that
    save you a trip.

    If you're looking for somewhere to practice, to get introduced to guns, or just someplace to sight your firearm, you're in the right place.

    1. Bring a physical photo IDA current government-issued ID: driver's license, pistol permit or passport.
    2. Leave the green-tip at homeNo steel-core ammunition. Rifle magazines and ammunition are checked, so arrive with magazines unloaded.
    3. Keep it holsteredLoaded firearms stay in a holster until you are on the range in your bay.
    4. Sights are requiredThey must be used. Absolutely no hip-firing.
    5. Mind the minimums25 yards minimum on the rifle range. 5 yards minimum on the pistol range.
    6. Bring a card or cashCash App, tap and Apple Pay are not accepted.

    The four basic rules of firearm safety

    1. Handle all firearms as if they were loaded.
    2. Always keep the firearm pointed in a safe direction.
    3. Keep your finger outside the trigger guard and off the trigger until your sights are on a safe target and you have decided to fire.
    4. Always be certain that your target and the surrounding area are safe before firing.

    These are just the basics. More rules are posted around the range. Questions? Ask a range worker or range safety officer.

    Quick answers
    How old do you have to be to purchase a firearm in Alabama?

    18 years old for long guns, 21 years old for handguns.

    Does HTF do background checks?

    Absolutely. Every time, every gun purchase.

    Can I return ammunition?

    Nope. Once it leaves the store, we do not accept ammunition returns.

    Do I need a reservation for the range?

    No. We are walk-ins only and the range pass is all-day. We accept our last shooter 1 hour prior to close.
