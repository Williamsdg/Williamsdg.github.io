How a job runs

One producer from first call to final delivery. You always know what stage the film is at and what we need from you next.

1

Pre-production

Weeks 1 to 2

Brief, treatment, script and shot list. We recce every location and lock the schedule before a camera comes out of its case.

2

Production

1 to 4 shoot days

A crew sized to the job, from two people and a gimbal to a full unit with lighting and sound. Rushes are backed up twice before we leave.

3

Post-production

Weeks 3 to 5

Edit, grade, sound mix and subtitles in house. Two rounds of changes are included, and you get every format you need for broadcast and social.

What we make

Six ways in
Brand films

Two to three minute films that explain who you are and why anyone should care. Scripted or interview led, with cut-downs for paid social.

Documentary

Longer pieces for broadcast, festivals and online. We develop the story with you, then follow it for as long as it takes.

Broadcast promos

Trails, idents and sponsorship bumpers delivered to UK broadcast spec, including clearance paperwork.

Social cut-downs

Vertical and square edits planned at the shoot, so they are framed for the format and never cropped as an afterthought.

Live and events

Multi-camera capture and same-day highlights for conferences, launches and gigs.

Post only

Bring your own rushes. We edit, grade, mix and subtitle.
