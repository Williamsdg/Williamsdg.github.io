The Byron's Story

A smokehouse doesn't become a tradition overnight.

Byron's opened in Auburn in 1989 and has been feeding this town ever since — early-morning regulars, students who wandered in for a biscuit and never stopped coming, families three generations deep, and every kind of person a football Saturday brings through the door.

Nothing about it has ever been fancy. That's the point. The pit still runs, the biscuits are still out before six, and the same address on Opelika Road has quietly become part of how Auburn people describe their own lives.

“More than just great food, friends and traditions… it's where Auburn gathers.”

— Byron's Smokehouse
