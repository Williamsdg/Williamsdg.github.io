2.Project Overview

Williams Digital will design, build and publish a mobile application for Joanna Peruzzi-Schaeper Sand & Gravel (the "Client"), available on both iOS and Android, together with a web-based admin panel and the backend that supports them.

The app is informational. It presents the company, its catalogue of sand, gravel, aggregate and construction materials, its five Texas locations and its delivery service, and lets customers send enquiries and reviews. It has no shopping cart, checkout or online payments.

6.Technology

The apps will be built with React Native and Expo, which produces iOS and Android apps from one shared codebase. This keeps both apps identical in features and lowers the cost of every future change.

The backend will be a hosted cloud service with a PostgreSQL database and secure file storage. The admin panel will be a web application connected to the same backend. Locations will use Google Maps.

7.Reviews & Moderation

Customers browse the app without creating an account. To submit a review, a customer gives a name and an email address, which is confirmed before the review is accepted. Email addresses are not shown publicly.

Every review is held until the Client approves it in the admin panel. Published reviews carry a report option, and the Client can remove any review at any time. These measures are required by the app stores for apps that show content written by customers.

The Client is responsible for moderating reviews and responding to reports after launch.

The app collects the names, contact details and messages customers enter in the contact form, and the names, email addresses and text of reviews. The Client owns and controls all of this data. Williams Digital provides and maintains the system only, and will not use, sell, share, or train any system on that data.

The app does not collect payment details. Williams Digital will supply a privacy policy page describing what the app collects, as the stores require; the Client is responsible for reviewing it and for its own legal compliance.

Williams Digital will delete any stored records within ten (10) business days of a written request.
