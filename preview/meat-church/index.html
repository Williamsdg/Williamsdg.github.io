02 — The Gospel

One bite of
brisket did this

Matt Pittman grew up cooking with his Grandma June in Alabama, learning the value of simple food that tastes like something. A trip to Central Texas and one bite of salt-and-pepper brisket turned that into an obsession.

That obsession became a run on BBQ Pitmasters, then a rub called Holy Cow, then a second one called Honey Hog. Orders started coming in the night the episode aired and never really stopped.

"It's about building a community of BBQ lovers who want to cook better, eat better, and have a damn good time doing it."
