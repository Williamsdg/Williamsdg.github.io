Hatch Chile Brisket Dip

Pit temp
350°F
Cook time
45 min
Vessel
12" cast iron
Category
Appetizer

Smoked brisket, roasted Hatch chiles and loads of melted cheese come together to make this the ultimate game-day favorite. Football season calls for a killer smoked dip, and this one checks every box.

We start with softened cream cheese and mix in roasted Hatch green chiles, chopped brisket, and cream of poblano soup to make this dip ridiculously rich and creamy. Then we load the top with a generous pile of freshly shredded cheese and bake the whole thing until it is hot, bubbly and perfectly melted.

This is an easy way to put leftover brisket to work, and the perfect dip for college football Saturdays, NFL Sundays, tailgates, or any game-day party.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb smoked chopped brisket
  • 16 oz softened cream cheese
  • 8 oz chopped Hatch chiles
  • ½ of a diced sweet onion
  • 1 can cream of poblano soup
  • 2 C shredded Monterey Jack cheese
  • 1 C shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 sliced jalapeño
  • 2 T minced cilantro
  • 5 oz queso fresco
  • Meat Church Garlic & Jalapeño BLANCO, to taste
  • Tortilla chips, for serving

Method

  1. Prepare your pellet grill

    Set your pellet grill to 350°F. We used pecan and cherry pellets.

  2. Compile the dip

    This fits a 12 inch cast iron skillet or a half foil pan. Press the cream cheese into the bottom of the skillet. Follow with the Hatch chiles, onion, brisket and cream of poblano soup in that order. Season evenly with Garlic & Jalapeño BLANCO. Add the shredded cheese and top with the sliced jalapeño.

  3. Smoke

    Place in the center of the grill for 45 minutes, or until the cheese is completely melted.

  4. Garnish

    Garnish with minced cilantro and queso fresco.

  5. Serve

    Let it cool for ten minutes. Dip with tortilla chips and enjoy alongside a cold beer.
