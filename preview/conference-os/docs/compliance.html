Compliance & Governance.

NCAA documentation, rule updates, school submissions, violations tracking, education modules, and the platform's master audit trail. The system of record for "what we filed, when, with whom, and how it was acknowledged."

01 · WHAT IT DOES

Two jobs: workflow + audit trail.

Compliance has two distinct functions. The first is workflow: schools self-report violations, request interpretive rulings, submit documentation; SAC compliance reviews and routes to NCAA; everyone acknowledges policy memos. The second is audit trail: an immutable record of every consequential action across the entire platform, retained 7 years per NCAA Bylaw 32.7.

Open Cases shows the active workflow queue with severity (L-I through L-IV) and routing status. Policies & Memos tracks active conference policies with per-school acknowledgment heatmap. Education Modules is the per-school + per-role compliance training tracker. NCAA Submissions is the SAC's outbound queue to the NCAA. Audit Trail is the universal log — searchable, exportable, immutable.

02 · WHO USES IT

Owned by VP Compliance & Governance.

  • VP Compliance (J. Foster) — primary owner, sees everything.
  • Conference compliance team — works the open-cases queue, drafts memos, manages NCAA submissions.
  • School Compliance Leads (16) — self-report, request interpretations, ack policies, submit documentation.
  • Legal counsel — escalation reviewer on L-I / L-II violations.
  • External: NCAA Office of Infractions — receives submissions via integration / portal.

04 · MONITORING APPROACH

Daily queue + monthly cadence.

Daily

Compliance team works the Open Cases queue — triage new submissions, push processing items, escalate L-I / L-II to VP. Monitor ack rates on freshly-distributed policies; chase laggard schools.

Weekly

VP review of in-flight NCAA submissions. Walk through any policy with < 90% ack rate. Education module compliance check — flag schools with role-types below target by 12/15 annual deadline.

Monthly

Member-school compliance lead briefing. Audit trail export for legal review if requested.

Annual

Bylaw compliance certification filed with NCAA on behalf of all 14 schools (10/31 deadline). Full audit log review with external auditors.
