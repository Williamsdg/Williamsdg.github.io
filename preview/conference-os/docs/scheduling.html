Module 05 · Operations Suite

Scheduling & Event Operations.

The conference-wide operational calendar — staffing every event across 21 sports, with credentialing, travel, venue coordination, and reusable templates. The system that makes sure 142 events in a single week all have everything they need.

01 · WHAT IT DOES

One master calendar, with everything wired to it.

A week in the SAC has 140+ events across 21 sports. Each one needs officials, staff, credentials, travel, hotels, venue ops, and broadcast coordination. The Scheduling module is where it all comes together — and where every other module pulls from for its operational reality.

The Week View shows every sport's events by day. Drill into a day for the event detail: kickoff times, broadcast partner, officials crew, venue gate times, staffing roster. The Staffing Board cross-checks every event against required roles and flags gaps. Credentialing is a queue of media/photo/VIP/official credential requests per event. Travel tracks every charter, commercial flight, hotel block, and per-diem clearance.

Event Templates make a tournament or championship reproducible — pick the template, change the dates and venue, and the system spawns all the workstreams, credentialing types, and staff roles.

02 · WHO USES IT

Owned by the Director of Event Operations.

  • Director of Event Ops — primary owner, sees everything, approves staffing assignments.
  • SAC office staff — see their own assignments and the events they're attached to.
  • School ADs (read + request) — see their sports' events, can submit credential/travel requests.
  • Travel Ops + Credentialing leads — work the travel and credential queues respectively.

04 · MONITORING APPROACH

Weekly publication cadence + daily review.

  1. Sunday: Director publishes next week's master view. Bot announces to relevant channels.
  2. Monday-Wednesday: Confirmation deadlines roll through. Credentialing leads work the credential queue. Travel ops books outstanding segments.
  3. Thursday: All credentials issued, all travel confirmed, all venues green.
  4. Friday-Sunday: Events execute. Director monitors the Staffing Board for last-minute gaps (sick crew, missed flight, etc.).

Daily watch items

  • Pending Confirmations — anyone who hasn't confirmed by Wednesday gets an automated reminder. Friday means manual call.
  • Schedule Conflicts — system flags double-bookings (a crew assigned to two games, a venue overlap, etc.).
  • Travel risk — weather-disrupted segments, hotel-block pickup below 80%, charter delays.
  • Credential queue depth — anything pending > 24h pre-event gets escalated.
