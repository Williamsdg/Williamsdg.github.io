Module 08 · Operations Suite

Incidents & Crisis Command.

The conference-wide incident desk — weather, security, medical, technical, PR. Auto-routed escalation chains, runnable playbooks, joint command with SACP / local LE / EMS, and after-action reports captured for every event.

01 · WHAT IT DOES

The conference's command desk when something goes wrong.

An incident is anything that requires coordinated response above the venue level — a weather hold, a fan medical, a player conduct event, a broadcast feed loss, a social/PR spike. This module is where every one of them lives from open to after-action.

Each incident has a severity (SEV-1 crisis through SEV-4 routine), an owner, an escalation chain (auto-engaged based on category), a chain-of-action log, and a SLA clock for acknowledgment + closure. The Active Board is the SAC's primary "what's happening right now" view; Playbooks are pre-baked runbooks for the 26 most-common situation types; AARs are auto-drafted post-incident.

02 · WHO USES IT

Owned by the Head of Security & Incidents.

  • Head of Security & Incidents — primary owner; on call 24/7 during conference season.
  • SAC Police (SACP) — joint command lead for security incidents.
  • On-duty conference coordinator — opens incidents from the Ops Center; first responder.
  • Local LE + EMS — joint command members at host venues, with limited dashboard access.
  • VP Communications — PR severity incidents route here for response coordination.

04 · MONITORING APPROACH

Always-on. Wall-displayed during events.

Standby (between events)

The Head of Security checks the Active Board daily for any open or monitoring items. Weekly review with VPs on the prior week's incidents.

Event-day

The Active Board is on a wall display next to the Ops Center. Every incident appearing is acknowledged within 3 minutes by the on-duty coordinator. Severity drives the playbook auto-run; the coordinator confirms each step in the chain executes.

Crisis (SEV-1 / SEV-2)

Direct mobile push to all members of the relevant escalation chain. Whitfield gets the SEV-1 page personally. Joint command auto-opens a voice room in the Comms Hub. Decision-log writes to the immutable audit store.

What to watch

  • Time-to-acknowledge on every new incident — target ≤ 3 minutes.
  • Open SEV-2+ incidents — should be zero outside of an actively-managed event.
  • AARs overdue — anything > 24h post-closure without an AAR escalates to the VP.
  • Playbook completeness — every new incident category that doesn't fit a playbook gets one drafted.
