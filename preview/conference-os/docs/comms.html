Module 04 · Core

Staff Communication Hub.

A purpose-built workspace for conference operations: sport channels, game-weekend threads, replay center voice rooms, incident escalation, and pinned playbooks. Slack-class quality, with the access-control and audit guarantees the SAC needs.

01 · WHAT IT DOES

Replaces the text-message chaos.

Today, SAC operations runs on a sprawling mix of email threads, Slack workspaces, text groups, and phone calls. Knowledge lives in inboxes; new staff can't catch up; nothing is searchable; nothing leaves an audit trail.

The Comms Hub is a single workspace with channels organized by sport and function — #fb-lighthouse-rivalry-2026, #fb-school-liaisons, #replay-center-bham, #compliance, #incidents-active. Game-weekend channels open automatically when a game is scheduled. Replay center voice rooms are always-on. Pinned resources at the top of each channel hold the playbooks, memos, and crew rosters relevant to that channel.

Critical difference vs. Slack: every channel is scoped by role + sport. Schools see school-liaison channels. Officials see crew channels. Broadcast partners see the partner channels. Nobody sees what they shouldn't.

02 · WHO USES IT

Everyone. That's the point.

The seven role types use different channels

  • SAC Executives — leadership channel, daily briefings, escalation DMs.
  • Sport Administrators — sport-ops channels, game-weekend threads, school-liaison threads.
  • Schools — school-liaison channels per sport + sport-specific game-weekend threads.
  • Officials — crew-only channels, sport-wide crew channels, memo distribution channels.
  • Replay Centers — replay coordination voice rooms, replay-to-crew channels.
  • Media Partners — partner channels for broadcast windows, on-air talent coordination.
  • Event Staff — championship-specific channels, venue ops threads.

Total active users: ~8,400 across the conference ecosystem.

04 · MONITORING APPROACH

The active vs. ambient distinction.

Comms Hub usage breaks into two patterns:

Active monitoring (game-day)

The Coordinator on duty has #fb-lighthouse-rivalry-2026 open on a side monitor next to the Ops Center. They're watching for: AD messages requiring same-day response, escalation alerts from the incident bot, and replay center status pings. Reading speed: skim.

Ambient (weekday)

Most of the time, Comms Hub is the asynchronous workspace. Coordinators check channels 3-4× per day. School liaisons drop in to ack memos. Most messages are FYI. The Hub's mobile app sends push notifications only for direct mentions and explicit alerts; channel-wide messages don't ping.

!

The "acknowledge" pattern.

When a memo is distributed, the bot post requires an "ack" reaction from each recipient role. The Comms Hub then tracks per-person ack status — visible in the Compliance & Governance module. This is how the conference replaces "did you get my email?" with actual signed-acknowledgment infrastructure.
