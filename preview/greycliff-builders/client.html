The Friday Letter

Every Friday, by 5pm.

The Friday Letter is the longest-running tradition at Greycliff — a hand-written note from your superintendent every Friday by 5pm, for as long as your build is active. What closed this week, what's next week, what's worrying us, and the photos to back it up.

Letter 21 · Friday, May 22, 2026
Carter and Annie —

This was a good week. We finished the last of the framing punches Monday, Wallace passed our pre-roof inspection first time on Thursday (rare for us this year), and Drexel showed up this morning with all 1,840 feet of copper. Diego's crew was on top by 9 a.m. — the photo of them up against the morning sky is at the top of the journal below, you'll see it.

Next week the roof is the whole story. We're tracking two storm systems for Wednesday and Thursday, so I'm pulling our dry-in forward to Tuesday afternoon — we will not be caught open on this house. Plan is full crew Tuesday, half day Saturday, and we should be paper-tight before the weather hits.

A few things you need from me — first, CO-014 for the beadboard porch ceiling is in your queue (you'll see it below). Second, master bath sconces — Annie, those two Visual Comfort options are both at the showroom; we need a pick by Friday so we don't hold electrical. Third, Saturday morning roof walk: I'd love to do it before the crew shows up at 7. Reply when works for you.

That's it. Quiet, productive week. We're on schedule and on budget.

Yours, Wes Superintendent · Greycliff Builders
