The Way We Build

Four things we don't compromise on.

Most builders will say the same things. We hold ourselves to them in writing — every contract, every superintendent log, every Friday update.

01

One superintendent. One build.

Your superintendent is on site every day your project is active. You'll know their name, their cell, and their handwriting in the daily log. They don't run three other jobs.

02

Decisions in writing.

Every change order, allowance call, and finish selection lives in The Site Office — signed, time-stamped, and visible to you the moment it's executed. No "I don't remember that."

03

The Friday Letter.

Every Friday by 5pm, your superintendent sends a written update: what closed this week, what's coming, what's holding us up, and the photos to prove it. Every single Friday.

04

A two-year warranty walk.

Twelve months and twenty-four months after closing, we come back. We walk the house with you, fix what's settled, and document everything in your warranty file — for you and the next owner.

Inside The Site Office

Twelve modules. One source of truth.

The Site Office is built around the way Greycliff actually runs a job — not the way a software company thinks construction works. Every module came out of a real problem we'd hit on a real build.

01

Project Overview

Mission control. Every active build at a glance — stage, schedule, delays, open issues, recent photos, and what your superintendents owe you today.

02

Build Stages & Timeline

Permits through final walkthrough — 16 stages with planned, projected, and actual dates, stage-by-stage notes, photos, and the contractors assigned.

03

Daily Logs

The superintendent's morning entry: who was on site, weather, materials delivered, inspections, safety notes, and the day's photo set. Legal-grade documentation.

04

Contractor Management

Subcontractor profiles with insurance, W-9s, license docs, performance ratings, availability, and payment status — a roster that gets smarter every job.

05

Homeowner Portal

The view your client opens at 9pm. Stage, photos, upcoming milestones, selections, change orders, documents, and a direct line to ask a question.

06

Photo & Video Journal

Organized by build, stage, and room. Drone shots, before/afters, inspection photos, finish references. Indexed and shareable to family or to social.

07

Change Order Management

Requested → costed → owner-approved → signed → executed. Each change carries a dollar impact, a timeline impact, and a permanent signature on file.

08

Punch List

Final-stage and warranty issues, assigned to a contractor with a due date, a priority, and a completion photo. Nothing closes without one.

09

Scheduling Calendar

Inspections, contractor blocks, deliveries, walkthroughs, closings. Syncs to Google Calendar, sends SMS reminders, pulls weather forecasts onto framing days.

10

Team Messaging

Conversations attached to a project, a stage, or a single photo. Tag a contractor, an inspector, or the homeowner. Notifications without the noise.

11

Budget & Draws

Allowances, invoices, sub payments, draw schedules, and profitability — at the build level and across the company. With your bank's draw format pre-filled.

12

Warranty & Aftercare

Twelve- and twenty-four-month walks, homeowner-submitted requests, repair tracking, and a permanent warranty file that survives ownership changes.

From First Call to Final Walk

The way a Greycliff build moves.

Most builds take 9 to 14 months from contract to closing. Here's the rhythm — what we own, what your architect owns, and what shows up in The Site Office at each stage.

Week 0–4

Feasibility

Site walk, land due diligence, preliminary budget, architect introduction if you don't have one. No commitment.

Month 1–3

Pre-Construction

Drawings, selections kickoff, allowance schedule, GMP contract. The Site Office opens to you the day we sign.

Month 3–11

Construction

Daily logs, weekly Friday Letters, monthly draw meetings. Stage-by-stage progress visible in real time.

Final 30 days

Punch & Close

Walkthroughs, punch list, final cleaning, professional photography. We hand you the keys and the warranty file.

Common Questions

Before you pick up the phone.

How many builds do you take on each year?
Between twelve and fifteen. Some years sixteen. We've turned away work every year since 2014 — not because we don't want it, but because going beyond that number means a superintendent has to run two jobs, which is the moment quality breaks.
How far ahead are you booked?
Our 2026 calendar is full. We are accepting feasibility conversations for builds breaking ground in late 2026 or 2027. Renovations under $750k can sometimes be slotted in earlier.
Do you work with my architect, or do you have one?
Both. We've completed builds with eighteen different Birmingham-area architects. If you don't have one yet, we can introduce you to three or four whose style and pace fit your build.
Is The Site Office included, or is it an upgrade?
It's included on every Greycliff build, every lane, every year. It's how we run our own company — we'd rather you see it than not.
What's the smallest project you take?
$425k is roughly our floor. Below that, we genuinely don't think we're the right answer — we'll happily refer you to two or three smaller builders we like.
How do you handle change orders?
Every change is costed, dated, and signed inside The Site Office before any work begins. We will not start a change on a verbal — it's the single biggest cause of friction in custom building, and we've decided it's not allowed on a Greycliff job.
