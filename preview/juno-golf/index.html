The idea

Fit your clubs to your swing. Yourself.

Juno Golf is building equipment that lets you do the customizing.

The usual way

Book a fitting

  1. Find a fitter and get on the calendar.
  2. Hit balls in a bay while someone else reads your numbers.
  3. Order clubs built to that one session.
The Juno way

Customize it yourself

  1. Start with clubs designed to be customized.
  2. Set them up to the way you swing.
  3. No fitting appointment required.

Our story

From the team behind Juno Athletics.

Juno Athletics was established in 2020 as a Florida and New Jersey apparel and equipment company, named in homage to Juno, the Queen of the Gods. It is known for its slowpitch bats.

Juno Golf brings that equipment work to the course.
