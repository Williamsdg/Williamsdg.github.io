Straight answers

The questions builders actually ask.

Is this real, or is it a mockup?

The interface is real and complete — around seventy screens you can click through right now, including all seven role workspaces, the homeowner portal, and a full single-build view. What you're looking at is a working front end running on demonstration data from a fictional builder.

The multi-tenant back end is being built now, with the founding cohort. That's exactly why the founding rate exists and why we onboard in small groups instead of opening a signup form. We'd rather tell you that plainly than pretend otherwise.

Who is this actually for?

Custom home builders and high-end remodelers doing roughly five to thirty homes a year, with somewhere between two and twenty people in the office. If you're a single-trade contractor doing hundreds of small jobs, you want a field-service tool instead — this is built around long, complex, one-of-a-kind builds with an owner who cares a great deal about the outcome.

Will my superintendent actually use it?

That is the question that decides whether any of this software works, and most of it fails there. Field Mode is a separate one-handed interface, not a shrunken desktop page — big targets, thumb-reach actions, and readable in direct sun. Logging a day takes well under a minute.

We'd also rather you start with one build that's just breaking ground than migrate a half-finished house. Clean test, no mess, and your super forms the habit on a job that doesn't have six months of history to backfill.

What happens to my data if I leave?

You export all of it — logs, photos, documents, budgets, change orders, messages — in one click, any time, including after you cancel. We think holding build history hostage is the single worst practice in this category, and we've written the export into the product rather than the marketing.

Can you import from Buildertrend or CoConstruct?

For founding partners, yes — by hand, at no charge. We'll sit down with your active jobs and move schedules, budgets, selections, contacts, and documents across. Self-serve importers come later, once we've done enough of these by hand to know what actually matters.

Why should I trust a small shop over a big platform?

Fair question, and the honest answer has two halves. You shouldn't expect the surface area of a company with hundreds of engineers — we're not going to out-feature them, and we're not trying to.

What you get instead is a product where every screen was designed around one question, a price that's published and locked, an export button that works, and a person who answers the phone and can ship a change you asked for that week. If you want the widest tool on the market, buy the widest tool on the market. If you've tried it and found it a mile wide and an inch thick, that's the problem we exist to solve.

Do I get charged for homeowners and subs?

No — unlimited and free on every plan, forever. Charging a builder for inviting the homeowner whose house they're building is backwards, and per-seat creep is the most common complaint about how this category prices.
