Three kinds of evenings

Whether it’s an intimate birthday celebration, a baby shower or a special milestone, Leaf & Petal provides a stunning backdrop. Guests can relax and enjoy the charm of nature, and we work closely with you on every detail, from floral arrangements to the overall ambiance.

Your reception or rehearsal dinner deserves a setting as beautiful and unique as your love story. Vibrant blooms and greenery surround you and your guests, and our team brings your vision to life with personalized floral designs and expert coordination.

For team-building retreats, corporate gatherings or a hands-on floral workshop, the greenhouse is a tranquil yet engaging setting that encourages collaboration. We tailor the space and floral designs to suit your needs.

How booking works

Leaf & Petal is batting a thousand with happy renters after many years and hundreds of events.

Good to know

Answers from our event overview and rental agreement.

Can we have our wedding ceremony here?

We’re a lovely place for rehearsal dinners and receptions, but we don’t feel we’re well suited to wedding ceremonies. There’s no good place for a wedding party to dress and gather, or to prepare a bride on site, so we respectfully encourage couples to hold the ceremony elsewhere.

Who handles food and drinks?

A professional catering company with service staff is required for any event with food, and the staff stay until the end. We keep a list of preferred caterers we’ve learned do an excellent job.

You’re welcome to bring and serve your own alcohol with a professional bartender. Caterers are usually happy to supply one. Alcohol can’t be sold without the licences the Alabama ABC Board and the City of Vestavia Hills require, so cash bars aren’t permitted unless properly licensed.

When can our caterer set up?

Caterers can start setting up in the late afternoon, as long as it doesn’t get in the way of shoppers or staff. Some counters and serving areas may not be cleared until the shop closes at 5:30.

What time does the evening end?

Guests leave by 10:00 PM. There’s a 15-minute grace period, until 10:15, for final cleanup and packing up your things. Cleanup usually takes 30 to 45 minutes, so build it into your schedule.

You or your caterer take trash to our dumpster, inside the fenced area at the north end of the building, and leave counters and floors as you found them.

Can we bring our own flowers?

Outside floral arrangements need written approval from our Event Coordinator first, and are approved only in limited cases, such as certain wedding arrangements. Our floral staff will plan your tables with you, and centerpieces can be rented for 25% of retail.

Is there a lower rate for a small party?

We’re not able to offer a reduced rate. The prep work and fixed costs, such as climate control, are the same as for a typical 40 to 60 person event, and the space stays mostly booked during available times.

Where do guests park?

Parking is limited: fewer than 30 spaces are available, because the rest are set aside for neighboring restaurants. Encourage guests to rideshare or carpool.

Can we use candles and decorations?

Decorations and decorating plans need advance approval. Candles must be enclosed in glass hurricanes or another approved holder. Taper candles aren’t permitted, and tealights must sit in votives at least 3 inches deep.

How does payment work?

A signed rental agreement and a non-refundable deposit of half the rental total hold your date. The balance and a $250 refundable security deposit are due 48 hours before the event. You can pay by check or by card, which carries a 2% processing fee.

Attendant fees are paid directly to the attendant. Your security deposit comes back within 72 hours, usually within 24.

What should we expect from a working garden shop?

We’re a garden and gift retailer first, open seven days a week. Large plant deliveries can arrive without warning, and in freezing weather tender plants may come into the greenhouse for protection, especially in March.

We guarantee clean restrooms, clear counters and plenty of the agreed room to seat guests and serve food and drinks. The look of the shop and parking area can vary with the season, particularly close to our busiest times of year.
