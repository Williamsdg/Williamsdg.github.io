2.Included in the Dashboard

Job pipeline
Every job moves through inquiry, quoted, scheduled, installed, takedown and stored, with holiday and year-round work kept apart.
Quotes & approvals
An itemized quote per job: package price, optional upgrades, a good-until date, and the customer's approval recorded from their quote page.
Payment tracking
Deposit and balance status for every job, recorded by the office. A job cannot be booked until its quote is approved and the deposit is marked received.
Schedule & crews
Installs and takedowns by day, crew assignment, and the property notes the crew needs on site.
Crew view
Each crew's stops for the day with directions, call and text, load list, an install or takedown checklist, and completion photos that appear on the job.
Stay-Lit service calls
Each issue is attached to the install it belongs to, with its design, crew and history.
Inventory & storage
On hand, committed and available by item, a load list per job, and the storage bin for each customer's lights.
Clients & rebooking
Contacts, approved designs and past seasons kept together, and a one-step invitation to rebook with last year's design and measurements.
Property measuring
Satellite roofline measuring inside each job, including the line the customer traced on the website.
Sign-in & shared records
Separate sign-ins for owner, office and crew, with everyone working from the same records and daily backups.
Website message
The booking message on the website can be changed from the dashboard without a developer.
Training
A walkthrough of the dashboard and crew view at launch, and a short written guide.

3.Online Payments & Automated Messages — Future Phase

Not part of this agreementThe concept shows a customer paying a deposit online and messages going out automatically. This build tracks payments that Let It Glow collects the way it does today, and the office records them in the dashboard. Taking card or bank payments through the customer quote page, and automatic texts or emails (quote follow-ups, install reminders, weather rescheduling, payment reminders), are separate phases and will be quoted separately if and when Let It Glow wants them.

4.Not Included

  • Online card or bank checkout and automated texting or email. See the Future Phase section.
  • Route optimization, accounting or bookkeeping integrations, and job profitability reporting.
  • A native app in the App Store or Google Play. The dashboard and crew view run in the phone's web browser.
  • Photography, copywriting or logo design. The site uses Let It Glow's existing logo, photos and wording, and any new photos Let It Glow supplies.
  • Entering historical records. Williams Digital will import one customer list supplied as a spreadsheet; past jobs are added by Let It Glow as needed.
  • Domain registration, business email, paid advertising or an ongoing SEO retainer.

5.Hosting & Third-Party Services

Let It Glow keeps its existing domain name, and Williams Digital will connect the new website to it at launch. Where the website is hosted, and any hosting cost, will be confirmed in writing before launch; no hosting fee is included in or added by this agreement.

The dashboard depends on outside services: a database with sign-in, and Google Maps for the satellite view and address search. These accounts are opened in Let It Glow's name so that Let It Glow owns them. Each provider bills Let It Glow directly, and Williams Digital adds no markup. Williams Digital will set the build up to stay within the providers' free allowances where they apply, and will confirm any expected cost in writing before launch. Providers may change their pricing; that is outside Williams Digital's control.

Ongoing maintenance after the support period in this agreement is not included and can be quoted separately.

8.Customer Data

The website and dashboard hold names, phone numbers, email addresses, property addresses, measurements and photos belonging to Let It Glow's customers.

Let It Glow owns and controls all data collected through the website and dashboard. Williams Digital provides and maintains the mechanism only, and will not use, sell, share, or train any system on that data.

Williams Digital will delete any individual record, or all stored records, within ten (10) business days of a written request, and will provide a complete export of the data at any time on request.

9.Ownership & Rights

On receipt of final payment, Let It Glow owns the finished website and dashboard: all page files, styling, the dashboard, the crew view and any custom graphics produced for this project.

Let It Glow owns and retains its domain name, its logo, its photos, its customer reviews and all content it publishes. Williams Digital claims no interest in any of them.

Williams Digital retains ownership of any underlying reusable code, libraries or components not created specifically for Let It Glow, and retains the right to display the finished work in its portfolio and marketing. Customer data is never shown.

10.Revisions & Support

The fee includes two rounds of revisions during the build phase. A round is a consolidated set of feedback delivered at one time.

Structural changes requested after approval (additional pages, new functionality, or reworking an approved section's structure) are out of scope and billed under the Fee & Payment Terms section.

Williams Digital will correct any defect in delivered work at no charge for ninety (90) days following launch.

11.Let It Glow's Responsibilities

  • Grant Williams Digital access to the domain's DNS settings for launch.
  • Open the database and Google Maps accounts with Williams Digital's guidance, and share access for setup.
  • Supply the names and email addresses of everyone who needs a sign-in, and whether each is owner, office or crew.
  • Confirm package prices, upgrade prices, the deposit percentage and the inventory list before the dashboard is built.

12.Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential any non-public business information disclosed by the other during this engagement, including the pricing in this agreement, and will not disclose it to third parties except as required by law.

Any credentials shared for launch, such as domain or account access, will be used only for that purpose, and Let It Glow may revoke them at any time.

13.Cancellation

Either party may cancel this agreement with written notice.

If Let It Glow cancels after work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable and Let It Glow is invoiced for any approved out-of-scope work completed to date. All work completed to the point of cancellation is delivered to Let It Glow.

If Williams Digital cancels, Let It Glow receives all work completed to date and a pro-rata refund of any amount paid for work not performed.
