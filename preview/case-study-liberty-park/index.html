The Challenge

Liberty Park Community Association is a master-planned HOA managing 1,547 homes across 15 neighborhoods in Vestavia Hills, AL. Before switching to Williams Digital, they used their previous management platform which charged $1.40–$5.00 per door per month and locked them into clunky workflows that forced staff to copy data between three systems and a portal that 76% of residents found impossible to use on mobile.

  • Annual SaaS cost approaching $32,000 with no flat-rate option
  • Residents calling the office because the old portal didn't work on iPhones
  • Jennifer's team spending 18 hours/week on manual ARC routing and payment reconciliation
  • No way to message individual neighborhoods without exporting CSVs and using a separate email tool

The Solution

Williams Digital designed and built a custom portal tailored to Liberty Park's actual workflow — a 12-page staff dashboard, 9-page homeowner portal, and a refreshed public-facing community website. The platform launched in 8 weeks with full data migration from their previous management platform.

The Results

Within 6 months of launch, Liberty Park Community Association reported:

  • $23,000 in annual savings compared to their previous management platform
  • 14 hours per week saved on payment reconciliation, ARC routing, and resident communications
  • 87% homeowner adoption within 60 days — up from 52% on the old platform
  • Collection rate up from 89% to 96.4% — Q1 collection rate hit 96.4% across all 15 neighborhoods
  • The board now receives auto-generated monthly reports — Jennifer no longer manually compiles them — board receives auto-generated monthly reports

"We were quietly losing tens of thousands of dollars a year to per-door fees and didn't even know it. Williams Digital's flat-rate platform paid for itself in the first quarter. The thing I didn't expect: my staff's mornings got 90 minutes shorter overnight."

— Jennifer Walsh, Community Director, Liberty Park Community Association

The Approach That Made It Work

  • Discovery first. Before any code was written, we audited Liberty Park's actual workflow with their director and front-desk staff to map every pain point.
  • Phased rollout. The portal launched in stages — payments first, then ARC, then community-wide — so staff and residents weren't overwhelmed.
  • Direct developer access. A shared Slack channel meant Liberty Park's team could ping Williams Digital directly. No tickets, no portals, no escalation paths.

What's Next

Year two of the partnership focuses on a new resident mobile app, a vendor portal for the maintenance team, and integration with Liberty Park's accounting platform. Williams Digital and Liberty Park meet monthly for ongoing strategy — a partnership pace that wasn't possible with the previous SaaS vendor.
