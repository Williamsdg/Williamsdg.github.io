Abstract submission concept

Submit an abstract.

All submissions are judged anonymously by the Program Committee. The system keeps the author's name apart from the abstract, so reviewers see only a number.

  • An individual may submit one abstract for an individual paper
  • A panel has a presider and three to six papers
  • A workshop has a presider and at least one presenter
  • A round-table discussion has one or two discussion leaders
  • Posters are open to undergraduates only
For the Program Committee. Reviewers sign in, read anonymous abstracts, and score them. The chair sees the scores together and sends decisions from one screen.
