01The short version

Cornerstone’s own site already says it: carriers call when a claim gets complicated. Everything below is built to make that the first thing a carrier, TPA or attorney understands — and to make the firm, rather than one person, the thing they are hiring.

  • Fourteen pages replacing the current Wix site, with every service line given its own page.
  • Two intake funnels — a structured assignment form, and an adjuster roster application.
  • A coverage map that shows the licensed reach, not just four state names in a sentence.
  • Six weeks from start to launch.
  • $1,500 as a founding client, against a $3,000 scope. $75 per month thereafter.

02What we found on the current site

Observations from cornerstoneadj.com as it stands today. Nothing here is a criticism of the work — it is a list of assets the current site is not letting you use.

01

Seven service lines share one page

Auto & Light Truck; Commercial Truck, Fleet & Heavy Equipment; Mechanical & Fuel Claims; Estimate Review; Post-Repair Inspection; Dispute Resolution; and RV, Motorcycle, Boat & Personal Watercraft all live inside a single Services page. None of them can rank on its own, and none can be sent to a carrier as its own link.

02

The strongest credentials are the hardest to find

I-CAR Platinum Pro Level 3 with 150+ courses completed, arbitrator for Arbitration Forums with a perfect record, Farmers University Gold Star, fifteen years appraising for a major national carrier — and curriculum written on how to handle problem claims. That is the entire argument for assigning Cornerstone a disputed file, and it currently sits partway down an About page.

03

Coverage reads smaller than it is

The site states Alabama · Georgia · Florida · Mississippi. The licensing actually reaches Oklahoma and roughly 25 reciprocal states. A carrier scanning for someone who can cover a multi-state fleet has no way to know that.

04

The firm reads as one person

Cornerstone already operates with two licensed managers — Clay in Alabama and the Florida Panhandle, Alex Franklin in Central Florida. The site does not reflect a firm with a roster, and nothing on it is working to recruit the next one.

05

Submit Assignment exists, but does not route

The instinct is already right — the CTA is in the navigation. What it collects is a general enquiry rather than a structured intake that captures loss type, unit details, location and access.

06

Built on Wix

Templated page structure and limited control over markup, page speed and per-page SEO. Fine for a brochure; limiting for a firm that wants to rank on “heavy equipment appraisal” in four states.

03What gets built

Design system
One typographic and colour system applied across every page, built to look like an engineering consultancy rather than an insurance brochure.
Structured assignment intake
Loss type, urgency, unit and serial/VIN, location, access contact, and document upload — routed to your inbox with the file already organised.
Adjuster roster application
Licensing by state, specialties, years, certifications, heavy-equipment and commercial experience, and resume upload.
Interactive coverage map
Toggles between states Cornerstone covers directly and the wider licensed and reciprocal reach.
Mobile-specific build
Not a shrunk desktop site — persistent assignment CTA, swipeable specialty cards, and a vertical intake flow.
Per-service SEO structure
Each service line gets its own indexable page, title and description, so it can rank and be linked independently.
Form automation
Submissions routed to inbox with confirmation email to the sender, spam filtering and file storage.
Migration from Wix
Content carried across, domain repointed at launch, redirects mapped from existing URLs.

04Not included

Named plainly so there are no surprises later.

  • The assignment & roster dashboard. The internal system for managing assignments, adjusters and status is a separate engagement, quoted separately.
  • Photography. Cornerstone supplies images, or licensed stock is purchased at cost with prior approval.
  • Copywriting from scratch. Included is structuring and editing what already exists plus one interview with Clay. Full brand copywriting is out of scope.
  • Client portal or claim-file storage. No handling of claim files, insured personal data or carrier systems.
  • CRM, email platform or phone system migration.
  • Paid advertising, ongoing SEO retainer or content production.

05How it runs

Week 1

Discovery & content

One interview with Clay, credential and licensing verification, gather any existing photography, confirm the exact coverage list.

Week 2

Structure & copy

Page-by-page content built from your real service lines and credentials. Everything factual comes back to you for approval before build.

Weeks 3–4

Build

All fourteen pages, the coverage map and both intake forms, built and reviewed on a private preview link.

Week 5

Automation & testing

Form routing, confirmation emails, file uploads, mobile and browser testing, redirect map from the current Wix URLs.

Week 6

Launch

DNS repointed to the new site. cornerstoneadj.com stays in Cornerstone’s name throughout.

Your domain stays yours

cornerstoneadj.com remains registered in Cornerstone’s name throughout. DNS is pointed at the new site on launch day. If we ever part ways, the domain and the site content go with you.
