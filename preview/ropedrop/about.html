What RopeDrop is

RopeDrop is a park-day launchpad — one screen that puts Disney's Lightning Lane and Virtual Queue tools one tap away, alongside a quick read on how crowded each resort is expected to be. The name comes from "rope drop," the park-fan term for arriving at park opening to maximize ride time before the crowds arrive.

What's on this page

This is a UI study. The four park buttons link to Disney's official Lightning Lane and Virtual Queue endpoints, and the crowd calendar runs entirely in your browser from a local heuristic. There is no booking automation here — see the Source link in the footer for the original open-source project (BG1 by Joel Face) that pioneered the bookmarklet approach RopeDrop is modeled after.

About the crowd calendar

The 1–10 crowd index is computed locally in your browser. Each of Disney's six resorts has its own ruleset tuned to local holidays and school calendars:

  • Walt Disney World & Disneyland — U.S. federal holidays, spring break around Easter, summer break, Thanksgiving, Christmas/NYE peak, Halloween season, WDW Marathon weekend.
  • Tokyo Disney Resort — Japanese New Year (Oshōgatsu), Golden Week (Apr 29 – May 5), Obon (mid-August), Silver Week, school summer break, Disney Halloween.
  • Disneyland Paris — French school holiday zones, Toussaint, Christmas markets, May bank holidays, European summer break.
  • Shanghai Disney Resort — Chinese New Year, National Day Golden Week (Oct 1–7), Labour Day (May 1–5), Tomb-Sweeping, summer school break.
  • Hong Kong Disneyland — Chinese New Year, mainland Golden Week visitors, Good Friday + Easter Monday, summer school break, Christmas.
  • Day-of-week patterns are tuned per resort (Tokyo and Asian parks skew far more toward weekends than U.S. parks).

It is not a forecast and does not call any live wait-time API. For the most accurate same-week predictions, refer to a dedicated crowd calendar service.
