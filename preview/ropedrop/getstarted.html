Get Started in 30 Seconds

RopeDrop is a fast launchpad for Disney's Lightning Lane and Virtual Queue, plus a worldwide crowd calendar. Pick the install path that fits your phone — none of them take more than 30 seconds.

Recommended 📱

Add to your Home Screen

Works like an app. No browser bar, opens full-screen, one tap from your phone.

  1. Open this site in Safari. (Chrome on iPhone doesn't support Add to Home Screen.)
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen — the square with the arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right. The RopeDrop icon appears on your home screen.
  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app on newer versions).
  4. Tap Add to confirm. The RopeDrop icon appears on your home screen.

💡 Tip: this turns RopeDrop into a Progressive Web App. It opens instantly, works offline for the crowd calendar, and feels native.

10 seconds 🔖

Just bookmark it

The simplest option. Tap a bookmark to land on the four park buttons + today's crowd level.

  1. Tap your browser's Share or menu button.
  2. Tap Add Bookmark or Add to Favorites.
  3. Save it to your Favorites Bar or Bookmarks folder for one-tap access on park day.

💡 Works on every browser, on every device — no special setup. Re-open it anytime by tapping the bookmark.

Advanced ⚡

Booking helper (optional)

A small bookmarklet that loads a streamlined booking interface on top of Disney's site, signed in with your existing Disney session. Disney does not offer a public API — this is the only realistic way to skip taps.

  1. On a desktop computer, drag the button below to your browser's Bookmarks Bar. (On mobile, copy the snippet and edit a bookmark to use it — see notes below.)
  2. From RopeDrop, tap any Lightning Lane or Virtual Queue button. You'll land on Disney's official page.
  3. Tap your new RopeDrop Helper bookmark. The streamlined interface loads on top.
⚡ RopeDrop Helper ← drag to bookmarks bar
Why a bookmarklet? Can't this work without setup?

Disney doesn't publish a public API. Browsers also (correctly) prevent any random website from reading your login session for another website. So any tool that "presses the book button for you" needs your own Disney session — which means it has to run inside Disney's page, which means a one-time bookmarklet, browser extension, or iOS Shortcut install.

The deep-link buttons on RopeDrop's main page need none of this — they just jump straight to Disney's official site where you book normally. The booking helper is for power users who want to skip those Disney taps.

iPhone only 🪄

"Hey Siri, book my Lightning Lane"

An iOS Shortcut that opens the right Disney URL with one voice command or a tap on your lock screen.

  1. On your iPhone, open the Shortcuts app (it's built in).
  2. Tap + to create a new shortcut.
  3. Add the Open URLs action and paste in https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/vas/ (or your park's URL).
  4. Name it "Book Lightning Lane" and tap Done. Use the Shortcut from your lock screen or by saying "Hey Siri".

💡 You can also pin the shortcut to your Apple Watch face or your iPhone's Action Button.

Quick FAQ

Will tapping the park buttons book a Lightning Lane for me?

No. The buttons jump to Disney's official Lightning Lane and Virtual Queue pages, where you book exactly like you would in the Disney app or website. RopeDrop is a launchpad and crowd-forecast tool, not a booking robot.

Do I need a Disney account?

For the crowd calendar and the deep-links — no. To actually book on Disney's site after you land there — yes, the same way you'd need one in the official Disney app.

Does the crowd calendar update automatically?

The 1–10 score is computed on the fly from a local heuristic that knows about U.S. holidays (WDW/DLR), Japanese holidays + Golden Week + Obon (Tokyo), French school zones (Paris), Chinese national holidays (Shanghai & Hong Kong), and worldwide seasonal patterns. It's not a forecast — it's a rules-based estimate.

Is this affiliated with Disney?

No. RopeDrop is a Williams Digital UI study, not affiliated with or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company.
