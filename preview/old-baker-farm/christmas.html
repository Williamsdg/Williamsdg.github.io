How it works

From the field to your car

  1. Pick your treeTake the hayride out and walk the rows until you find the one.
  2. We drill itTree stands are sold separately — we'll drill the hole for the stand you buy.
  3. Shake & netYour tree is shaken to remove dead needles, netted and readied for the trip home.
  4. Tie it downIf possible, we'll help you secure your tree to your vehicle.
