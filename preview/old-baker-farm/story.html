About Old Baker Farm

Take a step back in time

The Old Baker Farm stands as a historic marker of days gone by — homesteaded over 200 years ago, it withstood the perils of the Civil War and remains a traditional family farm today.

The farm through time

Two centuries of Bakers

  1. 200+ years ago
    Homesteaded

    The farm is settled — and later withstands the perils of the Civil War.

  2. 100+ years
    Worked by the Baker family

    Six generations of Bakers have farmed this land since its founding.

  3. 2000
    A Century Farm

    One of only 12 farms featured in the USDA 2000 calendar, “Millennium Celebration of Century Farms.”

  4. Today
    Jerry & Pam Baker

    Managed by fourth-generation proprietor and entrepreneur Jerry Baker and his wife, Pam.

“Each October, the Bakers invite the public to see what life is all about on a real working farm.”

What we grow

Over the years the farm has raised cotton, soybeans, corn, pumpkins and watermelons — as well as the garden that feeds the entire family. We also raise acres of Leyland Cypress, Virginia Pine and other Christmas trees grown in Alabama.
