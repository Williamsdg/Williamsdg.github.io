Field trips

A lesson for every season

Throughout the year we welcome school field trips and teach a variety of lessons depending on the season.

  • Spring — rebirth, with new plants taking root and the hatching of baby chicks.
  • Fall — the harvest, with pumpkins ready to carve and fields ready to be harvested.
  • All year — cattle, pigs, chickens, mules, horses, and a ram named Curly Horn. Some will eat right out of your hand!

Photographers

Shoot a session at the farm

We welcome professional and amateur photographers for scheduled photo sessions.

$50 per day + admission per person

The admission fee covers the photo session only — not farm activities or tours. Family snapshots during events are always free.

  • Sessions must be booked in advance — use the form or email us.
  • No sessions during annual events or any other scheduled event.
  • We're a working farm, so leave a detailed message with the date and time you'd like.
