Search inside the actual conversation, jump to exact moments and unlock clips, summaries and topic-level discovery.

PHASE 0303

Advanced personalization

Smarter recommendations, personalized feeds and targeted notifications for teams, shows, hosts, players or coaches.

PHASE 0304

Community

Ratings, reactions, fan lists and thoughtful social layers that deepen the shared Auburn listening experience.

Native listening & live audio

A fully native player, synchronized playback, downloads and live rooms for major Auburn moments.

These roadmap concepts are intentionally outside the proposed V1 scope and would be planned and priced separately after launch.
