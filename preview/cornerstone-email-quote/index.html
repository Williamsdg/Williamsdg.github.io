What you are dealing with

Three separate problems, from two separate causes.

ProblemWhy it happensWhat actually fixes it
Your email lands in people’s spam folders Your domain publishes no proof that a message really came from Cornerstone. Gmail and Outlook increasingly treat unproven mail as suspicious. Publishing that proof
Attachments over about 5 MB bounce A hard limit on the old mail platform your mailboxes sit on. It cannot be raised. Moving the mailboxes

This is worth being clear about: only the first problem is a settings issue. The other two are limits of the platform your email currently runs on, and no amount of configuration will fix them. That is the case for moving.

What we would do

Roughly a week, most of it waiting and watching rather than working.

  1. Set up Google Workspace in Cornerstone’s name

    The account is created as yours, with Kerissa as the administrator, billed directly from Google to Cornerstone. We are set up as a user on it so we can do the work, and you can remove our access at any time.

  2. Prove the domain is yours

    Google requires a verification record on the domain. This is coordinated with Sharon, as she holds the domain. Nothing visible changes at this stage and email keeps running normally.

  3. Create the mailboxes and shared addresses

    One mailbox per person. Shared addresses such as info@ and careers@ are set up as forwarding addresses, which Google does not charge for — more on that under Cost.

  4. Copy your existing mail across

    Old messages, folders and contacts are copied into the new mailboxes before anything switches over, so nothing is lost and there is no gap in your records. This runs in the background and takes as long as it takes.

  5. Switch over, one evening

    We change where your mail is delivered and publish the records that prove your mail is genuinely yours, in the same sitting. Done in the evening so a working day is never affected. There is a short window where mail is held and then delivered — nothing is lost, some messages simply arrive a little late.

  6. Set up everyone’s phone and computer

    We go person by person so nobody is left unable to send. This is usually the part people worry about and it is normally the easiest.

  7. Watch it for a week

    We monitor the delivery reports that start arriving once the new records are live, and correct anything that looks off. This is the part that confirms the spam problem is actually solved rather than assumed solved.

What we need from you

  • Who needs a mailbox of their own. Names and the addresses they use now.
  • Which addresses are shared rather than belonging to one person — for example info@, claims@, careers@. These cost nothing, so it is worth listing all of them.
  • The current password for each mailbox. This is what lets us copy the old mail across. Without it, that mailbox starts empty. Change them afterwards if you prefer.
  • An evening that suits you for the switch-over.
