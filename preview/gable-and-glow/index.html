FAQ

Good questions

What does it cost?

Roofline-only installs start at $649 in this demo. Trace your roof in the quote tool for a ballpark matched to your home.

What is included?

Design, all materials, installation, service calls during the season, take-down in January and storage until next year.

Who owns the lights?

We do. They are leased with the service, which is why storage, repairs and replacements are on us.

What if a strand goes out?

Text us a photo. We are back within 24 hours at no charge.

Can I pay online?

Yes. Deposits, balances and monthly plans can all be paid by card or bank transfer from a link we text you.

Do you need to come measure?

Usually not. We measure from satellite imagery and confirm the details by phone.
