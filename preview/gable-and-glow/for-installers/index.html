// What's in it

Four things your site probably doesn't do yet

01 · Satellite quotes

The customer traces their own roof

They type an address, see the home from the air and tap along the edges they want lit. The site totals the footage and matches it to one of your packages. You get the address and the measurement with the request.

02 · Quote builder

You price it from your desk

Open a request and the roof is already on screen. Trace or adjust it, add wreaths and mini lights at your rates, apply a discount and send the quote with a pay link.

03 · Payments

Deposits and balances online

Card, Apple Pay, bank transfer or a monthly plan through Stripe, or handed off to the invoicing tool you already use. Paid invoices update in the dashboard on their own.

04 · Season tools

Take-down zones and updates

Customers look up their take-down window by neighborhood. When weather pushes the schedule, you post the update from your phone and it is on the site in seconds.
