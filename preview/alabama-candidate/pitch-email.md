## TIPS FOR SENDING:

- **Timing**: Send Tuesday–Thursday, morning (8–10 AM). Avoid weekends and Mondays.
- **Facebook**: Use the shorter version. Message their campaign page, not personal profile. Follow the page first.
- **Email**: If you can find a campaign manager or communications director email, pitch them instead — they handle vendor decisions.
- **Follow up**: If no response in 5–7 days, send ONE short follow-up: "Hey [Name], just bumping this up — would love to chat if you're interested. The demo is still live at [link]."
- **Personalize**: Mention something specific about their campaign, a recent speech, or a policy position. Shows you actually care about their race.
- **Target**: Focus on state legislature, city council, mayoral, and county commission races — they have budget but usually lack a good web team. Statewide races (Governor, AG, etc.) typically already have agencies.
