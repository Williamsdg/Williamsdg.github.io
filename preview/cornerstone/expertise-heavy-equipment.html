Overview

Equipment losses aren’t
large auto losses.

Repair methods, component pricing, undercarriage wear, hour meters, attachments and downtime exposure all move the number. So does knowing when a machine is economically repairable and when it is not. Cornerstone handles equipment assignments for carriers, dealers, rental fleets and self-insured owners.

Assignment Types

What Cornerstone handles.

If the loss is not listed, ask. The list reflects the common assignments, not the limits.

/ 01

Physical Damage Appraisal

Damage assessment and repair estimating on excavators, dozers, loaders, skid steers, cranes, lifts, agricultural equipment and attachments.

/ 02

Actual Cash Value & Condition Reports

Pre-loss condition and value analysis supported by hours, maintenance history, market comparables and physical inspection.

/ 03

Theft, Fire & Vandalism

Loss verification, recovery condition assessment and documentation of what was actually taken or destroyed.

/ 04

Rollover, Transport & Jobsite Losses

Incidents involving machine operation, loading, hauling and jobsite conditions, including structural and frame assessment.

/ 05

Rental Fleet & Dealer Assignments

Fleet damage inspections, return-condition disputes and loss-of-use documentation for rental and dealer operations.

/ 06

Total Loss & Salvage Analysis

Repair-versus-replace analysis, salvage value assessment and support for total loss determinations.

How It Works

A simpler way to handle
complicated claims.

01

Submit

Send the assignment, the file documentation and the contact for the unit. Anything you already have helps; we’ll ask for the rest.

02

Review

Cornerstone evaluates the loss and assigns the right expertise — equipment, commercial, auto or consulting.

03

Inspect

An experienced appraiser handles the physical inspection, teardown coordination and documentation.

04

Report

Findings, photographs, estimate or valuation and supporting analysis are delivered back to the file.
