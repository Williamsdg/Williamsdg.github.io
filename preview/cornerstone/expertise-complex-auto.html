Complex Auto
Loss Appraisal.

The auto files that a photo estimate cannot close — disputed damage, suspect repairs, prior loss overlap and losses where the first appraisal did not hold up.

Overview

Some auto claims still
need a person on site.

Most auto claims should be handled fast and digitally, and Cornerstone has no argument with that. The exceptions are the files where the damage is disputed, the repair is questionable, the history is complicated or the exposure is large enough that being wrong is expensive. Those still need hands on the vehicle.

Assignment Types

What Cornerstone handles.

If the loss is not listed, ask. The list reflects the common assignments, not the limits.

/ 01

Re-inspection & Second Opinion

Independent review where an existing estimate is disputed, appears inflated, or does not match the documented damage.

/ 02

Teardown & Supplement Verification

Coordination and verification of teardown findings and supplement requests, with documentation of what the disassembly actually revealed.

/ 03

Prior & Unrelated Damage

Separating the loss in question from pre-existing damage, prior repairs and unrelated conditions.

/ 04

Repair Quality & Workmanship

Post-repair inspection where quality, method or completeness of the repair is in dispute.

/ 05

Total Loss & Valuation Review

Review of total loss determinations, valuation methodology, condition adjustments and comparable selection.

/ 06

Diminished Value

Documented analysis of value loss following repair, prepared to support or respond to a claim.
