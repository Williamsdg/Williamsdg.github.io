Appraisal, inspection, consulting and program support — delivered as documentation your claim file can rely on.

Service Index

Four service lines.
One point of contact.

Assignments range from a single inspection to a standing program. Cornerstone will tell you plainly if a loss falls outside what it should be handling.

Group 01

Appraisal

/ 01

Independent Damage Appraisal

Physical inspection, damage documentation and a written repair estimate on auto, commercial and heavy equipment losses.

/ 02

Re-inspection & Second Opinion

Independent review of an existing estimate where the scope, method or amount is in question.

/ 03

Total Loss Evaluation

Repair-versus-replace analysis, pre-loss condition assessment and salvage considerations.

/ 04

Actual Cash Value & Valuation Review

Valuation analysis and review of methodology, condition adjustments and comparable selection.

/ 05

Diminished Value Analysis

Documented post-repair value loss analysis prepared to support or respond to a claim.

Group 02

Inspection & Documentation

/ 01

Equipment Condition Reports

Pre-loss, pre-rental, return and periodic condition documentation for equipment and fleets.

/ 02

Teardown Coordination & Verification

Attendance and verification of teardown, with documentation of what disassembly revealed.

/ 03

Post-Repair Inspection

Verification that the repair matches the estimate in scope, method and quality.

/ 04

Photo & Scene Documentation

Structured photographic documentation built for the file, not for the phone.

Group 03

Consulting

/ 01

Appraisal Clause Representation

Service as a named appraiser under the policy’s appraisal provision.

/ 02

Litigation & Counsel Support

Technical review, estimate analysis and consulting support on contested claims.

/ 03

Claims Process Consulting

Review of estimating practice, vendor output quality and file documentation standards.

/ 04

Expert Analysis on Disputed Losses

Independent written analysis where two parties disagree on damage, method or value.

Group 04

Program Support

/ 01

Fleet Inspection Programs

Recurring inspection support with consistent reporting across units and locations.

/ 02

Catastrophe & Overflow Support

Additional appraisal capacity during volume surges and catastrophe deployment.

/ 03

Carrier & TPA Assignment Handling

Standing assignment relationships with defined turnaround and reporting expectations.

/ 04

Vendor Quality Review

Independent review of the work product coming back from other appraisal vendors.

Working With Cornerstone

Who assigns this work.

Cornerstone accepts assignments directly and works alongside existing vendor panels rather than trying to replace them.

/ 01

Carriers & TPAs

Overflow, specialty and complex-loss assignments where the standard panel is not the right fit.

/ 02

Attorneys

Independent technical analysis and consulting on disputed and litigated claims.

/ 03

Fleets & Self-Insured

Direct inspection support for organisations carrying their own risk.

/ 04

Dealers & Rental

Condition reporting, damage documentation and return-condition disputes.

Process

How an assignment works.

01

Submit

Send the assignment, the file documentation and the contact for the unit. Anything you already have helps; we’ll ask for the rest.

02

Review

Cornerstone evaluates the loss and assigns the right expertise — equipment, commercial, auto or consulting.

03

Inspect

An experienced appraiser handles the physical inspection, teardown coordination and documentation.

04

Report

Findings, photographs, estimate or valuation and supporting analysis are delivered back to the file.
