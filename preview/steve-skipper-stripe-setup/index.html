Stripe Setup

Everything needed to get Steve selling through Stripe, in the order it has to happen.

Two decisions already made

The Stripe account belongs to Steve, not Williams Digital — his money goes straight to his bank and never passes through you. And we're matching the Shift4Shop catalog exactly, so nothing he currently sells disappears.

Do not use your own bank details

You said you have the bank account information. Use it only to help Steve finish his onboarding — do not open the account under Williams Digital. If art revenue lands in your Stripe account it becomes your taxable income, you inherit chargeback liability on $4,545 canvases, and untangling it later is painful. Steve is the merchant; you are the developer.

01

Steve creates the Stripe account

Steve does this

He signs up at stripe.com using his own email — ideally a studio address he'll keep long-term, not a personal one he might lose access to.

What Stripe will ask him for:

  • Business type — Anointed Homes Art LLC is an LLC, so he picks Company, not Individual
  • EIN — the LLC's tax ID
  • Legal business name and address — must match his IRS registration exactly
  • His personal details — Stripe verifies the owner: name, DOB, home address, and usually the last 4 of his SSN. This is standard identity verification, required by law
  • Bank account — routing and account number for payouts
  • Statement descriptor — what shows on a buyer's card statement. Suggest SKIPPER ART or ANOINTED HOMES ART
This part he has to do himself

Stripe requires the beneficial owner to enter their own SSN and identity details. Don't do this step for him, and don't ask him to send you his SSN. Sit with him or get on a call if he wants help.

02

Steve invites you as a Developer

Steve does this
  1. In Stripe: Settings → Team and security → Team members → Invite
  2. Invite Dylan@williamsdigital.io
  3. Choose the Developer role
Why Developer and not Administrator

Developer lets you build, test and read API keys. It does not let you move payouts or change his bank details. That protects you both — if anything ever goes wrong financially, there's a clean record that you never had access to the money.

03

Export the real catalog from Shift4Shop

You do this

This is the step everything else depends on. We're matching the catalog exactly, and the prices I scraped off the storefront are not good enough to rebuild a business on.

  1. Log into 307j01402770941.s4shops.com/admin
  2. Go to Products → Product List, then look for Export (Shift4Shop puts it under an Actions or gear menu near the top of the list)
  3. Export all products as CSV
  4. Do a second export for product options / advanced options — this is where the sizes, editions and their prices live, and it is usually a separate file from the product list
  5. Also export Customers and Orders while you're in there
Options are the whole ballgame

A single artwork has up to 10 prices — mini-print, lithograph, giclée, canvas, limited edition, artist proof. If the options export is missing, we have 73 products with no prices and cannot achieve parity. If you can't find that export, tell me and I'll pull them product-by-product from the storefront instead.

Send me the CSVs

Drop them on the kickoff page or email them. I'll reconcile them against the 73 products and 8 categories I already mirrored, and flag anything that doesn't line up.

Good news on shipping

Stripe Checkout handles shipping rates and sales tax natively, so we don't have to build that from scratch. We just need Steve's real shipping costs — a mini-print and a framed 30x40 canvas are very different to ship.

06

Test with real money, then switch

Both
  1. Test-mode purchases across several price points first
  2. Then one real live purchase — ideally a high-value framed piece — to confirm tax, shipping, the receipt and the payout all behave
  3. Refund that test order and confirm the refund lands correctly
  4. Switch the site's buy links from Shift4Shop to the new checkout
  5. Redirect old product URLs so nothing 404s

Don't cancel it the day we launch. Give it at least one full billing cycle as a fallback, and make sure the order and customer exports are safely downloaded and readable first. The moment that account lapses, the data and images hosted there stop being reachable.
