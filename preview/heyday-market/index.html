Why "Hey Day"
Dating all the way back to World War II, Auburn University's student body pushed for a day that would unite the Auburn Family and promote a friendly atmosphere known as Hey Day.

Each year, the Student Government Association carries on this tradition of handing out name tags and encouraging students and faculty to greet one another. The market's name is an ode to it: a bright and airy 10,000-square-foot food hall where campus and community come together.

FAQ

Quick answers.

What are the hours of operation?

Open 7 days a week for lunch and dinner from 10:30 AM until 9:00 PM. Hey Day Market is closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, July 4th, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

Do you accept Tiger Card?

Yes, students can spend declining balance dollars and use their Tiger Club cards at each vendor except for The Bar. Block meals cannot be used in the Hey Day Market.

Can I use cash in Hey Day Market?

No, Hey Day is a cashless market.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is not available from Hey Day Market vendors at this time.

What is Hey Day Golf Club?

Five holes of mini-golf on the Greenspace, 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Visit The Bar stall inside Hey Day and grab your putters and score cards.

What about parking?

Public parking is available at the street level along College Street, and in nearby public parking decks downtown. Complimentary parking is available on floors 2 & 3 in the South College Street Parking Deck after 5 PM, Monday – Friday, and any time Saturday – Sunday. Valet parking is available for $10 at The Hotel at Auburn University.
