    Straight answers

    Ask it once.

    The questions the office hears most, answered before anyone has to fill out a form.

    What is Watersound?

    Watersound is a service mark of The St. Joe Company. Under the brand, St. Joe has developed a collection of communities, hotels and resorts, and experiences along the Gulf Coast of Northwest Florida, generally following the Intracoastal Waterway from Choctawhatchee Bay to West Bay.

    Can I rent a vacation house through Watersound?

    No. Watersound does not handle vacation rentals within its communities. Visit Panama City Beach and Visit South Walton both keep rental directories. If you would rather book a room, the ten hotels and resorts are here.

    I already rented a home here and have a question. Who do I call?

    The company or individual you rented from. Watersound does not manage short-term rentals, so the office cannot help with a booking.

    I want to buy a lot or a home. Who do I contact?

    The Watersound Discovery Center at Watersound Town Center, or the Latitude Margaritaville Sales Center for the 55-and-better community. Compare the neighborhoods first.

    How do I find out about leasing space?

    For town centers, commerce parks and professional offices, call (888) 836-8353 or email lease@joe.com.

    Is there commercial land for sale?

    Yes. Commerce parks in both Bay and Walton counties offer commercial land. The St. Joe commercial team is at (888) 836-8353 or lease@joe.com.

    What is the best way to get here?

    Three airports with major airlines serve the area. Northwest Florida Beaches International (ECP) is the closest. See all three.
