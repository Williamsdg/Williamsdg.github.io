Golf carts, in plain English

Before anyone takes the cart out.

The association's LSV policy runs three pages. Here is what it asks of owners, guests and renters, in the order people actually need it.

"Golf cart violations are the number one complaint reported to the HOA."Prominence POA LSV (Golf Cart) Policy, amended March and April 2022

What do I need?

Pick the one that fits.

    A summary for convenience. The signed policy is what governs.

    Who can drive

    • Licensed drivers only. An unlicensed driver may not drive, even with a licensed adult on board.
    • Guests and renters need the owner's written permission and a signed copy of the policy.

    On the road

    • Headlights and taillights on between sunset and sunrise.
    • Everyone seated and restrained while the cart is moving.
    • Speed limits and stop signs apply, the same as any vehicle.
    • No cart trailers parked on property.

    Outside the community

    • Carts on public roads must be street legal.
    • Only roads posted 35 mph or less.
    • No motorized vehicles on the 30A bike path.
    • Drive sober, no open containers, no texting.

    If a rule is broken

    First offense$100The homeowner is notified and is responsible for the fine.
    Second offense$100 moreAn additional fine, handled the same way.
    Third offenseCart bootedSeven days for an owner. For a guest, the rest of the stay or seven days, whichever is less.
    Repeat or interference6 months offPrivileges can be rescinded after more than six violations in 12 months, or for preventing security from doing its job.

    Source: Prominence POA LSV (Golf Cart) Policy, fillable PDF. Reckless driving is determined by Prominence security officers.
