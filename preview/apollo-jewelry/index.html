The House · Est. 1985

It began with a love story

In August 1983, on Santorini, Yiannis Psomas — a 26-year-old Greek jeweller already experienced in the trade — met Karin Steiner, a 22-year-old Austrian graduate in economics and business who was visiting the island on holiday. What began as a chance meeting soon became a lifelong partnership.

In 1985, driven by their shared vision, they opened their first small jewellery store in Santorini. Over the years Apollo Jewelry grew into three boutique stores, offering a carefully curated selection of high-quality jewellery — from modern designs to classic, traditional Greek and Byzantine handmade pieces in gold and silver.

A longer history

In ancient Greece, jewellery symbolized power, wealth, social status and devotion to the gods. The Mycenaean civilization (1600–1100 BC) marked a significant rise in craftsmanship: gold became the primary decorative material, and artisans created rings, necklaces, earrings, bracelets and pendants adorned with floral motifs, human figures and intricate engravings — many inlaid with pearls, emerald, garnet, carnelian, agate, sardonyx, chalcedony and rock crystal.

Authenticity & assurance

Stated materials

Every piece in this catalogue lists its exact metal — 14k or 18k solid gold, gold-plated, or sterling silver 925 — together with its stones, colour and, where the workshop specifies them, its dimensions.

Greek workshops

Pieces are made with small artisan workshops throughout Greece. The house describes preserving that craft as both a privilege and a responsibility.

If something is wrong

If an item arrives damaged, or the wrong item is received, notify Apollo Jewelry within 48 hours of delivery with clear photographs of the issue and the claim will be reviewed.

Please note: all sales are final. Because of the custom, handmade and personal nature of the jewellery, Apollo Jewelry does not offer refunds, returns or cancellations once an order has been placed and payment received. Exchanges are permitted in person at the store only, with prior approval, within 15 days of purchase, for an item of equal or greater value.

Payment

Four ways to pay, all of them secure

Credit & debit card

Processed through the Nexi XPay platform of Nexi Payments Greece S.A., using TLS 1.2 with 128-bit SSL encryption. Apollo Jewelry does not collect or store card details — which is why they must be re-entered each time.

PayPal

The transaction is completed on PayPal's own secure page, transferring from your PayPal account to the Apollo Jewelry account.

Bank transfer

Deposit into the company's Alpha Bank account, quoting your full name and order number. The deposit must be completed within 5 working days of order confirmation.

Cash on delivery

Available for Greece and Cyprus only, paid to the courier or Hellenic Post on receipt. Free of charge throughout Greece; €25 for Cyprus via General Post. Where an order is paid by card, it is handed to the cardholder in person on presentation of ID or passport and the card — or to a third party with written authorisation and ID.

An Apollo app,
if the house wanted one

Apollo Jewelry does not currently have a mobile app, and this concept does not assume one. But a house that sells to visitors who fly home the next morning — and to customers who buy again from the other side of the world — has an obvious case for one. Here is what it could hold.

  • Private collection previewsNew pieces shown to returning customers before they reach the shop floor.
  • Wish listsSaved across devices, and shareable — the way most fine jewellery is actually bought.
  • Appointment bookingReserve a time on Ipapantis before the ferry arrives.
  • Personal recommendationsBuilt from what a customer has saved and bought, not from a generic feed.
  • Sizing assistanceThe measurement guide, saved sizes, and a direct line to the family for anything unclear.
  • Order trackingDHL and Hellenic Post consignments followed from Fira to the door.
  • Exclusive releasesLimited workshop pieces offered to app customers first.
  • The store in your pocketThe full catalogue, with the same materials and prices shown here.

This is an illustration of what an app could do. No Apollo Jewelry app exists today, and nothing here is a commitment on the part of the business.
