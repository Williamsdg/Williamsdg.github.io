About the Admin

The admin at vestlakelibertypark.com/admin is where approved staff sign in to manage the site — calendar events, newsletters, and documents (bylaws, minutes, financials, forms). Everything published there appears on the public website immediately, and nothing can be permanently deleted — items are archived and can always be restored. Each staff member gets their own email/password sign-in with a self-service password reset. There is no monthly cost for any of this; the Association's only recurring cost remains the ~$30/year domain registration.
