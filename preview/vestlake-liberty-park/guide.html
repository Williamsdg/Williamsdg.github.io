Admin Cheat Sheet

How to update the community website — events, newsletters, and documents. Every task here takes about two minutes, and everything you publish appears on the site instantly.

Open the Admin →

1 Signing In

vestlakelibertypark.com/admin

  1. Go to vestlakelibertypark.com/admin and enter your work email and password.
  2. Forgot your password? Type your email, tap "Forgot password?", and a reset link is emailed to you — no need to call anyone.
💡 Bookmark the admin on your phone — it works just as well there as on a computer.

2 Posting a Calendar Event

Calendar Events → Add Event

  1. Open Calendar Events in the sidebar and tap + Add Event.
  2. Fill in the name and date. Time, location, and description are optional — an "all-day" toggle is there for things like Pool Closing.
  3. Check "Feature on homepage" if it should appear in the Upcoming Events section on the front page.
  4. Tap Preview to see how it will look, then Publish. It's on the public calendar immediately.
💡 Not ready to announce it? Use Save Draft instead — drafts never appear on the public site until you publish them.

3 Posting the Monthly Newsletter

Newsletters → Post Newsletter

  1. Keep sending the newsletter out to residents exactly the way you do today — nothing about your normal process changes.
  2. Then, to put it on the website: open Newsletters → + Post Newsletter, tap the upload box, and choose the same PDF.
  3. Pick the issue month and year — the title fills itself in.
  4. Tap Publish. It becomes the featured "Latest Issue" and files itself into the archive under the right year automatically.
💡 The website is the reading archive — residents can read every issue there any time, but your regular send-out stays your send-out.

4 Adding a Document

Documents → Add Document

  1. Open Documents → + Add Document and upload the PDF (minutes, budget, a form — anything).
  2. Choose which section of the public Documents page it belongs in: Governing Documents, Board Meeting Minutes, Financial Reports, or Forms & Resident Guides.
  3. Give it a title (it suggests one from the file name) and tap Publish.
💡 Every document automatically gets its own page where residents can read it right in the browser or download it.

5 Fixing Mistakes

Nothing is ever lost

  1. Edit anything after publishing — changes go live instantly.
  2. Archive removes something from the public site but keeps it in the admin — you can restore it any time.
  3. Nothing can be permanently deleted, so you can't break anything. Experiment freely.

Common Questions

How fast do changes show up on the website?
Immediately. Publish, then refresh the public page and it's there.
Can residents see my drafts?
No — only published items appear on the public site. Drafts and archived items are visible only inside the admin.
What file types can I upload?
PDFs, for both newsletters and documents.
