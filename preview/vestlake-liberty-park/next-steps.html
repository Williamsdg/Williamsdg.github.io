Getting Started

Next Steps for Your New Website

Thank you for moving forward with Williams Digital! Here's everything the board needs to do to get the Vestlake Communities at Liberty Park website live. Work through these four steps and send the items back to me — once I have them, your site comes together fast.

1 Register Your Domain Name

Where your website will live, e.g. vestlakelibertypark.com — registered in the Association's name, not mine.

Your domain is the web address residents will type to reach the site. It costs roughly $12–$20 per year and the Association owns it outright — there are no monthly hosting fees. Here's how to register one through GoDaddy:

  1. Go to godaddy.com and use the search bar at the top to look up a domain — for example vestlakelibertypark.com or vestlakehoa.com. Try a few options if your first choice is taken.
  2. When you find one that's available and you like, click "Add to Cart", then "Continue to Cart."
  3. On the cart page, decline the add-ons GoDaddy offers (email, website builder, "Full Domain Protection," etc.) — you don't need any of them. Just the domain itself.
  4. One add-on worth keeping: "Domain Privacy" (often free or a few dollars) hides the board's personal contact info from public domain records. Optional, but nice to have.
  5. Create a GoDaddy account (or sign in) using a board email the Association controls — ideally a shared board inbox rather than a personal address, so future board members keep access.
  6. Choose a registration term — 1 or 2 years is fine — and complete checkout. Turn auto-renew ON so the domain never lapses.
  7. Send me the login details (or add me as a delegate) so I can point the domain at your new website. I'll handle all the technical DNS setup — you don't need to touch any of that.
Tip: Pick a domain that's short and easy to say out loud. If you'd like, send me 2–3 names you're considering before you buy and I'll tell you which one works best.
Already own a domain? If the Association already has a web address from a previous site or vendor, you don't need to buy a new one — just let me know what it is and where it's registered, and I'll connect it.

2 Send Photos & Videos

The visuals that make the site feel like your community.

Great photos make a huge difference. Send me any images or short videos you'd like featured on the site and I'll place and optimize them. Don't worry about sizing or editing — just send the originals.

Helpful shots to include:

  • The neighborhood entrance / signage
  • The pool and any common amenities
  • Walking trails, lakes, green spaces, and landscaping
  • Community events, cookouts, or gatherings
  • Seasonal photos (spring blooms, fall color, holiday decorations)
  • Any existing logo or branding the Association uses

How to send them:

  1. Easiest: email them directly to Dylan@williamsdigital.io. If there are a lot, your email may split them into a few messages — that's fine.
  2. For large batches or videos: upload to a free Google Drive or Dropbox folder and send me the share link. This avoids email size limits.
  3. From your phone: you can text or AirDrop them and forward to my email — whatever's easiest for you.
No photos yet? No problem — I can start with tasteful stock and placeholder imagery in your brand colors, and we'll swap in real community photos as you gather them.
