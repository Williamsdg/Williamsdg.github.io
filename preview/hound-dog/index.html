Welcome Aboard the Hound Dog

Your Pensacola Beach fishing trip shouldn't feel like another tourist attraction.

Climb aboard, leave the beach behind, and spend the day fishing the Gulf with a local captain who knows these waters.

The Hound Dog is a 40-foot Infinity sportfisher built for offshore work — a big, stable deck, a flybridge view of the water, and room for your whole crew.

Good to Know

Questions? Answered.

Pensacola Beach Marina on Pensacola Beach Blvd — right on the beach, minutes from the hotels and condos. Park in the white gravel lot on the north side of Aloha Liquor and walk to the dock.

Up to 6 guests. Every trip is private — it's just your group, the captain, and the Gulf. No strangers, no crowds.

None at all. First-timers, kids, and seasoned anglers all fish the Hound Dog. The gear is rigged before you step aboard, and the captain coaches anyone who wants it.

Rods, reels, fishing gear, ice, and coolers are all covered. Bring food and drinks for your group, sunscreen, a hat, polarized sunglasses, and soft-soled shoes. And your camera.

Rates depend on trip length and time of year, so current pricing is a quick phone call away: 850-932-0304. Listed prices are cash-discount prices — credit cards add a 3.5% processing fee, and taxes and gratuity aren't included.

Safety calls belong to the captain. If the Gulf isn't safe to fish, your trip gets rescheduled — you'll never be taken out in conditions the captain doesn't trust.

Everything that's legal and in season goes home with you, iced from the moment it hits the deck. Dinner doesn't get any fresher.
