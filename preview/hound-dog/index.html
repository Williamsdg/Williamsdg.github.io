Plan Your Trip

First Time Deep Sea Fishing?

Quick answers that make the day easy — and one dock to find.

What should we bring?

Food, drinks, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, and comfortable clothing. Soft-soled shoes never hurt — and don't forget the camera.

Can kids come?

The Gulf makes core memories. Trip length matters with younger anglers, so run your crew's ages by Capt. Pasco and he'll point you to the right day.

Do we need fishing licenses?

On licensed Gulf charters, coverage typically rides with the boat — not each guest. Confirm when you book, and it's one less thing on your list.

Will you clean our fish?

Your keepers come off the boat iced. Ask about dockside cleaning when you book — a fresh Gulf dinner is the whole point.

What if the weather turns?

Safety calls belong to the captain. If the Gulf isn't fishable, your trip gets rescheduled — nobody goes out on a bad day.

How many can come?

Up to 6 guests, and every trip is private — just your group, the captain, and the water. Rates by phone: 850-932-0304.
