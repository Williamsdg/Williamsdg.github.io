1.Scope of Work

Williams Digital agrees to design, develop and deploy a complete rebuild of the Timely Entertainment website, deployed to the timelyent.com domain. The design direction and homepage are already built and available for review at the preview link above.

Homepage
The full custom build shown in the preview, including the entrance sequence, lighting system and scroll-driven sections.
Division Pages
Dedicated Athletes and Talent pages for the two personal management divisions.
About & Executive Team
Firm overview plus an individual biography page for each of the three executives.
Contact & Legal
Contact page with office details, and a Legal Policy page.
Registration & Login Styling
The existing Athlete Registration, Artist Registration and Login systems are retained and restyled to match the new site.
Mobile & Tablet
Every page laid out for phone and tablet as its own composition, not a scaled desktop view.
Accessibility
Keyboard navigation and a full reduced-motion mode for visitors who request reduced animation.
Content Migration
Existing copy carried across from the current timelyent.com and set into the new design.
Hosting & Deployment
Domain configuration, SSL, and launch to timelyent.com.
Revisions & Support
Two rounds of revisions during design, and 30 days of post-launch support.

2.Not Included

The following fall outside this agreement and will be quoted separately if wanted:

  • Rebuilding the Athlete Registration, Artist Registration or Login systems. Section 1 covers restyling the existing systems, not replacing the underlying member infrastructure.
  • timelynil.com and any other property. This agreement covers timelyent.com only.
  • Photography, videography and stock licensing. Existing client-supplied images will be retouched and colour-graded to match the design at no additional cost.
  • Copywriting beyond migrating and lightly editing existing content.
  • Domain registration, premium fonts and third-party licences, which are billed at cost if required.
