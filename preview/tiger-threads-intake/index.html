What we need per product for Shopify.

Hi Sherrie — here's the full checklist of what to send over for each item you want listed on the Tiger Threads website. The more you can include up front, the faster we'll have your shop live. Required fields can't be skipped; everything else makes the product page look better but is optional.

8 sections ~5 min per item once you're rolling Auto-saves your check marks locally
01 · The Basics

Identify the product.

The bare minimum we need to even create the listing in Shopify. Without these five things the product can't go live.

✓
Product title Required
The name customers will see. Keep it short and descriptive — what it is, not just a clever name. We can add a tagline elsewhere.
Good: "Cropped War Eagle Tee"  ·  Better: "Cropped War Eagle Tee — Burnt Orange"
Avoid: "TT-001" or "New Drop!!!"
✓
Category / collection Required
Which of the 8 shop pages it belongs to. A product can live in more than one (e.g., a vintage hat could be in both Hats and Vintage).
Apparel · Hats · Jewelry · Serveware · Tailgate Games · Home Decor · Gifts · Vintage
✓
Price (USD) Required
What you sell it for. If it's on sale, also give us the original "compare-at" price so we can show the strikethrough.
Price: $36  ·  Compare-at (if on sale): $48
✓
How many you have Required
Total quantity in stock. If the item comes in sizes/colors, break it down per variant (see §3). One-of-a-kind vintage = qty 1.
Single SKU: "18 in stock"  ·  Variant breakdown: "S: 4, M: 6, L: 5, XL: 3"
✓
At least one good photo Required
See §4 below for the full photo spec, but the absolute minimum is one clear, well-lit shot of the front of the item.
02 · The Copy

Help it sell itself.

Words that make the product feel desirable. Even one or two sentences from you in your voice beats anything we'd make up.

✓
Short description (1–2 sentences) Required
Shows on the product card in the grid and at the top of the product page. Punchy, scannable, tells someone why they want it.
Cropped War Eagle Tee: "Vintage-wash crop in burnt orange — soft, broken-in feel, fits like your favorite old tee."
✓
Long description (a small paragraph) Optional but recommended
The full pitch. Story behind the piece, who it's perfect for, what makes it special. Especially important for vintage and one-of-a-kinds.
For a vintage item: "Pulled from an Auburn alum's closet outside Birmingham — original 1989 Champion reverse-weave, single-stitch hem, no holes, no fading. Size large. When it sells, it's gone."
✓
"Highlights" — 3 to 5 bullet points Optional
Quick-scan features. Material, fit, what's included, care instructions, anything that answers a common customer question.
• 100% combed cotton
• Oversized fit — size down for a slim look
• Hand-embroidered "War Eagle" detail
• Machine wash cold, tumble dry low
✓
Tags / keywords Optional
Words you'd type if you were searching for this. Helps Google find it and helps customers filter on the shop pages.
auburn, war eagle, gameday, tee, cropped, vintage wash, women, burnt orange, fall, tailgate
03 · Variants

Sizes, colors, and options.

Anything where the same product comes in multiple versions. Each variant gets its own price (usually same) and its own inventory count.

✓
List every size you have Required if applicable
Apparel especially — give us the size run plus quantity per size. Use your normal sizing labels (S, M, L, XL, XXL, or numeric).
Cropped War Eagle Tee: "S — 4 left · M — 6 · L — 5 · XL — 3 · XXL — sold out"
✓
List every color / colorway If applicable
Same product, different color = different variant. Include a swatch photo or color name if it's not obvious.
Trucker Hat — colors: "Navy/Orange · Cream/Navy · All Orange"
✓
Other options If applicable
Anything else customers pick — necklace chain length, monogram letters, gift wrap yes/no, candle scent, etc.
Auburn Initial Necklace: "Letter: A–Z" · Custom Cornhole: "Family name (text field, 12 char max)"
✓
Price differences between variants If applicable
If XXL costs more than S, or the gold-fill version costs more than the silver, tell us so we can charge correctly.
Auburn Tee: "S–XL: $36 · XXL: $40 · XXXL: $42"
04 · Photos

Show the product.

This is the single biggest thing that affects whether a product sells online. Don't worry about being a pro photographer — natural light + a clean background is 90% of the way there.

📸 Phone photo cheat-sheet

Take photos near a window during daylight.

Use a plain background (white wall, neutral linen, wood floor). Hold the phone steady, tap to focus, and take 4–6 shots per product — front, back, detail/close-up, and one styled / on a model if it makes sense.

Shoot in portrait orientation for product cards (taller than wide). We'll crop and color-correct as needed.

✓
Main "hero" photo Required
Front-on, well-lit, clean background. This is the one shown on the product card in the shop grid.
✓
Additional angles (3–5) Strongly recommended
Back · close-up of detail or embroidery · tag/label · styled or worn shot · scale shot (next to something familiar for size reference on home decor).
✓
A photo of each color variant If applicable
If the same hat comes in 3 colors, send 3 hero shots — one per color. Customers can then click a color and see that variant's photo.
✓
Naming the files Helps us a lot
Name each photo with the product name so we don't have to guess. Use dashes, no spaces.
cropped-war-eagle-tee-front.jpg
cropped-war-eagle-tee-back.jpg
cropped-war-eagle-tee-detail.jpg
✓
File size Helpful
Phone originals are perfect. Don't shrink or screenshot — we'd rather have a 5MB original than a 200KB blurry version.
05 · Shipping & Physical

How it ships.

Shopify needs to know the weight and rough size to calculate shipping at checkout. Ballpark is fine — we don't need lab-grade measurements.

✓
Weight (with packaging) Required
Roughly. A kitchen or postal scale gets you close enough. If you don't have one, we can estimate from the category.
Tee: ~6 oz · Hat: ~4 oz · Tumbler set: ~2 lbs · Cornhole set: ~25 lbs
✓
Box / package size For oversized items
Only matters for bigger stuff (cornhole, ladder ball, large home decor). Length × width × height of the box you'd ship it in.
Cornhole set: ~ 24" × 16" × 6"
✓
Local-pickup option? Yes/No
Should Auburn-area customers be able to choose "pick up at the next pop-up" or "pick up from Sherrie" instead of paying for shipping? Easy to add per product.
✓
Any items that can't ship? Heads-up
Fragile vintage, oversized signs, anything you'd only sell in person — flag these so we can mark them "in-store / pop-up only."
06 · Inventory & SKUs

Keeping count.

Shopify will auto-generate SKU numbers if you don't have your own system. The only thing we really need from you is whether each item is one-of-a-kind or restockable.

✓
Is it one-of-a-kind or can you reorder? Required
If a tee can be reordered from your vendor, we'll show "back in stock soon" when it sells out. If it's vintage / 1-of-1, we'll mark it "Sold" permanently and remove it from the grid.
Restockable: Most apparel, hats, jewelry, serveware
One-of-a-kind: All vintage, hand-painted signs, monogrammed orders
✓
"Low stock" threshold Optional
When inventory hits this number, the Trailer Console flags it for reorder. Default is 5 — let us know if you want different per category.
✓
Your own SKU codes If you already use them
If you already have an organizing system (sticker numbers, vendor part numbers, anything), send those and we'll use them. Otherwise Shopify will auto-generate.
✓
Where is it stored Optional
Trailer · home storage · vendor drop-ship. Helps the Trailer Console show what's actually loaded vs. what's at the house.

08 · Advanced (mostly optional)

Nice-to-haves for SEO and ads.

Skip this whole section if it feels like a lot — we'll fill defaults in. But if you want max visibility on Google, Instagram Shopping, and Pinterest, these are the things that move the needle.

✓
SEO title & meta description We'll auto-fill if blank
What shows in Google search results. We can generate good defaults from the product info — only send custom ones if you have something specific in mind.
✓
Brand / vendor Optional
Who made it. "Tiger Threads" for in-house, or the actual brand name for things you resell (Comfort Colors, Champion, etc.).
✓
Material composition Apparel only
"100% cotton" / "60/40 cotton-poly" / "tri-blend." Customers filter on this and it helps with Google Shopping.
✓
Care instructions Optional
Wash cold · tumble dry low · hand wash · dry clean. Pre-empts returns and gets a tiny SEO bump.
✓
Country of origin / made in Optional
Required if you ever ship internationally; otherwise nice to have.
✓
Tax category We'll default to standard taxable
Alabama charges sales tax on most retail. Apparel = taxable. We default everything to standard rate unless you tell us otherwise.
TL;DR

The absolute fast path.

If you just want to send a batch and not think too hard about it — here's the bare minimum per item.

The 7-thing fast list

  • Title — "Cropped War Eagle Tee"
  • Category — Apparel
  • Price — $36
  • Sizes & quantities — S:4, M:6, L:5, XL:3
  • 1–2 sentence description — in your own voice
  • 3–5 photos — phone shots in natural light, named after the product
  • One-of-a-kind or restockable? — "restockable" or "1-of-1"
📂 How to send it

Easiest format: a shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder.

Make one folder per product, named the product title. Drop the photos in, plus a short text file (or a single shared Google Doc/Sheet) with the info above.

OR — if it's easier — text/email me the info per product and I'll organize it. Whatever's least friction for you.
