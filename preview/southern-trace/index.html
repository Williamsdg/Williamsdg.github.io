01 Around Southern Trace
The neighborhood

More than a neighborhood.

Southern Trace is a community where neighbors know one another, families put down roots, and everyday life feels a little more connected. The clubhouse and pool sit at the center of it — and most of what the association does happens within a few hundred yards of them.
