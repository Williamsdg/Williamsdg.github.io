NEW TO SOUTHERN TRACE?

Set Up Your Owner Account

Just closed on a home in Southern Trace? Get access to your owner portal in three steps.

1

Verify Your Address

Enter the address on your closing documents — we'll match it to your neighborhood and lot record.

2

Confirm Your Email

We'll send a one-time activation link to the email on file with the closing attorney or title company.

3

Set Up Auto-Pay

Optional but recommended — never miss an assessment and avoid late fees with bank draft or card on file.
