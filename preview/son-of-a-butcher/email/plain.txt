HOW THIS IMPROVES ON YOUR CURRENT SITE
- First impression: today, a standard restaurant template: a tagline over a photo, then gift-card and newsletter banners and three "Learn more" boxes. In the concept: An editorial storefront built on your own brand — Butch's portrait, your photography, and "Quality provisions for the common cook" front and center.
- Hours: today, open status sits in the footer; the full hours are a click away on the Contact page. In the concept: A live "Open now · till 6:30pm" in the header on every page, with today highlighted in the hours list.
- The counters: today, one long Products page of paragraphs and lists. In the concept: Meat, Seafood and Cheese & Specialty as tabbed menus that name the producers you buy from.
- Your people: today, staff picks sit at the bottom of the About page. In the concept: "Ask the Counter": tap Addam, Will, Rachel or anyone else and see their three favorite things in the shop.
- Sammies: today, a short paragraph on the Products page. In the concept: Their own menu board, with a status that flips to "On the counter now" at 11 (9 on Saturdays).
- Press & news: today, a row of small outlet logos. In the concept: A Journal for shop news, stories from the counter and every article written about you — somewhere for Emily's content to live and for Google to find.
- Shipping: today, a "Buy Online" link that leaves the site. In the concept: The Direct boxes, photographed and priced, right on the homepage.
- On a phone: today, a single "Order Now" button; hours, directions and products are behind the menu. In the concept: A Call · Directions · Order bar under your thumb, open status up top, and swipeable counters, team and boxes.
