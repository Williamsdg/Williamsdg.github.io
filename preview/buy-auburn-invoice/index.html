Scope delivered

Everything built and deployed under this engagement, March 23 – August 12, 2026.

Public website

  • Homepage with featured businesses and subscribe flow
  • Business directory with detail modals
  • Business application page
  • Privacy policy, terms of service, unsubscribe pages
  • Fully responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop

Brand & visual identity

  • Buy Auburn logo suite — six vector marks and variations
  • Auburn navy and orange color system, typography
  • Favicon, Apple touch icon, social share (OG) image
  • Branded email header artwork
  • Collectible trading-card business card design

Directory & search

  • State, city, industry and keyword filtering
  • “Near Me” geolocation search with radius control
  • Multiple locations per business
  • Industry icons, colors and verified badges
  • Collapsible mobile filter interface

Application & onboarding

  • Conversion-focused application form
  • Business logo upload with client-side compression
  • Local storefront / online business selector
  • Social profiles, referral capture, phone privacy option
  • Terms consent, email confirmation, honeypot and rate limiting

Admin dashboard

  • Secure login with password management
  • Approve, waive payment, edit, reject and remove listings
  • Full edit modal with photo upload
  • Filterable business and application views
  • Resend payment link for unpaid listings

Payments

  • Stripe integration for $300/year business listings
  • Webhook auto-activating listings on payment
  • Pricing tier selection in the approval flow
  • Written Stripe setup documentation for the client

Automated email

  • New application notification to admins
  • Approval email with payment link
  • Welcome, season-start and game-week campaigns
  • Away-game and travel alert subscriptions
  • Branded templates with one-click unsubscribe

The Barn podcast integration

  • Podcast segment management in the admin dashboard
  • Spotify embeds with auto-pulled title and artwork
  • Homepage carousel — swipe on mobile, arrows on desktop
  • Segments linkable to a featured business profile
  • Barn partnership banner

Backend & infrastructure

  • Supabase database, authentication and file storage
  • Seven serverless API functions on Vercel
  • Production hosting with continuous deployment
  • buyauburn.com, www redirect and SSL
  • auburnfannetwork.com redirect configuration

Analytics, security & performance

  • Page-view tracking with an admin analytics dashboard
  • Cross-site scripting protection across all user content
  • Submission rate limiting and bot filtering
  • Image pipeline rebuild — assets cut from 23.1 MB to 1.1 MB
  • Directory payload cut from 11 MB to 39 KB with lazy loading
