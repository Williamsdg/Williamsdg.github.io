    Good to know

    Before you go.

    What happens if the weather turns?

    If the meltemi blows too hard for a safe cruise, the skipper will change the route to the sheltered side, move you to another day, or refund you in full. You don’t lose money to the wind.

    Where do the boats leave from?

    Most cruises leave from Vlychada Marina on the south coast. We include a free hotel pickup and drop-off for every private charter.

    What licence do I need for an ATV?

    A full car licence (category B) that you’ve held for at least a year. Non-EU licences need an International Driving Permit alongside them. Helmets are compulsory and included.

    Are the experiences good for children?

    Most are. Use the “Family” filter above. Children under 12 ride free on private charters, and we bring child life jackets for every boat.

    Can we bring our own food or a cake?

    Of course. Tell us when you book and the crew will chill it, plate it and bring out the candles at the right moment.

    What’s the cancellation policy?

    Free cancellation up to 72 hours before a charter and 24 hours before a rental or experience. Weather cancellations are always refunded in full.
