2.Brand Direction

The Client has identified radianthairco.com as a reference for layout and overall feel. That site is a directional reference only. Williams Digital will design an original identity and layout for the Client, and will not copy the reference site's design, code, photography, copy or brand assets.

Direction is agreed on the homepage first. Once the homepage is approved, the remaining pages are built to match it, so the Client is not reviewing a full site built in a direction they did not want.

4.Initial Catalogue & Content Load

The Client supplies existing content, products, inventory information, photography, pricing and service detail. Williams Digital organises and loads all of it into the site as part of this agreement — the Client does not start from an empty site.

The included initial load covers up to 75 products (including variants such as length, texture and colour), up to 6 Wig Bible articles, up to 3 blog posts, all service descriptions, the non-profit page content, and all team biographies. Loading beyond those quantities is quoted at the hourly rate in Section 6 and agreed in writing first.

Williams Digital will not originate product specifications, prices, inventory counts, service pricing, biographies or credentials. All such material comes from the Client and must be confirmed in writing by the Client before launch.

5.Commerce Platform, Booking & Third-Party Subscriptions

Inventory and payments. Retail sales run on a commerce platform that owns products, inventory, orders, customers, tax, shipping and checkout. Williams Digital's recommendation is Shopify, and this agreement is priced on that basis. Williams Digital will set the account up, configure it and connect it to the site. If the Client prefers a different commerce platform, that is agreed in writing before development begins; a platform requiring a materially different build is re-quoted.

Booking. Appointments run on a dedicated scheduling system — for example Square Appointments, Acuity or a Shopify booking application. Williams Digital will recommend an option, configure services, durations, staff, availability and deposit rules, and embed it into the site styled to the brand. A custom-built booking engine is not part of this agreement.

Subscriptions and fees are paid directly by the Client to the providers: the commerce platform subscription, the scheduling subscription, payment processing and transaction fees, domain registration and renewal, any paid applications, and any premium fonts or licensed stock imagery. These are separate from the $1,500 build fee and from the $50 monthly fee in Section 7. Williams Digital will not add a markup and will not approve a paid application without the Client's written agreement.

8.Ongoing Content Management — What Each Party Handles

This section exists so that expectations are clear from the start. Williams Digital builds and maintains the systems; the Client runs the day-to-day content.

Williams Digital handles everything in Section 7 — hosting, storage, backups, security, the publishing system, the forms, technical support and reasonable minor updates. If something breaks, the Client contacts Williams Digital and it gets fixed.

The Client handles day-to-day operation after launch — adding and editing products, updating inventory and pricing, changing product photography, featuring new items, writing and publishing Wig Bible articles and blog posts, keeping the non-profit page current, managing the booking calendar, and social media. The site is built specifically so none of that requires a developer.

Williams Digital is happy to take on content work, merchandising, or article and blog writing as a separate quoted engagement, but it is not included in the build fee or the monthly fee and is not assumed in either.

9.The Client's Non-Profit

This agreement includes one page presenting the Client's non-profit — its mission, its work, how to get involved and how to contact it — built as part of this website and covered by the fee in Section 6. A separate website for the non-profit is not included.

The non-profit may be a distinct legal entity from the retail business named on page one of this agreement. The Client confirms it is authorised to publish the non-profit's name, logo, mission statement and materials on this website. All statements about the non-profit's tax status, registration, charitable status and use of funds are the Client's, and Williams Digital will not originate or verify them.

Online donation collection is not included. Taking donations through the website requires a separate payment or donation platform, its own account and fees, tax-receipting, and donor record keeping — none of which are part of this build, this fee or the monthly service in Section 7. Williams Digital is glad to add and configure a donation platform as separately quoted work, and will recommend options on request.
