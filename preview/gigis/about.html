Meet Susan and Katherine

We're two sisters and moms who believe dressing juniors, tweens and children can be both fun and stylish. We opened GiGi's Tween in 2008, just four years after starting its sister shop, The Lili Pad, which features children's clothing, in our hometown of Birmingham, Alabama.

As our own girls grew, we saw the need for tween clothing that both we and our daughters could appreciate. GiGi's became the place where we offered other moms the chance to shop with their daughters for stylish, quality pieces in a fun environment.

You could say we were destined to have a retail shop because it's in our blood. We grew up watching our mom, owner of Town and Country Clothes in Mountain Brook, serve clients and friends through her store. She passed away in 2002, and it's not lost on us that we're fortunate to get to remember and honor her every day through the work we do.

We believe small businesses are the backbone of our community, and this motivates us to be involved and give back. We partner with the Junior League of Birmingham and support other local charitable organizations.
