      Two sisters, two moms, one shop

      As our own girls grew, we saw the need for tween clothing that both we and our daughters could appreciate. GiGi's became the place where moms shop with their daughters for stylish, quality pieces in a fun environment.

      1. Where it startedWe grew up watching our mom serve clients and friends at her store, Town and Country Clothes in Mountain Brook.
      2. 2004We opened The Lili Pad, our children's clothing shop.
      3. 2008GiGi's Tween opened, just four years later.
