1The Proposal

Developer will design and build a custom website for Legacy Sports Media — a modern, mobile-first site that reflects the Legacy Sports Media brand system and presents the Client as a confident, professional voice in local sports coverage.

  • Custom design built around the Legacy Sports Media brand system.
  • Mobile-first, fast-loading, and search-friendly out of the box.
  • Clean, modern layout designed to read well across phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • One flat build price with clear payment milestones and a simple monthly plan to keep the site fast, secure, and maintained.

4Terms & Conditions

  • Getting started. Work begins once the 50% deposit is received and the Client has provided the content and materials needed for the build.
  • Going live. The final 50% is due when the website is complete and ready to go live. The site is published once final payment is received.
  • Monthly plan. The $30/month Hosting & Maintenance plan begins at launch and renews each month. Either party may cancel with 30 days’ written notice; hosting and maintenance services end at the close of the final paid month.
  • What’s included monthly. Hosting, data storage, security updates, basic maintenance, and minor updates & content tweaks. Larger additions or new features beyond minor tweaks may be quoted separately.
  • Ownership. Upon receipt of full build payment, the Client owns the website content and design delivered for their use. Developer may showcase the completed work in its portfolio.
  • Brand & content. The Client confirms it has the right to use all logos, names, text, and images it supplies for the website.
  • Late payment. If the monthly plan goes unpaid, hosting and maintenance services may be paused until the account is brought current.
