Recruiting Board: 12 Alabama Juniors Trending Up

LS Legacy Sports Staff • Updated Oct 28, 2026 • 6 min read

The Class of 2027 is starting to take shape, and across Alabama the offer lists are moving fast. Junior film is what college coaches lean on hardest, and the players who flashed this fall are watching the letters pile up. We've spent the last month talking to coaches, combing through tape, and tracking the boards — here's our updated list of twelve juniors trending up right now.

A quick note on how we build this: we care about who's actually getting offers and who's improving on film week to week, not just who looked good as a sophomore. These are the names whose recruitments are accelerating heading into the spring evaluation period.

The Headliner

You can't talk about the 2027 class in this state without starting in Saraland. Isaiah Robinson isn't trending up so much as he's already arrived — but his recruitment somehow keeps climbing anyway, and he sits at the top of our board for a reason.

"Robinson is the rare junior who changes how the other team has to game-plan. You don't just block him — you scheme around him. That's a different tier of prospect."

The Board

  • Isaiah Robinson · EDGE · Saraland — The crown jewel of the class. Double-digit Power Four offers and counting, with elite first-step explosiveness off the edge. Already drawing comparisons that make coaches nervous to say out loud.
  • Jaylen Carter · ATH · Auburn — A true two-way weapon and two-sport star. Offers are coming in at receiver, corner, and safety; whoever lands him gets a playmaker.
  • Marcus Whitfield · QB · Hoover — Poised, accurate, and a four-year varsity starter. His recruitment took off after a 300-yard performance against Thompson on film everyone has now seen.
  • DeShawn Hill · CB · Central–Phenix City — A lockdown corner with the hips and ball skills to play early. Picked up three SEC offers in a single September week.
  • Cam Dorsey · OT · Mountain Brook — 6-foot-6 with light feet and a frame built to add forty pounds. The film jump from sophomore to junior year is dramatic.
  • Tre Langford · WR · Vestavia Hills — Smooth route-runner with reliable hands and real top-end speed. Climbing fast after a breakout region slate.
  • Cole Banner · LB · Gardendale — A downhill thumper who diagnoses plays a half-second early. Coaches love his motor and his leadership tape.
  • Dré Calloway · RB · Saraland — Robinson's running mate gives the Spartans a second Power Four-caliber prospect. Contact balance and vision well beyond his years.
  • Eli Pruett · S · Pinson Valley — A rangy center-fielder type at safety who's a tone-setter in the box. Offer list quietly growing each week.
  • Will Hartsell · TE · Thompson — A matchup nightmare at 6-foot-5 who can flex out or put a hand in the dirt. The new-age tight end colleges covet.
  • Jordan Reese · IOL · Ramsay — A mauler on the interior with a nasty finishing streak. Trending up after camp testing turned heads this summer.
  • Antoine Davis · DL · Pinson Valley — Interior disruptor with a quick first step and heavy hands. The kind of three-technique that ages into a star.

On The Bubble

A handful of names just missed the cut and could force their way on by spring: a fast-rising Hoover edge rusher, a Mountain Brook point guard whose football offers are surprising even his own coaches, and a Gardendale receiver who's still growing into his frame. Recruiting boards are living documents — and this one will look different in three months.
