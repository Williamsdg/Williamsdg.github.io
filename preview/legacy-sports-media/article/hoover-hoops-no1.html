Hoover Hoops Opens Season Ranked No. 1 in 7A

LS Legacy Sports Staff • Updated Nov 2, 2026 • 4 min read

Tip-off is still days away, but the verdict is already in: Hoover is the No. 1 team in Class 7A, and it isn't particularly close. The Bucs claimed the top spot in our preseason poll by a near-unanimous margin, and after spending last March a single possession away from a state title, nobody on press row is arguing.

What separates this Hoover team from the rest of the field is simple — almost everyone is back. The Bucs return four of five starters and nine of their top eleven from a roster that reached the 7A Final Four, lost a heartbreaker to Vestavia Hills, and walked off the floor at the BJCC with a chip on its shoulder that hasn't gone anywhere since.

Built To Finish The Job

The engine is senior point guard Marcus Whitfield, a four-year starter who has quietly become one of the most complete guards in the state. He doesn't need 25 a night to dominate a game — last spring he was just as likely to finish with a 12-point, 11-assist line and a stat sheet full of deflections. Around him, Hoover has length, shooting, and the rarest commodity in high school basketball: experience that has been to the brink and remembers exactly how it felt.

Inside, 6-foot-9 junior Cam Dorsey has added real weight and a face-up jumper over the summer, and college coaches have noticed — his offer list grew steadily through the AAU circuit. Pair him with senior wing Tre Langford, a knockdown shooter who hit better than 40 percent from three a year ago, and the Bucs can hurt you at all three levels.

"We've got guys who have already played in the biggest game of their lives and come up short. That doesn't break you. It sharpens you."

The schedule will not let them coast. Hoover opens against a loaded Mountain Brook team in week one, travels to face a Central–Phenix City squad that returns its own star backcourt, and headlines a holiday tournament field that includes Auburn and a dangerous Gardendale group. By the time region play arrives, this team will have been tested in a way few preseason No. 1s ever are.

Title Or Bust

Let's be honest about the expectations in this gym: anything short of a banner is a disappointment. That's not pressure the program shies away from — it's the standard Hoover has set for itself. The talent is here, the depth is here, and most importantly, the hunger is here. The only thing missing is the one thing they couldn't quite grab last March.
