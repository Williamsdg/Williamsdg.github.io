The New Top 25 Alabama Football Rankings Are Here

LS Legacy Sports Staff • Updated Nov 3, 2026 • 6 min read

The regular season is in the books, the brackets are about to be set, and the picture at the top of Alabama high school football has never been more crowded. Thompson remains the standard — but for the first time all year, the Warriors can hear footsteps.

Our weekly Top 25 is built the way we believe rankings should be: by who you beat, where you beat them, and how you're playing right now — not by reputation or recruiting stars. Here's where things stand heading into playoff seeding week.

The Top 5

  • 1. Thompson (9–0) — Still undefeated, still the team to beat. A defense allowing under 11 points a game travels in November.
  • 2. Auburn (8–1) — The lone loss came by three on the road. Jaylen Carter has been the most explosive athlete in the state down the stretch.
  • 3. Hoover (8–1) — Drops one spot after a tight win over Thompson's region rival, but the Bucs are built for a deep run.
  • 4. Central–Phenix City (7–2) — Both losses to ranked teams. Nobody wants to draw the Red Devils in Round 2.
  • 5. Vestavia Hills (7–2) — Up two spots after a statement region win. The hottest team in 7A right now.
"Thompson is still the king. But Saraland is the team nobody in 6A wants to see in December."

The biggest mover this week is Saraland, who jumped into the top 10 overall after running its record to 9–0. The Spartans are averaging better than 40 points a game and have quietly assembled one of the most college-ready rosters in the state — led by sophomore edge rusher Isaiah Robinson, already holding double-digit Power Four offers.

On The Rise

Gardendale (9–0) continues to roll through 5A and has earned its place just outside our overall top 10. Pinson Valley and Mountain Brook remain neck-and-neck in 6A, and Ramsay's three-spot jump was the largest of the week after knocking off a previously unbeaten opponent.

What's Next

Seeding week is everything. A handful of these matchups will be decided by a single possession — and by the time the brackets are released Friday night, at least two of these top 10 teams could be on a collision course as early as Round 2.
