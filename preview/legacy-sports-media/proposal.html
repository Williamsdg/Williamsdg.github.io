04 · Voice & Tone

Do

  • Sound like a confident, in-the-know local broadcaster.
  • Honor the history — “Preserving History · Covering The Future.”
  • Rank by what happened on the field, not reputation.
  • Celebrate the athletes, schools, and communities.

Don't

  • Don't use emoji as the logo or in headlines.
  • Don't stretch, recolor, or crowd the torch mark.
  • Don't place gold text on light backgrounds.
  • Don't sound like a generic national outlet.
