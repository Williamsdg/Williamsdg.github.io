4.Athlete Information & Minors

The athlete application form collects personal information and, where the athlete is under 18, parent or guardian contact details. Southern Roots confirms that it has, or will obtain, the consents required to publish athlete names, images, statistics and social accounts on the website, including for any athlete who is a minor.

Williams Digital will not originate athlete statistics, honors, audience figures, schools, positions or social handles. All such material is drawn from Southern Roots' existing website, Southern Roots' published signing graphics, or materials Southern Roots supplies, and must be confirmed in writing by Southern Roots before launch.

Williams Digital will store submissions securely and will not sell, publish or share athlete information with any third party, and will not use athlete information, athlete photography or any Southern Roots materials to train artificial-intelligence models.

5.Custom Employee Email Addresses

Southern Roots already holds a Google Workspace subscription. Williams Digital will configure it so that every team member has a working custom @soroots.biz address — for example don@soroots.biz and info@soroots.biz — including domain verification, DNS and MX record configuration, mailbox creation for each person Southern Roots identifies, and help getting each person signed in and sending. Williams Digital charges nothing additional for this work.

The Google Workspace subscription itself, including its per-user fees and any additional mailboxes Southern Roots adds, is maintained and paid directly by Southern Roots with Google. It is not included in the $30 per month website fee in Section 7.

  • Third-party costs — premium fonts, licensed stock imagery, paid plugins or third-party services — are billed at cost and only with prior written approval.

12.Ownership & Rights

On receipt of final payment, Southern Roots Sports Management Group owns all rights to the website design, content and code developed under this agreement.

Southern Roots confirms it holds the rights to all photography, logos, copy and other materials it supplies. Williams Digital retains the right to showcase the project in its portfolio and marketing materials; Southern Roots may withdraw that permission in writing at any time.

Southern Roots' materials will not be used to train artificial-intelligence models, and no AI-generated text will be published on the site without Southern Roots' review and approval.

13.Confidentiality

Williams Digital may encounter non-public information about Southern Roots, its staff, its athletes and its athletes' families in the course of this work, and will keep that information confidential. Williams Digital will not request, and Southern Roots should not provide, executed representation agreements, athlete financial records or any other confidential client file material beyond what is needed to build and operate the website.

Nothing in this agreement creates an agency, representation or attorney-client relationship between Williams Digital and Southern Roots or any athlete.

14.Cancellation

If Southern Roots cancels after the deposit has been paid, the $600 deposit is non-refundable and covers work performed to that point. If Williams Digital cancels, any unused portion of the deposit is refunded and all work completed to date is handed over. Late payments beyond 14 days of the due date may result in the project being suspended until the balance is resolved.
