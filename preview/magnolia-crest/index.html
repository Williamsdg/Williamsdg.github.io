Since 1924

Some clubs are built around a golf course. Magnolia Crest was built around *an* *afternoon:* the walk up eighteen, the table that is somehow always yours, the children who learned to swim here teaching their own. We have spent a century *keeping* *it* *that* *way.*

A day at the Crest

From first light to last call

6:40 am
6:40am

Dawn patrol

The first group goes off in the mist. The greens crew waves from the fourth. Coffee is already on at the Halfway House.

9:00am

Clay before the heat

Ladies clinic on courts one through four, juniors on the hard courts, and a pickleball ladder that takes itself very seriously.

12:30pm

Lunch on the Veranda

Rocking chairs, ceiling fans, a tomato sandwich that people drive across the county for. Nobody is in a hurry.

3:00pm

Cannonballs

The family pool at full volume, lap swimmers in the quiet lanes, and frozen lemonade delivered to your chair.

6:15pm

The golden nine

Nine holes on the Crest Course with long shadows and no one behind you. The best light of the day lands on the sixteenth.

9:00pm

Fire pits and a nightcap

The windows of the 1924 Room glow, someone finds the piano, and the fireflies come up off the eighteenth fairway.

A 25-metre lap pool, a family pool with a slide nobody admits to using, and a swim team that has not lost at home since 2019.

    Heritage

    A century,one porch

    Drag or swipe through the years

    1924

    Nine holes and a farmhouse

    Eleven families buy a cotton farm on the ridge and lay out nine holes with a mule and a borrowed plan.

    1931

    The clubhouse opens

    Built from heart pine cut on the property. The veranda faces west on purpose.

    1948

    The Crest Course

    A second eighteen climbs the ridge. Members still argue about the walk up fourteen.

    1967

    Clay arrives

    Four Har-Tru courts replace the old croquet lawn. The croquet set is still in the attic.

    1989

    The pools

    A lap pool and a family pool open on Memorial Day. The swim team is founded that afternoon.

    2011

    The restoration

    The Magnolia Course is restored to its original lines, bunker by bunker, from the 1931 aerials.

    2026
