02

Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to design, develop, and deploy a custom community website for the Association. The project includes the following deliverables:

  • Homepage — A mobile-friendly landing page featuring community announcements, quick-access tiles, and a seasonal pool-reservation toggle.
  • Five Core Pages — Home, Community Calendar, Newsletters, Documents, and Resources, custom-branded in the Association's forest-green, gold, and cream palette.
  • Interactive Community Calendar — A monthly events calendar for board meetings, community gatherings, and deadlines.
  • Newsletter Publishing — A simple workflow allowing board members to add a title and month and publish a newsletter without coding.
  • Documents Hub — A page for HOA bylaws, covenants, meeting minutes, and other official documents.
  • Community Resources Page — A single-page hub for trash schedules, utility contacts, board contacts, and emergency numbers.
  • HOA Dues & ARC Links — Quick-access links to the Association's external dues-payment provider and architectural-review (ARC) submission portal.
  • Pool Reservation Link — A seasonal reservation link surfaced on the homepage and navigation.
  • Admin Content Management System (CMS) — Configuration of a GitHub-based, no-code editor (Decap CMS) enabling board members to update content directly.
  • Domain & Hosting Setup — Assistance registering a domain in the Association's name, and configuration of free hosting via GitHub Pages with automatic HTTPS.
  • Content Migration — Transfer of existing newsletters, documents, and contact information provided by the Board into the new site.

Training & Documentation

The Service Provider will conduct one (1) live training session (recorded for future reference) and provide a written cheat-sheet showing board members how to publish newsletters, add calendar events, and update content via the admin panel.

Revision Policy

The project includes up to three (3) rounds of revisions during the design and development phase. A revision round consists of a consolidated set of feedback submitted at one time by the Board's designated point of contact. Additional revision rounds beyond the included three will be billed at the Service Provider's hourly rate of $50/hour. Revisions must be requested within seven (7) days of receiving a design proof or deliverable; otherwise, the deliverable will be considered approved.

Change Orders & Scope Changes

Any work requested by the Board that falls outside the scope described above — including additional pages, custom integrations, or new features — will require a written Change Order. The Service Provider will provide a quote for the additional work, and no work will commence until the Board approves the Change Order in writing. Change Orders may affect the project timeline and will be billed separately from the build fee.

03

Fees and Payment

In consideration of the services provided, the Association agrees to compensate the Service Provider as follows:

Build Fee — Total $1,200.00 USD

A one-time flat build fee of $1,200.00 USD covers the complete website design, development, content migration, CMS configuration, domain and hosting setup, training, and launch. This fee is split into two installments:

  • Deposit — $600.00 USD due upon acceptance of this Agreement. Work begins after the deposit is received.
  • Final Payment — $600.00 USD due upon launch of the live website to the Association's domain.

No Monthly Retainer

This is a one-time build with no monthly hosting or maintenance retainer. Hosting is provided free of charge through GitHub Pages, and ongoing content updates are made by the Board through the admin panel at no additional cost. The Association is responsible only for its annual domain registration fee (approximately $15.00 per year) paid directly to its domain registrar.

Optional Future Support

Should the Board later request additional development, new features, design changes, or hands-on support beyond the 30-day post-launch support window (Section 5), such work may be quoted as a separate Change Order or billed at the Service Provider's hourly rate of $50/hour. There is no obligation to purchase ongoing support.

Payment Methods

Payment shall be submitted via the secure payment link(s) provided by the Service Provider, or by another method mutually agreed upon by the parties. Payment details and instructions will be included on the invoice.

Payment Terms

The deposit is due in full upon acceptance of this Agreement. The final build payment is due upon launch of the live website. A late fee of 1.5% per month may be assessed on any balance remaining unpaid beyond thirty (30) days from the invoice date. The Service Provider reserves the right to delay or suspend services for any account with an outstanding balance exceeding thirty (30) days.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Should the project be cancelled by the Board after work has commenced, the $600.00 deposit is non-refundable as it covers discovery, planning, and development work already performed. If the project is cancelled prior to launch, no final build payment shall be owed. If the Service Provider cancels before any work has been performed, the full deposit will be refunded.

Ownership & No Vendor Lock-In

Upon receipt of full payment of the build fee, the Association shall own all custom-designed deliverables, including the website files, source code, content, and the GitHub repository. The domain is registered in the Association's name, and the Board retains full administrative control of its hosting account and repository. There is no vendor lock-in: the Board may continue to operate, edit, or transfer the website independently at any time. The Service Provider retains the right to use general techniques, tools, and methodologies developed during the engagement. Third-party assets (fonts, stock images, open-source software) remain subject to their respective license agreements.

Portfolio & Credit Rights

The Service Provider retains the right to display and reference the completed website in their professional portfolio, marketing materials, case studies, and social media. A small, unobtrusive credit line (e.g., "Built by Williams Digital") may be placed in the website footer. If the Board wishes to opt out of the credit line, this must be agreed upon in writing prior to launch.

Project Timeline

The Service Provider will aim to complete the project within approximately four (4) weeks from receipt of the signed Agreement and deposit, subject to timely delivery of content and feedback from the Board:

  • Week 1 — Design and mockups for Board review.
  • Week 2 — Build-out of all five core pages.
  • Week 3 — Content loading and CMS configuration.
  • Week 4 — Launch, training session, and the start of the post-launch support window.

This timeline is a good-faith estimate. Delays in providing content, feedback, or approvals may extend the schedule accordingly, and the Service Provider shall not be held liable for such delays.

05

Board Responsibilities & Support

To facilitate the effective delivery of services, the Association, acting through its Board, agrees to:

  • Provide timely, accurate, and complete content, documents, newsletters, contact information, and branding materials as reasonably requested.
  • Designate a primary point of contact on the Board for all matters relating to this engagement.
  • Respond to communications, design proofs, and requests for approval within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Ensure that all content, documents, images, and materials provided are owned by the Association or properly licensed for use.
  • Maintain the Association's own accounts for its dues-payment provider, ARC portal, and domain registrar.
  • Designate which board member(s) will hold administrative access to the website and GitHub repository following launch.

Delays or failure to meet these responsibilities may impact the timeline and effectiveness of the services provided.

Post-Launch Support

The Service Provider will provide thirty (30) days of complimentary post-launch support beginning on the date the website goes live. This support covers bug fixes, configuration questions, and guidance on using the admin panel. Support beyond this window is optional and may be quoted separately as described in Section 3.

Project Pause & Board Inactivity

If the Board becomes unresponsive or fails to provide requested materials, feedback, or approvals for a period of fourteen (14) consecutive days, the project may be placed on hold. Projects paused for more than sixty (60) days may be treated as cancelled under the Cancellation & Refund Policy outlined in Section 3.

Launch Sign-Off

Prior to the public launch of the website, the Service Provider will present the completed site to the Board for final review and approval. The Board shall have five (5) business days to review and provide written approval or submit final revision requests. Upon Board approval, the Service Provider will proceed with launch and the final build payment of $600.00 will become due. If the Board does not respond within this period, the website will be deemed approved.

Term and Termination

Termination by Either Party

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing fourteen (14) days written notice to the other party. Upon termination, the Service Provider will provide the Association with all completed deliverables and the source code created to date, and assist with a reasonable transition.

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision and fails to cure such breach within five (5) days of receiving written notice.

Effect of Termination

Upon termination, the Association shall pay the Service Provider for all services rendered up to the effective date of termination. The Service Provider will deliver all completed work product and provide reasonable assistance for transition.
