    FAQ

    Good to know.

    What is VIP Day?
    VIP Day is Wednesday, November 4, 9:00 am–4:00 pm, before the show opens to the public. You get first access to new products and the best selection, plus a tote bag. Only VIP tickets are accepted that day.
    Can I use my VIP ticket on another day?
    Yes. If you miss VIP Day, the ticket works for one other day during general admission hours. You won't get the tote bag on another day.
    Do children need tickets?
    Kids under 6 are free every day. Kids 6–12 need a ticket. On VIP Day, everyone 6 and up needs a VIP ticket.
    I can't find the email with my tickets.
    You can have them sent again at tix123.com/tix123/resend.cfm.
    Will Santa be there for pictures?
    Yes. Santa takes photos during show hours on the top floor.
    Where do I park?
    The BJCC has several lots, and self parking runs about $20 a day. Check bjcc.org for current lots and prices.
