Ten ways
to be AUXO.

A logo for a football show has one job before any other: hold up at 24 pixels in a YouTube avatar, on a navy jersey, and stamped in one colour on a hat. Every direction below is drawn to survive all three. Pick a lane and it gets built out properly.

The options

Ten directions

Each shown as a full lockup on dark and on light — the two grounds AUXO actually lives on. Judge them small; that is where a mark either works or doesn't.

Rising Chevron

01

The mark already on the live site. Auxo is Greek for growth, so the chevron rises — and reads equally as goalposts. Simplest silhouette here, which is why it survives smallest.

Goalposts

02

The most literal football cue on the list, and the most instantly Auburn-adjacent. Uprights in orange, post in the base colour. Risk: every football brand has considered it.

Slab Block

03

Wordmark-led. The letters are knocked out of a solid block, so the logo is a shape before it is a word — the way a network bug behaves in the corner of a broadcast.

Hash Marks

04

Yard-line hashes stepping up to a centre peak. Abstract enough to avoid cliché, football enough to be unmistakable on a field graphic.

Collegiate Roundel

05

The traditional route: a circular badge that behaves like a team crest. Best of the ten on merchandise; the busiest at avatar size.

AU Ligature

06

Leans hardest on Auburn. A and U share a stroke. Strong equity — but the closest to Auburn University's own marks, so it carries the most trademark caution.

Crest

07

A shield with a chevron inside it. Says institution rather than show — the right call if AUXO wants to look like a network from day one.

Broadcast Bars

08

A waveform with the centre bar raised. The only direction that says podcast before it says football — useful if the show grows past Auburn.

Pennant

09

A gameday pennant on a staff. The warmest and most nostalgic of the ten; also the most decorative, so it needs the wordmark beside it to work.

Stripe Cut

10

A block with stripes driven through it at speed. Reads as motion and, at a glance, as a tiger stripe — without ever drawing an animal.

Colour

The palette underneath

Deliberately darker and grittier than Auburn University's official marketing palette. AUXO should read as media about Auburn, not as an official Auburn property — that distinction matters legally as well as visually.

Navy#0C2340
Orange#E87722
Cream#F4F0E6

Orange is an action colour, never decoration. If something is orange on the site, you can press it or it is live. That single rule is what stops an orange-and-navy brand turning into a traffic cone.

If you pick one

What gets delivered

Choosing a direction is the start, not the end. The chosen mark gets drawn properly and delivered as a real kit.

Every file you need

SVG, PNG at three sizes, and PDF. Full colour, all-white, all-black and a single-colour version for embroidery and one-colour print.

Locked lockups

Horizontal, stacked, and an avatar-only mark cropped for YouTube, X and Instagram — so the show never gets squeezed into a circle badly.

Clear space and minimums

A written minimum size and clear-space rule, so the mark does not get crushed into a lower third or a sponsor bar six months from now.

One honest caution

Direction 06 (AU Ligature) leans closest to Auburn University's own trademarks. It is the strongest equity play and the highest risk — an independent show using an AU monogram invites a letter. If that direction is the favourite, it is worth a trademark check before anything is printed. Every other direction on this page avoids the issue.
