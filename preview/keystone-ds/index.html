The Studio

A small Birmingham firm with the full BIM toolkit.

Keystone Design Services was built around a simple idea: every project deserves clean documents and a model you can trust. We sit between architects, engineers, owners, and contractors — producing the drawings, 3D coordination, and visuals that turn an idea into something a crew can actually build.

Our work spans churches, restaurants, automotive facilities, and community spaces — from St. Mary's to multi-site Chick-fil-A rollouts to a Mercedes-Benz addition. We're at our best when a project needs both technical precision and someone who picks up the phone.

How We Work

A predictable rhythm, from kickoff to keys.

Every Keystone engagement runs on the same four-beat cadence. You always know what we're producing, when it lands, and what we need from you next.

01

Discover

Walk-through, scan, and program review. We define the deliverables, software stack, and LOD up front — before anyone draws a line.

02

Model

BIM build in shared environment. Weekly internal QA + bi-weekly client-facing model sync so nothing surprises you at 60%.

03

Coordinate

Clash detection rounds with structural, MEP, and trades. Every clash gets an owner, a fix, and a sign-off date.

04

Deliver

Stamped CDs, renderings, drone reels, and a clean federated model. CA support stays available through punch-list.
