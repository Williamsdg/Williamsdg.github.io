How pumpkins work

Pumpkins aren't part of admission. Prices follow the market, so they can change through the season.

Pick one in the patch

Ride the wagon out and choose your pumpkin.

Buy a pumpkin ticket

At the gate with cash or card when you arrive, or on your way out to the car (cash only).

Show it as you leave

Your pumpkin ticket is your proof of purchase on the way out.

Your class's station rotation

Pumpkins

Have students decorate a canvas tote before the trip, or label plastic bags with their names. Staff carry the pumpkins up front after the wagon ride, so nobody has to carry one all day.

Lunch

Every school gets reserved tables at the picnic pavilion. Drop coolers and lunch boxes at the front entrance and staff bring them to your tables. Please leave the area as you found it.

Students

This is a working farm. Kids learn best through play, and staff need your help keeping everyone safe. Have your camera ready.

Our story

Three generations on Griffin Road.

It started as a dairy with 40 head of cattle. Today it's a family farm built by hand for families to enjoy.

  1. 1961

    The Lazy "G"

    Pat and Carolyn Griffin start a dairy with 40 head of cattle and raise Pam, Barry and Jeff on the farm.

  2. 1986

    B & L Dairy

    Barry buys the farm from his parents. At its peak, B & L Dairy milks 300 head of cattle.

  3. 1997

    A hard call

    Like most dairies, B & L has to close, and the land sits waiting.

  4. 2013

    Griffin Farms

    Barry and his son Brandon reinvent the farm and build every attraction themselves. It keeps growing every year.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

When are you open?

Every weekend in October only. Saturdays 10am–6pm and Sundays 12pm–6pm. Home School Day is Monday, October 12, 9am–1pm. Weekday field trips are by reservation.

How much is admission?

$10 for everyone 2 and older. It includes every Griffin Farms attraction. Pumpkins, batting cages, animal food, vendors and outside vendors like pony rides and the mechanical bull cost extra.

Is a pumpkin included?

Not with general admission. Pumpkin prices follow the market. Buy a pumpkin ticket at the gate with cash or card, or on your way to the car with cash only. Field trip admission does include one pumpkin per student.

Can I bring my dog?

No. Griffin Farms isn't pet friendly. Service animals under Title III of the ADA are always welcome.

Is there food?

Yes. Food and craft vendors are here every open weekend in October. There are no vendors on weekday field trip days.

Can we have a birthday party here in October?

Private party packages aren't available in September or October. You can still celebrate at the patch, but tables are first come, first served and general admission applies.
