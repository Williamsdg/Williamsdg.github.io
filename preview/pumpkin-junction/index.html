Your day, stop by stop

How Pumpkin Junction works.

Plan on about two hours on the museum grounds: an hour to settle in, then 75 minutes on the train and at the Junction.

60 min before

Arrive in Calera

I-65 and US-31 traffic is tough, so come early to park, pick up tickets at the Depot and use the Gift Shop restrooms.

While you wait

Explore the grounds

Shop the Gift Shop, tour the signal garden and the Depot Museum, and walk the cars in Clark Yard.

15 min before

Pick your car

Seats are unassigned. Choose an open-air car, a climate-controlled historic coach, or one that's over 100 years old.

About 45 min

The Junction

  • Pick your pumpkin
  • Hayride, inflatables, games & crafts
  • Food vendors (cash only)
  • Adults-only Ozan wine tasting, when available
The whistle blows

Ride back

Gather your pumpkins and climb aboard for the trip back to the Depot. Total trip: about 75 minutes.

Know before you go

Good to know.

Ticket office

Questions? We'll help.

Main office: Tuesday–Friday, 9–2. Saturdays, 9–3:30.

(205) 668-3435 Call tickets@hodrrm.org Email
Can I buy tickets at the Depot?

Yes, if seats remain. Walk-up tickets are sold at the Calera Depot before boarding on event days — buying online in advance is the surest way to get your train.

Are there restrooms on the train?

No. Our vintage railcars don't have working restrooms, so use the restrooms at the Gift Shop before you board.

Can we bring food or drinks?

Please don't bring food or drinks on the train, and finish refreshments before boarding. Snacks and drinks are sold at the Gift Shop and at the Junction; Junction vendors are cash only.

Where do we sit?

Cars and seats are unassigned. You'll choose your car and seat during boarding: open-air cars, climate-controlled historic cars, and a coach that's over 100 years old.

What if we can't make it?

Train tickets are non-refundable, but our ticket team is happy to apply your unused fare to a different date and time in the same operating season. See the museum's weather policy for rain days.

Is it accessible?

Wheelchair spaces are available in select coaches. Call (205) 668-3435 so we can plan the right car for your party.

Can adults join the wine tasting?

An adults-only wine tasting may be available at the Junction, courtesy of Ozan Winery & Distillery. Bring ID.
