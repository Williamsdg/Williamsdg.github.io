Module 10 · Executive

Executive Analytics.

The board-level dashboard — officiating performance, broadcast ratings, attendance, revenue, operating cost. Pulls from every other module to give the Commissioner and executive committee one accountable view of conference performance.

01 · WHAT IT DOES

One screen for board questions.

When the Commissioner walks into a board meeting and gets asked "how are we doing this season?", the answer should be ready. Today, it requires a week of slide-prep to pull together. This module makes it always-available.

Four big KPI tiles anchor the page: Total Attendance, Avg Football Rating, Media Revenue YTD, and Officiating Accuracy. Below sit two big charts: Attendance by Week (stacked by school) and Officiating Accuracy YTD Trend. The bottom row shows Top Crews leaderboard, Revenue Mix donut, and Operations Health gauges. Drill into any tile for the full breakdown; export to PDF for the board pack.

02 · WHO USES IT

The leadership cohort.

  • Commissioner — primary viewer; uses for board prep + media.
  • Deputy Commissioner + executive VPs — sport-level rollups + decisions.
  • SEC executive committee — quarterly review + annual budget cycle.
  • Member-school presidents / chancellors (read-only digest) — quarterly conference report.

04 · MONITORING APPROACH

The Monday morning + monthly cadence.

Monday morning

Commissioner + deputy review the weekend's tiles: attendance, ratings, officiating accuracy. Any movement > 5% in either direction triggers a question to the relevant VP.

Monthly executive meeting

VPs walk through their sport's KPI movement vs target. Module 10 produces the pre-read PDF; the meeting is about explanations and decisions, not data assembly.

Quarterly board cycle

Full quarterly export. Filters: season-to-date, prior season comparison, sport-specific cuts. Outputs PDF with charts + commentary fields editable by Comms team.
