Officials & Replay.

01 · WHAT IT DOES

One place to run the officiating program.

Today, officiating assignments live in spreadsheets, replay decisions live in audio recordings, and crew grades live in a Coordinator's head. This module consolidates the entire program into a single system of record.

The module is anchored by the crew board — every active crew, their current and upcoming assignments, travel status, and live YTD grade. Drill into a crew to see the full profile: tenure, season grade with weekly sparkline, spot accuracy, replay confirm rate, and outstanding school complaints. Below the board sit the replay timeline (every review across the conference today, with stage-by-stage progress), the travel desk (charter status, hotel blocks, weather-extended nights), and the memo distribution log (every rule update with per-official acknowledgment rates).

The replay surface is the operational nerve center on game day. It shows every review in flight with its stage (Trigger → Review → Confer → Rule → Communicate), elapsed clock, replay official assigned, and the recommended call. Going over the 90-second threshold automatically notifies the on-field referee per the new comms protocol.

02 · WHO USES IT

Owned by the Coordinator of Officiating.

Day-to-day owners

  • VP of Officiating (currently Steve Shaw) — sees everything; sets policy, grades crews, distributes memos.
  • Per-sport Coordinators of Officiating — Football, MBB, WBB, Baseball, Softball, etc. — own their sport's crew assignments, grading, and replay coordination.
  • Replay center leads at Birmingham, Atlanta, Nashville, Dallas — staff the replay command surface, log every ruling.

Constituent users

  • Active officials (102 in football, ~340 across all sports) — see their assignments, travel, and grades. Mobile-first.
  • Crew chiefs (Referees) — see the full crew, coordinate travel and pre-game logistics.
  • School ADs (read-only) — see which crew is assigned to their next game; can submit film/complaint requests against a crew via Module 03.

04 · MONITORING APPROACH

Weekly cycle + Saturday command.

Weekly cycle (Sun → Sat)

  1. Sunday: Coordinator publishes next week's assignments. Officials get push notification on the mobile app.
  2. Monday–Wednesday: Confirmation deadlines roll through. Travel ops books charters and confirms hotels. Memos drafted from prior weekend's notable rulings.
  3. Thursday: Memo distribution + ack tracking. School-side liaison posts in Comms Hub.
  4. Friday: Crews travel. Hotels confirmed via app check-in.
  5. Saturday: Game day. Coordinator + on-duty grading staff watch the replay timeline + live grades. Every ruling logged.

What to watch on game day

  • Replay rulings going over 90s — auto-notifies crew, but the Coordinator confirms communication happened.
  • Crew-Replay disagreement rate — overturns above 25% in a single game triggers a postgame review.
  • Live grade dropping below 88 — Coordinator opens a side note for the postgame conversation.
  • Travel disruptions — weather-extended nights, missed charters, alternate-crew activations.
