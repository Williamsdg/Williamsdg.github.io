Module 03 · Core

Film & Complaint Portal.

A two-sided workspace: schools upload tagged clips and request rule interpretations; the SEC office triages, reviews, rules, and routes outcomes back. The infrastructure that turns school frustration into structured feedback that improves officiating.

01 · WHAT IT DOES

Replaces the email chain.

Today, when a school wants to ask the SEC office about a call, an AD emails the Coordinator of Officiating with a clip. The Coordinator emails the crew. There's no audit trail, no SLA, no shared queue, and no way to turn the answer into training material for the next time the same situation comes up.

The portal has two views. School view: AD or compliance lead uploads a clip (or pastes a film-system link), tags the play with down/distance/time, selects a call type from the rule book, writes a short request, and submits. The clock starts on a 72-hour SLA.

SEC office view: a triaged queue of every active submission, with priority (A/B), assigned reviewer, status (NEW / IN REVIEW / REPLIED / ARCHIVED), and a thread per submission for the back-and-forth. Clips that produce noteworthy rulings can be one-click promoted to the Training Library for use in future officials education.

02 · WHO USES IT

Schools submit · SEC Coordinator reviews.

Submitters

  • School ADs and Directors of Sport Operations — submit on behalf of their program.
  • School Compliance Leads — submit interpretive requests not tied to a specific play.
  • Head Coaches (rare) — can be granted submission rights with AD approval.

Reviewers (SEC side)

  • Coordinator of Officiating (per sport) — final reviewer on every clip.
  • Replay Center leads — provide mic audio + ruling context when a play involved a review.
  • VP of Officiating — escalation reviewer for L-II+ severity.

04 · MONITORING APPROACH

The Coordinator's Monday morning.

The dominant monitoring cadence is weekly. After each weekend, the Sport Coordinator of Officiating opens the portal first thing Monday and works the queue:

  1. Triage — every new submission gets a priority (A = ruling-changing play; B = interpretive). A's get same-day reviewers assigned.
  2. Pull context — for each clip, the reviewer pulls the mic audio and replay context from the Officials module.
  3. Rule — draft a short written memo back to the school. If the play is rule-noteworthy, promote the clip to the Training Library.
  4. Close the loop — submission status flips to REPLIED. SLA clock stops. School AD gets a notification.

Real-time signals to watch

  • A new submission from a school whose program is upset — usually visible from the Social Listening module first; the Coordinator can preempt the email by reviewing fast.
  • A complaint pattern against a single crew — three complaints against one crew in two weeks triggers a manual review by VP Officiating.
  • SLA approaching — at 48 hours, automated reminder to reviewer. At 60 hours, escalation to VP.
