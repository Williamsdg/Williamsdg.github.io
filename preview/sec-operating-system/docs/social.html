Module 09 · Operations Suite

Social Listening.

Real-time monitoring of public mentions of SEC officials, crews, replay centers, and conference policy decisions across X / Twitter, Reddit, TikTok, and sports media. Auto-routes mention spikes to the incident desk and auto-drafts comms briefs.

01 · WHAT IT DOES

The early-warning system for officiating controversy.

Officiating decisions go viral in minutes. By the time the AD calls, the segment has already aired on Finebaum and the Reddit megathread has 1,800 comments. The SEC office historically learns about controversy from the third call. This module flips that.

The system continuously ingests public mentions of every monitored entity (102 football officials by name, 14 crew aliases, 6 replay centers, 21 sport coordinators, the conference + commissioner). It scores sentiment, detects mention-volume spikes vs. each entity's baseline, clusters topics, and triggers alerts when thresholds break. Sentiment ≤ −0.65 + mention volume ≥ 10× baseline opens a SEV-MON incident automatically. The auto-drafted comms brief is ready for VP Comms review within 5 minutes of the spike.

02 · WHO USES IT

Owned by VP Communications.

  • VP Communications (L. Stern) — primary owner; reviews briefs, decides on response.
  • Conference comms team — work the alert queue, manage the verified-voices watchlist.
  • VP Officiating — sees crew-specific spikes; informs officiating-side response.
  • Head of Incidents — receives SEV-MON incidents from this module.
  • Commissioner's office — daily digest of top mentions + sentiment trends.

04 · MONITORING APPROACH

Always-on with tiered alerting.

Tier 1 · Crisis flag (manual review required)

Sentiment ≤ −0.65 + mention volume ≥ 15× baseline + verified-voice amplification = direct page to VP Comms. Comms brief drafted automatically; VP decides on response within 15 min.

Tier 2 · Spike alert (auto-route to incidents)

Sentiment ≤ −0.65 + mention volume ≥ 10× baseline = SEV-MON incident opened. Comms brief drafted; reviewed in the next hour.

Tier 3 · Daily digest

Everything else rolls up into the daily 5 PM CT digest sent to the Commissioner's office: top 10 trending phrases, top 5 monitored entities, sentiment movement, notable verified-voice mentions.

!

Officials sentiment baseline is negative.

Officials are an unpopular profession year-round — the baseline composite for SEC officiating runs at −0.22. Alert thresholds are calibrated against each entity's own baseline, not a global zero. A football crew at +0.4 sentiment dropping to −0.1 is more notable than the conference umbrella sitting at −0.2.
