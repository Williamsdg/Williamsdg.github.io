Module 12 · Executive

School Portal.

Each of the 16 SEC schools' home base into the conference — requests, sports status, conference comms, compliance, and a quick-submit drawer for every common workflow. The platform's "school-side" front door.

01 · WHAT IT DOES

One door for the school side.

A typical SEC AD office juggles a half-dozen ways to reach the conference — emails, phone calls, the SEC compliance portal, separate sports platforms, the bowl/championship reporting tool. The School Portal collapses that into one role-gated workspace per school.

Each school sees its own dashboard: open requests with SLA timers, all 21 sports with live status, upcoming conference-coordinated events, the comms threads tagged with their school, and the all-important Quick-Submit drawer. The drawer is the most-used interaction — six pre-built forms (credentials, travel, media availability, compliance submission, film/complaint upload, incident report) route automatically to the right SEC team with SLA-tracking.

02 · WHO USES IT

Per-school identity-scoped.

  • Athletic Director — owner per school; can grant access to staff.
  • Directors of Sport Operations (per sport) — primary daily user; submits requests.
  • School compliance lead — handles policy acks, education modules, self-reports.
  • School comms staff — handle media availability requests, asset packages.
  • School executive (President / Chancellor) — read-only digest view of conference activity.

Each user only sees their own school's data, scoped by the identity layer. An Auburn user opening the portal sees Auburn's dashboard; LSU's data is invisible to them.

04 · MONITORING APPROACH

School-side daily. SEC-side per-school slice.

School-side

Sport Ops Director opens the portal as the first morning check. Reviews any in-progress requests, addresses Acks that are due, checks the day's tagged comms threads. Game-day: keeps the portal open as a side-tab to the Ops Center.

SEC-side

The SEC sport coordinators see per-school dashboards from inside their respective modules — same data, different lens. The portal exists primarily to give the school one place to act; the SEC office sees the same activity across all 16 schools via Modules 05–11.

✓

The Quick-Submit drawer is the killer feature.

Before this module, every school workflow required knowing the right SEC contact and email format. The drawer collapses that to one button + a fillable form. Routing happens server-side; SLA timers + audit logs are automatic.
