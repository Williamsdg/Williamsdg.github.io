Module 07 · Operations Suite

Media & Content Hub.

The asset distribution + broadcast coordination layer between the SEC, its 16 schools, and its rights-holder partners (CBS, ABC, ESPN, SECN, SECN+). One place for every package, every press conference, every sponsor deliverable, every talent assignment.

01 · WHAT IT DOES

The shared filing cabinet + control room for content.

Today, when CBS Sports needs the Iron Bowl talent reel, somebody emails it. When a school needs Sankey's pre-game audio, they call the comms office. When a sponsor needs branded b-roll, the request goes through three layers of approvals on Slack.

Media Hub centralizes this. The Distribution view shows every asset package being delivered today, organized by school + partner + asset type (b-roll, highlights, photo, graphic, audio). Broadcast Partners shows every live window and its status. Press Archive is a searchable, transcribed library of every presser ever held. Talent & Sideline tracks live talent assignments. Sponsor Assets handles deliverables for the conference's commercial partners.

02 · WHO USES IT

Owned by the VP of Communications.

  • VP Communications — primary owner, sees everything.
  • Conference comms team — distribute assets, manage talent, maintain press archive.
  • School comms staff (16 schools) — receive school-specific asset packages; submit local content for distribution.
  • Broadcast partners (CBS, ABC/ESPN, SECN, SECN+) — pull assets, post broadcast status updates.
  • Sponsors (Dr Pepper, Coca-Cola, etc.) — limited access to their deliverable workflows.

04 · MONITORING APPROACH

Weekly cycle + game-day live.

  1. Week's launch: Comms publishes the week's content calendar — every press conference, every asset drop, every sponsor deliverable.
  2. Daily: Asset packages distributed; press archive updated; pending media requests resolved.
  3. Game day: Broadcast window status monitored live (especially for partner-side issues like feed loss). Talent on-air status tracked. Post-game press archive populated within 30 min.

What to watch

  • Pending media requests > 4h old — auto-escalated to VP Comms.
  • Sponsor deliverable deadline approaching — auto-reminders 7 / 3 / 1 days out.
  • CDN cost trending above plan — flagged when monthly run-rate > budget.
  • Broadcast partner status anomalies — a window in "WX HOLD" mode triggers a coordination call.
