The case

Conferences operate like a media company, a sports league, and a federal agency — but run on email.

01

Information lives in inboxes

Officials assignments, replay rulings, school complaints, and weather delays all funnel through individual email threads that no one else can see.

02

No shared situational awareness

On a Saturday, no single dashboard shows the status of every SEC game — kickoffs, delays, scoring, injuries, replays — in one place.

03

Schools can't see what's happening

Athletic directors, compliance staff, and operations teams have no consistent portal for film submissions, complaints, or credentialing.

04

Replay is fast but invisible

Replay decisions happen in 90 seconds but get explained to fans, media, and schools across the next 72 hours — usually badly.

"

If you'd asked me on a Saturday at 3pm to tell you the status of every conference game — kickoffs, replays, weather, who's officiating — I couldn't have given you a single screen. It didn't exist.

Former Power-Conference Operations LeadComposite quote from public AD interviews
