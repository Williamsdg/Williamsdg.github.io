Grading Methodology

composite formula

Each crew grade is a weighted composite scored by the SEC Coordinator of Officiating + AI overlay across:

  • · Spot accuracy (35%) — frame-by-frame ball spot vs play-end
  • · Penalty correctness (25%) — called/declined accuracy
  • · Game management (15%) — pace, conferencing, clock
  • · Replay decisions (15%) — confirm/overturn rate calibration
  • · School complaint resolution (10%) — interpretation requests, outcome quality
Postseason eligibility: 90.0+ to be considered for Championship Week assignments.
