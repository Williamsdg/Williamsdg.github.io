The homepage — your terrace at dusk, and tonight's Oia sunset time, live.

Everything on it is yours. Your photography, your real lunch, dinner, vegan and dessert menus at your real prices, your Agape / Blue Dome / Privé packages, your press quotes, your arrival instructions, your hours. I invented nothing.

What I changed:

1. Your menus are PDFs today. On the concept they're real text on the page — searchable, filterable by plant-based or seafood, readable on a phone without pinching, and visible to Google. Right now, someone searching for lobster pasta in Oia cannot find yours.

Every menu on one page, with search and filters.

2. You sell a west-facing sunset seven months a year, and your site never says what time it is. The concept shows tonight's sunset over Oia, calculated live, counting down. As I write this it's 19:16.

3. One page. No reloads between the story, the menus, the romantic packages and the booking button — which still goes to your existing i-host reservation system. Nothing about how you take bookings has to change.

4. Your arrival instructions are genuinely useful and currently buried in a wall of text. They're now a four-way card: by car, on foot, by taxi, by cruise ship.

5. It was designed on a phone first — which is where most people booking a Santorini dinner are sitting.

On a phone, with Reserve always one thumb away.
