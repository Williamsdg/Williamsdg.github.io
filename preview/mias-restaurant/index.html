Discover our story · Since 2021

A declaration of unconditional love to the notion of family.

Inspired by the hospitality of her grandmother and the flavours of Greece she discovered as a young girl visiting from the USA, Sellia Georges chose an elegant Cycladic dwelling with a spectacular view, in the heart of Oia, to house the holistic and indulging experience that is MIA’S.

MIA’S started from Sellia’s expertise as a third-generation restaurateur from Chicago. In the beginning the core team was herself and her father, Chris Georges, who embraced and fully supported her vision. Since 2021 the team has grown to include people who give their fullest attention to the creation of that experience, daily.

At MIA’S we create culinary moments that fuse tradition with contemporary concepts and turn into experiences that nourish the soul. All these will make you come back time and again — because when MIA’S experience begins, it never ends.

The kitchen · Executive Chef Elli Mitrai

Fire, the Aegean, and a daily conversation with the island.

The kitchen team works daily with local purveyors and sources only the finest ingredients from the island of Santorini, Crete and the Aegean. The heart of MIA’S cuisine is chef Elli Mitrai, who transforms those ingredients into dishes that bring memories of home. The brigade stands next to her, preparing each dish through the open kitchen with precision and love.

Around you

Oia, the caldera, and what the view is actually looking at.

MIA’S sits on the quiet side of Oia’s caldera, facing west. What you are looking at, left to right, is the crater rim, the volcanic islands of Nea Kameni and Palea Kameni, Thirassia, and — for seven months of the year — the sun going into the Aegean.

Location

On the quiet side of Oia’s caldera.

MIA’S is located 10 metres from the old main road Firon–Ias, so it is easy to reach on foot, by car, or by transfer. Its wide viewpoint oversees the caldera’s volcanic islands, Thirassia and St. George’s blue dome, and faces west — with sunset colours and sunset views seven months of the year.

Arrival instructions

By car or motorcycle

Public parking at Perivolas area, roughly 8 minutes on foot.

Free street parking is available before Perivolas Market when approaching from the old road, Eparchiaki Odos Fira–Oia.

On foot, from within Oia

Walk the scenic caldera path — it is the most enjoyable way to arrive.

MIA’S is at the very beginning of Oia, at the end of the Fira–Oia hiking trail.

By transfer or taxi

Ask your driver to stop at Perivolas Market (bus stop). The restaurant is directly behind it.

We are happy to arrange a transfer on your behalf — please contact us in advance.

By cruise ship

Cable car from the Old Port up to Fira. From Fira, take a taxi or transfer, or the local bus to Oia, and get off at the Perivolas Market stop.

We can also arrange a private transfer from Fira — just let us know in advance.
