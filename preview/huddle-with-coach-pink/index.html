        Keyboard Shortcuts

        Press ? any time to open this. Esc to close.

        Playback

        • Space Play / pause
        • K Pause
        • J / L −5s / +5s
        • , / . Frame back / forward (MP4)

        Plays

        • I at the snap, O at the whistle, then M — captures the whole play and auto-loops it
        • M alone — drops a single-timestamp marker (no range)
        • [ / ] Previous / next play (respects active filters)
        • 1 / 2 / 3 Offense / Defense / ST
        • Shift+D Duplicate active play
        • Shift+click in sidebar to range-select. Cmd+click to multi-select.

        Range editing

        • Click a play → seeks to start, stays paused
        • Hit play → loops between IN and OUT
        • "Set OUT here" in Play Details extends the end of the active play

        Drawing

        • Esc Deselect drawing tool
        • Click a tool, then drag on the video
        • Undo / Redo / Clear in the chalk strip

        Capture

        • 📷 Snapshot exports the frame + drawings as PNG
        • ⤓ WebM exports the IN→OUT segment (MP4 only)
