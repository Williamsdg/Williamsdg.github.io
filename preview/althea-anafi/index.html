    Discover Anafi

    The island that was revealed

    Legend says Apollo made Anafi appear to the Argonauts as shelter from a storm — hence the name, “to reveal”. Today it is one of the calmest corners of the Cyclades, crowned by Kalamos, the great monolith that rises from the sea.

    Nearby beaches include Roukounas, Katsouni, Klisidi and Agioi Anargyroi — some reached by a short hike.

    Good to know

    Before you travel

    Anything else? Write or call — the Althea team is happy to help.

    Is breakfast included?

    An à la carte breakfast is available on request, for an additional charge. Every suite has a coffee machine, and some also offer kitchen facilities.

    Is there a pool?

    Yes — a shared pool, open to all guests, with loungers and a view over the sea.

    Can I bring my pet?

    Yes, we are pet-friendly.

    Can you help with my arrival?

    Yes. The port is a 10-minute drive from Chora and we can arrange a transfer or guide you. Because ferries often arrive at night, we recommend booking from the previous day.

    How do I book an experience?

    Send a request with your preferred date and party size. We check with our guides and partners and confirm the details with you — nothing is booked until we do.

    How do I get around the island?

    There are limited bus services connecting the port, Chora and some beaches. If you would like to rent a car or scooter, let us know and we will be happy to assist.

    What is in the suite?

    Wi-Fi, air-conditioning, a ceiling fan, a coffee machine, a stocked mini bar, a safe box, KORRES bath products and a welcome treat of local goods.
