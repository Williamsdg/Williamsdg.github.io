    Welcome to Eagle's Restaurant

    A fixture in this community since 1951

    Located minutes from downtown Birmingham and one block south of Acipco (American Cast Iron Pipe Company), Eagle's Restaurant has been a fixture in this community since 1951. Family-owned, our tradition has always been to offer delicious food at affordable prices.

    We are committed to serving the highest quality meats and vegetables available. With the Jefferson County Farmers Market being only 1 mile away from our restaurant, we consistently have the freshest collard greens, cabbages, sweet potatoes, lemons, bananas, peppers, and corn around.
