A Day With Us

From the dock
to the sandbar.

Every tour follows the same rhythm — meet, brief, cruise, snorkel, eat, lounge, and back. You move at the captain's pace, not a stopwatch.
1 2 3 4
1

Meet at the dock

Festival Place, slip 4. Five minutes from the cruise terminal, ten from Atlantis. We do the paperwork and the safety brief while you grab a coffee from the kiosk.

2

Cruise out

Out past Paradise Island, into the Tongue of the Ocean. Captain narrates if you want, lets you nap if you don't. Music on or off — your boat, your rules.

3

The main event

Pigs, reef, sandbar, sunset — whichever you booked. We stay long enough for you to actually enjoy it, not long enough to bake. Lunch served on board.

4

Back to port

Captain calls your tender 30 minutes before all-aboard if you're a cruise guest. We get the photo dump to your phone before you walk off the dock.

Frequently Asked

Before you
step onboard.

Still wondering? Message us on WhatsApp at +1 (242) 555-0192 — we usually reply within 10 minutes.

How early should I book?

For peak season (Dec–Apr), 2–3 weeks ahead is safe. Same-week is possible most weeks — call us, sometimes we have a captain free we haven't listed yet. Cruise guests should book before sailing.

What if the weather turns?

Our captains call it. If we cancel for weather, you get a full refund or a free reschedule — your choice. We will never sail you in unsafe conditions, and we don't pretend the weather is fine to keep your deposit.

Are tours kid-friendly?

Yes. Every boat carries USCG-approved life jackets in toddler through adult sizes, and the captains love a good first-snorkel tutorial. Pigs in particular are a hit with kids 4+.

Will I miss my cruise ship?

Never has happened, never will. Tell us your all-aboard time at booking and our captains build the route to land you back with a 60-minute cushion minimum. Our dock is a 5-minute walk from Festival Place.

What's actually included?

Captain, mate, fuel, snorkel gear (mask, fins, vest), reef-safe sunscreen, fresh fruit, water and soft drinks. Sunset & party-boat tours include alcohol. Lunch is included on all 4+ hour tours.

Can we customize a charter?

That's our specialty. Email charters@bahamaswatertoys.com with your group, dates, and the experience you have in mind. A captain (not a booking bot) writes you back within 24 hours with a route and quote.
