Why we exist

Every young person
deserves someone
in their corner.

T.I.M.E. Youth Dothan is dedicated to empowering at-risk youth in underserved and underdeveloped communities by providing the support and resources they need to reach their fullest potential.

Free tutoring. Free mentoring. Free workforce training. Ages nine to nineteen, third grade through twelfth, five days a week in the neighbourhood where they already live.

“Our mission, purpose, and promise is to help raise up our youth to become productive citizens and leaders in the community.” T.I.M.E. Youth Dothan

What grounds us

Christ. Community. Connection.

T.I.M.E.’s own motto, and the reason the mentoring programs look the way they do. This is a Christ-driven organisation that happens to run tutoring, sports and job training — not the other way round.

Christ

HOPE and Gents To Gentlemen are explicitly faith-based, built around devotional study and prayer. Monday TEEN Nights include fellowship alongside the food and the gym.

Community

Twenty-two Wiregrass churches, businesses and foundations keep the doors open. Dothan raised $200,000 for the Teen Center on its own.

Connection

Consistent, positive, safe mentorship. The same adults, in the same room, every week — which is the whole point.

He was once one
of the kids he is
fighting for today.

Nathan Patterson founded T.I.M.E. Youth Dothan in 2019 and still leads it as Executive Director. The reason he built it is the reason it works.

  1. 01

    Grew up in Dothan.

  2. 02

    Faced the same educational barriers, in an overlooked neighbourhood.

  3. 03

    Reached his own goals in spite of them, and graduated college.

  4. 04

    Built a successful clothing store in Atlanta.

  5. 05

    Came home — and found students still running into the very same barriers.

  6. 06

    Started giving them the T.I.M.E. he wished more of them had.

Nathan Patterson

Founder & Executive Director
