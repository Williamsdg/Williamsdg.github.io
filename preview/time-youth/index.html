Why we exist

Every young person
deserves someone
in their corner.

T.I.M.E. Youth Dothan is dedicated to empowering at-risk youth in underserved and underdeveloped communities by providing the support and resources they need to reach their fullest potential.

Free tutoring. Free mentoring. Free workforce training. Ages nine to nineteen, third grade through twelfth, five days a week in the neighbourhood where they already live.

“Our mission, purpose, and promise is to help raise up our youth to become productive citizens and leaders in the community.” T.I.M.E. Youth Dothan

Six free programs

There’s a place
for every student.

Nothing here costs a family a dollar. Pick the door a student is ready to walk through — tutoring, mentorship, service, or a first real job.

Meet Nathan Patterson

Sometimes purpose
brings you back home.

Nathan Patterson was a former at-risk Dothan student who reached his own goals in spite of the educational barriers inside an overlooked neighbourhood.

He graduated college. He built a successful clothing store in Atlanta. Then he came home — and found students still running into the very same barriers he had left behind.

He founded T.I.M.E. Youth Dothan in 2019 and still leads it as Executive Director.
