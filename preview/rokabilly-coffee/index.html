Meet Susan & Virginia

A mother, a daughter, and a dream for downtown.

Virginia always pictured a coffee shop on the square in Carthage. After six years in downtown development and three honing her craft at JoZoara in Murfreesboro, she opened the doors with her mom, Susan, on August 28, 2021.

Susan bakes everything by hand from her own mother's recipes — including the cinnamon rolls she rolls out with her grandmother's rolling pin, made by her father more than 80 years ago. The space itself was lovingly restored: exposed brick, the original chimney, and tall transom windows.

"We really just want to be a community space that encourages people to Love More."

— Susan & Virginia
