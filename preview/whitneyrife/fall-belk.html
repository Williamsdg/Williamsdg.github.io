Fall Staples from Belk: Prints, Classics & a Little Leopard

I popped into Belk this week and found so many fall staples worth adding to your closet. Think elevated basics with a little personality like printed shoes, statement heels, and yes… a whole lot of leopard (because when is leopard not a neutral for me?). This try-on is full of the pieces that make getting dressed in fall actually fun and the items that mix and match, work for every event, and instantly make you feel pulled together.

By Whitney Rife, Oct 2025

1. Leopard Love

You already know I’m a sucker for anything leopard and Belk delivered. I found a few dresses, a puffer everyone needs, and even a pair of leopard heels that make any outfit feel instantly styled.

2. Statement Shoes

Fall is the time to bring out a good statement heel. Whether it’s a pop of color, a fun pattern, or texture, Belk’s shoe section is packed right now. I grabbed a printed pump and a block heel that makes any outfit feel dressed up without trying too hard.

3. Everyday Staples

Think timeless prints, cozy accessories, and warm hues, those wear-everywhere pieces you’ll rotate all season. Belk’s fall line has a ton of mix-and-match options, and the quality feels amazing for the price. Add a tailored jacket or trench and you’re ready for anything from brunch to the office.

4. Layering Pieces You’ll Live In

I’m always reaching for lightweight layers, knits, and blazers this time of year. They’re for making every outfit feel more “fall.” Belk has tons of neutral tones: cream, camel, and olive, plus a few fun prints if you want a little extra.

You can shop everything from this post here on my LTK or check out the full round up for easy styling inspo. Can’t wait to see which pieces you love most — and yes, I fully support buying the leopard heels.
