iii. Philosophy
A Way of Doing Things

The hotel as a stage, the guest as the lead.

For four decades, we have built places not as projects but as performances — every detail, from the bellman's coat to the volume of the lobby, designed to put the guest at the center of the room.

It is what some call a sense of place. We call it a sense of timing.
