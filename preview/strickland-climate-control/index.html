Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free estimates?+
Yes — estimates on new system installations are always free and no-obligation. For repairs, we charge a flat diagnostic fee that's waived when you approve the repair.
How fast can you get here?+
We offer same-day service for most repairs and true 24/7 emergency response when you have no heat or no cooling. Call us and we'll give you a real arrival window.
What does it cost to replace a system?+
Most complete system replacements run between $5,900 and $12,500 depending on size, efficiency, and your home. We give you an exact, upfront flat-rate quote before any work — and financing from $99/mo is available.
Do you finance repairs and installations?+
Absolutely. We offer flexible financing with low monthly payments, 0% APR promotional plans, and quick approval. Use our calculator above to estimate a payment, then apply in minutes.
Which brands do you service?+
We repair and install all major makes and models — Trane, Carrier, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Daikin and more — and we're factory-authorized on the brands we sell.
Is the Comfort Club worth it?+
For most homeowners, yes. Members get seasonal tune-ups that extend equipment life, priority scheduling, and 10–20% off repairs — which usually pays for the membership in a single service call.
