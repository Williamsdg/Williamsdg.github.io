What actually happens at an M19 event?
Imagine your dream golf trip… but with elite business minds. We tee it up at bucket-list courses, then head into strategy sessions where real deals and breakthroughs happen. It’s part luxury golf retreat, part high-level mastermind.
Do I have to be good at golf?
No, but if you’re not… you better be good at business and love the game. This group is about connection. That said, most of our members are solid players.
Is this just another golf club or digital country club?
Not even close. We’re not selling you on access to golf courses — we’re building a network of high-performing business owners who just happen to close deals over birdies and bourbon.
How often do you host events?
All throughout the year — think bucket list courses, elite networking, and real business breakthroughs wrapped in once-in-a-lifetime golf trips.
