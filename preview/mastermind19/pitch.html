The website

What changed

Nothing was invented. Every sentence on the concept is already on mastermind19.com. The difference is structure, typography and craft.

One page became five

Home, Membership, Experiences, Founder and Apply. Each has one job, and the application has its own page instead of sharing the first screen with the headline.

The 19th hole, drawn

The hero opens with “The room where your next 7-figure deal is waiting” beside a yardage-book drawing of a hole numbered nineteen. Your four member benefits are drawn as holes one to four.

Who gets in, as a scorecard

Business size, connections and expertise, culture fit. Set out like a card in a member’s pocket, with your standard on the right.

Augusta colours, luxury type

Deep green, ivory and brass, with a Bodoni display face and a clean geometric sans. Your crest and “19” stay.

Your form, exactly

First and last name, email, phone, business website, yearly revenue, private club membership, handicap, and both consent statements as written. It can keep posting to HighLevel, which your site runs on today.

Built for phones

Every page was checked on an iPhone, an Android phone and a 320-pixel screen. The benefits become a swipe, the scorecard stacks, one Apply button stays in reach.

The members’ portal

The room between events

Your copy says the online community is where members connect, access private clubs and attend monthly trainings. This is that, in one place, under your crest.

Events

Every tournament, trip, Vegas day and training with spots taken, a reserve button, a waitlist that moves members in automatically when a spot frees, and a calendar file for each.

Members

A directory with business, industry, city, private club and handicap. A member asks for an intro and the team makes it.

Trainings

Upcoming sessions with a calendar file, and replays posted by the team the same day.

Account

Their profile, their reservations, and the text and marketing consents from your form, switchable by the member.

Announcements

Whatever the team posts shows on every member’s overview, alongside the next event they are in.

The team dashboard

Every application, judged against your standard

The website form lands here instead of in an inbox. The same fields you already ask for, read the way you already read them.

Applications

New, call booked, vetting, accepted, declined. Yearly revenue is checked against $1M+ as it arrives. Referrals show who sent them.

Members

The roster with tier, joined date and events attended. Export to a spreadsheet in one click.

Events and rosters

Reservations and waitlists update the moment a member reserves. Export any roster for the course or the hotel.

Trainings and announcements

Add a session and it appears for members immediately. Post an announcement and it is on every overview.

How it connects

Two options. Keep HighLevel for the form, texts and email, and connect the dashboard to it. Or move the form, member records and messaging into the new site and dashboard. Either way members sign in with their own account, and nothing in the demos is stored anywhere yet.
