The Discipline

MANAGEMENT.

An agent finds the deal. A personal manager oversees the entire professional ecosystem the deal lives inside — the business, the brand, the advisors, the assets and the decades that follow.

01

Business & Financial Oversight

Formation of LLCs, corporations and trusts, with liability shielding for touring, production and business ventures. Oversight of accountants, financial advisors and investment managers.

02

Brand & Media

Personal brand strategy, visual identity, messaging and social media oversight. Press coordination, media training, crisis management and reputation monitoring.

03

Deal Evaluation

Evaluation of recording contracts, touring agreements, licensing deals and brand partnerships — coordinated with counsel, and filtered objectively.

04

Lifestyle & Operations

Scheduling, travel logistics and personal staffing. Tour logistics, production coordination, budget oversight and vendor and crew management.

05

Career Strategy

Long-term career strategy and expansion into acting, directing, producing or writing. Oversight of copyrights, trademarks and licensing.

06

Legacy Planning

Career transition strategy, trust formation and wealth transfer. Philanthropic planning for the life and the name that outlast the career.
