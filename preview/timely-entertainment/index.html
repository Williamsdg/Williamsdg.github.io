The Discipline

MORE THAN
MANAGEMENT.

A personal manager is not an agent. An agent finds the deal. A personal manager oversees the entire professional ecosystem the deal lives inside — the business, the brand, the advisors, the assets and the decades that follow.

01

Business & Financial Oversight

Formation of LLCs, corporations and trusts. Liability shielding for touring, production and business ventures. Oversight of accountants, financial advisors and investment managers.

02

Brand & Media

Personal brand strategy, visual identity and messaging, social media oversight and audience engagement. Press coordination, media training and reputation monitoring.

03

Deal Evaluation

Evaluation of recording contracts, touring agreements, licensing deals and brand partnerships — with attorney coordination and objective opportunity filtering.

04

Lifestyle & Operations

Scheduling and calendar coordination, travel logistics, personal staffing, tour and production coordination, vendor and crew management.

05

Career Strategy

Long-term career strategy and expansion into acting, directing, producing or writing. Oversight of copyrights, trademarks and licensing, and protection of creative works.

06

Legacy Planning

Career transition strategy, trust formation, wealth transfer strategies and philanthropic planning — for the life that follows the career.
