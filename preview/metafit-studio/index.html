Why Meta

A studio that actually knows you.

You won't find front-desk turnover, fluorescent lighting, or coaches reading from a clipboard. Meta is built around four standards — and we don't compromise on any of them.

01 / Coaches

Experienced. Educated. Invested.

Every trainer holds a Bachelor's or higher in exercise science, kinesiology, or nutrition. They remember your name, your goals, and your last hamstring tweak.

02 / Environment

The opposite of a chain gym.

Natural light, full-spectrum sound, lockers with codes, towels folded warm. The moment you walk in, you stop being a guest and start being family.

03 / Equipment

Eleiko. Concept2. Keiser.

State-of-the-art, professionally maintained, and replaced before it wears. Including private Pilates reformers and a recovery suite with infrared and compression.

04 / Results

A proven, measured process.

InBody composition every 6 weeks. Quarterly performance assessments. Personalized programming that adjusts to your data — not a one-size template.

The Method

Transformation, by design.

We don't ask you to "see how you feel in a few weeks." Every member follows the same measured process — the difference is what we learn about you along the way.

01

Foundation

A 90-minute private intake. InBody scan, movement screen, lifestyle interview, and a written 8-week plan in your hands before you leave.

→
02

Build

Your first 6 weeks. Three to five sessions a week, mixing signature class and 1:1, with weekly check-ins that adjust load, recovery, and nutrition.

→
03

Recalibrate

Re-scan, re-assess, re-program. We tell you exactly what the data says and where your next 6 weeks should focus — strength, conditioning, body composition, or recovery.

→
04

Sustain

Become the version of yourself you came here for. Annual members receive quarterly performance reports, priority booking, and unlimited recovery suite access.
