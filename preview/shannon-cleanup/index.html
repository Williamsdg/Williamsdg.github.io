If something goes wrong

Deleted the wrong collection? Shopify keeps a deleted-collection log briefly. Contact Shopify support immediately, or recreate the collection with the same handle.

Products lost during a move? They aren't actually deleted by moving — just removed from a collection. They still exist in Products → All Products. Just re-add to the right collection.

Stuck? Stop and text Dylan before doing anything destructive.
