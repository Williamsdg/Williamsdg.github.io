The Foundation secures philanthropic funding for projects, programs and conservation efforts tied to the Park District's mission. The fund is administered by The Dayton Foundation.

Current campaign

Activity Center Park pickleball courts

The Park District plans to resurface six courts and add two more, with construction expected to begin in fall 2026. The Foundation is raising $50,000 for permanent shade, additional seating and rebounder enhancements.

Goal: $50,000

Staff would update the amount raised from one field. The concept shows no figure because the real total is not public.

About this page. The RFP asks for a Foundation section with its own identity and a prominent link from the Park District homepage. This page has its own header, colors and type, and would live at an address such as foundation.cwpd.org. The colors here are placeholders until the Foundation shares its logo and brochures.
