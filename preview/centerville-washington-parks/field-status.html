Home / Athletic field status

Athletic field status

Grouped by park, with a count at the top. Each status is written out and has its own symbol, so it does not depend on color.

Sample data. These are the 33 fields and statuses shown on cwpd.org on October 3, 2026. In the built site this page keeps reading from RainoutLine exactly as it does today, so staff change nothing about how they post updates.

When status is updated

  • Baseball and softball: weekdays 4:15 p.m.; Saturdays 8:30 a.m.; Sundays 11 a.m.
  • Football: weekdays 3 p.m.; weekends 8 a.m.
  • Lacrosse: weekdays 3 p.m.; weekends 8 a.m.
  • Soccer: weekdays 3 p.m.; weekends 8 a.m.

The Park District has the final authority regarding field closures due to unplayable field conditions. If Game Time Decision is indicated, the umpire or referee will determine the field closure at the scheduled game time.
