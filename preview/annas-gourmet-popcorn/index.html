Our Story

A Family Recipe, Popped Fresh on Broadway

Anna's started with a simple idea: gourmet popcorn made the way you'd make it for your own family — in small batches, with real ingredients, and a whole lot of flavor.

Today we hand-pop, season, and package every order from our little shop in downtown Lebanon, Ohio — and ship it fresh to all 50 states. From wedding favors to corporate gifts to your Tuesday-night craving, every bag is made to be shared.
