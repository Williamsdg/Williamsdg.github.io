Step One

Start signup

Open Google's signup page in a browser. Use Chrome or Safari on your laptop — the iPhone version is fine but a bigger screen makes the form easier.

Do this
  1. Open workspace.google.com/signup
  2. Click Get started
  3. Business name: Shannon Harris Designs (or whatever you want it to say on invoices)
  4. Number of employees: Just you (1)
  5. Country: United States
2
Step Two

Your info

Google asks for a name and an email to send confirmation to. This is NOT your new business email — it's just somewhere they can reach you while you set it up.

Do this
  1. First name: Shannon, Last name: Harris
  2. Current email: your existing personal email (Gmail or whatever you check)
  3. Continue
3
Step Three

Use your domain

Google asks if your business already has a website domain. Yes — you own shannonharrisart.com. Tell Google to use it.

Do this
  1. Question: Does your business have a domain? → click Yes, I have one
  2. Type: shannonharrisart.com
  3. Confirm on the next screen
4
Step Four

Pick your email address

Choose what goes before the @. This is the address customers, galleries, and stockists will email you at. You can always add more later, but pick one main one.

hello@shannonharrisart.com
Friendly, modern, brand-forward
shannon@shannonharrisart.com
Personal, direct, signs like a letter
studio@shannonharrisart.com
Professional, sounds like a working artist's practice
info@shannonharrisart.com
Classic, expected, easy to remember
Do this
  1. Type your chosen username (before the @)
  2. Set a password — write it down or save it in a password manager. You'll need it.
  3. Continue
5
Step Five

Pick a plan

Google offers a few tiers. For one inbox, Business Starter is the right choice — same Gmail, your custom address, plenty of storage.

Step Six

Payment + start trial

Google needs a credit card on file — but you get a 14-day free trial first. Nothing is charged until day 15. You can cancel anytime before then if you change your mind.

Do this
  1. Enter your business credit card or debit card
  2. Confirm billing address (matches the card)
  3. Click Try free for 14 days or whatever the confirmation button says

Step Seven · Dylan handles

Verify the domain

Google will ask you to prove you own shannonharrisart.com. This involves adding a special code (called a "TXT record") to your domain's DNS — that's the technical part where I take over.

Do this
  1. Google will show a screen with a verification code that starts with google-site-verification=...
  2. Don't close that screen.
  3. Text or call Dylan — send a screenshot of the screen Google is showing you.
  4. Dylan adds the DNS record on his side. Takes 5–10 minutes.
  5. Once Dylan tells you "go ahead," click Verify on Google's screen.
Text Dylan

Step Eight · Dylan handles

Turn on mail delivery

After verification, Google needs to actually be allowed to RECEIVE mail at your address. That's another set of DNS records (called "MX records"). Same drill — I handle it.

Do this
  1. Google's setup wizard will show an "Activate Gmail" or "Add MX records" screen
  2. Send Dylan a screenshot of whatever Google is showing
  3. Dylan adds the MX records. ~5 minutes.
  4. Dylan will tell you when to click Activate on Google's screen.
Text Dylan
9
Step Nine

Log in & send a test email

Your new mailbox is live. Time to check it works.

Do this
  1. Go to mail.google.com
  2. Sign in with your new full email (e.g. hello@shannonharrisart.com) and the password you set at Step 4
  3. Send a test email to your personal Gmail to confirm sending works
  4. Reply to that test email FROM your personal Gmail to confirm receiving works
  5. Add the mailbox to your iPhone: Settings → Mail → Accounts → Add Account → Google

All set.

Your business email is live and you're set to look like the working studio you already are.

Things to know

Don't cancel any existing email. If your current setup forwards mail to your personal Gmail, keep that running until the new one is verified and working. We won't break anything.

Don't lose the password. Use a password manager (Apple's iCloud Keychain on iPhone is fine — it's built in). If you do lose it, Google's recovery flow is slow.

Add it to your phone. Once it's live, add the account to your iPhone Mail or the Gmail app so you actually see incoming inquiries.

Stuck? Text Dylan. Anything that gets confusing — especially around the DNS steps — just send a screenshot. Don't wing it.
