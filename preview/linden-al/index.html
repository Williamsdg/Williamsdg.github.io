    About Linden

    Two hundred years as the county seat

    Linden has been the seat of Marengo County since 1819, and it has had a few names along the way.

    1. Before 1818 · "Screamersville"The first settlers' name for the place, from the cries of wild animals at night.
    2. 1819 · Town of MarengoChosen as the seat of the new Marengo County.
    3. 1823 · HohenlindenRenamed for the French victory of 1800, in honor of the county's Vine and Olive Colony settlers. Later shortened to Linden.
    4. 1870 · IncorporatedLinden is officially incorporated on March 1, 1870.
    5. 1902 · The railroad arrivesThe Louisville and Nashville line is built through town.

    The old courthouse

    Linden's 1850s courthouse still stands, one of Alabama's oldest in the Greek Revival style.

    Chickasaw State Park

    Picnic grounds, camping and tall pines, a few minutes outside the city on U.S. 43.

    ChiLLy Fest and the Parade of Lights

    Every December: a chili cook-off, the Christmas carnival, the Parade of Lights and fireworks downtown.

    Born in Linden

    Civil rights leader Ralph Abernathy and Autherine Lucy Foster, the first Black student at the University of Alabama.
