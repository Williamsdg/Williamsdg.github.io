FAQ

Unlike Squarespace or Wix, you own your website, domain and other tools.

Can I add pages or functions to the website?

Yes. We provide the basic website, set up the domain and the hosting. You can change whatever you want. It is your website. You can use your designer or use one of our consultants.

One of the nice things about using our consultants is that you can book in as little as 30 minute increments. So if you want a tweak or if it is an easy change, it can be very inexpensive.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. Your domain can be transferred to another service. It is no different than buying it from GoDaddy or Namecheap.

Can I port out my phone number?

Yes. Port out is available if you decide to move to another service. It is your number.

Can I be a consultant if I don't have Google Calendar or Outlook?

Yes. Our system supports Google Calendar and Outlook for direct booking. But if you use another service, you can still work with PolitiBox. You will need to coordinate appointment times with the client directly if you do not use the built in appointment tool.
