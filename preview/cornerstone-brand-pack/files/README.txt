THE TAGLINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The main logo files carry "Integrity - Experience - Results" beneath
the name. Where the logo has to sit small and the tagline would be
unreadable, use the "-simple" files instead. The seal comes two ways:
with the tagline, or with "Est. 2021 - Prattville, AL".

WHICH VERSION?
--------------------------------------------------------------------
On white or light  -> the normal (dark) files
On charcoal, photos
  or dark colour   -> the "-white" files
One colour only
  (fax, engraving,
  single-colour print) -> the "-black" files

FILE TYPES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
SVG  Vector. Infinitely scalable. The lettering is converted to
     outlines, so it looks right even on a computer that does not
     have the fonts installed. Send this to printers and sign makers.
PDF  Vector too. Use it when a supplier asks for PDF or cannot open SVG.
PNG  Everyday use: email, Word, websites, social media. Transparent
     background. Pick a width at least as wide as you need; never
     scale a PNG up.

FONTS  (both free for commercial use, SIL Open Font License)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Archivo   headings, the logo, buttons, figures
Inter     body text

To install: open the fonts folder, double-click each .ttf, choose
Install. On Windows, right-click and choose "Install for all users".
If you cannot install fonts on a machine, use Arial Bold where Archivo
would go and Arial where Inter would go.

RULES OF THUMB
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Leave clear space around the logo, at least the height of the red
  square, on every side.
- Smallest sensible size: 24px tall on screen, 0.3 inch in print, for
  the horizontal logo. Below that use the mark on its own.
- Do not stretch, squash, rotate, recolour, add shadows or outlines,
  or place the dark logo on a dark background.
- Do not rebuild the logo in Word or Canva. Use these files.
