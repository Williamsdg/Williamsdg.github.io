02

Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to design, develop, and deploy a custom direct-booking website for the Owner's vacation-rental property, connected to the Owner's property-management system for availability and reservations. The project includes the following deliverables:

  • Public Marketing Website — A branded, mobile-responsive website including a home page, property overview, photo gallery, local Auburn guide, guest reviews, an availability calendar, and a frequently-asked-questions page.
  • Direct-Booking Flow — A guest-facing booking experience that checks real-time availability, quotes nightly rates, fees, and taxes, collects guest and payment information, and confirms the reservation.
  • OwnerRez Integration — Connection of the website to the Owner's OwnerRez account so that availability and reservations remain synchronized across Airbnb, VRBO, and direct bookings through a single shared calendar.
  • Payment Processing (Stripe) — Configuration of secure card payment for direct bookings through the Owner's Stripe account, so that direct-booking payments are deposited to the Owner.
  • Owner Dashboard — A private, unlisted owner view presenting the master calendar (color-coded by booking source), upcoming reservations, reservation details, and revenue and occupancy overview.
  • Responsive Design — All pages built to display properly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices, including a persistent mobile booking bar.
  • Content Loading — Placement of the Owner's supplied photography, property details, and written content into the website.
  • Domain & Hosting Setup — Connection of the Owner's registered domain and deployment of the website to hosting managed by the Service Provider, including SSL/HTTPS.
  • SEO Foundations — Page titles, meta descriptions, and basic on-page search-engine optimization aimed at direct-booking discovery.

Property-Management System — OwnerRez

The direct-booking and calendar features of the website depend on the Owner maintaining an active OwnerRez account (or equivalent property-management system approved in writing by the Service Provider), with the Owner's Airbnb and VRBO listings connected to that account. OwnerRez serves as the source of truth for availability and reservations. The Service Provider does not create or maintain a separate calendar and is not responsible for availability errors, rate errors, or double-bookings originating within OwnerRez, Airbnb, VRBO, or any other connected channel.

The Owner is responsible for the OwnerRez subscription and its fees, billed to the Owner directly by OwnerRez, and for keeping the property's channel connections active.

Payments & Booking Terms — Owner's Responsibility

All direct-booking payments are processed through the Owner's own Stripe account and are deposited to the Owner. The Service Provider does not hold, receive, or have access to guest funds. The Owner is solely responsible for setting nightly rates, fees, taxes, minimum-stay rules, cancellation policies, guest communications, lodging-tax collection and remittance, and all obligations arising from a guest's stay. The website presents the terms the Owner configures; it does not set them.

Content Accuracy & Approval

All photography, property descriptions, rates, policies, and local recommendations used on the website will be drawn from material supplied or approved by the Owner. The Owner is responsible for reviewing and confirming the accuracy of all content — including rates, fees, taxes, sleeping capacity, and policies — prior to launch.

Revision Policy

The project includes up to three (3) rounds of revisions during the design and development phase. A revision round consists of a consolidated set of feedback submitted at one time. Additional revision rounds beyond the included three will be billed at the Service Provider's hourly rate of $50/hour. Revisions must be requested within seven (7) days of receiving a design proof or deliverable; otherwise, the deliverable will be considered approved.

Change Orders & Scope Changes

Any work requested by the Owner that falls outside the scope described above — including additional pages, additional properties, custom integrations, or new features — will require a written Change Order. The Service Provider will provide a quote for the additional work, and no work will commence until the Owner approves the Change Order in writing. Change Orders may affect the project timeline and will be billed separately from the build fee.

Build Fee — $750.00 USD

A recurring monthly fee of $30.00 USD covers website hosting, SSL certification, the server-side booking automation (the OwnerRez calendar-sync connection and the direct-booking checkout that keep availability accurate and reservations flowing), security and software updates, uptime monitoring, backups, and minor content updates — such as correcting text, swapping an existing photo, updating a rate note, or adjusting contact details.

The monthly fee does not include the Owner's OwnerRez subscription, the Owner's Stripe processing fees, seasonal photo or content overhauls, or any work constituting a new page, new feature, additional property, or redesign.

The Owner is responsible for costs paid directly to third parties, which are separate from and in addition to the fees above:

● Domain registration — The Owner purchases and maintains their own domain through GoDaddy or another domain registrar of their choosing (typically $12–$25 per year, billed by the registrar directly).

● OwnerRez subscription — Maintained by the Owner and billed by OwnerRez directly.

● Stripe processing fees — Standard per-transaction card-processing fees charged by Stripe on direct bookings, deducted by Stripe from the Owner's payouts.

Payment shall be submitted via the secure payment link(s) provided by the Service Provider, or by another method mutually agreed upon by the parties. Payment details and instructions will be included on the invoice.

The deposit is due in full upon acceptance of this Agreement. The final build payment is due upon launch of the live website. The monthly fee begins upon launch and continues until cancelled. A late fee of 1.5% per month may be assessed on any balance remaining unpaid beyond thirty (30) days from the invoice date. The Service Provider reserves the right to delay or suspend services for any account with an outstanding balance exceeding thirty (30) days.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Should the project be cancelled by the Owner after work has commenced, the $375.00 deposit is non-refundable, as it covers discovery, planning, and development work already performed. If the project is cancelled prior to launch, no final build payment shall be owed. The monthly hosting, automation, and maintenance fee may be cancelled at any time with thirty (30) days written notice. If the Service Provider cancels before any work has been performed, the full deposit will be refunded.

A small, unobtrusive credit line (e.g., "Site by Williams Digital") may be placed in the website footer. If the Owner wishes to opt out of the credit line, this must be agreed upon in writing prior to launch.

Upon termination and settlement of all outstanding balances, the Service Provider will provide the Owner with a complete copy of the website files and source code and will assist with transferring hosting to the Owner or a provider of the Owner's choosing within a reasonable period.

Project Timeline

The Service Provider will aim to complete the project within approximately three to four (3–4) weeks from receipt of the signed Agreement, the deposit, and the Owner's OwnerRez and Stripe access, subject to timely delivery of content and feedback from the Owner:

  • Week 1 — Brand and design review, content and photo gathering, and connection to the Owner's OwnerRez and Stripe accounts.
  • Week 2 — Build-out of all pages, content loading, and configuration of the booking flow.
  • Week 3 — Availability-sync testing, test bookings end-to-end, dashboard setup, SEO configuration, and Owner review.
  • Week 4 — Launch, walkthrough, and the start of the post-launch support window.

This timeline is a good-faith estimate. Delays in providing photos, content, feedback, approvals, or required OwnerRez/Stripe access may extend the schedule accordingly, and the Service Provider shall not be held liable for such delays.

Owner Responsibilities & Support

To facilitate the effective delivery of services, the Owner agrees to:

  • Purchase and maintain their own domain through GoDaddy or another registrar, and provide the access needed to connect it.
  • Maintain an active OwnerRez account with Airbnb and VRBO connected, and an active Stripe account, and grant the access required to integrate them.
  • Provide timely, accurate, and complete content, including high-quality property photography, descriptions, rates, fees, taxes, and policies.
  • Confirm the accuracy of all rates, fees, taxes, sleeping capacity, and booking policies prior to launch.
  • Designate a primary point of contact and respond to design proofs and approval requests within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Warrant that all photos, content, and materials provided are owned by the Owner or properly licensed, and that the Owner holds the legal right to rent the property on a short-term basis in compliance with applicable local ordinances, HOA rules, and lodging-tax requirements.

Delays or failure to meet these responsibilities may impact the timeline and effectiveness of the services provided.

Post-Launch Support

The Service Provider will provide thirty (30) days of complimentary post-launch support beginning on the date the website goes live. This support covers bug fixes and configuration questions. Ongoing hosting, automation, maintenance, and minor content updates thereafter are covered by the monthly fee described in Section 3.

Project Pause & Inactivity

If the Owner becomes unresponsive or fails to provide requested materials, access, feedback, or approvals for a period of fourteen (14) consecutive days, the project may be placed on hold. Projects paused for more than sixty (60) days may be treated as cancelled under the Cancellation & Refund Policy outlined in Section 3.

Launch Sign-Off

Prior to the public launch of the website, the Service Provider will present the completed site to the Owner for final review and approval, including a test of the end-to-end booking flow. The Owner shall have five (5) business days to review and provide written approval or submit final revision requests. Upon approval, the Service Provider will proceed with launch and the final build payment of $375.00 will become due. If the Owner does not respond within this period, the website will be deemed approved.
