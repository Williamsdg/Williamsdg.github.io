Frequently requested forms

These open the latest official versions on IRS.gov. Not sure which one you need or how to fill it out? We're always happy to help — just give us a call.

These links lead to the official IRS website, which always hosts the most current revision of each form. EIB Systems isn't responsible for content on IRS.gov.

  • Form W-4Employee's Withholding Certificate

    Complete this so your employer can withhold the correct federal income tax from your pay. Update it after major life changes like marriage, a new child, or a second job.

    Open Form W-4
  • Form W-9Request for Taxpayer ID Number

    Provide your correct Taxpayer Identification Number to a person or business that must file an information return (such as a 1099) for payments made to you.
