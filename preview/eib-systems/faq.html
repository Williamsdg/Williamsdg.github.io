Questions & answers

Good questions, friendly answers

Don't see your question? We're happy to help — just reach out or call (205) 854-1957.

Do I have to be a business to work with you?

Not at all. We help individuals and families with everything from a simple personal return to estate matters — and we love working with small businesses too. Whatever your situation, there's a friendly expert here for you.

Are you only busy during tax season?

We're a year-round partner, not a tax-season pop-up. Bookkeeping, payroll, planning, and questions don't stop in April, and neither do we. Call us in July and you'll still get a real person who knows you.

How does pricing work?

Every situation is a little different, so we'll give you clear, upfront pricing after a quick (free) conversation about what you need. No surprise invoices, ever.

Is my first consultation really free?

Yes. The first conversation is on us. We listen to your situation, answer your questions, and tell you honestly how we can help — with no pressure or obligation.

Do you offer a discount for first responders and military?

We sure do. As a thank-you for your service, we offer 10% off for active and retired first responders and military personnel.

Can you take over books that are behind or messy?

Absolutely — and we won't make you feel bad about it. We've cleaned up plenty of behind-or-tangled books. We'll get you caught up and keep you current going forward.

How do I switch to EIB from another accountant?

It's easier than you'd think. Say hello, and we'll handle the handover, gather what we need, and make the transition smooth and stress-free.

Where are you located and what are your hours?

Our office is at 7521 Gadsden Hwy, Trussville, AL 35173, open Monday through Friday from 9:00am to 5:00pm — stop by, coffee's on. Our mailing address is P.O. Box 1029, Trussville, AL 35173. Based in the greater Birmingham area, we also prepare tax returns and run payroll for clients nationwide.
