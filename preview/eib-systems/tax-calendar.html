Tax calendar

Key dates & deadlines

The federal deadlines that matter most for individuals and businesses. We'll remind our clients before each one — so you can forget about them.

Next up

The whole year, at a glance

  1. Jan 15

    Q4 Estimated Payment Due

    Self-employed & estimated filers

    Final quarterly estimated tax payment for the prior tax year (Form 1040-ES).

  2. Jan 31

    W-2s & 1099s Issued

    Employers & payers

    Deadline to furnish W-2s to employees and 1099-NEC forms to contractors.

  3. Mar 15

    S-Corp & Partnership Returns

    S-corps (1120-S) & partnerships (1065)

    Calendar-year pass-through returns are due — or file an extension (Form 7004).

  4. Apr 15

    Tax Day

    Individuals & C-corps

    Individual returns (1040), C-corp returns (1120), Q1 estimates, and IRA/HSA contributions for the prior year.

  5. Jun 15

    Q2 Estimated Payment Due

    Self-employed & estimated filers

    Second-quarter estimated tax payment.

  6. Sep 15

    Q3 Estimate + Extended Pass-Throughs

    Estimated filers; extended S-corps & partnerships

    Third-quarter estimate, plus extended 1120-S and 1065 returns are due.

  7. Oct 15

    Extended Individual Returns Due

    Individuals who filed an extension

    Last day to file a 1040 that was on extension.

  8. Dec 31

    Year-End Planning Deadline

    Everyone

    Last day for most tax-saving moves — retirement contributions, charitable gifts, equipment purchases.

Heads up: when a deadline falls on a weekend or federal holiday, it moves to the next business day. Dates can also shift due to IRS announcements or disaster relief. We always confirm the current year's exact dates for our clients.
