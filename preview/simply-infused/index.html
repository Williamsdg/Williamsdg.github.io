Pairing finder

Pick a bottle. We’ll pour its match.

Every suggestion here is the shop’s own. It’s the same advice you’d get at the tasting bar.

Vinaigrette

2 parts olive oil to 1 part balsamic. Whisk and serve. A dab of Dijon makes it creamy.

Marinade

Equal parts oil and balsamic, just enough to cover. Refrigerate at least six hours.

Fish & seafood

Rub with oil only for about 30 minutes, then drizzle the oil and balsamic over the finished dish.
