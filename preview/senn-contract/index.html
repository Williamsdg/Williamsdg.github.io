02

Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to design, develop, and launch a public marketing website for Senn Medicine, based on the design direction presented as “Concept 2” at williamsdigital.io/preview/senn-medicine/concept-2/, adapted using Client-supplied brand assets, photography, and copy.

The build includes:

  • Website Design & Development — A custom, responsive single-page marketing website covering: practice introduction, philosophy statement, the Senn Medicine care experience, physician biography and credentials, membership tiers and pricing, Auburn student membership, patient testimonials, frequently asked questions, practice location, and contact information.
  • Membership & Enrollment Links — Placement of the Client's existing Stripe payment links and Calendly consultation-booking link at the appropriate points in the page.
  • Mobile & Accessibility — Responsive layout across phone, tablet, and desktop, with attention to legibility, color contrast, and reduced-motion preferences.
  • Website Hosting & Deployment — Setup, configuration, and management of the hosting environment, including domain configuration and SSL certification.
  • Search Engine Optimization — On-page optimization, metadata configuration, and local-SEO best practices for the Auburn and Opelika area.
  • Website Hosting & Support — Ongoing hosting, security updates, backups, monitoring, and resolution of website problems, as described in Section 03. Content updates are quoted separately at a quarterly review.

Not Included in This Agreement

For clarity, the following are outside the scope of this Agreement and are not included in the fees below. Any of these may be added later by written Change Order:

  • Patient portal, patient login, or any authenticated area of the site
  • Electronic medical records (EMR/EHR) integration or migration
  • Any system that collects, stores, or transmits Protected Health Information (PHI)
  • HIPAA-covered forms, intake forms, or secure patient messaging
  • Custom photography or a photo shoot
  • Logo design or full brand identity development
  • Copywriting beyond light editing of Client-supplied material
  • Email hosting, practice management software, or billing systems
  • Paid advertising management or ongoing SEO campaign work

Important — Protected Health Information

The website delivered under this Agreement is a public marketing website only. It is not designed, and must not be used, to collect, store, or transmit Protected Health Information as defined by HIPAA. Any contact form included will carry a clear notice instructing visitors not to submit medical details.

Should the Client later wish to add patient intake, a portal, or any PHI-handling feature, that work requires a separate written agreement and, if applicable, a Business Associate Agreement.

Revision Policy

The initial project includes up to three (3) rounds of revisions during the design and development phase. A revision round consists of a consolidated set of feedback submitted at one time. Additional revision rounds beyond the included three will be billed at the Service Provider's hourly rate of $50/hour. Revisions must be requested within seven (7) days of receiving a design proof or deliverable; otherwise the deliverable will be considered approved.

Change Orders & Scope Changes

Any work requested by the Client that falls outside the originally agreed-upon scope will require a written Change Order. The Service Provider will provide a quote for the additional work, and no work will commence until the Client approves the Change Order in writing. Change Orders may affect the project timeline and will be billed separately from the build fee.

Content updates are not included in the monthly fee. What the monthly fee does and doesn't cover

Covered: keeping the website online, secure, backed up, and working correctly. If something breaks, it gets fixed at no additional charge.

Quoted separately: changing what the website says or shows. New content, new photography, new sections, and new functionality are quoted before any work begins.

The Client is responsible for ensuring all published content complies with applicable medical advertising rules, state licensing board requirements, and professional standards.

Patient testimonials, if used, will be published only when supplied by the Client together with the Client's confirmation that appropriate written patient permission has been obtained.
