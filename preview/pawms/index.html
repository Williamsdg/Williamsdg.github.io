Everything on the key rack

Six services under one roof. Daycare, boarding and baths book online. Grooming and training book by phone at your resort.

No. 1

Daycare

Open play with dogs of the same size and temperament, in covered outdoor yards with dog turf and play structures.

  • Comes home tired and happy
  • More comfortable around dogs and people
  • Reservation required
No. 2

Lodging

Indoor, climate-controlled runs and suites for dogs, plus a separate cat room. Bring a bed, a toy or their own food.

  • Family pets can share a run
  • Add daycare to any night
  • Tours welcome
No. 3

Grooming

Groomers on staff at Vestavia Hills, Avondale, Downtown, Tuscaloosa, Huntsville and Madison.

  • Booked by phone at your resort
  • 24 hours notice to cancel
Call to book
No. 4

Baths

Luxury shampoo, ear cleaning and nail trim, with extra brush-outs, mat removal and flea and tick removal available.

  • Priced by coat and weight
  • Free bath for dogs boarding 6 nights
No. 5

Training

Brooke McCarley, certified through Animal Behavior College, teaches with positive reinforcement.

  • Basic obedience and puppy training
  • Socialization and behavior work
  • Daycare and train, or board and train
Birmingham area
No. 6

Pick-up and drop-off

We come to your house, pick up your pet and bring them home again.

  • Call your resort to schedule
  • Monday through Thursday

Before you check in

The rules that are spread across the current site, in one list.

1
Shots up to dateRabies, Distemper and Bordetella. Bring records or have your vet email them.
2
Four months or olderPuppies must be at least four months old to board.
3
Spayed or neutered for daycareAt Tuscaloosa, Huntsville and Madison, dogs over 8 months must be fixed to join daycare. Lodging and grooming are exempt.
4
First visitFirst-time boarders pay in advance. First-time grooms pay $50 up front, applied to the groom.
5
Deposits up north and in TuscaloosaTuscaloosa, Huntsville and Madison take a 50% lodging deposit, refundable up to 3 days before the stay.
6
Cancel 24 hours aheadA late boarding cancellation costs one night. A late grooming cancellation is a flat $50.
7
Medication$2 per dose for up to two medications, $4 for three or more. Insulin is not administered.
8
ToursCome see where they will stay. Vestavia Hills runs kennel tours from 10am to 3pm.
