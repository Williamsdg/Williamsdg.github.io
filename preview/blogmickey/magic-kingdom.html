    Is the Disney Starlight Parade Taking a Break at Magic Kingdom?

    UPDATE (5pm): Good news: the break is off. The My Disney Experience app now shows Disney Starlight: Dream the Night Away at 6:45pm and 9:00pm on November 16, 18, 21, and 23, which were the four regular nights missing from the schedule.

    Mickey’s Very Merry Christmas Party opened its 2026 season at Magic Kingdom last night, and the sellouts haven’t slowed down since. Thursday, December 10th, sold out this morning, just hours after December 11th disappeared from the calendar. Only three party nights have tickets left. Here’s the latest.

    Mickey’s Very Merry Christmas Party 2026 Preview & Expectations

    Magic Kingdom switches into full Christmas mode tonight as Mickey’s Very Merry Christmas Party opens its 2026 season. The classic Once Upon a Christmastime Parade, Minnie’s fireworks, and free cookies all return, joined by a brand-new Stitch dance party and a few character shake-ups. Tonight is sold out, and so is most of the season.

    Free Cookie Lineup for Mickey’s Very Merry Christmas Party 2026

    Mickey’s Very Merry Christmas Party opens tonight at Magic Kingdom, and the free cookie lineup is out! A couple of returning treats moved to new spots around the park, and new flavors fill the rest of the lineup. There’s also an allergy-friendly treat waiting at every stop.

  • Animal KingdomNovember 6, 3:21 pm

    Disney World Extends Thanksgiving Week Hours

    Walt Disney World has added more time at three of its theme parks for Thanksgiving week, according to new park hours on the Walt Disney World website. Magic Kingdom has the most changes, with earlier openings, later closings, and a longer Thanksgiving Day.

  • Animal KingdomOctober 30, 2:59 pm

    Three Walt Disney World Parks Extend Mid-November Operating Hours

    Walt Disney World has changed the posted park hours at three Walt Disney World theme parks for mid-November. The updates were made this afternoon, and they affect Magic Kingdom, Animal Kingdom, and Hollywood Studios on three different days.
