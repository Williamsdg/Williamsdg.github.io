02
Our philosophy

Caring genuinely
for people.

We don't manage hotels. We operate them — with the discipline of a Fortune 500, and the attention of an owner who lives down the street.

Flynn Hospitality is a premier franchise operator of upper-midscale and extended-stay hotels under the Marriott and Hilton brand families. We bring decades of operational expertise — revenue strategy, brand standards, capital deployment, talent development — to every property in our portfolio.

What separates us is not what we know about hotels. It's what we believe about people. Our central organizing principle, "Caring Genuinely for People," shapes how we recruit and retain general managers, how we structure compensation, how we resolve guest issues, and how we evaluate every investment decision.

i.
Ownership mentality
Every GM operates as if they hold the deed. Local autonomy, central support.
ii.
Brand discipline
Marriott and Hilton standards, audited monthly. We don't drift.
iii.
Revenue strategy
Centralized RM, market-aware pricing, and disciplined capital cycles.
iv.
Talent first
Promote-from-within. Tenure measured in decades, not quarters.
03
What we do

Three lines of practice.
One operating system.

i. Operations
Hotel management
End-to-end operation of franchised hotels — staffing, daily ops, brand compliance, guest experience, and ongoing capital improvements.
  • General management & staffing
  • Brand standard compliance
  • Revenue management
  • Quality assurance audits
ii. Investment
Ownership & equity
Direct ownership and equity participation in select assets. We don't operate hotels we wouldn't own — and we own most of what we operate.
  • Direct hotel acquisition
  • JV and equity partnerships
  • Asset management
  • Disposition strategy
iii. Development
New-build & conversions
Site selection through opening day. We develop ground-up Marriott and Hilton properties and convert existing assets to brand standard.
  • Site selection & entitlement
  • Brand approval & PIP scoping
  • Construction management
  • Pre-opening & ramp-up
