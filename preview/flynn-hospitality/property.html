A note on extended-stay
i. The atmosphere
A working hotel for a working city.

Pittsburgh's North Shore lives in the shadow of two stadiums and beside three rivers. Our property reflects that: warm stone, dark steel, soft lighting in the residential corridors, and the kind of lobby people actually use — for early coffee, late laptop sessions, and the occasional impromptu meeting with the GM.

The 2024 renovation took the property to current Marriott Modern brand standard without losing the working-Pittsburgh feel. We don't manufacture atmosphere. We honor what's already there.

ii. The experience
Operated, not managed.

Every Flynn property runs under a single operating system: a tenured general manager with full local autonomy, regional support for revenue and brand standards, and a corporate team available when needed and quiet when not.

Our GM, Marcus Reilly, has been with Flynn eleven years and on this property for six. He hires, schedules, prices, and makes service-recovery decisions without a phone call. That's why our turnover is a third of the regional average and our QA scores sit in the 99th percentile this quarter.

iii. The location
Walking distance to a city.

Acrisure Stadium is six minutes on foot. PNC Park is eight. The Strip District is a short ride across the Roberto Clemente Bridge. Downtown Pittsburgh, the airport shuttle, the bike trail along the Allegheny — all of it sits within ten minutes of the front door.

Extended-stay travel is about geography first, comfort second. We picked this site for both reasons.
