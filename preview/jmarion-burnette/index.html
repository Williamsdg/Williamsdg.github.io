The Story

Four Chapters

Chapter One

The Early Years

Andalusia is a town of about nine thousand in Covington County, close enough to the Florida line that people drive down for the beach. Burnette played varsity there as a freshman and never came off the field again.

By his sophomore year he carried it 297 times for 2,070 yards. Teammates called him "Phat" — a nickname he still runs on his own social handles.

2,070 yards as a sophomore · 14 games

01Andalusia, AL

Chapter Two

The Recruitment

His junior year settled it: 1,473 yards, 17 touchdowns, and a 4A state championship on a 14-1 team. Alabama, Georgia, Michigan, Florida State and Arkansas all came calling.

In March 2023 he committed to Auburn — the in-state program, the dream offer. Eight months later he reopened his recruitment and walked it back. Choosing uncertainty over the comfortable answer is a pattern with him.

4A state title · Top-20 national RB

022023

Chapter Three

The Detour

A senior-year injury cost him four games. He still averaged 7.6 a carry when he played. He signed with Houston, and for two seasons the ball mostly went elsewhere — 71 carries across 13 games behind a crowded Big 12 backfield.

The tape that matters from that stretch is a 39-yard touchdown catch. Given space, he was still the guy who ran away from Alabama 4A defenses.

71 carries · 264 yards · 1 receiving TD

03Houston, 2024–25

Chapter Four

Boone

In January 2026 he entered the portal and chose App State — one of 35 transfers into Dowell Loggains' second-year rebuild. New offense, clean depth chart, a conference where backs get fed.

The season opens September 5 at Kidd Brewer Stadium. For the first time since high school, the carries are there for the taking.
