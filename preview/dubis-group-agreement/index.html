1.What This Agreement Covers

Williams Digital will convert the design concept already produced for The Dubis Group — viewable at the link above — into a custom WordPress theme, installed and working on The Dubis Group's own hosting.

The design itself was produced at no charge and has been reviewed and approved by The Dubis Group. This agreement covers the development work required to turn that design into a functioning, editable WordPress site. It does not re-open the design.

2.What Gets Built

A custom WordPress theme
Purpose-built theme files — header, footer, front page, functions and stylesheet — carrying the approved design exactly as presented. Not a purchased theme, and not a page-builder reconstruction.
Editable content fields
The content of the page wired to clearly labeled fields in the WordPress admin: the hero headline and subheading, the four service areas, the credentials, the About copy and the contact block. Edit the words; the layout holds.
The original front-end, intact
The hand-coded stylesheet and JavaScript carried over as-is — the sticky header, the mobile menu and the scroll reveals behave in WordPress exactly as they do in the concept.
Mobile-first layout preserved
The small-screen composition is part of the build, not an afterthought. The theme is tested on phone, tablet and desktop widths before handoff.
Installation on your server
The theme installed and activated on The Dubis Group's InMotion hosting, against the existing WordPress installation, and confirmed working before the balance is due.
A walkthrough for your team
A live walkthrough of the editable fields for Mark and Tori, so updates can be made in-house from day one without coming back to Williams Digital.

3.Also Included

  • Basic on-page SEO structure — title, meta description, heading hierarchy and descriptive alt text
  • Image optimization and compression for the assets used in the design
  • Accessible markup — semantic headings, keyboard-operable menu, visible focus states
  • Favicon and social-share preview image
  • Self-hosted web fonts, so the site does not depend on an external font service
  • A child-theme-safe structure, so future changes do not fight the theme
  • Two rounds of revisions on the build (see Revisions below)
  • Fourteen (14) days of post-launch support for defects in the delivered work

4.No Page Builder

A deliberate technical choiceThis theme is built as hand-coded WordPress template files. It is not built in Elementor, Divi, WPBakery or any other page builder, and it is not designed to be edited inside one. Page builders re-create a layout in their own markup, which is where the mobile-first composition and the page-load speed are lost. Content is edited through labeled fields in the WordPress admin instead. If The Dubis Group later wishes to rebuild the site inside a page builder, that is entirely its right — but it falls outside this agreement, and Williams Digital does not warrant the result.

5.What Is Not Included

  • Hosting, domain and SSL. The Dubis Group hosts the site on its own InMotion account and holds its own domain registration at Namecheap. Williams Digital does not host, bill for or manage these.
  • Ongoing maintenance. WordPress core, plugin and PHP updates, backups, uptime monitoring and security remain with The Dubis Group. There is no retainer attached to this agreement.
  • Content writing and photography. The copy used is The Dubis Group's own, reorganized. New copy, new photography or licensed stock is not included.
  • Additional pages and templates. This agreement covers the single-page design as presented. Additional page templates, a blog archive, case-study templates or a portfolio system are separate work.
  • E-commerce, memberships, forms beyond contact, and integrations. Any CRM, booking, payment, membership or third-party integration is outside this scope.
  • Migration of existing content. Moving posts, pages or media from the current site into the new theme is not included unless separately agreed.
  • Email configuration. Mailbox, DNS email records and deliverability work are not part of this agreement.
