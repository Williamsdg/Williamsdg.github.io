2.What It Takes to Go Live

1. The Depot's changes
The Depot tells Williams Digital what it would change: wording, photos, colours, sections to add or drop. The concept is a starting point.
2. Every detail checked
Williams Digital goes through the menus, wine list, hours and events with The Depot so that every item and price is right before launch.
3. The inquiry form connected
The private event inquiry form is connected to wherever The Depot wants those inquiries to go, with a confirmation sent to the guest.
4. Switched on
The new site goes live at allaboardauburn.com. The web address stays the same, and guests simply see the new site.
Phones and computers
One design that works on phones, tablets and computers, checked at each size before launch.
Readable by search engines
Menus, the wine list and events are real text, with a page title and description set for search.

3.The Systems The Depot Already Uses

Nothing changes for your staffReservations, event tickets, online ordering, catering, gift cards and event inquiries keep going to the systems The Depot uses today. The new website links to them; Williams Digital does not replace, rebuild or cancel any of them, and each continues to be billed by its own provider directly to The Depot. Before launch, Williams Digital and The Depot will check together that every one of these links works from the new site.

4.Content Accuracy & Approval

The concept's menus, wine list, hours and events were taken from The Depot's current website on October 6, 2026. The Depot's word is final on every item and price. Williams Digital will go through them with The Depot before launch and will not publish anything The Depot has not confirmed.

The Depot is responsible for the accuracy of the menus, prices, allergen and dietary labels, hours, event details and history it approves, and confirms that it owns, or has permission to use, every photo, logo and piece of writing on the site.

5.Not Included

  • The Hound's website. This agreement covers The Depot only.
  • Replacing reservation, ticketing, ordering, catering or gift card systems. See Section 3.
  • Photography or copywriting from scratch. The site uses The Depot's existing photos and wording, and any new photos or text The Depot supplies.
  • Domain registration, business email, paid advertising or an ongoing SEO retainer.

9.Guest Inquiries & Data

The private event inquiry form collects guests' names, contact details and event information.

The Depot owns and controls all data submitted through the website. Williams Digital provides and maintains the mechanism only, and will not use, sell, share, or train any system on that data.

Williams Digital will delete any individual submission, or all stored submissions, within ten (10) business days of a written request, and will provide a complete export at any time on request.

10.Ownership & Rights

On receipt of final payment, The Depot owns the finished website: all page files, styling and any custom graphics produced for this project.

The Depot owns and retains allaboardauburn.com, its name, logo, photos, menus and all content it publishes. Williams Digital claims no interest in any of them.

Williams Digital retains ownership of any underlying reusable code, libraries or components not created specifically for The Depot, and retains the right to display the finished work in its portfolio and marketing. Guest data is never shown.

11.Revisions & Support

The fee includes two rounds of revisions during the build phase. A round is a consolidated set of feedback delivered at one time.

Structural changes requested after approval (additional sections, new functionality, or reworking an approved section's structure) are out of scope and billed under the Fee & Payment Terms section.

Williams Digital will correct any defect in delivered work at no charge for ninety (90) days following launch.

12.The Depot's Responsibilities

  • Send its changes to the concept, and any new photos or wording, at kickoff.
  • Confirm every menu item, price, dietary label, wine, hour and event before launch.
  • Tell Williams Digital where private event inquiries should go.
  • Provide access to the domain's DNS settings, or make the DNS change when instructed, for launch.
  • Provide consolidated feedback within five (5) business days of each review request.
  • Designate a single point of contact authorised to approve content and sign off on launch.

Schedule commitments are contingent on The Depot meeting these responsibilities. Delays in feedback, content or access extend the schedule accordingly.

13.Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential any non-public business information disclosed by the other during this engagement, including the pricing in this agreement, and will not disclose it to third parties except as required by law.

Any credentials shared for launch, such as domain access, will be used only for that purpose, and The Depot may revoke them at any time.

14.Cancellation

Either party may cancel this agreement with written notice.

If The Depot cancels after work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable and The Depot is invoiced for any approved out-of-scope work completed to date. All work completed to the point of cancellation is delivered to The Depot.

If Williams Digital cancels, The Depot receives all work completed to date and a pro-rata refund of any amount paid for work not performed.

15.Governing Law & Entire Agreement

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Alabama. Any dispute arising under it will be brought in the state or federal courts located in Jefferson County, Alabama. The parties agree to attempt good-faith resolution directly before pursuing any formal proceeding.

This agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to The Depot's website and supersedes any prior discussions, proposals or representations. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
