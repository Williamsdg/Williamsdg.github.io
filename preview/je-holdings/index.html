Asset protection is that body of knowledge which governs and articulates the Rules, Principles, and Vehicles of wealth protection and preservation. Applied early, it is ordinary planning. Applied late, it is evidence.

What this addresses

  • Separation of ownership from control. Structures that let a client direct assets they do not personally hold.
  • Exposure mapping. Identifying which assets are currently reachable, and by whom.
  • Pre-event timing. Planning built before a claim, a filing, or a dispute exists.
  • Court-resilient design. Traditional, compliance-driven vehicles rather than high-risk schemes.

The standard we hold

Privacy without secrecy. Control without exposure. Every structure is built to be explained in a deposition, not hidden from one.

Estate planning decides what transfers. Legacy architecture decides how it transfers, to whom, under what conditions, and how much of it survives the process.

What this addresses

  • Generational transfer. Moving wealth to heirs without moving the exposure with it.
  • Probate avoidance. Keeping the disposition of an estate private and out of public record.
  • Beneficiary protection. Structures that hold up when an heir faces divorce, creditors, or judgment.
  • Continuity of control. Succession that does not stall when the principal is unavailable.

Who this is for

High-net-worth families, business owners with operating entities to hand down, and clients whose estates would otherwise be settled in public view.

The entity is the instrument. Formation is not paperwork — it is the decision that determines what a plaintiff can reach five years from now.

Vehicles

  • Limited Liability Companies. Operating and holding LLCs, single- and multi-member.
  • Corporations. Formation, structuring, and corporate governance.
  • Holding companies. Layered ownership that isolates operating risk from accumulated assets.
  • Land trusts. Title held privately, with beneficial interest structured separately.

Ongoing advisory

JE Holdings advises startups and mid-size companies beyond formation — governance, structure maintenance, and the business decisions that either preserve or quietly dissolve the protection originally built.

Proven domestic structures first. International strategy only where the facts warrant it — and never as a substitute for a defensible onshore foundation.

Onshore

  • Domestic entities and trusts built on established, court-tested law.
  • Jurisdictional selection within the United States, chosen for statute rather than marketing.
  • Full compliance posture — reportable, explainable, and filed.

Offshore

  • International structuring for clients with genuine cross-border exposure or holdings.
  • Reporting discipline. Offshore is not opacity; disclosure obligations are met, not avoided.
  • Risk assessment. We decline offshore-only arrangements that cannot survive scrutiny.

Concentrated income, compressed earning windows, and public visibility. The exposure profile is different, so the structure has to be.

What this addresses

  • Income structuring. Contract, endorsement, and performance income routed through protective entities.
  • Name, Image & Likeness. NIL and publicity rights held and licensed rather than personally exposed.
  • Intellectual property. Marks, catalog, likeness, and brand assets held in structures separate from the individual.
  • Privacy. Ownership records that do not publish an address or a net worth.
  • Anticipated claims. Planning ahead of divorce, paternity actions, or litigation — before the claim exists.

The Philosophy
Wealth ENsurance™

Not insurance against loss.
Assurance by structure.

Insurance pays after the event. Wealth ENsurance™ is JE Holdings' philosophy of long-term, durable protection — built before the event, so the asset was never reachable in the first place. It is the difference between recovering value and never surrendering it.

Privacy without secrecy
Ownership that isn't published, using vehicles that are fully disclosed where disclosure is required. Nothing hidden. Nothing advertised.
Control without exposure
Direction over assets that are not held in a client's personal name — the operative meaning of "own nothing, control everything," legally and ethically.
Durability over cleverness
Proven domestic structures in preference to high-risk offshore schemes. A plan is only protection if it survives a courtroom.
