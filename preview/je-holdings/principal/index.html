Rupert
Francisco, JD

President & Principal · JE Holdings, LLC
Placeholder — replace with Rupert’s photograph

Rupert Francisco is President of JE Holdings, LLC. An honors graduate of the University of California, Riverside and a graduate of UCLA School of Law, he served as a California State Assembly Fellow working on crime victims compensation legislation, and later taught undergraduate business law.

In 1983 he became the first African American attorney to establish a law practice in Century City. He practiced law in California from 1983 to 1989 before turning to company building — founding six companies across entertainment, holdings, and advisory over the following three decades.

His work concentrates on asset protection, estate planning, business formation, wealth strategy, and counsel to startup and mid-size companies.
