2.Included in the Build

Custom basketball-themed design
A design built around Hoopsology's existing logo and colors, with a mobile-specific layout tested on iPhone, Android and desktop.
Automatic episode & Shorts sync
New uploads to the Hoopsology YouTube channel appear on the site automatically, usually within about two hours. Nothing is uploaded twice.
In-page players
Episodes and Shorts play on the site without sending visitors away. Shorts preview on hover and play with sound on click or tap.
Listen-anywhere links
Prominent links to the show on YouTube, Spotify and Apple Podcasts throughout the site.
Studio: stories
Write, edit, schedule and publish news stories with a formatting toolbar, cover images, categories, bylines and an optional attached episode. Paste from Google Docs or Word.
Studio: site controls
Feature a story on the homepage, pin any episode to the homepage hero, or hide a video from the site.
Branded newsletter
A sign-up form on the site, and the ability to send a story or roundup to subscribers from the Studio. Includes a few reusable Hoopsology-branded email designs to choose from.
Contact & inquiries
A guest and sponsor inquiry form delivered to Hoopsology's inbox, with an automatic confirmation to the sender.
Search & sharing basics
Page titles and descriptions, podcast structured data, social sharing previews, and submission to Google Search Console.
Launch & walkthrough
Connection of Hoopsology's domain, SSL, Studio logins for the team, and a walkthrough of how to publish at launch.

3.The Locker Room Community — Future Phase

Not part of this agreementThe design concept includes a preview of the Locker Room: chat channels, episode threads and subscriber-only rooms. This build includes the Locker Room page and its early-access sign-up list only. The live community itself, with member accounts, moderation tools and subscriber access, is a separate phase and will be quoted separately if and when Hoopsology wants to launch it.

4.Content & Editorial Responsibility

Hoopsology is the publisher of everything on the site. Stories, newsletters and any other written content are written or approved by Hoopsology. Episode titles, descriptions and thumbnails are pulled directly from Hoopsology's own YouTube channel.

The concept's starter stories were drafted from Hoopsology's published episode descriptions to show how the News section works. Hoopsology will review, replace or approve them before launch. Williams Digital will not invent quotes, statistics, guest details or claims about players, teams or anyone else.

Hoopsology confirms it owns, or has the right to publish, every episode, clip, photograph, logo and piece of writing it publishes on the site or sends in the newsletter.

5.Not Included

  • The live Locker Room community (chat, forum, member accounts, subscriber rooms). See the Future Phase section.
  • Writing stories or newsletters. Hoopsology writes its own content; Williams Digital builds the tools to publish it.
  • Podcast hosting or audio distribution. Episodes stay on YouTube and Hoopsology's existing podcast host.
  • Paid memberships, merchandise stores or payment collection.
  • Logo or brand identity design. The site uses Hoopsology's existing logo and colors.
  • Domain registration, business email (Google Workspace), paid advertising or an ongoing SEO retainer. Google Workspace setup can be quoted separately; Google bills its own fees directly.

6.Domain Name — Owned by Hoopsology

Hoopsology will purchase (or already owns) its domain name in its own name and on its own account, so it owns and controls it permanently, whether or not it continues with Williams Digital. The domain fee is paid by Hoopsology directly to the registrar and is not included in the website fee or the monthly fee.

Hoopsology will give Williams Digital delegate access to the domain so it can be connected to the site at launch. Williams Digital will change only the records needed to point the domain at the website.

Larger changes are billed under the Fee & Payment Terms section.

10.Subscribers & Sign-up Data

The newsletter, contact form and Locker Room sign-up collect names and email addresses from Hoopsology's audience.

Hoopsology owns and controls all data collected through the website. Williams Digital provides and maintains the mechanism only, and will not use, sell, share, or train any system on that data.

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link and Hoopsology's contact details, as email marketing law requires. Hoopsology will only email people who signed up. Williams Digital will delete any individual record, or all stored records, within ten (10) business days of a written request.

11.Ownership & Rights

On receipt of final payment, Hoopsology owns the finished website: all page files, styling, the Studio, email designs and any custom graphics produced for this project.

Hoopsology owns and retains its domain name, its logo, its episodes and all content it publishes. Williams Digital claims no interest in any of them.

Williams Digital retains ownership of any underlying reusable code, libraries or components not created specifically for Hoopsology, and retains the right to display the finished work in its portfolio and marketing.

12.Revisions & Support

The fee includes two rounds of revisions during the build phase. A round is a consolidated set of feedback delivered at one time.

Structural changes requested after approval (additional pages, new functionality, or reworking an approved section's structure) are out of scope and billed under the Fee & Payment Terms section.

Williams Digital will correct any defect in delivered work at no charge for ninety (90) days following launch.

13.Hoopsology's Responsibilities

  • Confirm the domain name, and grant Williams Digital delegate access for launch.
  • Supply the names and email addresses of team members who need Studio logins.
  • Choose the newsletter sender name and reply-to address.
  • Review, replace or approve the starter stories before launch.
  • Provide consolidated feedback within five (5) business days of each review request.
  • Designate a single point of contact authorised to approve content and sign off on launch.

14.Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential any non-public business information disclosed by the other during this engagement, including the pricing in this agreement, and will not disclose it to third parties except as required by law.

Any credentials shared for launch, such as domain registrar access, will be used only for that purpose, and Hoopsology may revoke them at any time.

15.Cancellation

Either party may cancel this agreement with written notice.

If Hoopsology cancels after work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable and Hoopsology is invoiced for any approved out-of-scope work completed to date. All work completed to the point of cancellation is delivered to Hoopsology.

If Williams Digital cancels, Hoopsology receives all work completed to date and a pro-rata refund of any amount paid for work not performed.

16.Governing Law & Entire Agreement

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Alabama. Any dispute arising under it will be brought in the state or federal courts located in Jefferson County, Alabama. The parties agree to attempt good-faith resolution directly before pursuing any formal proceeding.

This agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the website and supersedes any prior discussions, proposals or representations. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
