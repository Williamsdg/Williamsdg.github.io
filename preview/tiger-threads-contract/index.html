02

Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to provide the Client with professional digital services, which include but are not limited to:

  • Public Marketing Website — Custom design and development of a responsive marketing website for Tiger Threads Auburn, including the homepage, story/about section, pop-up schedule, Tiger Threads Home Parties section, "Book the Trailer" inquiry form, and contact information.
  • Shop Category Pages — Build-out of eight (8) product category pages (Apparel, Hats, Jewelry, Serveware, Tailgate Games, Home Decor, Gifts, and Vintage), each with product grids, filters, sorting, and add-to-bag functionality.
  • Shopify Product Integration — Connection of the website's shop pages to the Client's Shopify product catalog so that products, inventory, pricing, and variants stay in sync with Shopify as the system of record.
  • Square Checkout Integration — Configuration of Square as the checkout/payment processor for online and in-trailer sales, with the option to switch to Shopify Payments in the future if the Client determines it is preferable for the business. Migration to Shopify Payments, if elected, will be performed at no additional build-fee cost during the initial engagement.
  • Owner "Trailer Console" Dashboard — A private operator dashboard for the Client featuring the Pop-Up Calendar, the Rolling Rack inventory view (synced to Shopify), Home Parties bookings, sales overview (combined Square + Shopify), Auburn Family CRM, marketing & social queue, vendors, and settings.
  • Booking & Inquiry Flow — A "Book the Trailer" form for tailgates, home parties, bridal showers, markets, corporate events, and other engagements, routed to the Client's email.
  • Website Hosting & Deployment — Setup, configuration, and management of the web hosting environment including domain configuration (tigerthreadsauburn.com) and SSL certification.
  • Website Maintenance & Updates — Ongoing maintenance, content updates, schedule changes, bug fixes, and performance optimization.
  • Brand & Visual Identity — Light development of digital brand elements (Auburn navy & burnt orange palette, typography, visual treatments) for web use, in alignment with the Tiger Threads identity.
  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO) — On-page optimization, metadata configuration, and local-SEO best practices to improve visibility for Auburn, Opelika, Lake Martin, and the surrounding region.
  • Digital Consulting — Strategic guidance on digital presence, technology selection, and online business solutions.

The specific services to be provided shall be determined in consultation between the Service Provider and the Client and may be further detailed in a separate Statement of Work or engagement letter.

Revision Policy

The initial project includes up to three (3) rounds of revisions during the design and development phase. A revision round consists of a consolidated set of feedback submitted at one time. Additional revision rounds beyond the included three will be billed at the Service Provider's hourly rate of $50/hour. Revisions must be requested within seven (7) days of receiving a design proof or deliverable; otherwise, the deliverable will be considered approved.

Change Orders & Scope Changes

Any work requested by the Client that falls outside the originally agreed-upon scope of services will require a written Change Order. The Service Provider will provide a quote for the additional work, and no work will commence until the Client approves the Change Order in writing. Change Orders may affect the project timeline and will be billed separately from the build fee.

Third-Party Platforms (Shopify & Square)

The Client is responsible for maintaining active accounts in good standing with Shopify (for product catalog and inventory) and Square (for payment processing), and for paying any subscription, transaction, or processing fees charged directly by those platforms. The Service Provider is responsible for integrating these platforms with the Client's website but is not responsible for outages, fee changes, policy changes, or service interruptions caused by Shopify, Square, or any other third-party service.

03

Fees and Payment

In consideration of the services provided, the Client agrees to compensate the Service Provider as follows:

Build Fee — Total $750.00 USD

A one-time build fee of $750.00 USD covers the initial website design, development, eight shop category pages, Shopify + Square integration, the Trailer Console dashboard, and deployment. This fee is split into two installments:

  • Deposit — $375.00 USD due upon acceptance of this Agreement. Work begins after the deposit is received.
  • Final Payment — $375.00 USD due upon launch of the live website to the Client's domain.

Monthly Maintenance Fee

A recurring monthly fee of $40.00 USD for ongoing website hosting, maintenance, updates, monitoring, and support. This fee covers hosting, SSL certification, security updates, routine content updates (such as schedule changes, new pop-up dates, featured-product swaps, or home-party bookings), continued upkeep of the Shopify / Square integration, and ongoing technical support and minor updates. The monthly maintenance fee begins on the date the website is launched live.

Payment Methods

Payment shall be submitted via the secure payment link(s) provided by the Service Provider. Payment details and instructions will be included on the invoice.

Payment Terms

The deposit is due in full upon acceptance of this Agreement. The final build payment is due upon launch of the live website. The monthly maintenance fee begins upon launch and continues until cancelled. A late fee of 1.5% per month may be assessed on any balance remaining unpaid beyond thirty (30) days from the invoice date. The Service Provider reserves the right to delay or suspend services for any account with an outstanding balance exceeding thirty (30) days.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Should the project be cancelled by the Client after work has commenced, the $375.00 deposit is non-refundable as it covers discovery, planning, and development work already performed. If the project is cancelled prior to launch, no final build payment shall be owed. The monthly maintenance fee may be cancelled at any time with thirty (30) days written notice. If the Service Provider cancels before any work has been performed, the full deposit will be refunded.

Intellectual Property & Ownership

Upon receipt of full payment for the build fee, the Client shall receive ownership of all custom-designed deliverables, including website files, graphics, and content created specifically for the Client. The Service Provider retains the right to use general techniques, tools, and methodologies developed or utilized during the engagement. Third-party assets (fonts, stock images, plugins, Shopify themes, Square SDK components) remain subject to their respective license agreements.

Portfolio & Credit Rights

The Service Provider retains the right to display and reference the completed website and related deliverables in their professional portfolio, marketing materials, case studies, and social media. The Client agrees that a small, unobtrusive credit line (e.g., "Site by Williams Digital") may be placed in the website footer. If the Client wishes to opt out of the credit line, this must be agreed upon in writing prior to the commencement of work.

Domain & Hosting Ownership

Any domain names registered on behalf of the Client (including tigerthreadsauburn.com) shall be owned by the Client. The Service Provider will register domains under the Client's name and contact information. Hosting accounts managed by the Service Provider remain under the Service Provider's administrative control for the duration of the service engagement. Upon termination and settlement of all outstanding balances, the Service Provider will assist in transferring access to the Client within a reasonable timeframe.

  • Use confidential information solely for the purposes of fulfilling obligations under this Agreement.

05

Client Responsibilities

To facilitate the effective delivery of services, the Client agrees to:

  • Provide timely, accurate, and complete content, assets, branding materials, product photography, and information as reasonably requested by the Service Provider.
  • Maintain an active Shopify account with up-to-date product listings, inventory, and pricing, and provide the Service Provider with the necessary access (API keys, admin permissions) to complete the integration.
  • Maintain an active Square account (or, if elected, Shopify Payments) and provide the Service Provider with the necessary access to complete the checkout integration.
  • Respond to communications, design proofs, and requests for approval within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Designate a primary point of contact for all matters relating to this engagement.
  • Review and provide feedback on deliverables within the timeframes specified, understanding that delayed feedback may extend project timelines.
  • Ensure that all content, images, product photos, and materials provided to the Service Provider are owned by the Client or properly licensed (including any photography of vintage items or branded merchandise).
  • Maintain accurate pop-up schedules, home-party bookings, product information, and contact information, and notify the Service Provider of any updates needed beyond what the Client manages directly in the Trailer Console.

Delays or failure to meet these responsibilities may impact the timeline and effectiveness of the services provided, and the Service Provider shall not be held liable for any resulting delays.

Project Pause & Client Inactivity

If the Client becomes unresponsive or fails to provide requested materials, feedback, or approvals for a period of fourteen (14) consecutive days, the project will be placed on hold. A reactivation fee of $100.00 USD may apply to resume work after a project pause. Projects paused for more than sixty (60) days may be treated as cancelled under the Cancellation & Refund Policy outlined in Section 3.

Launch Sign-Off

Prior to the public launch of the website, the Service Provider will present the completed website and Trailer Console dashboard to the Client for final review and approval. The Client shall have five (5) business days to review and provide written approval or submit final revision requests. Upon Client approval, the Service Provider will proceed with launch and the final build payment of $375.00 will become due. If the Client does not respond within this period, the website will be deemed approved and the Service Provider may proceed with launch.

Term and Termination

This Agreement shall become effective upon acceptance by the Client and shall remain in effect until the completion of the agreed-upon services and the duration of the monthly maintenance engagement, unless earlier terminated as follows:

Termination by Either Party

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing thirty (30) days written notice to the other party. Upon termination, the Service Provider will provide the Client with all completed deliverables and assist with a reasonable transition period.

Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision and fails to cure such breach within five (5) days of receiving written notice.

Effect of Termination

Upon termination, the Client shall pay the Service Provider for all services rendered up to the effective date of termination. The Service Provider will deliver all completed work product and provide reasonable assistance for transition, including handoff of Shopify and Square integration credentials and the Trailer Console dashboard codebase. All confidentiality obligations shall survive termination as specified in Section 4.
