Services

Comprehensive financial support, tailored to your business.

From clean monthly books to strategic tax planning, every engagement is designed around how you actually run your business — not a one-size-fits-all checklist.

Bookkeeping

Monthly or quarterly bookkeeping that keeps your records accurate, reconciled, and audit-ready. QuickBooks Online, Xero, and custom workflows supported.

Tax Preparation

Federal and state returns for individuals and businesses — Schedule C, S-Corp (1120-S), partnership (1065), and C-Corp (1120). Every return reviewed twice.

Tax Planning & Advisory

Quarterly check-ins so April never surprises you. Estimated payments, withholding strategy, retirement contribution planning, and entity-level moves before year-end.

Entity Selection

Sole prop, LLC, S-Corp, or C-Corp — a clear-headed look at what fits your revenue, owner count, and goals. Includes registration guidance and S-Corp election filings.

Payroll Setup & Support

Get payroll running cleanly — Gusto, ADP, or QuickBooks Payroll setup, owner reasonable-comp analysis for S-Corps, and quarterly 941 / annual W-2 oversight.

Financial Statements

Compiled P&L, balance sheet, and cash-flow statements — the kind banks and investors want to see, prepared on a schedule that fits your reporting cadence.
