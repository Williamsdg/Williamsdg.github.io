FY27 funding priorities

Five areas of care we are funding this year.

Every application should address health disparities in Montgomery County and fall into at least one of these areas.

  • Primary healthcareMedical homes, preventive care, and access to care as Medicaid changes arrive.
  • Maternal healthcareCare and counseling for mothers before and after birth.
  • Dental healthcarePlace-based dental care for residents who go without it.
  • Mental and behavioral healthcareSupport for youth, families, and veterans, close to where they live and learn.
  • Chronic disease managementOngoing help for residents managing long-term conditions.

    How to apply

    1. Check that you are eligible501(c)(3) health nonprofits based in Montgomery County, or with county-based health programs.
    2. Introduce your organizationNew to HIF? Send a short email about your mission, programs, and clients. We usually visit before a first grant.
    3. Attend a grant orientationOne representative attends a virtual session or watches the recording.
    4. Apply in the grant portalCreate one account for your organization, then find open applications on your dashboard.
    What we fund
    • Starting or growing well-conceived programs and organizations
    • Innovative ways to improve the quality, accessibility, or delivery of health and wellness services
    • Replicating and scaling programs that have worked here or elsewhere
    • Building organizational capacity for sustainability and better service delivery
    • Work that leverages other resources, such as partnerships or matching funds
    What we do not fund
    • Endowment funds or annual campaigns
    • Lobbying or political activities
    • Grants to individuals
    • Bricks-and-mortar capital expenses
    • Replacement of government funding
    • Religious activities, though secular health programs run by a religious institution may qualify

    Our history

    It started with a $2,000 grant for an ambulance.

    More than fifty years later, the focus has never moved: health and wellness for the people of Montgomery County.

    1. 1973Founded as the Suburban Hospital Association Foundation. The first grant helps Bethesda-Chevy Chase Fire and Rescue buy an ambulance.
    2. 1980Giving broadens, and the name changes to the Suburban Maryland Health Foundation.
    3. 2007The board hires the Foundation's first staff member and begins building a public presence.
    4. 2012The Healthcare Initiative Foundation becomes an independent 501(c)(3) private foundation.
    5. 2024More than 100 community partners gather in Germantown to mark 50 years.
