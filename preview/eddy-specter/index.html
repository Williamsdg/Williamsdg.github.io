Why Galveston is so haunted

An island that remembers.

The stories on this tour aren't invented for a scare. They grow out of what really happened here.

1894

The Grand opens

The Grand 1894 Opera House opens on Postoffice Street. Eddy has led paranormal investigations inside it.

1900

The Great Storm

On September 8, a hurricane destroys much of the island and kills an estimated 6,000 to 8,000 people. It is still the deadliest natural disaster in U.S. history.
