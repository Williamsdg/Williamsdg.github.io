Our Land

A fifty-year plan, in plain English.

The Bay-Walton Sector Plan is a long-term master plan for 110,500 acres of St. Joe land in Bay and Walton counties. Nearly half of it is set aside for conservation.

48% conserved
52% planned for future use
53,229 acres of connected conservation and preservation areas110,500 acres total
What is a sector plan?

A long-range framework that lays out where homes, businesses and conservation land will go across a very large area, instead of deciding one parcel at a time. Florida law did not require one here. St. Joe volunteered for the process.

Who approved it?

Bay County, Walton County and the State of Florida approved the plan in 2015, after public workshops, open houses and hearings held from 2013 to 2015.

What happens before anything is built?

St. Joe prepares a Detailed Specific Area Plan covering at least 1,000 acres at a time. Each one adds detail on land use for that area.

Our Story

From timberland to town centers.

The company's history is tied to the history of this region. Ninety years in a few stops.

1923

A vacation turns into an investment

Alfred I. duPont visits Florida looking for a vacation home and begins buying timberland across Northwest Florida.

1936

St. Joe is founded

Edward Ball incorporates the St. Joe Paper Company. Operations begin at the paper mill in Port St. Joe.

1982

Real estate division formed

The focus begins to shift from industrial operations to land development.

1990

Listed on the NYSE

The company earns a listing on the New York Stock Exchange.

1998

The St. Joe Company

The company takes its current name, the bluebird logo and the ticker symbol JOE.

1999

WaterColor begins

Construction starts on the 499-acre WaterColor community and WaterColor Inn, the company's first hotel.

2010

An airport on donated land

St. Joe donates 4,000 acres for Northwest Florida Beaches International Airport, and moves its headquarters to Northwest Florida.

2015

Sector plan approved

County and state governments approve the long-term Bay-Walton Sector Plan covering 110,500 acres.
