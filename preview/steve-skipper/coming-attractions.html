The Enforcer

John Hannah — the greatest offensive lineman in football history

It is consensus among players and coaches at collegiate and professional levels that John Hannah is the greatest offensive lineman in football history — included in every Hall of Fame, making the judgement seriously authentic.

John played the offensive line position with a defensive frame of mind, dominating his opponent time after game-extensive time. Coach Paul W. Bryant called him the most dominant offensive lineman he had ever coached.

Artist Steve R. Skipper has produced an original painting and limited edition celebrating the illustrious career of “The Enforcer” — the one who put warranted fear in the hearts of any defensive player who happened to be in his blocking alley.
