The Artist

Steve R. Skipper

An ex-gang member and drug addict transformed by the unparalleled hands of Christ into a fine artist with international exposure and respect.

The Art of Steve R. Skipper

Sanctioned. Recognized. Collected.

Steve Skipper is the first African American to complete artwork sanctioned by NASCAR, the University of Alabama, the PGA and the PBR — with unveilings at The MGM Grand, Monte Carlo in Las Vegas, and Chelsea Piers in New York City.

His work has been recognized by Civil Rights icon Ambassador Andrew J. Young; NFL legends Bart Starr, Ozzie Newsome, Ken Stabler and Doug Williams; University of Alabama head football coach Nick Saban; and many others.

He works in oils, pastels, acrylics and pencils, and has mastered fine-art reproduction through the science of lithography and the French method of giclée.

The Transformation

From the streets to the easel

With no formal training in art, Steve Skipper built an internationally respected career through faith, discipline, craftsmanship and thousands of hours at the easel.

He credits the transformation to his faith in Jesus Christ — a journey from gang involvement and drug addiction into a life as a fine artist with international exposure and respect.

“So, King Agrippa, I was not disobedient to the heavenly vision.” Acts 26:19 AMP

The book

Dream On — A Journey to Deliverance, written with Don Keith in 2017, covers his early life, gang involvement, salvation, deliverance, and his establishment in the fine art business.

In His Own Words

Artist Statement

For over 30 years I have been blessed to combine my love for Art and Sports and form a business of which I am very proud. Sports Art can be very demanding, requiring many studies and extreme attention to detail. The individuality of an Athlete or Coach is second to none. The study of the uniforms, logos, equipment, and the anatomy of the Players can be much.

Yet that is not nearly enough as you are required to capture the movement and magic of each individual personality and style. From year to year with equipment changes, hairstyles, tattoos, and team uniform alterations, you can't stay in a rut. You have to constantly study. Paying attention to each detail.

The difference in the size of each athlete from position to position and Sport to Sport. From Football to NASCAR to International Equine Competition. Accepting the challenge of expression on the faces, in the eyes and body language, and complex diversities of anatomy from human to animal and even to race car. These are a few things that the Players appreciate and things that most Artists fail to care about.

“We have decided to stay in the ways of the Old Masters which require much care, much time, and work.” Steve R. Skipper

The new digital forms of Art with only demand a couple of hours, no brushes, pencils, or paint. We have decided to stay in the ways of the Old Masters which require much care, much time, and work. Many meticulous hours to reach and complement the style of Super Realism which demands as close to a photographic look without losing the touch of the Fine Artist.

Taking a supervising role in the Reproduction process as well as the Custom Framing choices have proven to be an Art within themselves in which I relish as well as the work at the easel in Studio.

I've had a chance to meet some of the greatest Professional and Collegiate Players and Coaches in the world. To sign partnership agreements with the selling and distributing of my work all over the country and outside. It's been a blessing beyond blessings to fulfill a Calling while taking care of my family at the same time.

Honors & Recognition

2023 Sports Artist of the Year

Recognized by the United States Sports Academy based on a unanimous selection by their Art Committee.

The award is a key part of the Academy's annual Awards of Sport program, which serves as a “Tribute to the Artist and the Athlete.” The program honors the highest accomplishments in sport and art and is held on the university campus at the American Sport Art Museum and Archives in Daphne, Alabama.
