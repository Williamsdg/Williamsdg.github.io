The Anointed Homes Art Foundation

Art with a purpose

Creating opportunities for gifted young artists — and for those the world has written off.

Steve Skipper established the foundation to provide fine-art scholarships for high school seniors, supporting gifted young artists nationally and internationally.

8

Original horse paintings completed

INITIATIVE ONE

Fine-Art Scholarships

Steve has completed eight original paintings featuring horses in the realistic style. Proceeds from these originals and their limited editions are intended to fund fine-art scholarships for high school seniors.

“Horses are universally loved sculptured masterpieces from the creative hands of God.”

The works are being reproduced by Gallery Street in Atlanta, Georgia.

Explore the Equine Collection

INITIATIVE TWO

Prison Art Ministry

In partnership with Pastor Kris Erskine, the foundation aims to reach incarcerated men and women with artistic talent — offering motivation, materials, and the opportunity to exhibit and sell their work.

The goal is to enable them to exchange crime for paintbrushes and pencils, and to support themselves upon release.

A calling extended to those the world has counted out.
