The Woodrow Lowe Original

Paul W. Bryant Museum  ·  Tuscaloosa, Alabama

The unveiling of the original painting celebrating the life and career of Mr. Woodrow Lowe.

Mr. Skipper will be joined by Mr. Woodrow Lowe Jr. and family.

Woodrow Lowe Sr. was an All-SEC and All-American linebacker for the University of Alabama under Coach Paul W. Bryant — an Alabama and San Diego Charger legend.

The Hank Aaron Commemorative

Official painting & limited edition

Mr. Skipper has received a formal commission from the family and estate of Mr. Hank Aaron to create the official commemorative painting and limited edition celebrating his life and career.

The painting will be unveiled in February of 2027.
