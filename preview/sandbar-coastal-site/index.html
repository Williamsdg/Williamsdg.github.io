What We Do

Built for the real work.

Every job we take on, we take seriously. Here's the work we do best — and the work we'll honestly tell you when we're not the right call.

Sunset Sail · 2hr
Cocktail-hour sail aboard our 28′ cat. Bring your own bottle, we provide ice and the view.
Dolphin Watch
90-minute cruise through Roanoke Sound. We see them on 9 of 10 trips. Calm-water boat, kid-friendly.
Kayak the Sound
Three-hour guided kayak through the marsh and back-bay islands. Lunch at a hidden sandbar.
Wild Horse Beach Tour
Four-wheel-drive trip up to Carova to find the Banker horses. 2.5 hours, small groups, naturalist on board.
Surf Lesson for Beginners
90-minute lesson, soft-top boards included. Most students stand up by the second wave.
Lighthouse Climb
Self-guided history walk and the Bodie Island lighthouse climb. Easy add-on to a beach day.
Our Story

Founded in 2014.

Sandbar opened in 2014 with one Cape Hatteras-built catamaran and a list of friends willing to come along on the test runs. A decade later we run six experiences across the Outer Banks — sunset sails, dolphin watches, kayak tours through the sound, wild horse trips up to Carova, surf lessons for first-timers, and the lighthouse climb at Bodie Island.

Our captains hold USCG Master licenses. Our naturalist guides know the migrations. We run small groups by design — six on a kayak tour, fourteen on a sunset sail. You don't have to fight for the rail.

We don't run when the conditions don't run. If a squall rolls through and we have to push your sunset sail by a day, we'll do it. The water won't apologize, but we will.

— Captain Reed MaddoxUSCG Master 100T · Owner
