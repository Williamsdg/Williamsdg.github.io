What we found

THE SITE ISN'T
SAYING WHAT YOU DO.

01

The strongest material is buried

Four decades, more than a thousand recording, television and motion picture transactions, a UCLA J.D., and the only personal manager who is also an asset protection specialist. Almost none of that is visible before a visitor gives up.

02

Two divisions, one undifferentiated page

Athletes and Talent are genuinely different offerings with different audiences. Today they read as the same page twice.

03

No visual claim to the category

Nothing on the page signals Los Angeles, prestige, or representation. A prospective client cannot tell Timely apart from a booking agency.

04

Two different addresses on record

The Athlete Registration terms give a principal place of business in Albuquerque, New Mexico and specify New Mexico law. The Contact page gives the Howard Hughes Center in Los Angeles. Worth resolving before launch — we have flagged it rather than guessed.

IN THE
