iii. How it works

Four steps from inquiry to the moment you open the gallery.

— 01 —

The conversation

A 20-minute call, in person or on the phone. I'd rather understand the day than sell the package. Free, no calendar pressure.

— 02 —

The plan

You get a written shot plan, timeline, and a single page with everything in it. Half-deposit holds the date. The other half is due a week before.

— 03 —

The day

I'm there early, I stay late, and I disappear into the background. You'll barely notice the camera. That's the whole idea.

— 04 —

The gallery

Sneak peeks within 48 hours. Full edited gallery in 3 weeks (weddings: 5). Delivered through your private Client Studio link — yours forever.

v. Who I am
Self-portrait · 35mm · winter 2025

I'm Jonathan. I make pictures that wait for things.

I've been a working photographer and videographer for eleven years, mostly out of Birmingham, Alabama. I shoot weddings, families, churches, brands, and the live events that bring all four together.

My approach is quiet. I'd rather catch the look between two people than ask them to pose for it. I'd rather show up forty-five minutes early than rush the light. And I'd rather under-promise what a single image can carry than oversell what it can't.

If you want loud, busy, "smile-at-the-camera" coverage — I'm the wrong photographer. If you want something that holds up in twenty years — let's talk.

— Jonathan Goff
