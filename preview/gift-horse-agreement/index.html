1 Scope of Work

Williams Digital will design, build and launch a custom storefront for Gift Horse. The storefront is written from scratch rather than assembled from a Shopify theme, and sits in front of the Client’s existing Shopify store, which continues to run the business behind it.

The build comprises:

Homepage

As shown in the approved preview: hero, new arrivals, gift finder, category tiles, price-band section, the greeting-card wall, shop photography, maker features, staff picks, a Nashville section and store information. Desktop and mobile are laid out separately rather than one being a narrowed version of the other.

Collection pages

A template serving every collection in the Client’s Shopify store, with filtering by product type, price and tag, and sorting. Covers their existing collections including Cards, Prints, Home Goods, Local Artists, Nashville Goods, Staff Picks and Gifts Under $25.

Product pages

A template serving every product, with image gallery, variant/option selection, price, stock state, description, maker attribution and related products.

Cart

Add, remove, adjust quantity, and hand off to Shopify’s hosted checkout.

Search

Full-catalogue search with type-ahead suggestions grouped by product type, and a results view.

Gift Finder

The three-question finder shown in the preview, filtering the live catalogue on product tags and price. No third-party service or subscription is required to run it.

Illustration and lettering system

The bespoke illustration set built for Gift Horse — horse, horseshoe, guitar, star, sparkle, flower, heart, smiley, envelope, arrows, squiggle, starburst, washi tape and circle mark — plus the hand-lettering used for headings and annotations. Delivered as scalable vector files usable beyond the website.

Launch essentials

  • Existing product and collection URLs preserved, with permanent 301 redirects for any URL that changes, and http to https.
  • Page titles and descriptions, structured data, XML sitemap, robots.txt and canonical URLs.
  • Analytics and Google Search Console setup.
  • Built to WCAG 2.2 Level AA, targeting Core Web Vitals in the “good” range.
  • Newsletter capture, connected to the Client’s existing email provider.

2 What Stays in Shopify

Shopify remains the system of record. The Client keeps their Shopify subscription and continues to manage the business exactly as they do now. Nothing is migrated out.

  • Products, variants, images, descriptions, collections and tags
  • Inventory and stock levels
  • Pricing, discount codes and gift cards
  • Customer accounts and order history
  • Taxes and shipping configuration
  • Checkout and payments — the storefront hands off to Shopify’s own hosted checkout, so Shop Pay, saved payment methods, discounts, tax and shipping calculation all continue to work unchanged

Adding, editing or removing products is done in Shopify as usual and appears on the storefront without developer involvement.

3 Shopify Apps & Theme Extensions

This is the single most common source of unexpected work on a project of this kind, so it is set out plainly here.

Many Shopify apps work by injecting code into the Liquid theme. Because this storefront does not use a Liquid theme, those apps will not carry over automatically. This typically affects product reviews, loyalty and rewards, pop-ups, upsell and cross-sell widgets, wishlists, subscriptions, back-in-stock alerts and similar tools.

Before development begins, Williams Digital will inventory every app installed on the Client’s store and provide a written list showing which will continue to work, which need re-integration, and which have no equivalent. Re-integrating an app is quoted separately and only proceeds with the Client’s written approval.

Apps that operate inside the Shopify admin — accounting, shipping labels, inventory management, purchase orders, point of sale — are unaffected, because they never touch the storefront.

4 Content, Photography & Materials

The Client supplies brand artwork, product photography, shop photography and written content. Williams Digital will use the Client’s existing Shopify product data and imagery as the source for all product content.

The Client confirms they own, or hold the necessary rights to use, all photography and artwork supplied for the website, including any images produced by a third-party photographer. Where photography carries a credit or licence condition, the Client will tell Williams Digital what it requires.

Please check before launch: several of the shop photographs supplied are credited to a photographer. Confirmation that these may be used on the website, and whether a credit line is required, is needed before they are published.

Williams Digital will not originate factual claims about the shop — staff names, maker biographies, awards, press mentions, product origins or store history. Any such content is drawn from Client-supplied or Client-published sources and confirmed by the Client before launch. Placeholder names and text used in the preview are for layout only and will be replaced with Client-supplied copy.

Cancelling the monthly service does not cancel the Client’s ownership of the delivered work.
