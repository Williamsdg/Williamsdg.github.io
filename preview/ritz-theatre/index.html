About the Ritz

The Ritz Theatre is a 1927 former silent movie house renovated in 1985 by the Tennessee Valley Art Association to accommodate the organization’s performing arts programs. The original art deco interior style has been preserved.

  • 1927Opens as a silent movie house.
  • 1985Renovated by the Tennessee Valley Art Association. Center Stage Community Theatre moves in.
  • TodayHosts theatrical productions, concerts and films, and is available as a rental space.
