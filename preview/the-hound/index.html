    The Lounge

    Just steps away from our hand-crafted cedar top bar. Comfortable couches and chairs, low top tables made from reclaimed pecan wood, a giant Elk head, and dim lighting. High-top cocktail tables can be added to the space if desired.

    Seats up to 36

    The Private Dining Room

    Reminiscent of the industrial era with exposed brick walls and concrete flooring. Large windows framed by burlap curtains and natural wood tables make the room warm & inviting. A perfect space for seated dinner events or business gatherings.

    50 seated · 75 reception

    The Fox Transformed

    At the rear of the restaurant and distinct from the main dining area. A popular local coffee shop during the day, a sophisticated event venue in the evening. Open rafter ceilings, exposed brick and cement block walls, and distinctive artwork.

    Reservations

    Good to know before you book.

    1. Reservations are available online or over the phone for up to 60 days out.
    2. For any party over 6, please call the restaurant directly.
    3. We hold reservations for 10 minutes past your scheduled time. Please contact us if you are unable to make it.
    4. Depending on volume and the size of your party, we may not be able to seat your group partially. Please try to have all guests present at the time of the reservation.

    A family affair

    Simple, delicious food. Bacon, bourbon, community, and family.

    Owners Matthew Poirier and Jana (Caruthers) Poirier moved back to Auburn, AL with the dream of opening a local watering hole featuring simple, delicious food with a focus on bacon, bourbon, community, and family.

    Jana, her brothers Ben and Brian Caruthers, and parents John and Susan Caruthers (Mama Su), along with almost all extended family, are Auburn alum that have all come together to make The Hound a family affair and new Auburn tradition.

    We feature bacon from heritage breed pork as well as other organic and humanely raised meats.
