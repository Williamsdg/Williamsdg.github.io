FAQs

The practical bits.

Quick answers to the questions every visitor asks before booking.

Do I need a passport to visit The Bahamas?

Yes. All visitors require a valid passport. Many nationalities can enter visa-free for short tourist stays — check current entry requirements through the Bahamas Ministry of Tourism before traveling.

When is the best time to visit?

The Bahamas enjoys 340+ days of sunshine. December through April brings the driest, coolest weather. May through November is warmer with occasional afternoon showers and lower hotel rates. Junkanoo on Boxing Day and New Year's Day is a peak-experience window.

How do I get between islands?

Inter-island flights, private aviation, and scheduled ferries connect the main islands. Most major hotels can arrange transfers. See Island Hopping.

What currency is used?

The Bahamian Dollar (BSD), pegged 1:1 with the US Dollar. US dollars are accepted virtually everywhere.

Is the tap water safe to drink?

Tap water is generally safe in Nassau, Paradise Island, and Freeport. On smaller islands, bottled water is widely available and recommended.

What should I pack?

Light clothing, swimsuits, reef-safe sunscreen, sunglasses, a light layer for evenings, and good water shoes if you plan to snorkel. A light rain shell in summer.
