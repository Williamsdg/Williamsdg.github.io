Events & Festivals

Junkanoo. Living history at its very best.

Months of work and preparation result in an irresistible burst of colour, music, and dance. The main events take place on Boxing Day and New Year's Day — the whole country dancing, Bahamian joy embodied.

26December · Boxing Day

The Main Event

Bay Street fills with thousands of costumed performers, drums, brass, and dance. Months of preparation, one unforgettable night.
01January · New Year's Day

A Second Burst

An encore parade that pulls the country into the new year on the rhythm of cowbells and goombay drums.
365Days a Year

Year-Round Festivals
