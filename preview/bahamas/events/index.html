Events & Festivals

Living history at its very best.

Junkanoo on Boxing Day and New Year's Day — the whole country dancing. Plus year-round regattas, seafood festivals, and homecomings that turn every island into a party.

the heartbeat of the Bahamas Junkanoo

Junkanoo. Bahamian joy embodied.

Months of work and preparation result in an irresistible burst of colour, music, and dance. The main events take place on Boxing Day and New Year's Day — and every Bahamian on Bay Street is in costume, in motion, or both.

Cowbells. Goombay drums. Brass bands. Tens of thousands of costumed performers parading through Nassau into the small hours. It's living history at its very best.
