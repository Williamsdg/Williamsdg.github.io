Embrace Bahamian Culture

True Bahamian spirit.

The Bahamas isn't just a destination — it's a feeling you carry home. Music, food, art, faith, family. The whole country dancing.

straight from the sea Fresh Flavours

The flavor of home.

Conch fritters fried golden and served with hot sauce. Cracked conch with peas 'n rice. Caught-this-morning lobster grilled at a roadside fish fry. Eleuthera pineapples so sweet they barely need a knife. And island rum poured generous.

Eat where the locals eat. The best meals in the Bahamas don't come with menus.

From the Studio

"Galleries, artist studios, craft markets across every island."

art imitates life Galleries & Craft

Art imitates life.

The National Art Gallery of the Bahamas in Nassau. Working artist studios in Harbour Island. Junkanoo costume makers stitching feathers and beads in basements year-round. Craft markets selling straw work, conch jewelry, and Androsia batik.

Every island has its own creative current. Follow it.

connect with a local People-to-People

Meet a Bahamian.

People-to-People is a program as old as the islands themselves. Connect with a local family, share a meal, get the version of The Bahamas that doesn't show up in guidebooks. A Sunday dinner. A church choir. A fishing day. A new friend.

It's free. It's mutual. And it's the part of the trip you'll remember longest.

Mt. Alvernia · Cat Island

"The highest point in The Bahamas."

go higher Reach New Heights

Climb. Kite. Surf.

Mt. Alvernia on Cat Island is the highest point in The Bahamas. The Queen's Staircase in Nassau is 66 steps cut from solid limestone. Cat Island's east shore serves up some of the best wind and waves in the chain — kiteboarding, surfing, paddling.

If you came here to slow down, fine. If you came here to climb something — there's a mountain waiting.
