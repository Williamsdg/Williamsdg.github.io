Experiences

Find your island rhythm.

Adventures unfold in the blink of an eye. From swimming with pigs in The Exumas to meeting flamingos in Inagua — the Bahamas is built for the kind of stories you'll tell for the rest of your life.

Signature Experiences

The four you've been dreaming about.

Some experiences exist nowhere else on earth. These are the ones you came to The Bahamas for.

No. 01
The Exumas

Swim with the Pigs

On Big Major Cay, friendly Bahamian pigs paddle out into turquoise water to greet you. There's nothing else like it on earth — and yes, it's exactly as charming as it looks.

See how
No. 02
Inagua

Meet a Flamingo

Three national parks. Over 80,000 flamingos and 140 species of birds. Inagua is the largest breeding colony of West Indian flamingos in the world — and the most pink place you'll ever stand.

See how
No. 03
Bimini · Andros · Long Island

Diving & Snorkeling

Dean's Blue Hole — the world's second-deepest blue hole. The Andros Barrier Reef. Wrecks, dolphins, coral gardens, and underwater caves. The Bahamas was made for divers.

See how
No. 04
Long Island · Bimini · Ragged Island

World-Class Fishing

Bonefishing on shallow flats. Deep-sea charters chasing marlin. Tournament fishing in Bimini's Gulf Stream. Whatever you fish for, it's bigger here.

See how
