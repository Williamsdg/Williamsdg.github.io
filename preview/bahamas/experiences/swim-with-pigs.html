Swim with the Pigs.

On Big Major Cay, friendly Bahamian pigs paddle out into turquoise water to greet you. There's nothing else like it on earth — and yes, it's exactly as charming as it looks.

The most famous pigs in the world.

No one knows for sure how they got there. Some say they swam ashore from a shipwreck. Some say sailors left them as a future meal that was never collected. Either way, the pigs of Big Major Cay have made themselves right at home — paddling out from the beach to meet every boat that anchors in the bay.

"There's nothing else like it on earth."

You'll come by boat from Staniel Cay or Great Exuma — a quick run across some of the clearest water you've ever seen. As you anchor, the pigs notice. They wade in, then swim out, expecting snacks and a swim.

Photographers come for the shot. Families come for the story. And everyone leaves a little bit changed by the strangest, most charming wildlife encounter in the Caribbean.

How to Do It

Four steps. One unforgettable afternoon.

Easier than you think. Most travelers do it on a half-day boat tour out of Staniel Cay or Great Exuma.

01

Fly to Exuma

Daily flights into GGT (Exuma International) from Nassau and Florida.

02

Book a Boat Day

Half-day charters depart Staniel Cay and Great Exuma every morning.

03

Swim with the Pigs

Wade out, swim alongside, take the photo of a lifetime. Be respectful — they're wild animals.

04

Combine the Day

Most tours pair pigs with Thunderball Grotto, Compass Cay, and the swimming iguanas.
