Getting Here

Closer than you think.

From the Florida coast, you're just a short flight, ferry, or sail away. Every island, every way — commercial flights, charter flights, private aviation, cruise lines, and pleasure craft.

wheels up Flying

Direct in under an hour.

Commercial and charter flights into 30+ airports across the islands. Nassau (NAS) is the main hub, 55 minutes from Miami. Freeport (FPO) is even closer — just 40 minutes from Florida. Out island airports across the chain handle smaller hops, often on short turboprops with bigger windows than seats.

stepping onto the dock Cruise

The slow way in.

Major cruise lines call on Nassau, Freeport, and a growing list of private cays. It's the easiest way to sample several islands in one trip — and the views from the deck on approach are worth the price of the ticket.

Nassau PortFreeport PortPrivate Cays
your own captain Boating

Chart your own course.

The Bahamas is a pleasure-craft paradise — close enough to Florida that you can sail across in a day. Modern marinas, Boating Ambassadors who'll help you plan, and the annual Boating Fling flotilla make it easier than ever to skipper your own trip.

a different island every day Island Hopping

Inter-island, effortless.

Private aviation, charter flights, and inter-island ferries make moving between islands genuinely easy. Pig Beach in the morning. Pink Sands by sunset. The Bahamas is built for travelers who can't decide.
