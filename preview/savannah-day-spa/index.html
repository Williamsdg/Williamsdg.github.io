Spa Etiquette

  • Please arrive 15 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment to allow time to relax and prepare.
  • A 24-hour cancellation notice is appreciated to avoid charges.
  • Late arrivals may result in shortened treatment time so subsequent guests are not delayed.
  • Please silence mobile devices upon entering the spa to preserve a tranquil environment.
  • Gratuity is not included in service prices and is always appreciated.

Career Opportunities

We're always looking for licensed massage therapists, estheticians, and front-of-house team members who share our standards of care. To inquire about open positions, send your résumé to reception@savannahdayspa.com or call (912) 234-9100.
