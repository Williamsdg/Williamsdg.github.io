How to submit a new product

Five minutes per product — phone photos are perfectly fine
1
Make a folder for the product inside Product Uploads

One folder per product, named after the product — e.g. Tiger Stripe Gameday Tee. Keep every photo for that product inside its folder.

2
Add the photos

Front, back, and a detail shot is the sweet spot. Number them so the main photo comes first: 1-front.jpg, 2-back.jpg, 3-detail.jpg. Square-ish photos in good natural light look best on the store grid.

3
Include the product info

Add a note in the folder (a Google Doc, or just name a text file) — or text it — with:

Product name (as it should read on the store)
Price
Sizes carried (S / M / L / XL…) — or "one size"
How many of each size
One or two sentences describing it
4
Williams Digital stages it in Shopify

Photos, sizes, prices, and inventory counts get loaded as a draft — nothing shows on the store yet.

5
Review & publish

Once the draft looks right, it gets flipped to live. Heads up: quantities loaded here track Shopify's inventory — in-person Square sales at the trailer won't update them automatically, so treat one system as the source of truth on gameday.
