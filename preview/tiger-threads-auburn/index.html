Home Parties

Want the trailer experience inside your living room? Host a Tiger Threads Home Party and we'll bring a curated rolling rack, jewelry case, and a hand-picked selection straight to your front door.

  • Personal styling for you and your guests
  • Curated mix of new arrivals + vintage one-of-a-kinds

  • Free for hostess to book — minimum 6 guests

How It Works

Four steps from idea to party.

From bridal showers and birthdays to teacher appreciation and book club nights — a Tiger Threads Home Party turns shopping into the main event.

  1. Pick a date We'll confirm availability and lock it in — typically 3+ weeks out.
  2. Tell us your vibe Game day, bridal, holiday, gift-focused — we'll tailor the rack to your group.
  3. Invite your people We send you a digital invite kit. You bring the snacks, we bring the boutique.
  4. Shop, sip, share Personal styling, jewelry try-ons, easy checkout — you earn store credit on every sale.
