Rental Policies & House Rules

Please read carefully — "the fine print"

Check-In / Check-Out

  • Check-in is after 4:00 PM CST. If the unit is vacant and an early check-in is available, we'll let you know the day before arrival.
  • Check-out is 10:00 AM CST firm. Your key code stops working at checkout time. If the housekeeper arrives and the premises are occupied, a $50 charge applies, plus $50 per additional half hour.

Damage Deposit ($250)

The deposit is not applied toward rent. It is fully refunded within 14 days of departure, provided:

  • No damage beyond normal wear and tear, and all RFID armbands are returned to their dock. $50 per missing or swapped wristband.
  • NO smoking in the unit — smoking results in complete loss of the deposit.
  • No charges incurred for goods/services before, during, or after your stay (including extra cleaning).
  • All garbage removed and placed in proper receptacles.
  • All dirty dishes placed in the dishwasher and started before departure.
  • No linens lost or damaged — bring your own beach towels; linens/towels may not leave the unit.
  • Comforters/sheets not stained by food, drink, or sunscreen — this is a full deposit loss. Do not eat or drink in or around beds.
  • iPod left in its dock — $50 to replace if lost.
  • Turn A/C off when patio doors are open (running A/C with doors open can freeze the unit; maintenance calls are charged).
  • Pack-n-Play repacked & clean — $20 if left dirty or unpacked.
  • Beach cart, chairs, umbrella & anchor cleaned and stowed — $25 if left dirty or improperly stowed.
  • Floats/toys deflated & stored in the patio box — $10 per item not properly stored. (If you can't agree, simply don't use them.)

If property damage exceeds the deposit, the guest is responsible for the full cost to restore the unit, plus any legal fees. Legal proceedings shall be filed in Okaloosa County, Florida.

Age Requirement

No rentals to anyone under age 25. Reservations made under false pretenses will be evicted with no refund.

Rent Payment

Full rent is due one month prior to arrival. Reminders are not sent. If rent isn't received 30 days prior, the deposit is forfeited and dates are released.

Payment Types

Payment by e-check (fast, free, secure). Credit card is a last resort with a non-refundable 5% surcharge. For reservations within 30 days of arrival, immediate payment & contract are required.

Cancellations & Changes

  • More than 60 days' notice: Deposit & rent returned, less a $100 administrative fee.
  • 30–60 days: Full deposit lost; any paid rent refunded.
  • Less than 30 days: Full deposit & rent lost. We'll attempt to re-rent; if successful, rent is returned (less any discount required).
  • Cancellations/early departures due to weather or beach conditions do not warrant a refund.

Pets, Phone & Access

  • Pets are prohibited on all Waterscape property and illegal on the beaches. HOA fines apply; guests may be evicted with no refund.
  • There is no phone in the unit — bring a personal cell phone.
  • Guest shall allow the homeowner reasonable access for repair & inspection.
  • Lock your car and secure valuables at pools and beaches.

House Rules

  • Wear your armbands at all times — they grant access at the gates.
  • No glass on the beach or by the pools — Florida law, subject to a $500 fine.
  • No food or drinks in the bedrooms. Staining comforters, shams, or mattresses means full loss of the deposit.
  • Pools open 8 AM–10 PM. No reserving chairs with towels. No drinks within 4 feet of the pool or hot tub.

Maximum Occupancy

Occupancy is 6 guests, including infants. Armbands match the party list provided. Armbands are never issued beyond occupancy — even for daytime visitors. Exceeding occupancy means eviction with no refund. No-tolerance policy.

Hurricane / Storm Policy

No refunds unless the National Weather Service orders a mandatory evacuation for our specific area. In a Hurricane Warning area under mandatory evacuation, unused rent for affected nights is refunded.

Liability & Indemnification

Owners/property manager are not responsible for accidents, injuries, illness, or loss of belongings. By accepting this reservation, all guests assume the risk of harm or theft and agree to release, defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the owners/manager from any claims, including legal costs and attorney's fees.

Unavailability

If a condo becomes uninhabitable (fire, flood, A/C/utility failure, etc.), we'll relocate guests to comparable accommodations or refund unused nights — your choice. No refund will exceed the amount paid; no refunds for flights, rental cars, or trip expenses. Trip insurance is recommended (compare at InsureMyTrip.com).

No Assignment or Subletting

The renter may not assign or sublet. The contract holder must be present for the entire stay, and all guests must be named on the contract. Breach results in eviction with no refund.

Severability

If any provision is found invalid, the remainder of the agreement remains enforceable to the fullest extent permitted by law.
