The Connected Workflow

Watch a pothole travel through the whole system

Report it in the resident app, and it appears on the staff work-order board. Assign a crew, and the resident gets a push notification. That loop is what cuts the phone calls.

📱 Resident reports it→ 🏛 Lands on the Command Center→ 👷 Assigned to a crew→ 🔄 Status updates flow back→ ✅ Resident notified — with photo proof
