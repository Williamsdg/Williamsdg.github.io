Experiences at The Lonely Broomstick

One hour in a wizarding classroom.

For over three years, The Lonely Broomstick has offered one of the most enchanting experiences in the world. Brew a potion in a real cauldron, or let a handcrafted wand choose you. Both run for one hour in rooms meticulously designed to feel just like the real thing.

The Potion-Making Experience

One hour, £15 a person, every Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

An immersive Potion-Making Experience that transports you straight into a wizarding classroom, where you create your very own magical potion. Step into our meticulously designed potion room, crafted to feel just like the real thing.

  • Brew your potion in a real cast-iron cauldron, choosing from hundreds of mystical ingredients.
  • Master wand movements and spells.
  • Brave the challenge of uprooting a screaming mandrake. Earmuffs required.
  • Interactive challenges: answer questions to earn points for your house.
Book the Potion Experience

The Wand Experience

One hour, £19.99, and the wand that chooses you is yours to keep.

Every wand holds its own story, with a unique wood type, magical core and length. In this experience you choose three wand boxes by only looking at these attributes. Trust your instincts as you select, swish and flick each wand you summon, feeling the connection grow stronger with every movement.

At the end of your journey, the wand that feels truly yours is yours to keep: a one-of-a-kind magical artifact, handcrafted in Scotland by the talented Shadowthorn Wandmaker. Each wand is individually crafted, so no two are alike, with their own hidden character and charm.

This immersive one-hour experience invites you into the ancient tradition of wandlore, where the right wand doesn't just find you; it chooses you.

Deep, insightful tarot readings for love, career and life decisions. Pendulum divination for precise yes or no answers. Oracle card readings for spiritual guidance and affirmations. And a warm, welcoming energy to help you feel at ease.
