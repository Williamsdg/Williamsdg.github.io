The Potion-Making Experience

One hour, £15 a person, every Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

An immersive adventure that transports you straight into a wizarding classroom, where you create your very own magical potion.

  • Brew your potion in a real cast-iron cauldron, choosing from hundreds of mystical ingredients.
  • Master wand movements and spells.
  • Brave the challenge of uprooting a screaming mandrake. Earmuffs required.
  • Answer questions in interactive challenges to earn points for your house.

The Wand Experience

One hour, £19.99, and the wand that chooses you is yours to keep.

Every wand holds its own story, with a unique wood type, magical core and length. Choose three wand boxes by those attributes alone, then select, swish and flick each wand you summon and feel the connection grow stronger with every movement.

The wand that feels truly yours is yours to keep: a one-of-a-kind magical artifact, handcrafted in Scotland by the talented Shadowthorn Wandmaker, so no two are alike.

The Scottish Ghost Emporium

Adopt a Scottish ghost, if you dare.

Restless spirits are searching for a new home. Will yours be next?

Each of our handcrafted Scottish ghosts carries a haunting tale of their untimely demise. Some tragic, some mysterious, but all forever bound to the echoes of the past. No two spirits are alike, making each one a unique and eerie companion.

Every ghost comes with an official certificate detailing its name, birth date, death date, and the chilling story of how it met its fate. Each has the official Original Scottish Ghost stamp on the back, and they are available for worldwide adoption.

Questions, answered.

Do you ship worldwide?

We ship to the majority of countries around the world. UK shipping is tracked and free. International orders are charged at £8.99 per order.

Where is my order?

We dispatch all orders on Tuesdays and Fridays, within 3 to 5 working days. Once dispatched, UK orders generally take 2 to 3 working days to arrive and international orders usually take 1 to 2 weeks depending on customs. Customs fees are not included in your order and may be charged on entry into your country.

Orders are tracked. Once dispatched you will receive an email with your dispatch notification and tracking information.

I have a question about my order

For any enquiries regarding your order, or just in general, email hello@lonelybroomstick.co.uk, call the shop on 01324 627829, or message us on Facebook or Instagram.

Do you wholesale your products?

We don't just stock magical products, we create them. We stock over 100 magical shops around the world, and we wholesale our collections exclusively on Faire. Sign up using our link to start ordering our magical stock for your shop.

Where is your shop, and when are you open?

The Lonely Broomstick and our sister shop Whimsic Alley are both on Falkirk High Street in beautiful Scotland, at 36 High Street, Falkirk, FK1 1EU. We are open 9am to 5pm Monday to Friday, and 10am to 6pm on Saturday and Sunday.

When does the Potion Experience run?

Our Potion Experience is available every Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Book online to choose your time.
