Classes at Studio 103

Anthe has taught since 1995 — children, adults, and a great many people who had not drawn since school. Classes run in the gallery itself, surrounded by work in progress.

01

Drawing from Music

A warm-up that releases spontaneous, intuitive mark-making onto the page.

Students are introduced to a deliberately wide range of musical genres — the music is the catalyst. It sets mood and movement, and the hand follows before the head can interfere. It is the fastest way past the fear of the blank page that anyone has found, and it is where Anthe's own paintings begin.

  • 2 hours
  • All levels
  • No experience needed
  • Materials provided

02

Drawing for All Ages

Fundamentals in the right order: seeing first, then line, then form.

Children and adults work side by side, each at their own level — which sounds unlikely and turns out to be the best thing about the room. Grandparents come with grandchildren. Nobody is the only beginner.

  • 2 hours
  • Ages 8 to adult
  • Materials provided
Register interest
03

Collage & Mixed Media

Composition without a single drawn line.

Torn paper, found texture, layered surface. You build the image edge by edge rather than drawing it — which teaches shape, value and colour relationships faster than a pencil does, because you cannot fudge an edge you have to tear.

  • 3½ hours
  • All levels
  • Materials provided
Register interest
04

Painting on Glass

A surface that holds light instead of absorbing it.

Colour behaves differently on glass — it stays transparent, it stacks, and it will not forgive a muddy mix. The class starts exactly there, and you leave with a finished piece that changes through the day as the light moves.

  • 2½ hours
  • All levels
  • Glass provided
Register interest
05

Painting on Clothing

Take home something you will actually wear.

Fabric technique, colour that survives a wash, and one finished garment. Bring a piece you love and have stopped wearing — it usually comes back out of the wardrobe afterwards.

  • 3 hours
  • All levels
  • Bring a garment

06

Private & Small Group

One to one, or a small group, in your medium and at your pace.

Anthe has taught privately since 1995 across drawing, acrylic, watercolour, collage, printmaking, glass and metal. Good for someone returning to work they set down years ago, and good for a group who would rather have the room to themselves.

  • By arrangement
  • Any medium
  • Groups welcome
