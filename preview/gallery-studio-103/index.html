The studio

It started with cardboard.

“As a little girl, I couldn’t wait for my father’s laundered shirts to arrive — not because of the shirts, but because of the crisp cardboard inserts tucked inside. Those blank rectangles were my first canvases.”

She grew up singing and dancing, and that is still where the work comes from. Whether the piece is bold acrylic, luminous watercolour or torn paper, it carries movement first and subject second — a line set down before the thing it describes.

A graduate of the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts and an award winner in drawing, collage, mixed media, acrylic, watercolour and oil, Anthe has been teaching since 1995. At Gallery Studio 103 she paints, teaches and mentors in the same room.

“Watching a student discover their own creative voice — that moment of breakthrough and delight — fills me just as much as finishing a piece of my own.”

Learn

Six ways in.

No experience assumed, ever. Most people who walk in have not drawn since school — that is the room these classes are built for.

01

Drawing from Music

A warm-up that releases spontaneous, intuitive mark-making onto the page. A wide range of musical genres becomes the catalyst — mood and movement first, uninhibited expression after.

2 hoursAll levels
02

Drawing for All Ages

Fundamentals in the right order: seeing first, then line, then form. Children and adults work side by side, each at their own level.

2 hoursAges 8 to adult
03

Collage & Mixed Media

Torn paper, found texture and layered surface. Composition without a single drawn line — building an image edge by edge.

3½ hoursAll levels
04

Painting on Glass

A surface that holds light instead of absorbing it. Colour behaves differently on glass, and the class starts exactly there.

2½ hoursAll levels
05

Painting on Clothing

Take home something you will actually wear. Fabric technique, colour that survives a wash, one finished garment.

3 hoursAll levels
06

Private & Small Group

One to one, or a small group, in your medium and at your pace. Anthe has taught privately since 1995.

Behind the site

Five hundred pieces, one row each.

Your photographs live in Photos. Your descriptions live on certificates. Your inventory is becoming a spreadsheet. None of that has to change.

This site is built to read one table — the same shape as the spreadsheet you are already making. Add a row and the piece appears. Mark it sold and it comes off the wall. Print its certificate from that same row. No re-entering anything anywhere else, and no per-piece fee for adding work.
