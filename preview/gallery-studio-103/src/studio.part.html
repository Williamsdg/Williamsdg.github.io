What this is

One table. Everything else reads from it.

You said your photos and descriptions sit in Photos and on certificates, and that the inventory is becoming a spreadsheet. This is that spreadsheet, kept where it already is — with the website reading from it instead of asking you to type everything a second time.

Add a row and the piece appears on the site. Attach a photograph and it joins the collection. Change the status to sold and it comes off the wall by itself. Print the certificate from the same row. There is nothing to publish and nothing to pay to add a painting.
