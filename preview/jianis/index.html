The essence of the pizza is all in the dough.

To make the best pizza we take this seriously. Our dough is freshly made using a combination of the finest Italian flours, pre-fermented for 48 hours, giving each pizza the perfect airy crust, full of fragrance and texture and easy to digest.

No additives, no purée.

Our homemade sauce is made from San Marzano tomatoes D.O.P with fresh and fragrant basil, a drizzle of Italian extra virgin olive oil and a touch of salt. That is why our sauce is always fresh and juicy.

All our toppings are locally sourced and completely meat free, including our fior di latte.

450 degrees. 90 seconds.

Every pizza is stone baked with temperatures reaching 450 degrees and cooked in 90 seconds, delivering a flavoursome and satisfying pizza.

I mean, who doesn't love pizza!

Hi, my name is Ronnie B, founder of Jiani's in Stanmore, North West London. Along with my wife we serve freshly made stone-baked Neapolitan style pizzas and mouth-watering tiramisus.

During lockdown I was roped into making vegetarian pizzas for the family using ready-made dough, which clearly didn't live up to my standards. There my obsession began: to create the perfect Neapolitan pizza using vegetarian ingredients. After some extensive research and inspiration from professional pizzaiolos, I finally created the taste of Naples in my home and was able to bring the real experience to the local community.

The only caveat was that it had to be easily digestible, which is why we make a 48 hour pre-fermented dough using a combination of the finest Italian flours.

My only request is for you to share these pizzas with your family and friends and make your event a truly memorable one!

Passion for flavoursome food runs in our family.
