Town HallWhat hours is Town Hall open?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM, at 21 Main Street. Phone (205) 489-5447, fax (205) 489-3529.

StreetsWhen is limb pickup?

The first Monday of each month. Call (205) 489-5447 to request a pickup. Household items are not accepted.

CourtWhen is Municipal Court held?

The third Wednesday of each month at 3:00 PM at 21 Main Street. If a Double Springs police officer wrote your citation, the case is on this docket. You can pay many fines online without coming in.

FireWhat is the fire fee and when is it due?

Double Springs Fire and Rescue sends a yearly fire fee notice in July. It is $60, due August 1 and delinquent October 1. The money stays with the department and buys equipment. Outfitting one firefighter with boots, pants, coat and helmet costs over $2,000.

WaterHow could I have used this much water?

You may not have. The meter numbers may have been transposed or hard to read, or a toilet or faucet may be leaking where you cannot see it. Call the office and they will work through it with you.

WaterMy bill is past due. Can I get an extension?

Contact the office. A one-time extension is available for extenuating circumstances.

WaterWhat do I do about low water pressure?

Check your meter and the area around it for leaks, then call the office and report low pressure for your area.

WaterWhy is my water discolored?

A recent repair can let air into the line, which gives the water a milky look.

WaterWhy do I have a previous balance when I sent my payment?

It may have arrived after the due date, or not at all. Call the office and they will help sort it out. If you pay by mail, send it five to seven business days before the due date.

LibraryHow do I get a library card?

Apply in person at the Double Springs Public Library in the Community Center Complex. The first card is free for residents of Winston or Walker County, and you get it the same day. A replacement card is $5. Anyone over 15 needs ID showing a current home address.

LibraryCan I use a computer at the library?

Yes. There are three computer terminals with wi-fi. You need a valid library card and to have read the internet policy. Use is free; printing has a per-page fee.

SeniorsWhat does the Senior Center offer?

Open 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM, Monday to Friday, for ages 60 and older. Lunch is at 11:30. Homebound meals are available to qualifying participants; sign up at the center. Director Joanie Elliott, (205) 489-5479.

DaycareWhat does the Seymour Bevill Daycare Center cost?

$110 a week. Pre-K is $10 a week. Before or after school care is $45 a week, or $60 for both. Open 6:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Director Susie Brewer, (205) 489-3267.

ParksHow do I book the gym or a pavilion?

Call Parks and Recreation at (205) 489-2291. Mike Gilbreath Memorial Park has a walking track, a playground, covered pavilions and a tennis and basketball court that is first come, first served.

JobsHow do I apply for a job with the town?

Fill out an application and mail it or drop it off at Town Hall. You can attach a resume. The town does hire part-time employees and is an Equal Opportunity Employer.

AnimalsWho do I call about animal control?

Call (205) 489-2115.

PoliceHow do I reach the police when it is not an emergency?

Call (205) 489-5447 during the day or the Sheriff's dispatch at (205) 489-2115. For an emergency, dial 911.

Visit Double Springs

Seat of the Free State of Winston.

A sandstone courthouse square inside the William B. Bankhead National Forest, four miles from Smith Lake.

The county that asked to be left out of the war.

In 1861 the hill farmers of Winston County wanted no part of secession. Their delegate, a 22-year-old schoolteacher named Christopher Sheats, refused to sign the ordinance. At a meeting at Looney's Tavern, traditionally dated July 4, 1861, Unionists passed resolutions asking to stay neutral. People have called it the Free State of Winston ever since.

Dual Destiny, the bronze in front of the courthouse, tells the story in one figure: a single soldier, half Union and half Confederate. Branko Medenica sculpted it, and it has stood on the square since 1987.

The sandstone courthouse behind it dates to the 1890s and has been on the National Register of Historic Places since 1987. Double Springs has been the county seat since 1883.

Yellow Jackets football

All around town

Bankhead National Forest

181,230 acres, with the ranger district office here in town. It holds the Sipsey Wilderness, the largest in Alabama, known as the Land of 1000 Waterfalls.

Four miles out

Lewis Smith Lake

More than 500 miles of shoreline and the deepest water in Alabama. Corinth Recreation Area has a campground, a swim beach and a boat launch.

East on US 278

The Houston Jail

Built in 1868 from squared logs, and the only log jail known to survive in Alabama. Houston was the county seat before Double Springs.
