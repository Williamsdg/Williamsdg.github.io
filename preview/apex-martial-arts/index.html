Before You Start

Common Questions

Do I need any experience to start?
None at all. The vast majority of our members walked in with zero martial arts background. Every class has a beginner-friendly track, and your first week is free so you can try with no pressure.
Is martial arts safe for my child?
Absolutely. Our kids' classes emphasize control, respect, and supervised technique — not getting hurt. Coaches are background-checked, and we keep age and skill groups separate so kids progress safely.
I'm out of shape. Will I keep up?
Yes. You go at your own pace and build up. Our fitness-focused classes meet you where you are — many members start specifically to get in shape, and the community keeps you accountable.
What should I bring to my first class?
Just comfortable workout clothes and water. We provide loaner gear for your trial, and we'll set you up with a uniform when you join.
Am I locked into a long contract?
No long-term contracts. Memberships are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime. We'd rather earn your membership every month than trap you in one.
