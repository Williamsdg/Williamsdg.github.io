Our Story

A recipe that came full circle.

Full Circle started with a single recipe card — handwritten by a grandmother in 1967, folded into a cookbook, and forgotten for forty years. When we found it tucked between two cake pans, the corners were dusted with cinnamon and the ink had bled into the page.

We've spent the last two years rebuilding that recipe from scratch. The yeast rises slow. The dough rests overnight. The glaze pours warm. Nothing about it is fast — and that's the point.

Every doughnut we sell starts the same way it did sixty years ago: with patience, real butter, and the belief that the best things are worth waiting for.

How We Bake

Three steps. Twenty hours.

01

Slow ferment

The dough rests overnight in a cool proof box — eighteen hours, no shortcuts. That's where the flavor lives.

02

Hand cut & fried

Every ring is cut by hand and fried in small batches at 360°F. We pull them out the moment they go golden.

03

Glazed warm

Glaze goes on while the doughnut is still warm — that's what gives you that crackling, just-this-side-of-melted finish.

FAQ

Common questions.

If yours isn't here, send a note to hello@fullcircledoughnuts.com — we usually reply by the next morning's bake.

When do you open each morning?

The case is full at 6:30 AM, Monday through Saturday. We typically sell out by 11 AM on weekdays and earlier on Saturdays. If you have a flavor you can't live without, get there early.

Can I pre-order or reserve doughnuts?

For orders of one dozen or more, yes — just give us 24 hours' notice. Email catering@fullcircledoughnuts.com or call the shop. We don't take individual holds on the daily case.

Do you offer gluten-free or vegan options?

We bake one rotating vegan cake doughnut every day. Gluten-free is harder — we share a fryer with our wheat dough, so we can't guarantee a true GF environment. We're working on it.

Where do you source coffee?

From friends at Filter and Domestique — both local Birmingham roasters. We rotate the espresso seasonally and offer two single-origin pour-overs on the bar.

Will the recipe ever change?

The Original never. The Saturday Drop always. Everything in between is on the table.
