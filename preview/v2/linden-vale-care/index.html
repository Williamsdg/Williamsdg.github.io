      Your first visit, step by step

      No surprises. Here is what the first hour looks like, from choosing a time to heading home.

      1. Before you come

        Choose a type of visit and a time. You get a short list of what to bring, written for that kind of visit.

      2. When you arrive

        New patients arrive 15 minutes early. The front desk checks your photo ID and insurance card and hands you a short paperwork packet.

      3. In the room

        Your clinician starts by asking what matters most to you today. You're welcome to bring someone with you.

      4. Before you leave

        You leave with the plan written down and, if you need one, a follow-up time.

      Coming in for a behavioral-health intake?

      It's a conversation, not a test. You can share as much or as little as you like, and you can pause at any point. This website never asks about your health.
