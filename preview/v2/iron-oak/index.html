        One room that changes its shirt three times a day.

        Iron Oak is a corner storefront on Example St. The idea is plain: a neighborhood room should earn its keep all day, not just at one rush.

        The espresso machine warms up first. By eleven the flat-top is going and the pastry case has turned into a sandwich line. When the light goes, the chairs turn toward a small stage and the taps take over.

        We print our own posters, write the board by hand, and keep the menus short so each one can be done well. That’s the whole pitch.
