        Construction journal

        Short dated notes from site, kept for the file. Sample entries for No. 24.

        1. Fri 11 Sep 2026 Roofing

          Fascia steel on site

          Twenty-two lengths of blackened steel fascia arrived and were laid out against the shop drawings. Two go back to the fabricator for a corrected corner miter; the rest go up Monday.

          Wes, site superintendent
        2. Fri 4 Sep 2026 Roofing

          Low roofs dried in

          Membrane finished over the living pavilion and garage ahead of Thursday's rain. Every roof drain walked and water-tested with the architect's representative.

          Wes, site superintendent
        3. Fri 28 Aug 2026 Envelope

          Limestone washed down

          Masons finished pointing the east wing and washed the wall. The sample panel stays on site as the colour reference through finishes.

          Wes, site superintendent
        4. Fri 21 Aug 2026 Framing

          Framing inspection closed

          Two small notes from the inspector — a missing strap at the study header and a blocking detail — both corrected and signed off the same afternoon.

          Wes, site superintendent

        Friday Letter · No. 31

        Friday, 11 September 2026

        Carter and Annie —

        A steady week. The low roofs are dry, so the house can take a hard rain without anyone losing sleep, and the steel fascia arrived Thursday. Two pieces are going back for a better corner. I'd rather wait four days than look at a poor miter for forty years.

        Next week is about the edges: fascia goes up Monday through Wednesday, then the copper crew measures the upper roof on Friday.

        Wes

        This week

        • Low roofs dried in before the rain
        • Fascia steel delivered and checked
        • East wing limestone washed down

        Needs your eye

        Exterior sconce finish — both samples are in the site office. A choice by Friday 18 September keeps the electrician on schedule.
