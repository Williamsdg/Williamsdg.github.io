Conference OS / What this demonstrates

Who it's for: conference operations staff running a full Saturday football slate from one room.

The problem: weather, crew conflicts and broadcast gaps arrive by phone and text; decisions — and who was told — live in people's heads.

What it shows: an urgency-ranked exception queue, policy-guided incident response, crew conflict resolution, and an audit trail of every decision.

Simulated: sample Saturday, deterministic data, no real games, officials, networks or messages. Time moves only when you press “Advance 15 min”.

Disclosure: Conference OS is a concept product demo. The Kestrel Plains Conference, every school, venue, official, staff member, score and weather reading shown here is fictional sample data. Nothing is connected to real games, weather services, broadcasters or messaging — actions are saved in this browser only.
