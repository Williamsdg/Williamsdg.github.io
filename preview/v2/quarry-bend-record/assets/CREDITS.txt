The Quarry Bend Record — image credits
======================================

No photographs are used on this concept.

Every illustration is an original hand-authored SVG engraving in assets/cuts.js,
drawn for this project by Williams Digital:

  cut-bridge      The Ash Street span
  cut-quarry      The north pit high wall
  cut-schoolhouse Pell Valley Middle School
  cut-creek       Tannery Creek flood bands (schematic map)
  cut-runner      The last rise at Ridge Farm
  cut-awning      The depot platform

This is deliberate. The Record is a fictional newspaper carrying invented
headlines, so no real photograph should ever appear beneath one. Nothing here
is licensed from a stock library and nothing needs attribution.
