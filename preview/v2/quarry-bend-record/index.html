How this front page works

The Record files the same stories once and lays them out three ways. Broadsheet is the full page, with a lead story, a rail and section columns. Digest is the seven-item morning catch-up, each story with a plain line on why it matters. Wire strips the design away and lists everything filed today, newest first. The reader picks; the newsroom does not file three times.

Every illustration here is a drawn engraving rather than a photograph. On a concept newspaper that is deliberate — no real photograph should ever sit beneath an invented headline.

Sample form. Nothing is sent anywhere — the confirmation below is written by this page, in this browser.

The Quarry Bend Record is not a real newspaper. It is an original concept built to show how a local newsroom site could be designed and built. The town, the staff names, the stories, the figures and the forecast are all invented. No address, telephone number or inbox on this page reaches anyone.
