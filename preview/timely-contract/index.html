4. Ownership & Rights

Upon receipt of final payment, Timely Entertainment, LLC owns all rights to the website design, content, and code developed under this agreement, including the WordPress theme files. Williams Digital retains the right to showcase the project in its portfolio and marketing materials.

5. Revisions & Support

This agreement includes up to two rounds of revisions during the design phase. Post-launch support is included for 30 days to address any bugs, configuration issues, or questions. Additional revisions or ongoing support can be arranged under a separate maintenance agreement.

6. Cancellation

If Timely Entertainment cancels the project after the deposit has been paid, the $400 deposit is non-refundable and covers work performed to that point. If Williams Digital cancels, any unused portion of the deposit will be refunded. Late payments beyond 14 days of the due date may result in project suspension until the balance is resolved.
