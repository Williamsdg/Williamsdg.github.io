How wire fraud works

Most of the hard work is done before you ever walk through the door.

Your lender, real estate agent and title company work together behind the scenes so that, by closing day, you can focus on signing and celebrating.

  1. The title is ordered

    Your agent, your lender or you can order online. We make it easy, and our quick quote keeps you moving along.

  2. We search 30 years of records

    A 30-year search and examination is required for both purchase and refinance transactions. We scrutinize the public record for anything lurking in the property’s past:

    • Unpaid liens
    • Easement issues
    • Limits on future development
    • Contested ownership claims

  3. You receive a title commitment

    It spells out the requirements to insure the property, along with any exceptions that run with the land. We also arrange a survey, so the boundaries are what they are presented to be.

  4. We clear what comes up

    “Sometimes things do come up in title like a tax lien or a judgment. That is a part of our role, to make sure everything is cleared ahead of time so that nothing comes up once we get to the closing table.”

  5. Closing day

    Your closing team walks you through each document. Funds are collected and distributed, ownership is transferred, and title insurance policies are issued for you and your lender.

How long does it take? Set aside 10–14 days for a complete title search. The older the house, the longer it takes, because there are more records to investigate.
