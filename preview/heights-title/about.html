Service is our main focus.

Heights Title brings dedication and expertise to every transaction with the single goal of providing exceptional title and settlement experiences for all of our customers.

Locally owned in Cahaba Heights

Heights Title is a locally owned and operated full-service real estate title company located in Cahaba Heights, a community just outside of Birmingham, Alabama.

Whether you are a buyer, seller, real estate agent, builder or lender, as your closing provider we will deliver truly remarkable service and responsiveness.

With years of experience in the industry, and a depth of resources throughout the real estate industry, our passionate staff will see you through even the most challenging obstacles. Built on a foundation of integrity, knowledge and commitment to the customer, Heights Title wants to assure satisfaction for all involved and stand out in the industry.
