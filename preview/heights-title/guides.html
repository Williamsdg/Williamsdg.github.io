Before you wire a single dollar

Wire fraud doesn’t have anything to do with your title or your deed, which is exactly why it catches so many people off guard. It targets the actual money moving between you, your bank, and your title company right before closing.

  • Scammers often impersonate your title company by email right before closing.
  • Never wire money based only on instructions you got through email.
  • Always confirm wire instructions by phone, using a number you already have, not one from the email itself.
  • We will never change wire instructions by email without a phone call to confirm first.

Think you already sent money to a scammer?

Contact your bank immediately and ask them to attempt a wire recall, then file a report with the FBI’s Internet Crime Complaint Center. Speed matters, though recovery is never guaranteed.

We’ll never be offended if you call to double-check something that seems even slightly off.
