Est. March 1, 1991

From Oysters & Shrimp to a Pensacola Beach Legend

Peg Leg Pete's opened selling only oysters, shrimp, and a few sandwiches. What started small grew into one of the hottest spots on Pensacola Beach.

Locals love the great prices year-round. Tourists are treated as if they live here. We've always been known for our uncompromising commitment to quality food and friendly service.

— The Peg Leg Pete's Family
