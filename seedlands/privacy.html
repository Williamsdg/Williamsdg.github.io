Seedlands — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Seedlands is designed to respect your privacy. The short version: we do not collect any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Seedlands has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your best scores for individual map seeds locally on your device (using on-device storage). This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Analytics and tracking

Seedlands contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

Sharing

If you choose to share a seed, the app uses your device's standard share sheet to send a link you select (for example, to Messages). We are not involved in and do not see that activity.

Children's privacy

Seedlands does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
