## Description
```
Every seed grows a unique world.

Seedlands is a bite-sized map roguelike. Type a seed — any word — and watch it
generate an entire world: rivers, forests, mountains, glittering gems, and
deadly hazards. Explore through the fog, manage your energy, and reach the
portal before you strand.

The twist: a seed is just a tiny string, and the same seed always rebuilds the
exact same map. So when you find a brutal world, share the seed — your friends
play the identical map and you battle for the high score.

• INFINITE WORLDS — no two seeds are alike, generated instantly on your device.
• ONE-TAP SHARING — challenge anyone with a single seed link.
• DAILY SEED — a fresh world for everyone, every day.
• PURE & FAST — pick-up-and-play runs in under a minute.
• PLAYS OFFLINE — no account, no sign-up, no tracking.

Swipe, tap, or use the pad. Grab the gems, dodge the hazards, find the portal.
How far can your seed take you?
```
