3 Brand Assets & Photography Do First

The new site needs real photos of your art and you. Gather what you have in a Google Drive or Dropbox folder and share it with me.

  • High-resolution product photos of your art — at least 1500px wide. Existing photos from your Grace Gallery site work fine to start.
  • 2–3 lifestyle/in-context shots — your art hanging in a room, in your studio, on a wall in a customer's home, etc.
  • A professional headshot of you for the About section — or a good photo of you painting in your studio.
  • Logo files — if you have any existing logo work or sketches you want me to incorporate. I've already drafted a clean wordmark logo for you, but I'd love to see what you've imagined.
  • Your "About" story in your own words — 2–3 paragraphs about your art journey, what inspires you, and the story behind Chord & Canvas. I can write this for you if you'd prefer, just share notes.
4 Shopify Access & Setup Do First

Your existing Shopify store is going to power the checkout and inventory for the new site. I just need access to wire it up.

  • Add me as a staff member on your Shopify account. Login → Settings → Users and permissions → Add staff. Use my email: dylan@williamsdigital.io
  • Confirm your Shopify plan supports the Storefront API (Basic Shopify $39/mo and up all do). If you're on the trial or a lower plan, we may need to upgrade.
  • Clean up your Shopify product catalog — make sure each product has at least one good photo, a name, price, and description. Anything missing should be added or hidden.
5 Auburn Bookstore Partnership Details Coordinate

To build the wholesale portal for Auburn, I need to know how the partnership is structured. Get answers from your Auburn contact and let me know.

  • Confirm the deal structure with Auburn: wholesale buying (they buy upfront), consignment, or dropship? This affects how I build the portal.

6 Your Fulfillment Team Coordinate

Your admin dashboard needs login accounts for the printer and shipper. Get their contact info so I can set them up.

  • Printer's name + email — they'll get a login that only shows the print queue.
  • Shipper's name + email — they'll get a login that only shows the ship queue and shipping label tools.
  • Confirm the 2-week fulfillment promise with both of them so they know the expectation. If they have concerns about the timeline, we should adjust.
  • Shipping carrier preference — USPS, UPS, FedEx? This decides which service we integrate for printable shipping labels.
