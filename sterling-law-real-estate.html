Real Estate Law

From billion-dollar commercial developments to residential closings, our real estate practice provides the legal expertise needed to navigate complex property transactions, disputes, and regulatory challenges.

Building Your Real Estate Vision

Sterling & Associates' real estate practice serves developers, investors, landlords, tenants, and homeowners across every phase of property ownership. Whether you're acquiring a commercial portfolio or resolving a boundary dispute, our attorneys deliver strategic counsel grounded in deep market knowledge.

We work at the intersection of law, finance, and development — understanding not just the legal requirements, but the business realities that drive real estate decisions. Our team has closed transactions across 30 states and in international markets.
