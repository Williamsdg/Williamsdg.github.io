Full-Service Contracting

Your Single Source for Complete Construction

As a full-service general contractor, APEX takes complete responsibility for your construction project. We coordinate every trade, manage every timeline, and ensure every specification is met to the highest standards. When you hire APEX as your GC, you get a dedicated partner who owns the outcome from first shovel to final inspection.

Our general contracting services cover the full spectrum of construction needs. We maintain long-standing relationships with the region's top subcontractors and suppliers, ensuring competitive pricing, reliable scheduling, and consistent quality across every trade on your project.

  • Pre-Construction Services Budgeting, scheduling, constructability reviews, permit coordination, and value engineering before construction starts.
  • Subcontractor Management Vetted subcontractor network with competitive bidding, performance monitoring, and quality oversight for every trade.
  • Site Supervision Full-time on-site superintendents who manage daily operations, safety protocols, and quality standards.
  • Permit & Code Compliance Navigation of all building codes, zoning requirements, inspections, and regulatory compliance throughout the project.
  • Warranty & Aftercare Comprehensive warranty coverage with responsive aftercare support to address any post-construction needs.
