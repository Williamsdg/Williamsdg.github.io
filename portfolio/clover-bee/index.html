Clover + Bee

Clover + Bee is a long-established women’s boutique in Birmingham. We kept their Shopify store and built them a new front door: a storefront that becomes a Christmas shop, a game day shop, or a spring shop on schedule, without anyone rebuilding a page.

The problem

The shop had a strong Shopify catalogue, loyal customers, and a theme that looked like every other boutique. Every season meant hand-editing the homepage. Switching platforms was never an option: order history, rewards balances, and email flows all lived in Shopify and the apps the team relies on every day.

What we built

A custom storefront on Shopify’s Hydrogen framework, hosted on Oxygen. Shopify still owns products, inventory, customers, and checkout. The storefront has eight chapters, from spring through Christmas and game day, each with its own photography, colours, announcement bar, and featured collections, switched by a calendar the owner controls. Campaigns such as Black Friday and the gift guide layer on top without touching the rest of the site. Around that: a style quiz that lands on curated collections, Shop My Season colour palettes, saved favourites, a pick-up-at-the-shop line on every product page, and a customer account area with the shop’s rewards tiers.

How it is built

Chapters, copy, image slots, and the main menu live in Shopify metaobjects, so the owner edits them from a password-protected admin page we built and never needs a developer. Klaviyo email, Smile rewards, customer accounts, and order history all carried over on launch day, because the new storefront reads from the same store. Hosting on Oxygen is included with their Shopify plan, so there is no separate server bill.
