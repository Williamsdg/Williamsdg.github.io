Personal Injury

When negligence causes harm, victims deserve more than sympathy — they deserve justice. Our personal injury attorneys fight relentlessly to secure the compensation you need to rebuild your life.

Standing Up for the Injured

At Sterling & Associates, we believe that injury victims should never have to bear the financial burden of someone else's negligence. Our personal injury team takes on insurance companies, corporations, and healthcare providers with the same intensity we bring to our highest-profile cases.

We work on a contingency fee basis — meaning you pay nothing unless we win your case. From the initial investigation through trial or settlement, our attorneys are personally invested in your recovery and your future.
