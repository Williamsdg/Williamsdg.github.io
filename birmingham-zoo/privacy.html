Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how Birmingham Zoo collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Updated: January 1, 2026

The Birmingham Zoo ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our visitors, members, donors, and website users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, purchase tickets, sign up for memberships, make donations, or interact with us in any other way.

Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Purchase tickets, memberships, or gift shop items online or at the zoo
  • Make a donation or contribute to a fundraising campaign
  • Register for events, camps, education programs, or birthday parties
  • Sign up for our email newsletter or text alerts
  • Submit a contact form, survey, or feedback
  • Apply for employment or volunteer positions
  • Create an online account

This information may include your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, payment card details, date of birth (for children's programs), and any other information you choose to provide.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and usage, including:

  • IP address, browser type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs
  • Device type, screen resolution, and language preferences
  • General geographic location (city/state level)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process ticket purchases, memberships, donations, and event registrations
  • Send order confirmations, receipts, and membership renewal reminders
  • Deliver our email newsletter, event announcements, and promotional offers (with your consent)
  • Respond to your inquiries, feedback, and customer service requests
  • Improve our website, exhibits, programs, and visitor experience
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud
  • Administer contests, promotions, and surveys

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. These include:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core website functionality such as ticket purchasing and account login.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., Google Analytics). These cookies collect aggregated, anonymous data.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements and measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns on third-party platforms.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences for a better experience on return visits.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Third-Party Services

We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business, including:

  • Payment Processors: To securely process credit card transactions for ticket sales, memberships, and donations.
  • Email Marketing Platforms: To send newsletters, event announcements, and promotional communications.
  • Analytics Providers: To analyze website traffic and visitor behavior (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Ticketing & Registration Platforms: To manage online ticket sales, event registrations, and camp enrollments.
  • Social Media Platforms: If you interact with our social media content or use social login features.

These third parties are obligated to keep your information confidential and use it only for the purposes for which it was shared. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted data transmission (SSL/TLS), secure payment processing compliant with PCI-DSS standards, and restricted access to personal data within our organization.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. For zoo education programs, camps, and events involving minors, we collect information from a parent or legal guardian. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Opt-Out of Marketing: You can unsubscribe from our email communications at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email or by contacting us directly.
  • Access & Correction: You may request access to the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct any inaccuracies.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Cookie Management: You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Photography & Video at the Zoo

By entering the Birmingham Zoo, visitors acknowledge that photography and video recording may be taking place for promotional, educational, and security purposes. Images captured in public areas of the zoo may be used in our marketing materials, website, and social media channels. If you have concerns about the use of your image, please contact our marketing department.

Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including partner organizations, sponsors, and social media platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
