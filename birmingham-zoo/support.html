Support Your Zoo

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, your generosity helps us care for 900+ animals, protect endangered species, and inspire the next generation of conservationists. All donations are tax deductible.

Donate to the Birmingham Zoo

The Birmingham Zoo is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, and every dollar you give is tax deductible. Your donation goes directly to animal care, habitat improvements, conservation programs, and education. Recent investments include new shade structures for elephants, custom feeders for giraffes, and enhancements to the Alabama Wilds barn completed in 2024.

Conservation

Fund Species Survival Plans, breeding programs for endangered animals, and field conservation research that protects wildlife around the world.

Animal Care

Provide nutritious diets, enrichment activities, veterinary care, and habitat enhancements for our 900+ animal residents across 195+ species. Recent projects include new shade structures for the elephants and custom feeders for the giraffes.

Education Programs

Support Zoo School, summer camps, field trips, and community outreach that educate over 640,000 visitors annually about the natural world.

Ready to make a difference? You can make a one-time tax-deductible gift of any amount or set up a recurring donation to provide sustained support for the zoo's mission.

Monthly Giving

Become a sustaining supporter of the Birmingham Zoo by joining our monthly giving program. Recurring gifts provide reliable, predictable funding that allows us to plan ahead and invest in long-term projects for our animals and conservation work.

$10/month

Provides enrichment items and toys that keep our animals mentally stimulated and physically active throughout the year.

$25/month

Covers the daily food costs for a small mammal or bird and supports their nutritional wellness program.

$50/month

Funds a full day of veterinary care and medical supplies, helping keep our animals healthy and thriving.

$100/month

Supports habitat improvements and exhibit upgrades that create more naturalistic environments for our residents.

Monthly donors receive special updates about the animals their gifts support, invitations to behind-the-scenes events, and recognition on our donor wall. Consider pairing your monthly gift with a zoo membership for even more benefits.

Corporate Partners

Partner with Alabama's only AZA-accredited zoo and connect your brand with conservation, community, and family-friendly experiences. Corporate sponsorships support the zoo's mission while providing meaningful visibility for your organization among our 644,000+ annual visitors.

Event Sponsorship

Put your brand front and center at signature events like Tails in the Trails, Community Science Weekend, and Summer Zoo Camp.

Exhibit Sponsorship

Align your organization with a specific exhibit or animal habitat. The Wojciechowski family sponsored Cougar Crossing, and Senator Jabo Waggoner sponsored the flamingo sculpture. Includes prominent signage, digital presence, and naming opportunities.

Education Sponsorship

Fund field trips, Zoo School, and outreach programs that bring conservation education to schools and communities across Alabama. The Alabama Power Foundation sponsors ZooSchool, helping bring hands-on learning to children year-round.

Partnership Benefits

  • Logo placement on zoo signage, website, and printed materials
  • Complimentary zoo memberships and event tickets for employees
  • Private event hosting opportunities at the zoo
  • Social media recognition and co-branded marketing
  • Employee engagement opportunities including volunteer days and behind-the-scenes tours
  • Tax-deductible contribution supporting a beloved community institution

Interested in becoming a corporate partner? Contact our development team to learn about available sponsorship levels and custom partnership packages.
