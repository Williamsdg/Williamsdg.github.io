Learning & Engagement

From field trips to teen career programs, the Birmingham Zoo connects learners of all ages with the natural world through hands-on, inquiry-based experiences.

Field Trip Experiences

Schools & Groups

Bring your students to explore the wonders of the animal kingdom with guided, standards-aligned field trip experiences at the Birmingham Zoo.

🐘

550+ Animals

Students encounter over 550 animals representing approximately 180 species from around the globe, providing real-world connections to biology, ecology, and geography.

🔬

Hands-On Learning

Our field trip programs go beyond observation. Students engage in interactive, curriculum-aligned activities led by experienced zoo educators that bring classroom lessons to life.

🌍

180 Species

From African elephants to Alabama red-bellied turtles, students discover species diversity across continents and learn about the habitats that sustain wildlife around the world.

🦏

Endangered Species

Students learn firsthand about endangered and threatened species, the challenges they face in the wild, and the conservation efforts underway to protect them for future generations.

Book a Field Trip
Homeschool Families

Homeschool at the Zoo

The Birmingham Zoo offers specially designed programs for homeschool families that blend structured learning with the freedom of outdoor exploration.

🦎

Live Animal Demonstrations

Students get up close with ambassador animals during live demonstrations led by trained zoo educators and animal care professionals.

💬

Interactive Discussions

Facilitated group discussions encourage critical thinking, scientific questioning, and meaningful connections between animals and their environments.

🔍

Inquiry-Based Experiences

Hands-on, inquiry-based activities allow students to explore, hypothesize, and discover answers through direct observation and investigation.

🌿

Nature Exploration

Our 122-acre campus becomes a living classroom where students explore diverse habitats, observe seasonal changes, and connect with the outdoors.

🗺️

Guided Tours

Expert-led guided tours take homeschool groups through the zoo with a focus on specific themes such as animal adaptations, ecosystems, or conservation.

Learn More About Homeschool Programs
A School Like No Other

Alabama Power Foundation ZooSchool

A groundbreaking partnership with Birmingham City Schools where 7th graders attend school at the zoo for an entire year, immersed in an innovative, hands-on, standards-based curriculum.

🤝

Birmingham City Schools Partnership

ZooSchool is a unique collaboration between the Birmingham Zoo and Birmingham City Schools, providing public school students with an extraordinary educational experience at no extra cost.

🎓

Full-Year 7th Grade Program

Selected 7th graders attend school at the zoo full-time for the entire academic year, replacing the traditional classroom with 122 acres of living, breathing curriculum.

📚

Standards-Based Curriculum

The innovative, hands-on curriculum meets all Alabama state standards while integrating science, math, language arts, and social studies through the lens of zoology and conservation.

🧪

Hands-On Discovery

Students learn through direct animal observations, field research, habitat studies, and real-world problem-solving that connects every subject to the natural world around them.

Outreach Programs

Zoo to You

Can't make it to the zoo? We'll bring the zoo to you. Our outreach programs deliver unforgettable wildlife experiences to schools, offices, libraries, and community events across Alabama.

🏫

School Visits

Zoo educators bring ambassador animals and interactive presentations directly to your school, aligning content with grade-level standards and curriculum goals.

🏢

Office & Corporate Events

Add a wild twist to team-building days, corporate gatherings, and workplace events with live animal encounters and conservation talks from zoo professionals.

📖

Libraries & Community Centers

Our educators visit public libraries and community centers with live animals and engaging programs that inspire curiosity and a love for learning in all ages.

🎪

Community Events

From festivals to fairs, the Birmingham Zoo brings animal ambassadors and interactive educational booths to community events throughout the region.

🐾

Zoo Educators & Animal Care Professionals

Every Zoo to You visit is led by trained zoo educators and animal care professionals who share expert knowledge and a genuine passion for wildlife conservation.

Virtual Learning

Digital Field Trips & Discovery Programs

Where nature meets technology. Our digital programs bring the Birmingham Zoo experience to classrooms and homes anywhere in the world through live, interactive virtual sessions.

💻

Nature Meets Technology

Our digital programs mix the wonder of the natural world with the power of modern technology, creating immersive virtual experiences that captivate students of all ages.

🌐

Long-Distance Learning

No matter where your classroom is, the zoo is just a click away. Our virtual field trips bring live animal encounters and expert-led lessons to students across the country and beyond.

🎥

Live Interactive Sessions

Students participate in real-time virtual sessions with zoo educators, ask questions, observe animals live, and engage in guided discussions and activities.

📋

Curriculum-Aligned Content

Every digital program is designed to align with educational standards, making it easy for teachers to integrate zoo learning into their existing lesson plans and curricula.

Ages 12–17

Teen Programs

Discover what it takes to work in the world of zoos, aquariums, and conservation. Our teen programs give young people hands-on experience and mentorship from zoo professionals.

🌱

Conservation & Careers Program

Designed for teens ages 12 to 17, the Conservation & Careers program introduces young people to the wide range of career paths available in zoos, aquariums, and wildlife conservation.

🦁

Careers in Zoos & Aquariums

From animal care and veterinary science to education, marketing, and conservation biology, teens explore the diverse professions that keep modern zoos and aquariums running.

🧑‍🔬

Interact with Zoo Leaders

Participants get the rare opportunity to interact directly with zoo leaders, animal care professionals, veterinarians, and conservation scientists who share their expertise and career journeys.

🌍

Conservation in Action

Teens participate in real conservation projects, learn about Species Survival Plans, and understand how zoos play a critical role in protecting endangered wildlife around the world.
