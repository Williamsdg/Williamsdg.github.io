Careers at Birmingham Zoo

Birmingham Zoo is more than just a place to visit -- it is a workplace where passion meets purpose. Our team includes animal care professionals, educators, horticulturists, guest services staff, marketing specialists, maintenance crews, and many more dedicated individuals who all share one common goal: inspiring passion to conserve the natural world.

As Alabama's only AZA-accredited zoo and a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, we offer career opportunities across a wide range of departments. Whether you are an experienced zookeeper, a recent graduate in biology or education, or someone looking for a meaningful career change, Birmingham Zoo may have the right fit for you.

We are committed to building a diverse, inclusive workforce that reflects the community we serve. Birmingham Zoo is an equal opportunity employer.

To view current job openings or to submit an application, please contact us at (205) 879-0409 or visit our contact page for more information.

🌟

Benefits of Working at a Zoo

A career at the zoo is unlike any other workplace. Here are just a few reasons why our team members love what they do:

  • Make a Real Difference: Every role at the zoo contributes directly to wildlife conservation, animal welfare, and public education
  • Work in an Inspiring Environment: Spend your days surrounded by nature, wildlife, and 122 acres of beautiful grounds
  • Continuous Learning: Gain unique skills and knowledge through on-the-job training, professional development opportunities, and collaboration with conservation experts
  • Be Part of a Mission-Driven Team: Join a passionate community of professionals dedicated to protecting endangered species and educating over 640,000 visitors each year
  • Competitive Benefits: Full-time employees enjoy health insurance, retirement plans, paid time off, and free zoo admission for family members
  • Community Connection: Be part of an organization that has served Birmingham since 1955 and is a beloved destination for families across Alabama
