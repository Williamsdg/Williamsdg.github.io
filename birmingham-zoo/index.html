The Problem

Where the Current Experience Falls Short

We studied birminghamzoo.com and 1,173 TripAdvisor reviews. Here's what's costing you engagement, tickets, and memberships.

Hard to Plan a Visit

Hours, tickets, Nourish 205 menus, and zoo maps are scattered across pages. Families planning a trip to Cahaba Road give up before they even arrive.

Ticket Flow Takes Too Many Steps

The current ticketing path requires too many clicks. Every extra step loses potential visitors — especially on mobile.

Events Are Buried

Tails in the Trails, Zoolight Safari, Zoo Year's Eve — your best events are hard to discover. If visitors don't see them, they can't attend.

Mobile Experience Isn't Optimized

Parents in the parking lot searching on their phones hit a cluttered, desktop-first layout that makes everything harder.

Membership Conversions Lost

Memberships pay for themselves in 3 visits — but that message doesn't appear when someone is buying tickets. The highest-intent moment is wasted.

70 Years of Story — Not Being Told

Wojciechowski Cougar Crossing just opened. Baby Mopane was born. You have the only bachelor elephant herd in the country. None of this is front-and-center online.

The Business Case

Designed to Increase Revenue & Engagement

This isn't just a prettier website. Every design decision is built to drive measurable outcomes.

Faster Ticket Purchases

Prominent "Buy Tickets" CTA on every page. Pricing visible immediately — no hunting. Fewer steps from intent to purchase.

Membership Upsells at the Right Moment

"Pays for itself in 3 visits" messaging appears when visitors are already browsing tickets — the highest-intent moment.

Events Get Seen

Tails in the Trails, Zoolight Safari, Boo at the Zoo, Community Science Weekend — all front-and-center with dates, descriptions, and ticket links. More visibility = more attendance.

Donation Visibility

Support CTAs woven into stories about Bulwagi's bachelor herd, Juniper's rescue from Montana, and Mopane's birth. Visitors give when they feel connected.

Critical for a Zoo

Built for Families On the Go

Your audience is parents on phones in parking lots. Every screen was designed for that moment.

Buy tickets in seconds

One-tap ticket purchase. No zooming, no pinching, no frustration.

View today's events instantly

What's happening right now, what's feeding when, what's worth seeing — all immediately visible.

Simple navigation inside the park

Clean mobile menu with dropdowns that actually work. Find dining, restrooms, exhibits — fast.

Mobile-friendly exhibit browsing

Animal pages with large touch targets, readable text, and fast load times on any connection.

Where Birmingham Families Create Lasting Memories

A zoo website isn't just a booking tool. It's where a parent plans their child's first time meeting Bulwagi, Ingadze, and Lutsandvo — the only all-male elephant bachelor herd in a U.S. zoo. Where a teacher discovers Alabama Power Foundation ZooSchool. Where a teenager finds a conservation career they never knew existed.

The digital experience should feel as warm, inviting, and full of wonder as walking through the gates on Cahaba Road on a Saturday morning — past the bronze Flamboyant Flamingo, through Trails of Africa, and up to the Kiwanis Giraffe Encounter to feed baby Mopane.
