Become a Zoo Member

Unlimited visits, exclusive perks, and the satisfaction of supporting wildlife conservation every single day.

Member Benefits

A Birmingham Zoo membership is your year-round pass to adventure. From free admission to special events, members enjoy exclusive perks that make every visit even better.

Free Daily Admission

Visit the zoo as often as you want, every open day of the year. No tickets needed -- just scan your membership card and walk right in.

25% Off Guest Tickets

Bring friends and family along for less. Members receive 25% off general admission tickets for their guests at the gate.

Exclusive Member Events

Get early access and invitations to members-only events, including preview nights, holiday celebrations, and behind-the-scenes experiences.

Discounts on Experiences

Save on giraffe encounters, behind-the-scenes tours, zoo train rides, carousel rides, and other premium experiences throughout the zoo.

Support Conservation

Your membership directly funds animal care, Species Survival Plans, habitat improvements, and education programs that reach 640,000+ visitors annually.

Gift Shop & Dining Discounts

Enjoy savings at the Zoo Gift Shop and dining locations throughout the zoo on every visit. Discounts vary by membership tier.

Membership Tiers

Choose the membership level that fits your household. All tiers include core benefits like free admission, guest discounts, and member event access.

Membership Level Annual Price Includes
Individual $75 1 named adult, free daily admission, 25% off guest tickets, member event access, 10% gift shop discount
Dual $95 2 named adults, free daily admission, 25% off guest tickets, member event access, 10% gift shop discount
Family $125 2 named adults + children under 18, free daily admission, 25% off guest tickets, member events, 10% gift shop discount
Family Plus $155 2 named adults + children under 18 + 1 guest per visit, free daily admission, member events, 15% gift shop discount, experience discounts
Patron $250 2 named adults + children under 18 + 4 guest passes per visit, free daily admission, VIP member events, 20% gift shop discount, free parking, behind-the-scenes tours

Most memberships pay for themselves within 3 visits

General admission is $22 for adults and $17 for children. A Family membership at $125 saves a family of four over $100 per year with just monthly visits -- and you can visit every single day the zoo is open.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does my membership start?

Your membership is active immediately upon purchase and is valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. You will receive a temporary digital membership card right away, with physical cards arriving by mail within 2 weeks.

Can I upgrade my membership?

Yes! You can upgrade your membership at any time by paying the difference between your current tier and the new tier. Visit Guest Services at the zoo or call (205) 879-0409 to upgrade.

Do memberships include free parking?

Parking at the Birmingham Zoo is free for all visitors. Patron-level members also receive reserved premium parking near the main entrance.

Are memberships transferable?

Memberships are non-transferable. Named adults on the membership must present a valid photo ID at entry. Children listed on Family and Family Plus memberships must be accompanied by a named adult.

Can I use my membership at other zoos?

The Birmingham Zoo participates in AZA reciprocal admission programs. Dual-level memberships and above may receive discounted or free admission at participating zoos and aquariums. Check with Guest Services for current reciprocal partners.

How do I renew my membership?

You will receive a renewal reminder by email 30 days before your membership expires. You can renew online, by phone at (205) 879-0409, or in person at Guest Services. Renewing early extends your membership from the current expiration date.

What if I lose my membership card?

Replacement cards can be issued at Guest Services at the zoo. Bring a valid photo ID matching the name on your membership. You can also use your digital membership card in the meantime.
