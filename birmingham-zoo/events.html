21+ Adults Only

Tails in the Trails

The zoo after dark. Birmingham's wildest night out for grown-ups.

Tails in the Trails is the Birmingham Zoo's signature adults-only evening event. Guests 21 and over get exclusive after-hours access to the zoo with craft cocktails, local food vendors, live music, and up-close animal encounters -- all under the stars. It is one of Birmingham's most anticipated nights out.

Craft Cocktails & Local Brews

Sip on signature zoo-themed cocktails and local craft beers from Birmingham's best breweries as you stroll the trails. Multiple bars stationed throughout the zoo.

Food Trucks & Vendors

Sample dishes from Birmingham's top food trucks and local restaurants. From BBQ to gourmet bites, there's something for every palate along the trail.

Live Music & Entertainment

Enjoy live bands and DJs performing at stages throughout the zoo. Dance under the trees or enjoy acoustic sets while watching the animals settle in for the night.

Animal Encounters

Meet ambassador animals up close with keepers stationed along the route. Special nighttime feedings and keeper talks offer a unique after-dark perspective.

VIP Experience

Upgrade to VIP for exclusive lounge access, premium open bar, private animal encounters, and early entry before general admission doors open.

Event Details

Held select Saturday evenings, 6:00 PM - 10:00 PM. Must be 21+ with valid ID. General admission and VIP tickets available online. Members receive 15% off.

Kids & Teens

Summer Zoo Camp

Week-long adventure camps where kids explore nature, meet animals, and make memories that last a lifetime.

Birmingham Zoo Summer Camps offer week-long, hands-on adventure for kids of all ages. Campers explore the zoo behind the scenes, participate in animal encounters, enjoy outdoor exploration, and learn about wildlife conservation from real zookeepers and educators. Camps run Monday through Friday from early June through late July.

Wild Tots (Ages 4-5)

Half-day camp introducing our youngest explorers to the animal kingdom. Story time, crafts, nature walks, and gentle animal meetings designed for little hands and big imaginations.

Zoo Explorers (Ages 6-8)

Full-day camp with hands-on animal encounters, habitat hikes, science experiments, and creative projects. Each week features a different theme from rainforests to oceans.

Wildlife Rangers (Ages 9-12)

Full-day adventure camp with behind-the-scenes tours, keeper shadowing, conservation projects, and outdoor survival skills. Campers become junior conservationists.

Conservation Crew (Ages 13-15)

An immersive experience for teens who love animals. Work alongside zoo professionals, assist with animal care, conduct field research, and explore conservation careers.

What to Expect

Each camp week runs Monday - Friday, 9 AM - 3 PM (half-day: 9 AM - 12 PM). Extended care available 7:30 AM - 5:30 PM. All supplies, snacks, and a camp t-shirt included.

Registration & Pricing

Registration opens in February each year. Member discount of 15% on all camp sessions. Multi-week and sibling discounts available. Scholarships offered for qualifying families.

Celebrate at the Zoo

Birthday Parties

Throw a wild party they'll never forget. Birthday packages with animal encounters, private spaces, and zoo-themed fun.

Make your child's birthday unforgettable with a party at the Birmingham Zoo. Our birthday packages include private party spaces, zoo admission for all guests, animal encounters with our ambassador animals, and a dedicated party host to handle the details so you can enjoy the celebration.

Safari Party Package

Our most popular option. Includes a private party room for 2 hours, zoo admission for up to 20 guests, one animal encounter with an ambassador animal, party supplies, and a zoo gift for the birthday child.

Explorer Party Package

The ultimate zoo birthday. Everything in the Safari package plus a behind-the-scenes tour, two animal encounters, a giraffe feeding experience, and a keepsake photo package.

Party Add-Ons

Customize your celebration with additional animal encounters, carousel and train rides, catered meals from Nourish 205, custom zoo cakes, and extra guest passes.

What's Included

All packages include a dedicated party host, themed decorations, paper goods, zoo admission for all guests, and a special birthday announcement on the zoo's welcome sign.

Booking Info

Parties are available Wednesday through Sunday. Book at least 3 weeks in advance. A $100 non-refundable deposit is required to reserve your date. Members save 15% on all packages.

Group Events

Planning a larger celebration or group event? We also host school field day parties, scout troop events, and corporate family days. Contact our events team for custom quotes.
