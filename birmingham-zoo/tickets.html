💳

Payment Information

Please note that a 3% non-cash fee applies to all credit and debit card transactions at the ticket booth. This fee does not apply to online purchases.

Cash is available at the ticket booth for those who prefer to pay without the non-cash fee. ATM services are not available inside the zoo, so please plan accordingly.

We recommend purchasing your tickets online to skip the line and avoid the non-cash fee at the gate.

🎉

Ready for an Adventure?

Skip the line and save time by purchasing your tickets online. Your tickets will be valid for the date you select at checkout.

The zoo is open Wednesday through Sunday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Last entry is one hour before closing.
