Press & Media

News, press releases, and media resources from Birmingham Zoo. We welcome coverage that inspires conservation awareness.

Media Contact

For press inquiries, interview requests, and media information, please contact our Communications Department.

Communications Department

Recent Press Releases

📄

Birmingham Zoo Welcomes New Red Panda Cub

March 15, 2026 — Birmingham Zoo is thrilled to announce the birth of a red panda cub, born on March 8 as part of the Species Survival Plan. The cub and mother are healthy and bonding behind the scenes. Public viewing dates will be announced soon.

📄

Zoo Launches New VR Conservation Experience

February 1, 2026 — A groundbreaking virtual reality experience transports visitors to endangered habitats around the world. The immersive exhibit opens to the public on February 15 and is included with general admission.

📄

Record-Breaking Attendance in 2025

January 10, 2026 — Birmingham Zoo recorded over 644,000 visitors in 2025, marking the highest attendance in the zoo's 70-year history. New exhibits, expanded education programs, and community events contributed to the milestone.

📄

Trails of Africa Expansion Complete

November 20, 2025 — The final phase of the Trails of Africa expansion is now open to visitors, featuring enhanced habitats for the zoo's bachelor elephant herd, new shaded viewing areas, and interactive education stations.

📄

Birmingham Zoo Renews AZA Accreditation

October 5, 2025 — The Association of Zoos and Aquariums has renewed Birmingham Zoo's accreditation for another five years, recognizing the zoo's excellence in animal care, conservation, education, and guest experience.

Photo & Media Requests

We maintain a library of high-resolution images and b-roll footage available for editorial and news use. To request media assets, please note the following:

📷

Image Library

High-resolution photos of animals, exhibits, events, and zoo grounds are available for credentialed media upon request. All images must be credited to "Birmingham Zoo" and used for editorial purposes only.

🎥

On-Site Filming

All on-site filming, photography, and drone operations by media organizations require advance approval. Please submit your request at least 5 business days in advance with details about the scope, crew size, and intended use.

🎤

Interviews & Spokespeople

Zoo leadership, veterinary staff, and animal care specialists are available for interviews on topics including animal welfare, conservation science, wildlife education, and zoo operations. All interview requests should be directed to the Communications Department.

Quick Facts About Birmingham Zoo

  • Founded: 1955
  • Location: 2630 Cahaba Road, Birmingham, Alabama 35223
  • Size: 122 acres
  • Animals: 900+ animals representing 195+ species
  • Annual Visitors: 644,000+
  • Accreditation: Association of Zoos & Aquariums (AZA) — Alabama's only AZA-accredited zoo
  • Mission: Inspiring passion to conserve the natural world
  • Key Exhibits: Trails of Africa, Predator Building, Primates, Children's Zoo, Reptile House, Flamingo Lagoon
  • Signature Experiences: Giraffe Encounter, Behind the Scenes Tours, Zoo Express Train, Endangered Species Carousel
  • Education: Zoo School (Birmingham City Schools partnership), field trips, homeschool programs, summer camps, teen conservation careers program, Zoo to You outreach, and digital learning programs
  • Conservation: Participates in numerous AZA Species Survival Plans and community science initiatives
  • Hours: Wednesday through Sunday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Media Guidelines

  • All media visits must be scheduled through the Communications Department in advance.
  • Reporters and camera crews must check in at the front office upon arrival and display a media badge at all times.
  • Drone use on zoo property requires written authorization and FAA compliance documentation.
  • Animal interactions for media purposes are arranged at the discretion of the animal care team and are subject to the animals' well-being and comfort.
  • Images and footage provided by Birmingham Zoo may not be used for commercial or advertising purposes without a separate licensing agreement.
  • Please credit all zoo-provided images and footage to "Birmingham Zoo."
