Our Animals

900+ animals across 195+ species call Birmingham Zoo home.

Meet the Residents

Mammals

From the mighty African elephant to the tiny fennec fox, our mammal collection spans five continents and dozens of species.

🐘

African Elephants

Our bachelor herd roams the expansive Trails of Africa habitat, one of the largest elephant exhibits in the Southeast.

🦏

Eastern Black Rhinos

Critically endangered, Birmingham Zoo participates in the Species Survival Plan for this incredible species.

🦁

African Lions

The king of the savanna calls our Predator Building home. Watch them lounge, play, and survey their domain.

🦒

Reticulated Giraffes

Hand-feed our gentle giants at the Kiwanis Giraffe Encounter platform in Trails of Africa.

🦍

Western Lowland Gorillas

Observe our gorilla troop in their naturalistic habitat, complete with climbing structures and foraging areas.

🦧

Sumatran Orangutans

Critically endangered and remarkably intelligent, our orangutans never fail to captivate visitors.

🐆

Jaguars

The largest cat in the Americas, our jaguars showcase the power and beauty of these elusive predators.

🔴

Red Pandas

Fan favorites! These adorable, tree-dwelling mammals are always a must-see near the Predator Building.

🦊

Fennec Foxes

The world's smallest fox with the biggest ears. These desert dwellers are full of personality.

🐾

Cougars

Also known as mountain lions, these powerful cats are native to the Americas and are stealthy ambush predators.

🐾

Bobcats

Alabama's native wild cat can be spotted near the Alabama Wilds area of the Children's Zoo.

🐾

Coyotes

Adaptable and clever, our coyotes are part of the Predator Building's collection of North American carnivores.

🐻

Black Bears

Walk the Black Bear Trail in the Children's Zoo to see these Alabama natives in a forested habitat.

🦭

Sea Lions

Watch our California sea lions swim, play, and show off during daily keeper talks and enrichment sessions.

🦓

Zebras

No two stripes are alike! Our zebras share the Trails of Africa savannah with giraffes and other hoofstock.

Feathered Friends

Birds

With 80+ species, Birmingham Zoo is home to one of the region's most diverse bird collections.

🦩

Flamingo Lagoon

One of the first sights as you enter the zoo, our Caribbean flamingos dazzle with their vibrant pink plumage. Watch the flock wade, preen, and socialize at this iconic lagoon.

🦜

Lorikeet Interactive Aviary

Open seasonally, step inside the aviary and hand-feed colorful lorikeets as they land on your arms and shoulders. A favorite interactive experience for all ages.

🐦

Ostriches

The world's largest living bird calls our Trails of Africa exhibit home. These flightless giants can run up to 45 mph and weigh over 300 pounds.

🦅

80+ Bird Species

From raptors to waterfowl, parrots to penguins, our avian collection represents species from every continent. Keep your eyes open throughout the zoo for birds large and small.

Scales & Shells

Reptiles

Explore our impressive collection of reptiles from swamp-dwelling alligators to the formidable Komodo dragon.

🐉

Komodo Dragons

The world's largest living lizard, growing up to 10 feet long. Our Komodo dragons are a highlight of the Reptile Building and a reminder of just how amazing reptiles can be.

🐊

American Alligators

Visit the Southern Bayou exhibit to see these iconic reptiles in a habitat designed to recreate the wetlands of the Gulf Coast, complete with native plants and waterways.

🌿

Southern Bayou

This immersive outdoor exhibit transports you to the swamps and marshes of the American South, featuring alligators, turtles, and native aquatic species in a naturalistic setting.

🏛️

Reptile Building

Step inside to discover snakes, lizards, tortoises, and more from around the world. The climate-controlled building houses species from tropical rainforests to arid deserts.

Signature Exhibit

Trails of Africa

Opened in 2011, Trails of Africa is Birmingham Zoo's premier exhibit spanning multiple acres of naturalistic African habitats.

Take a walking safari through this award-winning exhibit and experience the sights and sounds of the African savannah right here in Birmingham. Trails of Africa is home to some of the zoo's most beloved animals and offers unforgettable encounters you won't find anywhere else in Alabama.

🐘

Bachelor Elephant Herd

Birmingham Zoo is home to a rare bachelor herd of African elephants. This specialized program provides social companionship for male elephants and supports AZA breeding recommendations.

🦒

Giraffe Encounter

Step up to the Kiwanis Giraffe Encounter feeding platform and hand-feed our reticulated giraffes. This up-close experience is one of the most popular activities at the zoo.

🦏

Rhino Habitat

Our Eastern black rhinos inhabit a spacious yard designed to mimic their native bushland. Birmingham Zoo plays a critical role in the Species Survival Plan for this critically endangered species.

🦓

Savannah Hoofstock

Zebras, ostriches, and other African hoofstock share a mixed-species savannah, just as they would in the wild. The panoramic viewing areas offer stunning views across the entire exhibit.

Family Fun

Children's Zoo

The Junior League of Birmingham Hugh Kaul Children's Zoo is a world of hands-on discovery designed for our youngest explorers.

🐑

Alabama Barn

Meet friendly farm animals including sheep, goats, and pigs at this interactive barnyard. Kids can brush and pet the animals while learning about Alabama agriculture.

🌲

Alabama Wilds

Discover the wildlife of our home state in this native-species exhibit. From bobcats and coyotes to red-tailed hawks, Alabama Wilds celebrates the biodiversity right in our backyard.

🐻

Black Bear Trail

Wind through a forested path to observe American black bears in a naturalistic woodland habitat. These Alabama natives are a highlight of the Children's Zoo experience.

🦋

Granny's Butterfly House

Open seasonally, step inside this enchanting greenhouse to walk among hundreds of free-flying butterflies. Learn about butterfly life cycles and the native species of Alabama.

🎠

Endangered Species Carousel

Take a spin on this beautifully crafted carousel featuring hand-painted endangered species. A family favorite and the perfect way to wrap up your Children's Zoo adventure.

🐾

Stay in the Loop

Get the latest zoo news, event announcements, and animal updates delivered to your inbox.
