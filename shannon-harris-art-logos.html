The Brushstroke

An elegant "S" formed from a single continuous brushstroke, accented with a sage leaf and blush paint drop. The freestanding mark works without a frame, letting the brushwork breathe. This concept nods to Shannon's signature scraper technique while keeping a refined, gallery-ready feel.

The Palette

Three overlapping color blocks — gold, sage, and blush — suggesting layered paint swatches or scraper strokes. This abstract approach emphasizes Shannon's gift for color balance and placement. Works beautifully on dark backgrounds and merchandise, and the overlapping forms hint at depth and texture.

The Monogram

A refined typographic monogram using oversized "S" and "H" letterforms in the serif typeface. The layered opacity creates depth, while the gold paint drop on the S and blush period add artistic personality. This concept is clean and timeless — perfect for labels, tags, and watermarks on prints.

The Oak

A reimagined acorn rendered entirely in brushstrokes — honoring the original Grace Gallery logo's symbolism of regrowth and resilience while evolving it for the Shannon Harris Art brand. The loose, hand-drawn quality reflects the organic nature of Shannon's painting process. This concept carries her story forward.

Typography

Primary — Headlines
Cormorant Garamond
Light weight, generous letter-spacing. Used for all headings, hero text, and the logo wordmark. Conveys elegance and artistry without feeling precious.
Secondary — Body
Montserrat
Light to regular weight, clean sans-serif for body copy, navigation, labels, and buttons. Provides structure and readability against the serif headlines.
