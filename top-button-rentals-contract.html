02

Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to provide the Client with professional digital services, which may include but are not limited to:

  • Website Design & Development — Custom design and development of a responsive vacation rental marketplace including property listings, search functionality, and user-facing application forms.
  • Platform Features — Development of rental listing management tools including property photo integration from Airbnb, geolocation-based "Near Me" search, filtering by location/type/price, and property detail modals.
  • Admin Dashboard — Development of a secure administrative panel for managing property applications, approving listings, and monitoring platform activity.
  • Website Hosting & Deployment — Setup, configuration, and management of web hosting environments including domain configuration and SSL certification.
  • Website Maintenance & Updates — Ongoing maintenance, content updates, feature enhancements, bug fixes, and performance optimization.
  • Airbnb Integration — Automated listing data import from Airbnb including property photos, descriptions, ratings, and guest capacity via structured data extraction.
  • Brand & Visual Identity — Development of digital brand elements including logos, color systems, typography, and visual assets for web and digital use.
  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO) — On-page optimization, metadata configuration, and best practices to improve search engine visibility.
  • Digital Consulting — Strategic guidance on digital presence, technology selection, and online business solutions.

The specific services to be provided shall be determined in consultation between the Service Provider and the Client and may be further detailed in a separate Statement of Work or engagement letter.

Revision Policy

The initial project includes up to three (3) rounds of revisions during the design and development phase. A revision round consists of a consolidated set of feedback submitted at one time. Additional revision rounds beyond the included three will be billed at the Service Provider's hourly rate of $50/hour. Revisions must be requested within seven (7) days of receiving a design proof or deliverable; otherwise, the deliverable will be considered approved.

Change Orders & Scope Changes

Any work requested by the Client that falls outside the originally agreed-upon scope of services will require a written Change Order. The Service Provider will provide a quote for the additional work, and no work will commence until the Client approves the Change Order in writing. Change Orders may affect the project timeline and will be billed separately from the original setup fee.

03

Fees and Payment

In consideration of the services provided, the Client agrees to compensate the Service Provider as follows:

Setup Fee

A one-time setup fee of $700.00 USD is required for the initial website design, development, and deployment. This covers custom design, responsive development, platform features, admin dashboard, hosting configuration, and launch of the completed website. The full setup fee is due upon acceptance of this Agreement.

Monthly Platform Fee

A recurring monthly fee of $30.00 USD for ongoing platform hosting, maintenance, updates, monitoring, and support. This fee covers server hosting, SSL certification, database management, security updates, content updates, and ongoing support and minor updates. The Service Provider will conduct scheduled check-ins on the 1st and 15th of each month to review platform health, address any issues, and ensure everything is running smoothly.

Payment Methods

Payment shall be submitted via the secure payment link provided by the Service Provider. Payment details and instructions will be included on the invoice.

Payment Terms

The one-time setup fee is due in full upon acceptance of this Agreement. The monthly platform fee begins upon launch of the live website. A late fee of 1.5% per month may be assessed on any balance remaining unpaid beyond thirty (30) days from the invoice date. The Service Provider reserves the right to delay or suspend services for any account with an outstanding balance exceeding thirty (30) days.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Should the project be cancelled by the Client after work has commenced, the setup fee is non-refundable as it covers discovery, planning, and development work already performed. The monthly platform fee may be cancelled at any time with thirty (30) days written notice. If the Service Provider cancels before any work has been performed, the full setup fee will be refunded.

Intellectual Property & Ownership

Upon receipt of full payment for the setup fee, the Client shall receive ownership of all custom-designed deliverables, including website files, graphics, and content created specifically for the Client. The Service Provider retains the right to use general techniques, tools, and methodologies developed or utilized during the engagement. Third-party assets (fonts, stock images, plugins) remain subject to their respective license agreements.

Portfolio & Credit Rights

The Service Provider retains the right to display and reference the completed website and related deliverables in their professional portfolio, marketing materials, case studies, and social media. The Client agrees that a small, unobtrusive credit line (e.g., "Powered by Williams Digital") may be placed in the website footer. If the Client wishes to opt out of the credit line, this must be agreed upon in writing prior to the commencement of work.

Domain & Hosting Ownership

Any domain names registered on behalf of the Client shall be owned by the Client. The Service Provider will register domains under the Client's name and contact information. Hosting accounts managed by the Service Provider remain under the Service Provider's administrative control for the duration of the service engagement. Upon termination and settlement of all outstanding balances, the Service Provider will assist in transferring access to the Client within a reasonable timeframe.

Both parties acknowledge that in the course of this engagement, confidential and proprietary information may be disclosed. Each party agrees to:

  • Maintain the strict confidentiality of all information received from the other party.
  • Not disclose, reproduce, or distribute any confidential information to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party.
  • Use confidential information solely for the purposes of fulfilling obligations under this Agreement.
  • Take reasonable precautions to protect the confidentiality of the information, no less protective than those used for their own confidential information.

This confidentiality obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement and continue for a period of five (5) years following termination or expiration.

05

Client Responsibilities

To facilitate the effective delivery of services, the Client agrees to:

  • Provide timely, accurate, and complete content, assets, branding materials, and information as reasonably requested by the Service Provider.
  • Respond to communications, design proofs, and requests for approval within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Designate a primary point of contact for all matters relating to this engagement.
  • Review and provide feedback on deliverables within the timeframes specified, understanding that delayed feedback may extend project timelines.
  • Ensure that all content, images, and materials provided to the Service Provider are owned by the Client or properly licensed.
  • Manage and respond to property listing applications submitted through the platform in a timely manner.

Delays or failure to meet these responsibilities may impact the timeline and effectiveness of the services provided, and the Service Provider shall not be held liable for any resulting delays.

Project Pause & Client Inactivity

If the Client becomes unresponsive or fails to provide requested materials, feedback, or approvals for a period of fourteen (14) consecutive days, the project will be placed on hold. A reactivation fee of $150.00 USD may apply to resume work after a project pause. Projects paused for more than sixty (60) days may be treated as cancelled under the Cancellation & Refund Policy outlined in Section 3.

Launch Sign-Off

Prior to the public launch of the website, the Service Provider will present the completed website to the Client for final review and approval. The Client shall have five (5) business days to review and provide written approval or submit final revision requests. If the Client does not respond within this period, the website will be deemed approved and the Service Provider may proceed with launch.

06

Term and Termination

This Agreement shall become effective upon acceptance by the Client and shall remain in effect until the completion of the agreed-upon services, unless earlier terminated as follows:

Termination by Either Party

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing thirty (30) days written notice to the other party. Upon termination, the Service Provider will provide the Client with all completed deliverables and assist with a reasonable transition period.

Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision and fails to cure such breach within five (5) days of receiving written notice.

Effect of Termination

Upon termination, the Client shall pay the Service Provider for all services rendered up to the effective date of termination. The Service Provider will deliver all completed work product and provide reasonable assistance for transition. All confidentiality obligations shall survive termination as specified in Section 4.
