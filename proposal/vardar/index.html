What if we need changes after launch?

Small edits (pricing tweaks, new fleet vehicles, photo swaps, copy fixes) are included in the monthly fee — no per-edit cost. Bigger features or new sections are quoted as add-ons, but you'll always know the price first.

Do we own the website and our data?

Yes. The website, all of your client and trip data, and your domain belong to Vardar. If you ever decide to leave, we hand it all over — no lock-in.

How do online bookings actually reach us?

When a customer fills out the form on the public site, it instantly shows up in the "Inquiries" area of your dashboard, and you get a text + email notification with the details so you can quote and confirm fast.

Can our drivers use this on their phones?

Yes. The driver chat and trip view are fully mobile. Drivers get their own login that only shows what they need — their schedule, their assigned trips, and messages from dispatch.

What does the $30/month actually cover?

Hosting (so the site is always online), SSL & security, daily backups, software updates, bug fixes, content edits, and direct support from us. Without it, the workspace and chat features stop working — that's why it's required.

Is there a contract?

No contract on the monthly. Cancel anytime. The build fee is one-time and covers the work whether you stay on the monthly or not.
