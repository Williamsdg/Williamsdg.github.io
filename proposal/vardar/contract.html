02

Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to design, develop, deploy, and support a two-piece package for the Client, consisting of:

A. Public Booking Website

A custom-designed, mobile-responsive marketing site presenting Vardar's services, fleet, and booking flow. Features include: animated hero, quick-book inquiry form, services grid (weddings, concerts, SEC game days, airport transfers, corporate travel, self-drive rentals), side-by-side self-drive vs. chauffeured pricing, a three-vehicle fleet showcase with rates, a featured game-day / tailgate package, testimonials, an FAQ, and footer with the Client's phone number and contact details.

B. Operations Workspace (Dashboard)

A private, login-protected internal workspace with the following modules:

  • Today / Home — daily triage view: live and upcoming trips, unanswered chats, quotes awaiting customer action, and vehicles due for service.
  • Quotes — build, edit, and send quotes via SMS or email; track status (draft, sent, viewed, accepted, declined); customer-facing quote acceptance page with deposit capture.
  • Calendar — hourly availability board across the entire fleet, color-coded by event type (airport, wedding, corporate, concert, game day, self-drive, maintenance), with day-by-day navigation.
  • Clients — searchable customer records with trip history, lifetime value, preferences, and notes.
  • Drivers — driver roster with credentials (CDL, DOT medical, FMCSA hours), trip history, and internal notes.
  • Chat — separate inboxes for client and driver conversations, with the ability to send quote cards and itineraries inline.

C. Setup & Onboarding

Domain configuration, branded email forwarding, SSL certificate, mobile-responsive optimization, and a one-hour onboarding walkthrough so the Client's team can use the workspace from day one.

Any work outside the scope above (e.g., custom integrations beyond what is listed, additional dashboard modules, branded mobile apps, paid advertising, or print materials) will be quoted separately in writing and is not included in the fees in Section 03.

03

Fees and Payment

In consideration of the services provided, the Client agrees to compensate the Service Provider as follows:

Build Fee — Total $700.00 USD

A one-time build fee of $700.00 USD covers the initial design, development, and deployment of both the public website and the operations workspace. This fee is split into two equal installments:

  • Deposit — $350.00 USD (50%) due upon acceptance of this Agreement. Work begins after the deposit is received.
  • Final Payment — $350.00 USD (50%) due once the live website and operations workspace are deployed and accessible at the Client's domain.

Monthly Service Fee — $30.00 USD / month

A recurring monthly fee of $30.00 USD covers ongoing hosting, security, support, and maintenance, including:

  • Hosting on a fast, secure platform with an SSL certificate.
  • Daily off-site backups of both the website and the operations workspace.
  • Software updates and security patches for the underlying platform.
  • Bug fixes and minor content edits (pricing changes, fleet vehicle additions, photo swaps, copy adjustments).
  • Direct email and text support, generally responded to within one business day.
  • Cloud storage and access for the Client's customer, trip, quote, driver, and chat data.

The monthly service fee begins on the date the website and workspace go live. The Client's first month is provided at no additional charge. The fee may be cancelled by the Client at any time with thirty (30) days written notice; cancellation results in the discontinuation of hosted services described in Section 02.

Payment Methods

Payment shall be submitted via the secure payment link(s) provided by the Service Provider. Acceptable methods include Stripe (credit/debit card), Zelle, or ACH bank transfer. Payment details and instructions will be included on each invoice.

Payment Terms

The 50% deposit is due in full upon acceptance of this Agreement. The remaining 50% final build payment is due upon launch. The monthly service fee is billed on or around the same day of each month following launch. A late fee of 1.5% per month may be assessed on any balance remaining unpaid beyond thirty (30) days from the invoice date. The Service Provider reserves the right to suspend services for any account with an outstanding balance exceeding thirty (30) days.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Should the project be cancelled by the Client after work has commenced, the $350.00 deposit is non-refundable as it covers discovery, planning, design, and development work already performed. If the Client cancels prior to launch, no final build payment shall be owed. The monthly service fee may be cancelled at any time with thirty (30) days written notice. If the Service Provider terminates the project before any work has been performed, the full deposit will be refunded to the Client.

Upon receipt of full payment for the build fee, the Client shall receive ownership of all custom-designed deliverables created specifically for the Client, including website files, design assets, and content. Portfolio & Credit Rights

The Service Provider retains the right to display and reference the completed website and workspace in their professional portfolio, marketing materials, case studies, and social media. Upon termination and settlement of any outstanding balances, the Service Provider will provide the Client with an export of all such data and assist in transferring access within a reasonable timeframe.
