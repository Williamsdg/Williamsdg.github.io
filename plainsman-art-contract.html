1. Scope of Work

Williams Digital agrees to design, develop, and deploy a custom Auburn-themed e-commerce site and an internal team dashboard for Plainsman Art. The build is delivered as two coordinated products — a public storefront ready for Shopify integration, and a role-based admin for the owner, partner, and fulfillment team.

Custom Auburn-Themed Storefront
Navy & orange brand system, Playfair + Inter typography
6 Curated Collections
Gameday, Historic, Toomer's, Signed, Skylines, Iron Bowl
Product Showcase & Filters
Filterable product grid with Signed / Limited / New tags
Giveaway Module
Signed-print giveaway block with 3-step entry flow
Custom Commission Section
Photo-to-print process with submission CTA
Cart Drawer & Shopify Hooks
Cart UI ready for Shopify Storefront API checkout
Three-Role Team Dashboard
Owner, Partner, and Fulfillment Team views
Order Pipeline & Reports
Kanban pipeline, customer LTV, revenue reports
Signed Editions & Giveaways Admin
Partner queue for signing sessions and giveaway management
Print & Ship Queues
Rush-prioritized print queue, ship-label workflow, completed/tracking
Mobile-First Responsive
Full storefront and admin polished for phone & tablet
SEO & Open Graph Setup
Meta tags, social previews, and shareable links

2. Timeline

  • Phase 1 (Week 1): Brand assets, photography, and product catalog gathered. Storefront content populated with real imagery and copy.
  • Phase 2 (Week 2): Shopify Storefront API integration, live cart and checkout flow, Printful product mapping.
  • Phase 3 (Week 3): Admin dashboard wired to live order data, role-based access set up, team training.
  • Phase 4 (Week 4): Domain cutover to plainsmanart.com, final QA, launch.

Estimated completion: 4 weeks from contract signing and deposit receipt.

Upon receipt of final payment, Plainsman Art owns all rights to the website design, content, and code developed under this agreement. Williams Digital retains the right to showcase the project in its portfolio and marketing materials.

6. Revisions & Support

This agreement includes up to two rounds of revisions during the design phase. Post-launch support is included for 30 days to address any bugs, configuration issues, or questions. Additional revisions or ongoing support can be arranged under a separate maintenance agreement.

7. Cancellation

If the Client cancels the project after the deposit has been paid, the deposit is non-refundable and covers work performed to that point. If Williams Digital cancels, any unused portion of the deposit will be refunded.
