OUR LOCATIONS

Find Your Nearest Cuban Coffee Queen

Five locations across Key West — from the cruise port to upper Duval, we're never far from your next cafecito.

Clinton Square

Cruise Port Favorite
291 Front Street #6, Key West, FL 33040
7:00 AM – 7:00 PM daily
(305) 922-2896
Street parking and nearby garage

Steps from the cruise terminal, Clinton Square is our most tourist-friendly location. Grab a cafecito and Cuban mix before or after your cruise. Fast service built for port-day schedules.

Best For

Cruise ship visitors, Clinton Square shoppers, and families looking for a quick authentic Cuban breakfast before heading out on the water.

Downtown (Key Lime Square)

Heart of Downtown

7:00 AM – 7:00 PM daily
(305) 294-7787

Right at the intersection of Southard and Duval, our Downtown location is where the locals meet the tourists. Full menu, shaded seating, and the best people-watching spot in Key West.

Downtown foot traffic, Duval Street shoppers, local regulars, and anyone exploring the heart of Old Town Key West.

Waterfront

The Original
284 Margaret Street, Key West, FL 33040
6:30 AM – 7:00 PM daily (opens earliest!)
(305) 292-4747
Street parking on Margaret Street

Our original waterfront window is where it all started. Opens at 6:30 AM — the earliest of all our locations. Grab your café con leche and watch the shrimp boats come in on the Key West Bight.

Best For

Early risers, waterfront walkers, fishing charter visitors, and anyone who wants to start their Key West morning the authentic Cuban way.

Duval Street

Upper Duval
1130 Duval Street, Key West, FL 33040
7:00 AM – 7:00 PM daily
(305) 985-5233

Our Upper Duval location brings Cuban Coffee Queen to the quieter end of Key West's most famous street. Less crowds, same incredible coffee and food. Great for a laid-back afternoon pick-me-up.
