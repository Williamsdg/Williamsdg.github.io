Welcome to the sunshine

A summer made of swimming, friendships & happy chaos.

For 28 years, Camp Sunnyside has been the place where kids unplug, get muddy, make their very best friends and discover what they're awesome at. Our 25-acre lakeside camp is a safe, joyful world built entirely for childhood.

Frequently asked questions

Core camp runs 8:30 AM–4:30 PM, Monday through Friday. Early drop-off (7:30 AM) and late pick-up (until 6:00 PM) are available as add-ons for $45/week each.

A swimsuit & towel, refillable water bottle, sunscreen, closed-toe shoes, a hat and a change of clothes. We provide lunch, snacks and all activity gear. Please leave electronics at home!

Every waterfront activity is supervised by certified lifeguards, all campers complete a swim assessment on day one, and we use a buddy-check system. Counselors are CPR & first-aid certified, and a nurse is on-site daily.
