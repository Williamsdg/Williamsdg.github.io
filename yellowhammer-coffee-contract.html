02

Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to design, develop, and deliver a complete custom website for the Client, including the following:

  • Main Website Homepage — A custom-designed, responsive landing page showcasing Yellowhammer Coffee's brand, story, locations, and menu highlights.
  • Join the Team Page — A dedicated careers and hiring page with position listings and application information for all locations.
  • 7 Individual Location Pages — Custom pages for each Yellowhammer Coffee location, featuring unique details, hours, directions, menus, and photography:
        Downtown Mobile • Dauphin Street • The Bank • Moffett Road • Providence • Bay City Trailer • Snowport
  • Responsive Design — All pages fully optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices with modern CSS and fluid layouts.
  • Custom CSS & JavaScript — Tailored styling, animations, and interactivity aligned with the Yellowhammer Coffee brand identity.
  • SEO Optimization — On-page metadata, structured markup, and best practices to support search engine visibility for each location.

Total Deliverables: 9 custom web pages with supporting assets (stylesheets, scripts, optimized images).

The initial project includes up to two (2) rounds of revisions during the design and development phase. A revision round consists of a consolidated set of feedback submitted at one time. Additional revision rounds beyond the included two will be billed at the Service Provider's hourly rate of $75/hour. Revisions must be requested within seven (7) days of receiving a deliverable; otherwise, the deliverable will be considered approved.
